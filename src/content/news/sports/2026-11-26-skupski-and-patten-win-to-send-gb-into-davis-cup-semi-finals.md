Great Britain's Neal Skupski and Henry Patten won a decisive doubles match against Germany's Kevin Krawietz and Tim Puetz. This victory secured Great Britain's place in the Davis Cup semi-finals for the first time since 2019.

## Key facts
- Skupski and Patten defeated Krawietz and Puetz in the doubles match.
- The win secured Great Britain's advancement to the Davis Cup semi-finals.
- This marks Great Britain's first semi-final appearance in the Davis Cup since 2019.
