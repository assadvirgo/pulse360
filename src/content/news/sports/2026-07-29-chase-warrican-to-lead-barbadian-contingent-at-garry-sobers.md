## Chase and Warrican to Represent Barbados at Garry Sobers' State Funeral

In a poignant moment for the cricketing community, Barbadian cricketers Roston Chase and Jomel Warrican are set to lead a contingent from Barbados to the state funeral of the legendary cricketer Garry Sobers. The funeral is scheduled to take place in Barbados, and both players will be traveling from Trinidad, where they are currently part of the West Indies Test squad preparing for an upcoming series against Pakistan.

Garry Sobers, who passed away recently, is widely regarded as one of the greatest cricketers in the history of the sport. His illustrious career spanned over two decades, during which he became known for his exceptional batting, bowling, and fielding skills. Sobers' contributions to cricket have left an indelible mark, not only in Barbados but across the globe. His ability to perform in all formats of the game has inspired generations of cricketers.

The decision for Chase and Warrican to attend the funeral underscores the respect and admiration that current players hold for Sobers. Both cricketers have expressed their deep sorrow at the loss of a figure who has been a source of inspiration for many in the cricketing fraternity.
