Keith Dudgeon, a right-arm fast bowler, has made a significant impact in English county cricket since his arrival. His performance during the 2026 season with Kent was particularly noteworthy, as he was instrumental in their successful campaign for promotion. Dudgeon's ability to swing the ball and maintain pace has made him a valuable asset in the competitive landscape of county cricket.

Having played for Kent for several seasons, Dudgeon showcased not only his bowling prowess but also his capacity to contribute with the bat when necessary. His all-round capabilities have drawn the attention of Sussex, who are looking to enhance their squad depth ahead of the upcoming season.
