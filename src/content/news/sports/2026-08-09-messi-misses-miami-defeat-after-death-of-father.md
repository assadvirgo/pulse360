## Lionel Messi Misses Match Following Father's Passing

Lionel Messi, the renowned Argentine footballer and captain of Inter Miami, was absent from the team's recent Leagues Cup match against Monterrey due to the death of his father, Jorge Messi. The match, held on Saturday night, ended in a disappointing defeat for Inter Miami, with Monterrey securing a 2-0 victory.

### A Personal Loss

Jorge Messi, who was 65 years old, passed away after a prolonged illness. The news has deeply affected not only Messi but also the global football community, which has expressed its condolences and support for the family during this difficult time. Messi's father was known to be a significant influence in his life and career, having played a crucial role in nurturing his son's talents from a young age.

### Impact on Inter Miami

Messi's absence was felt acutely by the team, which has been striving to establish itself in Major League Soccer (MLS) since his arrival in July 2023. The defeat against Monterrey marks a challenging moment for Inter Miami, as they aim to build momentum in the Leagues Cup. The team has faced difficulties in recent matches, and the loss against Monterrey adds to the pressure on both the players and coaching staff.

Head coach Gerardo "Tata" Martino acknowledged the team's struggles but emphasized the importance of focusing on future matches. "We have to regroup and find our rhythm again. It's a tough time for everyone, especially for Leo," Martino stated in a post-match interview.

### The Road Ahead

As Inter Miami prepares for its upcoming fixtures, the team will need to adapt to playing without their star player for the time being. Messi's contributions on the field have been instrumental in raising the profile of the club and attracting attention to MLS. His absence, however, serves as a reminder of the personal challenges athletes often face off the pitch.

The football world is rallying around Messi, with fans and fellow players expressing their sympathies through social media and public statements. The support highlights the strong sense of community within the sport, transcending team loyalties.
