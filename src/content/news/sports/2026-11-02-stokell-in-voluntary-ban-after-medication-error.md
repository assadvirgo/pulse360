Ireland cricketer Rebecca Stokell has begun a voluntary suspension following an inadvertent breach of reporting requirements related to prescription medication. Stokell stated that she will fully accept responsibility for the error.

- Rebecca Stokell is an Ireland cricketer.
- She has initiated a voluntary suspension.
- The suspension follows an inadvertent breach of reporting requirements regarding prescription medication.
- Stokell has expressed her intention to fully accept responsibility for the incident.
