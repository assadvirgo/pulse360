In a thrilling encounter that echoed the excitement of the 2025 final, Worcestershire emerged victorious against Hampshire, showcasing their resilience and skill on the cricket field. His innings was marked by a blend of aggression and composure, allowing Worcestershire to set a competitive total. Cullen's ability to anchor the innings while accelerating at crucial moments proved vital in the context of the match.

On the bowling front, Fateh Singh continued to impress with his consistent wicket-taking ability. Singh's performance included several key dismissals that stifled Hampshire's momentum and kept the pressure firmly on the opposition. His contributions with the ball have been a cornerstone of Worcestershire's bowling attack throughout the tournament.
