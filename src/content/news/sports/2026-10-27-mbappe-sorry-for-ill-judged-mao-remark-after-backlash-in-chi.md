Kylian Mbappe has apologized for referring to Mao Zedong as a "dictator" during an interview. His comments have led to backlash in China and resulted in him being dropped by Chinese brands.

## Key facts
- Mbappe made the remark during an interview about memes.
- He described Mao Zedong as a "dictator."
- Following the comments, he faced backlash in China.
- Chinese brands have dropped their association with him.
