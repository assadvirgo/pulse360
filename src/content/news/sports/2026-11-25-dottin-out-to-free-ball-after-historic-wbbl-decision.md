Brisbane's Sarah Glenn made history in the Women's Big Bash League (WBBL) by bowling a 'free ball' to dismiss Melbourne Renegades' Deandra Dottin. This occurred after Dottin was deemed too slow to reach the crease and face her first delivery.

## Key facts
- Sarah Glenn bowled to an empty set of stumps to dismiss Deandra Dottin.
- Dottin was ruled to have taken too long to face the delivery.
- This incident marks a historic moment in the WBBL.
