## Welsh Fire Hand Southern Brave First Defeat of the Season

In a gripping encounter at Cardiff, Welsh Fire secured a dramatic four-wicket victory over Southern Brave, marking the first defeat for the league leaders in this season's Hundred. The match, held at Sophia Gardens, showcased a blend of strategic gameplay and individual brilliance, ultimately shifting the momentum in favor of the Fire.

### Match Overview

Southern Brave, who entered the match with an unbeaten record, were put to the test by a determined Welsh Fire side. The Brave batted first, aiming to set a challenging target. However, they faced early setbacks as Welsh Fire's bowlers applied pressure, taking crucial wickets at regular intervals. The Brave's innings concluded at a total of 145 runs, a score that would prove to be less than sufficient.

Welsh Fire's bowling attack was led by their standout performer, who took three wickets and was instrumental in dismantling the Brave's batting order. The match reached a thrilling climax as Welsh Fire chased down the target with just a few balls to spare, highlighting their ability to perform under pressure.
