## Renshaw's Century Resurrects Australia After Mulder's Four-Wicket Haul

In a gripping display of resilience, Australian batsman Matt Renshaw scored a remarkable century, guiding his team to a total of 277 for 8 at lunch on the second day of the ongoing Test match. Renshaw's innings proved crucial in stabilizing the Australian side after a challenging morning session, particularly following the impressive bowling performance by South African pacer Wiaan Mulder, who claimed four wickets.

Renshaw's innings of 102 not out showcased his skill and determination, as he navigated the South African bowling attack with poise. His century not only marked a personal milestone but also served as a beacon of hope for the Australian team, who found themselves in a precarious position early in the match. Renshaw's ability to anchor the innings allowed other players to settle in and contribute, ultimately leading to a more competitive score.
