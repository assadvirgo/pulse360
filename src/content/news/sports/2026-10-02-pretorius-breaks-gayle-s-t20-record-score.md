## Lhuan-dre Pretorius Shatters T20 Record with Unbeaten 188

In a remarkable display of batting prowess, South African cricketer Lhuan-dre Pretorius has etched his name into the record books by scoring an unbeaten 188 runs in a domestic T20 match. This extraordinary feat surpasses the previous record held by West Indies legend Chris Gayle, who scored 175 runs in a T20 match in 2013.
