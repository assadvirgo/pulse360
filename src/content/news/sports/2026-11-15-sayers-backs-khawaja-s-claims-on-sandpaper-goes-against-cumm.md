Chadd Sayers has supported Usman Khawaja's claims regarding the sandpaper incident during the South Africa tour, contradicting Pat Cummins' statements. Sayers, who played in the Test match, stated that he assisted in removing sandpaper from the field before the Cape Town Test.

## Key facts
- Chadd Sayers is a one-cap Test pace bowler who participated in the South Africa tour.
- Sayers claims that everyone was aware of the plans related to the sandpaper incident.
- He stated that he helped take sandpaper from the field in the match before the Cape Town Test.
- Sayers backs Usman Khawaja's recollection of events over that of Pat Cummins.
