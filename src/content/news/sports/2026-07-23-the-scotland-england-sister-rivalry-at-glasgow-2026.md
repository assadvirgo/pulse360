Astrid and Ursula Carroll, both in their teenage years, have been training rigorously in the pool, honing their skills and preparing for the prestigious Commonwealth Games. Their upcoming competition will not only showcase their individual talents but also highlight the complexities of national identity and familial bonds in the world of sports. ### The Significance of the Commonwealth Games

The Commonwealth Games, often referred to as the "Friendly Games," bring together athletes from across the Commonwealth of Nations, fostering camaraderie and competition. The 2026 edition in Glasgow is particularly significant, as it marks a return to Scotland for the Games, which last hosted the event in 2014. The presence of the Carroll sisters adds an intriguing layer to the competition, as spectators will witness not only the athletic prowess of these young swimmers but also the emotional stakes involved in their rivalry.
