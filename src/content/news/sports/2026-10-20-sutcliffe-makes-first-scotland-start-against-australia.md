Glasgow Warriors hooker Aicha Sutcliffe will make her first Scotland start in the final WXV Global Series match against Australia in Canberra. This decision is part of three changes made by interim head coach Dave Butcher.

## Key facts
- Aicha Sutcliffe is a hooker for Glasgow Warriors.
- Sutcliffe will make her first start for Scotland.
- The match against Australia is part of the WXV Global Series.
- The match is scheduled to take place in Canberra on Friday.
- Interim head coach Dave Butcher made three changes to the team.
