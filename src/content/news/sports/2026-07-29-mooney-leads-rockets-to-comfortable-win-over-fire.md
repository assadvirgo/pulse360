In a commanding performance, the Trent Rockets secured their second win in The Hundred women's competition, defeating Welsh Fire by eight wickets in Cardiff. The match showcased the Rockets' strong batting and strategic gameplay, solidifying their position in the tournament.

Her ability to anchor the innings while also accelerating the scoring rate proved crucial.
