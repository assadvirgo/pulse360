Australia secured the men's rugby league World Cup title by defeating New Zealand 32-12 in the final. This victory marks Australia's fourth consecutive World Cup win and their ninth title in the last ten tournaments.

## Key facts
- Australia scored six tries in the final match.
- The final score was 32-12 in favor of Australia.
- This is Australia's fourth World Cup title in a row.
- Australia has now won the tournament nine times in the last ten editions.
