Grimsby Town, a League Two side, came from behind to defeat Stafford Rangers in the FA Cup first round. After trailing by two goals, Grimsby equalized at 3-3 and ultimately won 3-2 in the penalty shootout.

## Key facts
- Grimsby Town faced Stafford Rangers, the lowest-ranked team in the FA Cup first round.
- Grimsby recovered from a two-goal deficit to level the match at 3-3.
- The match concluded with a penalty shootout, where Grimsby won 3-2.
- This victory secured Grimsby a place in the second round of the FA Cup.
