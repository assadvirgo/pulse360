An amateur cricketer has received a two-year ban due to his involvement in the 'Clicky Ponting' cheating scandal, which gained international attention. The incident became widely known after online footage circulated during the English summer.

## Key facts
- An amateur cricketer is banned for two years for his role in the 'Clicky Ponting' cheating scandal, according to BBC Sport.
- The scandal made headlines globally, as reported by ESPN Cricinfo.
- The controversy went viral during the English summer following the circulation of online footage of the incident.
