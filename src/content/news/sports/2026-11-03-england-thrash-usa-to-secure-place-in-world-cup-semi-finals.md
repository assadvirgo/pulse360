England secured a place in the semi-finals of the Wheelchair Rugby League World Cup with a significant victory over the USA. The match ended with England scoring 23 tries, resulting in a 134-0 win at the Wollongong Entertainment Arena in New South Wales.

## Key facts
- England won 134-0 against the USA.
- The match took place at the Wollongong Entertainment Arena in New South Wales.
- England scored 23 tries during the game.
- This victory secured England's place in the semi-finals of the Wheelchair Rugby League World Cup.
- England achieved this with one group match remaining.
