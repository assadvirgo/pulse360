Hull KR, who have struggled to find form in recent matches, approached this game with a renewed sense of determination. The victory not only serves as a turning point for the team but also as a significant psychological advantage in their ongoing campaign.

Throughout the match, Hull KR showcased their resilience and tactical prowess. Despite facing a spirited performance from Hull FC, the visitors capitalized on critical opportunities. Hull FC, on the other hand, faced challenges in maintaining their composure under pressure. For Hull KR, this victory is a much-needed respite from a series of disappointing results.
