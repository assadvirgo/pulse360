Alice Kinsella completed her return to gymnastics after a year away due to giving birth, performing a "brilliant" floor routine in the women's all-around final at the Artistic Gymnastics World Championships in Rotterdam, according to BBC Sport.

- Kinsella performed in the women's all-around final.
