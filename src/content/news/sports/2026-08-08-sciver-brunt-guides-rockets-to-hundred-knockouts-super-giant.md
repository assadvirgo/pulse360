## Sciver-Brunt Leads Trent Rockets to Knockout Stage in The Hundred

In an impressive display of skill and determination, Nat Sciver-Brunt's commanding innings of 67 runs propelled the Trent Rockets into the knockout stage of The Hundred, following a decisive nine-wicket victory over MI London. This match, held at the iconic Trent Bridge, showcased Sciver-Brunt's ability to perform under pressure, as she guided her team with a blend of aggression and finesse.

### Match Highlights

The Trent Rockets, needing a win to secure their place in the next round, faced MI London in a crucial encounter. After winning the toss, the Rockets opted to field first. MI London struggled to build momentum, with their batting lineup faltering against a disciplined bowling attack. The Rockets' bowlers executed their plans effectively, restricting MI London to a modest total.

Sciver-Brunt, known for her elegant stroke play, stepped onto the crease with the intent to chase down the target. Her innings was marked by a series of boundaries that not only showcased her technical prowess but also energized the crowd. Alongside her teammates, she orchestrated a successful chase, leading the Rockets to a comfortable victory and ensuring their progression in the tournament.

### Manchester Super Giants Triumph Over Southern Brave
