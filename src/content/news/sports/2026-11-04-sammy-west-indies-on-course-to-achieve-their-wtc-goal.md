West Indies coach Daren Sammy has expressed confidence in the team's progress and ambitions as they approach their last match of the 2025-27 World Test Championship cycle. He highlighted the team's unbeaten Test series run.

- The team is currently on an unbeaten Test series run.
- They are preparing for their last match of the 2025-27 World Test Championship cycle against Bangladesh.
