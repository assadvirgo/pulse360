## The Hundred 2026 Squads Announced

As anticipation builds for the 2026 editions of The Hundred, both men's and women's competitions, teams have begun to unveil their rosters. The Hundred, a unique format of cricket introduced in 2021, has quickly gained popularity in the United Kingdom, attracting both seasoned players and emerging talents. 

### Overview of The Hundred

The Hundred is a professional franchise cricket tournament featuring eight men's and women's teams. Each match consists of 100 balls per side, designed to create a fast-paced and engaging viewing experience. The format has been praised for its accessibility, making cricket appealing to a broader audience. 

### Key Signings for 2026

In preparation for the 2026 season, franchises have made strategic signings to bolster their squads. Notable players from around the globe have committed to the competition, promising to elevate the level of play and enhance the spectacle for fans.
