Seamer Jai Moondra achieved a record by finishing with the best-ever bowling figures for an Ireland bowler in one-day international cricket. His performance helped Ireland level their five-game series against Namibia with a 130-run victory.

## Key facts
- Jai Moondra took 6 wickets for 26 runs.
- This performance is the best ever for an Ireland bowler in one-day internationals.
- Ireland's victory was by a margin of 130 runs.
- The series against Namibia is a five-game series.
