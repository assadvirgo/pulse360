### Overview of The Hundred

The Hundred is a professional franchise 100-ball cricket tournament in England and Wales, designed to attract a broader audience to the sport. Each match consists of 100 balls per side, with teams comprising both established stars and emerging talents. The format aims to provide a fast-paced and entertaining experience for spectators, making it an appealing option for families and younger fans.
