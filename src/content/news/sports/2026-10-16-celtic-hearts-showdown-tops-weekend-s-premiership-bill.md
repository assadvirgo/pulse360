Their recent performances have been marked by a blend of experienced players and emerging talent, making them a formidable opponent for any team.

On the other hand, Heart of Midlothian, often referred to simply as Hearts, has also demonstrated resilience and skill throughout the season. Known for their passionate fan base and competitive spirit, Hearts will aim to capitalize on any weaknesses in the Celtic lineup.
