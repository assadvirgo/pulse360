Former South Africa batter AB de Villiers has claimed that Australia was tampering with the ball during the first Test of 2018. He expressed certainty about the allegations, stating that the ball reversed too early in the match.

## Key facts
- AB de Villiers stated that there is "absolutely no doubt" that Australia altered the ball during the opening Test in 2018, according to BBC Sport.
- He noted that the ball reversed "way too early" in the first Test, as reported by ESPN Cricinfo.
