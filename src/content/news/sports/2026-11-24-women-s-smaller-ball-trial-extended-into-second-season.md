World Rugby has extended the women's 4.5 ball trial into a second season. This decision follows the release of data indicating improvements in various aspects of the game, along with overwhelmingly positive feedback from players and coaches.

## Key facts
- World Rugby has extended the women's 4.5 ball trial for a second season.
- The extension is based on data showing improvements in several areas of the game.
- Players and coaches provided overwhelmingly positive feedback regarding the trial.
