## Hughes Focused on Albania Before World Cup Dream

As the excitement builds for the upcoming international fixtures, Wales forward Elise Hughes has expressed her determination to focus on the crucial match against Albania. For Hughes, qualifying for the World Cup represents not just a significant milestone in her career but also the fulfillment of a lifelong ambition.

### A Lifelong Dream

Elise Hughes, who has been making waves in women's football, shared her thoughts on the journey leading up to this pivotal moment. "Reaching the World Cup would be a dream come true," she remarked. The forward has been a key player for the Welsh national team, showcasing her skills and commitment on the pitch. Her aspirations are not just personal; they reflect the growing ambitions of women's football in Wales.

### The Importance of the Match Against Albania

The upcoming match against Albania is a critical step in Wales' quest for World Cup qualification. Hughes emphasized the importance of this fixture, stating that every game counts in the journey towards the World Cup. "We know what’s at stake, and we are prepared to give our all," she said, highlighting the team's focus and determination.

With the pressure mounting, Hughes and her teammates are aware that they must perform at their best to secure a favorable outcome. The match is not only a test of skill but also of mental fortitude, as the team aims to navigate the challenges ahead.

### Building Momentum

Wales has been steadily improving in recent years, and the momentum they have built is palpable. Hughes has been an integral part of this progress, contributing both on and off the field. Her experience and leadership are invaluable assets as the team gears up for this important clash.

The support from fans and the growing interest in women's football in Wales have also played a significant role in motivating the players. Hughes noted, "Having the backing of our supporters makes a huge difference. We want to make them proud."

### Looking Ahead

As the World Cup qualifiers approach, the focus remains on the immediate task at hand. Hughes and her teammates are committed to putting in the hard work necessary to achieve their goals. The prospect of competing on the world stage is a powerful motivator for the squad.

In conclusion, Elise Hughes' ambition to reach the World Cup is emblematic of the aspirations shared by many in the Welsh women's football community. With the match against Albania looming, all eyes will be on Hughes and her teammates as they strive to turn their dreams into reality. The journey is challenging, but with determination and teamwork, the Welsh national team is poised to make its mark on the international stage.
