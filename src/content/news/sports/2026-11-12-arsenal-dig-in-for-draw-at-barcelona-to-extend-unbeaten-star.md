Arsenal extended their unbeaten start to four matches in the UEFA Women's Champions League by holding Barcelona to a 1-1 draw at the Camp Nou. This result positions Arsenal in the top four, enhancing their chances of automatically qualifying for the knockout stages.

## Key facts
- Arsenal played Barcelona to a 1-1 draw in the UEFA Women's Champions League.
- The match took place at the Camp Nou.
- Arsenal's unbeaten streak now stands at four matches.
- The draw places Arsenal in the top four of their group. 
- This positioning improves their chances for automatic qualification to the knockout stages, according to BBC Sport.
