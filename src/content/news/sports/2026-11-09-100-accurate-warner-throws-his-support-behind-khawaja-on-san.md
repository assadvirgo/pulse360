Cricket Australia has stated that it is "comfortable" with the findings of its integrity process regarding the 2018 ball-tampering scandal. David Warner has expressed his full support for former teammate Usman Khawaja, who claimed that all Australian players were aware of the ball-tampering.

## Key facts
- Cricket Australia commented on its investigation into the 2018 ball-tampering scandal.
- David Warner supports Usman Khawaja's assertion that all Australian players knew about the ball-tampering.
- Warner described Khawaja's claims as "100% accurate."
