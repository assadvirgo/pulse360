Scotstown manager David McCague has acknowledged the anger expressed by St Eunan's following a score controversy in their Ulster Club SFC semi-final match. St Eunan's has lodged a formal objection regarding the decision to award Scotstown a late equaliser during the game.

## Key facts
- David McCague is the manager of Scotstown.
- St Eunan's has lodged a formal objection.
- The controversy arose from a late extra-time equaliser awarded to Scotstown.
- The match took place at Brewster Park.
