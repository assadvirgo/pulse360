### Overview of The Hundred

The Hundred is a professional franchise cricket tournament that features eight men's and eight women's teams, each representing major cities across England and Wales. The format is designed to be fast-paced and engaging, with each match consisting of 100 balls per side. This unique approach has attracted a diverse audience, including families and younger fans, contributing to the growth of cricket in the region.
