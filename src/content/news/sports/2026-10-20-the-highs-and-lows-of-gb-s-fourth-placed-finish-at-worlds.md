Great Britain finished fourth in the men's team final at the 2026 Artistic World Championships, narrowly missing a podium finish. The British squad led the competition after four rotations but experienced costly slips on the final apparatuses.

## Key facts
- Great Britain finished fourth in the men's team final at the 2026 Artistic World Championships.
- The British squad led the competition after four rotations.
- Costly slips on the final apparatuses prevented them from securing a medal.
- Japan won the gold medal.
- Great Britain will have another chance to qualify for the LA28 Olympics at the World Championships in Chengdu next year.
