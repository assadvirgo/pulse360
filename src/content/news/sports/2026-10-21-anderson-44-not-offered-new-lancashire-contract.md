James Anderson, the all-time leading wicket-taker for England, has not been offered a new contract by Lancashire, which may signal the end of his career. The decision raises questions about his future in cricket.

## Key facts
- Lancashire has opted not to offer James Anderson a new contract.
- Anderson is 44 years old and is England's leading Test wicket-taker.
- The decision could lead to Anderson's retirement.
