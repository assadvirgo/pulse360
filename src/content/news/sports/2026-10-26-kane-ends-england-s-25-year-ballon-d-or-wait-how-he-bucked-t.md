Harry Kane has won the 2026 Ballon d'Or, marking the first time an English player has received this award since Michael Owen in 2001. The ceremony took place in London.

## Key facts
- Harry Kane was crowned the world's best player at a ceremony in London.
- He is the first English player to win the Ballon d'Or in 25 years.
- Michael Owen was the last Englishman to win the award in 2001.
