Bettors linked to Emmanuel Clase reportedly wagered thousands of dollars on specific pitches ahead of two Cleveland Guardians games in June 2025. This information was presented during the federal gambling trial of Clase and Luis Ortiz, according to testimony from a FanDuel attorney.

## Key facts
- Bettors risked thousands of dollars on specific pitch outcomes.
- The wagers were placed ahead of two Cleveland Guardians games.
- The testimony was given during the federal gambling trial of Emmanuel Clase and Luis Ortiz.
