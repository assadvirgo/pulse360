## Luke Littler Triumphs at World Matchplay, Sets New Records

In a remarkable display of skill and determination, Luke Littler has successfully defended his title at the World Matchplay, held in Blackpool, by defeating Gerwyn Price with a score of 18-9. This victory not only solidifies Littler's status as a top competitor in the sport but also sees him setting new records for both tournament average and final average.

Littler's performance throughout the tournament has been nothing short of extraordinary. His average score during the event surpassed previous records, showcasing his precision and consistency under pressure.
