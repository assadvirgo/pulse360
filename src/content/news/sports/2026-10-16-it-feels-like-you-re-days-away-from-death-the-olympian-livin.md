Beth Dobbin, an Olympian and world record holder, has shared her health struggles after retiring from competitive sports. She is currently bedbound for more than 22 hours a day due to her condition.

## Key facts
- Beth Dobbin is a sprinter, a world record holder, and a Commonwealth Games medal winner.
- She has been bedbound for more than 22 hours a day.
- Dobbin shared her experiences with BBC Sport months after retiring from the sport for good.
