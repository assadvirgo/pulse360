Britain's Sonay Kartal lost the Wuhan Open title to Maja Chwalinska, who was the runner-up at the French Open. Kartal was defeated with a score of 2-6, 6-4, 6-2. This final marks a significant achievement for Kartal, as it is only her third event since returning from a six-month lay-off due to a back injury.

## Key facts
- Sonay Kartal lost to Maja Chwalinska in the Wuhan Open final.
- The final score was 2-6, 6-4, 6-2.
- This was Kartal's third event after a six-month lay-off due to a back injury.
