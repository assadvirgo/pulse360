Zak Crawley has signed a one-year contract extension with Kent after his England central contract was not renewed. This extension means that Kent will become Crawley's primary employer again following six years with the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB).

## Key facts
- Zak Crawley signed a one-year contract extension with Kent.
- His England central contract was not renewed.
- Crawley will return to being primarily employed by Kent after six years with the ECB.
