Spanish soccer club Tenerife honored Jesús de Miguel in their first match following his death. Midfielder Nacho Gil displayed De Miguel's jersey after scoring the opening goal shortly before halftime.

## Key facts
- Tenerife played their first match since the death of Jesús de Miguel.
- Nacho Gil held up De Miguel's jersey after scoring the opening goal.
- The tribute occurred shortly before halftime.
