## West Indies Bat First in Final Test Against Pakistan

In a crucial match for both teams, the West Indies have opted to bat first in the third and final Test against Pakistan, aiming to secure a series sweep after winning the first two matches. The match is being held at the National Stadium in Karachi, where conditions are expected to favor batting.

### Series Context

The West Indies currently lead the series 1-0, having won the second Test convincingly. With the series on the line, the West Indies are looking to capitalize on their momentum and secure a clean sweep against their opponents. The team has shown resilience and skill throughout the series, particularly in their batting lineup, which has been bolstered by several key players stepping up in crucial moments.

### Changes in the Pakistan Squad

In a notable development, Pakistan has made significant changes to their lineup for this Test. Fast bowler Mohammad Abbas has been dropped from the squad, a decision that reflects the team's strategy to inject fresh talent into the playing XI. In his place, Awais Zafar and Ubaid Shah have been handed their Test debuts, marking an important milestone in their cricketing careers. Both players are expected to bring new energy and perspective to the team as they look to level the series.

### Expectations from Debutants

Awais Zafar, a promising young batsman, has been in impressive form in domestic cricket, and his inclusion is seen as a long-term investment in Pakistan's batting future. Ubaid Shah, a fast bowler, has also shown potential in the domestic circuit and will be keen to make an impact in his first Test appearance. The success of these debutants could be pivotal for Pakistan, who are looking to rebound from their previous losses.

### Key Players to Watch

As the match unfolds, all eyes will be on the West Indies' batting order, particularly their experienced players who have been instrumental in their recent successes. The likes of Kraigg Brathwaite and Jason Holder will be crucial in setting a strong foundation for the innings. For Pakistan, the performance of the debutants will be closely monitored, as well as the contributions from established players like Babar Azam and Shaheen Afridi, who are expected to lead the charge for their team.

### Conclusion

With the West Indies batting first and Pakistan making strategic changes to their lineup, the final Test promises to be an exciting contest. Both teams have much to play for, and the outcome will not only determine the series winner but also provide insights into the future direction of both cricketing nations. As the match progresses, fans and analysts alike will be keen to see how the debutants perform under pressure and whether the West Indies can maintain their winning streak.
