Chinese painter and filmmaker Qiu Jiongjiong has created a new film titled “Fuxi: Four Tales of Feasting,” which is competing at the Tokyo Competition. The film was shaped during the pandemic, reflecting a collective longing for everyday life and communal gatherings.

## Key facts
- Qiu Jiongjiong began shaping his film during the pandemic.
- The film's concept is rooted in the urgent desire to return to everyday life and gather around a table.
- “Fuxi: Four Tales of Feasting” is competing at the Tokyo Competition.
