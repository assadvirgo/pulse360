A new Japanese anime awards program has been named the Nippon Anime Awards, with the inaugural ceremony scheduled for November 21, 2027, in Tokyo. The Association of Japanese Animations (AJA) announced the name during an event in Tokyo.

## Key facts
- The Nippon Anime Awards will hold its first ceremony at SGC Hall Ariake in Tokyo’s Ariake district.
- The announcement was made on November 2 at Tokyo Tatemono Brillia Hall.
- The awards program is organized by the Association of Japanese Animations (AJA).
