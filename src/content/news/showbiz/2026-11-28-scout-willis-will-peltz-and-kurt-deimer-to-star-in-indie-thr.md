Scout Willis, Will Peltz, and Kurt Deimer will star in the indie thriller "The Prostitute," directed by Paul Boyd. The film features Peltz as a young filmmaker living in his van in Hollywood who becomes obsessed with a mysterious woman played by Willis.

## Key facts
- The film is described as a dark psychological thriller.
- Peltz is known for his roles in "Euphoria," "Manifest," and "Unfriended."
- The character played by Peltz becomes fixated on a blonde woman he meets on the streets.
