Darren Criss held a one-night-only concert at the Royal Albert Hall in London, where he surprised the audience by bringing out Chris Colfer for a performance of "Baby It's Cold Outside." This event served as a reunion for the "Glee" cast, as Criss and Colfer portrayed characters Kurt Hummel and Blaine Anderson on the show.

## Key facts
- Darren Criss performed at the Royal Albert Hall in London.
- Chris Colfer joined Criss onstage for a rendition of "Baby It's Cold Outside."
- The performance was an homage to their characters from "Glee."
