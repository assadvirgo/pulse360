## Brad Bird Discusses the Shift from Pixar to Skydance for 'Ray Gunn'

In a recent interview, acclaimed director Brad Bird shared insights into his decision to take his latest animated project, 'Ray Gunn', to Skydance Animation rather than Pixar, where he has enjoyed a successful career. Bird, known for his work on iconic films such as 'The Incredibles' and 'Ratatouille', emphasized the importance of creating animated films targeted at teens and adults, asserting that this should not be a controversial choice.

### A New Direction for Animation

Bird's comments come at a time when the animation industry is experiencing a significant evolution. Traditionally, animated films have been viewed primarily as family-friendly entertainment. However, Bird believes there is a growing demand for content that resonates with older audiences. "Making animated films for teens and adults shouldn’t be a controversial decision," he stated, highlighting the need for diverse storytelling that appeals to a broader demographic.

### The Creative Environment at Skydance

The decision to partner with Skydance Animation reflects Bird's desire for creative freedom and a fresh approach to storytelling. Skydance, known for its innovative projects and willingness to explore new narratives, offers an environment that Bird feels is conducive to his vision for 'Ray Gunn'. The project is expected to blend humor, action, and mature themes, setting it apart from traditional animated fare.

During a recent recording session at Abbey Road Studios in London, Bird and a 74-piece orchestra brought the film's score to life. The iconic studio, famous for its rich musical history, provided a fitting backdrop for the ambitious project. The orchestra, featuring a diverse array of instruments including violins, cellos, and flutes, contributed to the film's unique sound, further enhancing its appeal to an older audience.

### The Future of Animated Storytelling

Bird's transition to Skydance marks a pivotal moment in his career and the animation industry as a whole. With the landscape of animated films rapidly changing, he is at the forefront of a movement that seeks to redefine what animation can achieve. By prioritizing stories that resonate with older viewers, Bird is challenging the status quo and encouraging other creators to follow suit.

As the industry continues to evolve, the success of 'Ray Gunn' could pave the way for more animated films aimed at teens and adults. Bird's commitment to this vision reflects a broader trend in entertainment, where creators are increasingly willing to push boundaries and explore complex themes through animation.

### Conclusion

Brad Bird's decision to take 'Ray Gunn' to Skydance Animation underscores a significant shift in the animation landscape. By focusing on narratives that appeal to older audiences, Bird is not only expanding the horizons of animated storytelling but also challenging the conventions that have long defined the genre. As he embarks on this new journey, the anticipation surrounding 'Ray Gunn' continues to grow, promising a fresh and exciting addition to the world of animation.
