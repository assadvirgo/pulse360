The International Documentary Association has announced the nominations for this year's Documentary Awards, with several films receiving multiple nominations. "Concrete Land" leads with six nominations, followed by "To Hold a Mountain" with five nominations.

## Key facts
- "Concrete Land" received six nominations.
- "To Hold a Mountain" received five nominations.
- Other nominated films include "A Fox Under a Pink Moon" and "Rehearsals for a Revolution."
