The Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences plans to expand the list of festivals that can qualify films for the International Film Oscar category, according to AMPAS CEO Bill Kramer. This announcement was made during an interview at the Tokyo Film Festival.

## Key facts
- The Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences will expand the list of festivals for the International Film Oscar category.
- AMPAS CEO Bill Kramer confirmed this information during an interview.
- The announcement took place at the Tokyo Film Festival.
