Oscar-nominated filmmaker Ava DuVernay expressed concerns about the upcoming midterm elections in the U.S., stating that the situation does not look promising regardless of the outcome. She made these comments during the Airbnb.org Gala last week.

- Ava DuVernay is an Oscar-nominated filmmaker.
- She warned that the midterm elections are not looking great for the U.S.
- DuVernay believes that either outcome of the elections will lead to trouble.
