Firefighters have made progress on the Bryson Fire north of Malibu, achieving 39% containment due to improving wind conditions. The fire has burned approximately 147 acres, according to the Los Angeles County Fire Department.

## Key facts
- The Bryson Fire is now 39% contained.
- The fire has consumed an estimated 147 acres.
- Improving wind conditions have aided firefighting efforts.
