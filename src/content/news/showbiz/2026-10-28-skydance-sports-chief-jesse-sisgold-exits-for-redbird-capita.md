Longtime Skydance Sports chief Jesse Sisgold is leaving his position to take on an advisory role at RedBird Capital. He was previously the president and COO of Skydance, which has undergone significant changes under David Ellison's leadership.

## Key facts
- Jesse Sisgold is exiting his role at Skydance Sports.
- He will move to an advisory position at RedBird Capital.
- Sisgold was formerly president and COO of Skydance.
- The changes are part of a restructuring led by George Cheeks.
