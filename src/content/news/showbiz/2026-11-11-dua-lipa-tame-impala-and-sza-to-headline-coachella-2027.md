Dua Lipa, Tame Impala, and SZA are set to headline the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival in 2027. The announcement follows a social media tease from SZA regarding the headliners. Daddy Yankee and Justice will also have special slots at the festival.

## Key facts
- Dua Lipa, Tame Impala, and SZA will headline Coachella 2027.
- Daddy Yankee and Justice will have special slots at the festival.
- The festival is scheduled for April 9-11 and April 16-18, 2027.
