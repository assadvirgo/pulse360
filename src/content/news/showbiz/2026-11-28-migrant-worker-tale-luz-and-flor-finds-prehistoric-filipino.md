The film project "Luz and Flor," directed by Filipino filmmaker Glenn Barit, features two prehistoric humans from the Philippines and Indonesia who meet as laborers in Spain. The project is also referred to as "Luzonensis and Floresiensis" and is part of the 2026 JAFF Future Project selections at the JAFF Market.

- The film depicts two prehistoric humans from the Philippines and Indonesia.
