Vietnamese film "Karaoke Murder" will open in cinemas across North America, Europe, Australia, and Asia on the same weekend, marking a first for a film from Vietnam, according to Variety. The film, titled "Án Mạng Karaoke" in Vietnamese, is set to be released nationwide in Vietnam on October 30.

## Key facts
- "Karaoke Murder" will open in cinemas on four continents simultaneously.
- This is the first time a film from Vietnam has achieved such a release.
- The film will be released nationwide in Vietnam on October 30.
- U.S.-based specialty distributor 3388 Films is handling the release.
