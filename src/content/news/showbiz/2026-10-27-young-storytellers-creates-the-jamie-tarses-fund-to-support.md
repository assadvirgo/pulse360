Young Storytellers, a nonprofit organization in Los Angeles, has established The Jamie Tarses Fund to support writing programs in public schools. The fund is named in memory of Jamie Tarses, a television executive and producer who was a founding board member of the organization and passed away in 2021.

## Key facts
- The Jamie Tarses Fund was created by Young Storytellers.
- The fund aims to support writing programs in Los Angeles public schools.
- Jamie Tarses was a founding member of Young Storytellers' board.
