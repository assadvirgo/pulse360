Jessy Moussallem’s film "Faux Bijoux" and Laila Abbas’ film "America Is Behind the Door" won the top prizes at the Atlas Workshops during the Marrakech Film Festival. Moussallem's film received the post-production prize, while Abbas' film won the development prize.

## Key facts
- "Faux Bijoux" won the post-production prize at the Atlas Workshops.
- "America Is Behind the Door" won the development prize at the same event.
- The Atlas Workshops took place from November 22 to 26 as part of the Marrakech Film Festival. 
- Moussallem's film received a post-production grant of €30,000 ($33,000).
