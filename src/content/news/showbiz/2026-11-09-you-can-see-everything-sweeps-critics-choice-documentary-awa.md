The documentary "You Can See Everything" received multiple accolades at the Critics Choice Documentary Awards, held in New York. The film, directed by Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, won all major categories in which it was nominated.

## Key facts
- "You Can See Everything" chronicles the final days of Elizabeth Holmes before her imprisonment.
- The documentary swept all major categories at the Critics Choice Documentary Awards.
- Ron Howard and Jehane Noujaim were honored at the event.
