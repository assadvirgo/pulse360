Netflix released a new trailer for "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth," directed by David Fincher. This film is a follow-up to Quentin Tarantino's "Once Upon a Time in Hollywood," focusing on Brad Pitt's character, stuntman Cliff Booth.

## Key facts
- The film is directed by David Fincher.
- Quentin Tarantino wrote the screenplay but is not directing the film.
- The release date is set for November 25.
