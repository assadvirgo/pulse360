Oscar-winning costume designer Sandy Powell will present this year’s Film London Jarman Award on November 24. She will also be the guest of honor at the event, which celebrates U.K.-based artist-filmmakers in the film industry.

## Key facts
- Sandy Powell is an Oscar-winning costume designer.
- She will present the Film London Jarman Award on November 24.
- Powell was mentored by Derek Jarman, after whom the award is named.
- The award was established in 2008.
