Ameer Fakher Eldin will direct the Neon-backed biopic "Caesar," which focuses on Syrian military officer Farid Al-Madhhan. The film tells the story of Al-Madhhan, who exposed torture and killings by the regime of Syrian president Bashar Assad. Taim Hassan has been cast in the lead role.

## Key facts
- Ameer Fakher Eldin is known for his previous works "The Stranger" and "Yunan."
- The film is titled "Caesar."
- The story centers on Farid Al-Madhhan, a Syrian military officer.
- Taim Hassan will portray the lead character in the film.
