Starz Entertainment reported an increase in overall and streaming revenue for the third quarter, which ended in September. The company also significantly reduced its losses compared to the previous year as it concentrates on owned originals and adjusts its strategy in the direct-to-consumer market.

## Key facts
- Starz Entertainment's overall and streaming revenue rose for the three months ending in September.
- The company significantly narrowed its losses year-on-year.
- Starz is focusing on owned originals and refining its strategy in the direct-to-consumer landscape.
