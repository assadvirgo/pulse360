Jacqueline Lentzou’s film “A Day in the Life of Jo: Chapter Phaedra” won the Golden Alexander at the Thessaloniki Film Festival. The film received the top prize in the Arrivals international competition section.

## Key facts
- The film is Jacqueline Lentzou’s sophomore feature.
- It premiered in the Venice Film Festival’s Horizons strand.
- The film won the Golden Alexander for best feature film.
