Viewer interest in Indian shows on IMDb has significantly increased over the past decade, despite a decline in the number of new shows and seasons commissioned in the country for two consecutive years, according to a new IMDb study.

## Key facts
- Viewer interest in Indian shows on IMDb rose about 17-fold over the past decade.
- The number of new shows and seasons commissioned in India has dropped for two straight years.
- The report traces the growth of Indian streaming through its most popular titles.
