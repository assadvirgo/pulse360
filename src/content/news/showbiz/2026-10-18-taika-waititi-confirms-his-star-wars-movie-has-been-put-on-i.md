Taika Waititi has confirmed that his "Star Wars" movie is on hold indefinitely. The director announced this while promoting his latest film, "Klara and the Sun."

## Key facts
- Waititi's "Star Wars" movie has been in development since 2020.
- The project is officially on hold, according to Waititi.
- He stated that they are working on a different project together.
