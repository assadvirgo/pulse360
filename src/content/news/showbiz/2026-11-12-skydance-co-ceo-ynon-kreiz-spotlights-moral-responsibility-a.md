Skydance Co-CEO Ynon Kreiz addressed the audience at the Simon Wiesenthal Center's gala, where he received the 2026 Humanitarian Award. In his speech, he emphasized the importance of combating antisemitism and injustice.

## Key facts
- Ynon Kreiz stated that defending Israel does not mean it is beyond criticism.
- He described the fight against antisemitism as a fight against hatred in any form.
- Kreiz was presented the award by his co-CEO David Ellison, Gal Gadot, and Jeffrey Katzenberg.
