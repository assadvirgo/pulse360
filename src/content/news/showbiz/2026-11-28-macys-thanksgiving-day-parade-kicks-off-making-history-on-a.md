The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade commenced in New York City, marking a historic occasion as the first parade to take place in Manhattan in its 100-year history. The event began at 8:30 a.m. ET under dry and breezy conditions.

## Key facts
- The parade was held in mid-40s temperatures with light breezes and no rain.
- This year’s parade is the first to occur in Manhattan in the event's 100-year history.
- The event was postponed before taking place.
