South Korean director Hur Jin-ho expressed gratitude to the Singapore International Film Festival (SGIFF) for defending freedom of expression during the opening of his film "The Assassin(s)." This event marks the 37th edition of the festival, which faced pressure to withdraw the film from conservative opposition in Korea.

## Key facts
- Hur Jin-ho thanked SGIFF for protecting artistic freedom.
- "The Assassin(s)" is a political thriller.
- The festival opened its 37th edition on Wednesday.
- There was a request from Korea's conservative opposition to pull the film from the festival.
