Disney has appointed Carol Choi as President of Asia-Pacific, succeeding Luke Kang, who is retiring after 16 years with the company. Choi, currently the Executive Vice President of Disney Asia Pacific, will assume her new role on December 1.

## Key facts
- Carol Choi will take over as President of Asia-Pacific on December 1.
- She is currently the Executive Vice President of Disney Asia Pacific.
- Luke Kang is retiring after 16 years with the Walt Disney Company.
- Choi will oversee Disney's studios, streaming, original content, and consumer products businesses in the region.
