Pamela Abdy made her first industry appearance following her departure from Warner Bros. at the Women in Film Honors at the Beverly Hilton. She was announced as the inaugural Vanguard Award recipient on the morning of October 1.

## Key facts
- Pamela Abdy's departure from Warner Bros. was announced a month prior to her appearance.
- She attended the Women in Film Honors event at the Beverly Hilton.
- Abdy was recognized as WIF’s inaugural Vanguard Award recipient. 
- She previously served as co-chair and CEO of Warner Bros.
