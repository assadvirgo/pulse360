Russell Brand has asserted his innocence regarding the charges for which he is currently imprisoned. His wife, Laura Brand, conveyed his message during an episode of their Sunday Service show on YouTube.

## Key facts
- Russell Brand claimed he is "not guilty for the offences I have been jailed for."
- The message was delivered by his wife, Laura Brand.
- Brand is currently incarcerated in Littlehey Prison, a facility for men.
