Korean drama "The Remarried Empress" has achieved a record as the most viewed local original premiere on Disney+ globally, just three days after its launch on November 3. The series has also become the number one Korean title premiere in over 50 global markets.

## Key facts
- "The Remarried Empress" is the most viewed local original premiere of all time on Disney+ globally.
- The show launched on November 3 and set the record within three days.
- It ranks as the number one Korean title premiere in over 50 global markets, including the U.S., UK, France, Spain, Germany, Mexico, Chile, and Australia.
