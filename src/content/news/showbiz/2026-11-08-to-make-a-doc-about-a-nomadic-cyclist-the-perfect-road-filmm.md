Filmmakers Adam and Christian Bonke, along with their cinematographer, spent three months in a campervan to create a documentary about nomadic cyclist Maximilian Schnell. The team followed Schnell in a compact vehicle loaded with equipment to capture footage for the film, which is currently screening in Tallinn.

## Key facts
- Adam and Christian Bonke are the filmmakers behind the documentary.
- They followed cyclist Maximilian Schnell during the filming.
- The filming took place over a period of three months.
- The crew used a "not so big" vehicle with "a lot of equipment."
- The documentary is now screening at Tallinn.
