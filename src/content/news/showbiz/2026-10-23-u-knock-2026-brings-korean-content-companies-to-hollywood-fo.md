U-KNOCK 2026 is set to take place from November 4 to 6, 2026, at The Biltmore Los Angeles, connecting Korean content and technology companies with U.S. investors and entertainment partners. The event is hosted by South Korea’s Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and organized by the Korea Creative Content Agency (KOCCA). This marks the third year of the U-KNOCK program. 

## Key facts
- U-KNOCK 2026 will occur at The Biltmore Los Angeles.
- The event will connect Korean companies with U.S. investors and entertainment partners.
- It is hosted by South Korea’s Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism.
- The Korea Creative Content Agency (KOCCA) is organizing the event.
- This is the third year of the U-KNOCK program.
