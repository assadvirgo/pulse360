Thai singer-songwriter and actor Jeff Satur has signed with Universal Music Southeast Asia as he aims to promote T-pop globally. His first major release under this new deal will be his second studio album, which is scheduled for 2027.

- Jeff Satur's music has surpassed 2 billion streams.
- He has signed with Universal Music Southeast Asia.
- The new deal aims to take T-pop to a global audience.
- Satur's second studio album is set to be released in 2027.
