Zosia Mamet expressed uncertainty about a potential revival of the HBO comedy "Girls," stating that she had begun to think it might not happen. The actress, who played Shoshanna Shapiro, noted that the revival had been in development for a long time.

## Key facts
- Zosia Mamet starred in the HBO comedy "Girls."
- She was unsure if a revival would occur after nearly 10 years since the show's conclusion.
- The revival project had been in development for a long time, according to Mamet.
