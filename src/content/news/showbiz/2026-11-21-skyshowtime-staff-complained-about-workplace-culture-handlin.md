Comcast and Skydance announced plans to shut down SkyShowtime for commercial reasons. Reports indicate that this decision follows complaints from employees regarding workplace culture and the handling of incidents related to racist conduct.

## Key facts
- A group of SkyShowtime staff filed a formal complaint in June about the company's workplace culture.
- The complaints included issues related to the handling of racist conduct.
- The announcement to shutter SkyShowtime was made this week by Comcast and Skydance.
