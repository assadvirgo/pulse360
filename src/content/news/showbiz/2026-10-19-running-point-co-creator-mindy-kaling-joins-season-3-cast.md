Mindy Kaling has joined the cast of Season 3 of the Netflix series "Running Point" in a recurring role. Kaling, who is a writer, executive producer, and co-creator of the show, will portray Divya, described as an outspoken and confident CEO of a popular gambling app.

## Key facts
- Mindy Kaling is a writer, executive producer, and co-creator of "Running Point."
- She will play the character Divya in Season 3.
- Divya is characterized as an outspoken and confident CEO of a popular gambling app.
- Kaling's role follows Ike Barinholtz's recurring role as cousin Benny in Season 2.
