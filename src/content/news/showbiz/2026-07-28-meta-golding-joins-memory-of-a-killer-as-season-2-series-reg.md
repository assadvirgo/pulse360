## Meta Golding Joins Season 2 of 'Memory of a Killer'

Meta Golding, known for her roles in "The Pitt" and "Ransom Canyon," has been confirmed as a series regular for the second season of Fox's "Memory of a Killer." This announcement adds a new layer of intrigue to the series, which stars Patrick Dempsey in a dual role as Angelo Flannery/Doyle, a character leading a perilous double life.

## Background of the Series

"Memory of a Killer" is inspired by the acclaimed 2003 Belgian film "De Zaak Alzheimer," which has garnered critical acclaim and several awards. The series adaptation explores themes of identity, morality, and the complexities of human relationships, all while maintaining a suspenseful narrative. The show delves into the life of Flannery/Doyle, who is not only grappling with his own demons but also entangled in a web of crime and deception.

## Golding's Role

While specific details about Golding's character have not been disclosed, it has been confirmed that she will portray someone from Angelo's past.
