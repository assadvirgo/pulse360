In the upcoming DC Studios film "Clayface," director James Watkins confirmed that Aaron Paul makes a brief cameo appearance. Paul portrays the character of Matt's father, who raised him in the slums of Gotham City.

## Key facts
- Aaron Paul appears in "Clayface" as Matt's father.
- The character is described as having raised Matt in the slums of Gotham City.
- Director James Watkins referred to the cameo as "a real brief Easter egg."
