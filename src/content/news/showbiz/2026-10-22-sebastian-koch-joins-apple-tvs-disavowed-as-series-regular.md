Sebastian Koch has joined the cast of Apple TV's upcoming action-thriller series "Disavowed" as a series regular. The show is created by Art Marcum and Matt Holloway, and Koch's role details are currently undisclosed.

## Key facts
- Sebastian Koch has joined the cast of "Disavowed" on Apple TV.
- The series is an action-thriller created by Art Marcum and Matt Holloway.
- Koch will play a series regular role, with details about his character under wraps.
- Other previously announced stars include James Marsden, Norman Reedus, Sylvia Hoeks, and Bokeem Woodbine.
