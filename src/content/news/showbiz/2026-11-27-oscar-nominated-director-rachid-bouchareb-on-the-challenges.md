Franco-Algerian filmmaker Rachid Bouchareb is preparing to shoot his next feature, "Reggane," in the Algerian Sahara. He faces several challenges, including rebuilding a French military base and sourcing military vehicles from the final years of French colonial rule.

## Key facts
- Rachid Bouchareb is an Oscar-nominated director.
- The film "Reggane" will be shot in the depths of the Sahara.
- Challenges include rebuilding a French military base and sourcing military vehicles.
- The production will involve dressing around 2,000 extras.
