Byron Allen has sold all of his shares in Starz, which he had acquired from Steve Mnuchin earlier this year. The sale resulted in a profit for the investor and media entrepreneur.

## Key facts
- Byron Allen sold all 1.8 million shares of Starz.
- He had acquired the shares from Steve Mnuchin, a former Hollywood producer and Treasury Secretary.
- The sale was executed after Allen's initial purchase earlier this year.
