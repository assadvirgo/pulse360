## ‘September Afternoon’ Explores the Beauty of the Mundane

In a world often captivated by the extraordinary, German writer-director-editor Nicolaas Schmidt's debut feature-length film, "September Afternoon," invites audiences to find beauty in the mundane. The film is set to make its world premiere at the prestigious Locarno Film Festival, where it promises to challenge conventional narratives surrounding relationships and existential crises.

## A Unique Narrative

"September Afternoon" follows a couple grappling with the transition from summer to autumn, a metaphor for the inevitable changes in life and relationships. The film's protagonists refuse to accept the end of summer, clinging to the warmth and familiarity of their shared experiences. This refusal serves as a poignant commentary on human nature's tendency to resist change, even when it is unavoidable.

Schmidt's approach to storytelling emphasizes the significance of everyday moments, suggesting that in an age saturated with sensationalism and extraordinary events, the plain and ordinary can be profoundly stimulating. As he aptly puts it, "In an age of arbitrary extraordinariness, plain banality is stimulating as f***." This perspective encourages viewers to reflect on their own lives and the beauty that can be found in the seemingly trivial.

## Thematic Depth

The film's narrative is underscored by a looming sense of a "contemporary cosmic catastrophe," hinting at broader existential themes. This juxtaposition of the couple's intimate struggles against a backdrop of impending chaos invites viewers to consider the fragility of human connections in a world that often feels overwhelming. Schmidt's exploration of these themes is both timely and relevant, resonating with audiences who may feel disillusioned by the relentless pace of modern life.

## Visual and Auditory Aesthetics

While specific details about the film's cinematography and score remain under wraps, early indications suggest that Schmidt employs a visually striking style that complements the film's themes. The use of natural light and intimate settings is expected to enhance the portrayal of the couple's relationship, allowing viewers to immerse themselves in their world. Additionally, the film's sound design is anticipated to play a crucial role in conveying the emotional landscape of the characters, further engaging the audience.

## Anticipation at Locarno

The Locarno Film Festival, known for its commitment to showcasing innovative cinema, provides an ideal platform for "September Afternoon" to reach a global audience. As Schmidt's first feature-length film, it represents not only a personal milestone for the director but also an opportunity to contribute to the ongoing conversation about the role of cinema in reflecting societal truths.

As the festival approaches, anticipation builds around Schmidt's unique voice and vision. "September Afternoon" promises to resonate with viewers who appreciate films that delve into the complexities of human relationships while celebrating the beauty of ordinary life.

In a cinematic landscape often dominated by spectacle, Schmidt's film stands as a refreshing reminder of the power of simplicity and authenticity. As audiences prepare to experience "September Afternoon," it is clear that this debut will mark a significant entry into contemporary German cinema.
