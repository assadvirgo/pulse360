ABC's "World News Tonight" achieved its highest week-long daily ratings since the COVID pandemic, following a week filled with significant news events. The ratings surge came after the midterm elections and was driven by incidents such as the Microsoft Outlook blackout and the nationwide grounding of flights.

## Key facts
- ABC's "World News Tonight" posted its highest week-long daily ratings since COVID.
- The ratings increase followed the midterm elections, which generated large audiences for evening newscasts.
- Significant news events included the Microsoft Outlook blackout and the nationwide grounding of air flights.
