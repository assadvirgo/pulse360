## Marjoe Gortner, Influential Preacher and Actor, Passes Away at 82

Marjoe Gortner, a multifaceted figure known for his unique journey from child preacher to Oscar-winning documentary subject and actor, has died at the age of 82. His wife, Susan Magestro, confirmed his passing to Variety, stating that he died on Friday in Santa Fe, New Mexico, after enduring years of health issues.

### Early Life and Preaching Career

Born on January 14, 1944, in Long Beach, California, Gortner was thrust into the spotlight at an early age. He was the son of a preacher and began preaching himself at just four years old. By the time he was a teenager, he had already gained notoriety for his fervent evangelical style, leading revival meetings across the United States. His early experiences in the church were both formative and tumultuous, shaping his views on faith, performance, and authenticity.

### Oscar-Winning Documentary

Gortner's life took a significant turn when he became the subject of the groundbreaking documentary "Marjoe," released in 1972. The film, which won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature, provided a candid look at Gortner's life, revealing the complexities of his upbringing and the often exploitative nature of the evangelical revival circuit. It showcased his struggle with the dichotomy of faith and performance, ultimately leading him to renounce his childhood beliefs. The documentary remains a critical exploration of religion and showmanship, and Gortner's story continues to resonate with audiences.

### Acting Career

Following the success of "Marjoe," Gortner transitioned into acting, leveraging his unique background to carve out a niche in Hollywood. He appeared in several notable films, including the disaster movie "Earthquake" (1974), where he played a supporting role alongside established stars. Gortner also had a recurring role in the popular television series "Falcon Crest," which aired from 1981 to 1990, further solidifying his presence in the entertainment industry.

### Legacy and Impact

Gortner's contributions to both the religious and entertainment spheres have left a lasting impact. His candidness about his past as a preacher and the insights he offered into the world of faith-based performance have sparked discussions about authenticity in both religion and entertainment. Through his work, he challenged societal norms and encouraged audiences to question the nature of belief and performance.

In recent years, Gortner remained a figure of interest, with his life story serving as a reference point for discussions around the intersection of faith, fame, and personal truth. His passing marks the end of an era for those who followed his journey, both as a preacher and as an artist.
