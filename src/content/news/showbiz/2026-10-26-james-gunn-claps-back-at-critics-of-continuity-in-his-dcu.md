James Gunn responded to online critics questioning the continuity of the DC Cinematic Universe (DCU) he is developing with Peter Safran. The discussion arose as DC's Clayface premiered and Marvel's Avengers: Doomsday approaches release.

## Key facts
- Critics have been comparing the connectivity of recent projects in the DCU.
- Six recent projects were mentioned in the discussions, including Superman and the Peacemaker series.
- The conversation gained traction as Clayface was released in theaters.
