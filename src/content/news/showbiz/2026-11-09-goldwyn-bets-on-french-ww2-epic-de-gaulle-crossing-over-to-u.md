Antonin Baudry's two-part World War II film "De Gaulle" premiered in the U.S. at the American French Film Festival in Los Angeles. The film received a seven-minute standing ovation after both installments were screened back-to-back.

## Key facts
- "De Gaulle" is a two-part epic about Charles de Gaulle.
- The film had its U.S. premiere at the American French Film Festival in Los Angeles.
- It drew a seven-minute standing ovation after the screenings.
- Each installment of the film runs for more than two and a half hours.
- The production budget for the film was €74 million.
