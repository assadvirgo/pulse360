  Finds

A survey of film and TV industry professionals reveals a strong desire for climate-related storytelling, coinciding with the United Nations Climate Change Conference COP 31. The results indicate that while 71% of respondents want to incorporate climate themes into their work, 55% reported facing barriers to doing so.

## Key facts
- 71% of surveyed film and TV professionals want to include climate-related storytelling in their work.
- 55% of respondents reported facing barriers to incorporating climate themes.
- The survey is described as a first-of-its-kind international survey.
