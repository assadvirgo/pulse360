Club Kid achieved the best limited expansion of the year, earning $756,000 in its second week across 27 screens. Meanwhile, Paper Tiger, directed by James Gray, recorded the highest per-screen opening for the director at $35,800 on six screens in New York and Los Angeles.

## Key facts
- Club Kid earned $756,000 in its second week.
- The film played on 27 screens.
- The audience for Club Kid was 80% under 35 years old.
- Paper Tiger had a per-screen opening of $35,800.
- Paper Tiger was shown on six screens in New York and Los Angeles.
