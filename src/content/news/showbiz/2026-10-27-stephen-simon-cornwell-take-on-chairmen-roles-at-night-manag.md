Stephen and Simon Cornwell have assumed chairmen roles at The Ink Factory, the production company behind "The Night Manager." This change comes alongside several promotions within the company.

## Key facts
- Stephen and Simon Cornwell are now chairmen at The Ink Factory.
- Michele Wolkoff has been promoted to President and Chief Creative Officer.
- Ian Spence has been appointed Chief Financial Officer.
