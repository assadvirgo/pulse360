Netflix has announced the start of production on the domestic violence drama "Patricia," starring and produced by Penélope Cruz. The film is directed by Juan Diego Botto and is the first narrative feature produced by Cruz's company, Moonlyon.

## Key facts
- "Patricia" is a domestic violence drama starring Penélope Cruz.
- The film is written and directed by Juan Diego Botto.
- Production has officially begun.
- The film is the first narrative feature produced by Cruz's audiovisual company, Moonlyon.
