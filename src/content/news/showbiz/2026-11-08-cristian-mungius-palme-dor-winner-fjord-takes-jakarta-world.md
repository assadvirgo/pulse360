Cristian Mungiu's film "Fjord," which won the Palme d'Or, received the audience award at the Jakarta World Cinema. The festival concluded with the Thai horror film "Inherit" by Banjong Pisanthanakun and provided funding for three Indonesian short filmmakers to create their debut features.

## Key facts
- "Fjord" won the Jakarta World Cinema Audience Award, the only feature prize at the event.
- The festival concluded with the screening of "Inherit," a Thai horror film by Banjong Pisanthanakun.
- Three Indonesian short filmmakers were awarded funding for their first feature films.
