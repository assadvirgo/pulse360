Jack Champion, Owen Patrick Joyner, Grace Van Dien, and Georgie Murphy are set to lead the young adult romance film "The One Before The One." The film was launched by Blue Fox Entertainment at the Cannes market earlier this year.

## Key facts
- Jack Champion is known for his role in the Avatar franchise.
- Owen Patrick Joyner has appeared in "Final Destination: Bloodlines."
- Grace Van Dien is recognized for her role in "Stranger Things."
- Georgie Murphy starred in "Stumble."
- Production of the film wrapped in Austin, Texas last month.
