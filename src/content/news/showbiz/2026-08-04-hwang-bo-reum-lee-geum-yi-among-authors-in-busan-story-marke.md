## Hwang Bo-reum and Lee Geum-yi Highlight Busan Story Market's 2026 Selections

The Busan Story Market (BSM) has announced its official selections for 2026, featuring a diverse array of 25 titles that showcase original intellectual property (IP) from four Asian countries. Among the notable authors included in this year's lineup are Hwang Bo-reum, an international bestseller, and Lee Geum-yi, a two-time finalist for the prestigious Hans Christian Andersen Award.

### A Platform for Asian Narratives

The BSM is an integral part of the Asian Contents & Film Market (ACFM), which serves as the official market for the Busan International Film Festival (BIFF). This platform is dedicated to promoting and facilitating the exchange of Asian stories, providing creators with opportunities to connect with global audiences and industry stakeholders.

The selection of Hwang Bo-reum and Lee Geum-yi underscores the market's commitment to highlighting prominent voices in Asian literature. Hwang, known for her compelling narratives and engaging storytelling, has garnered a significant following both domestically and internationally. Lee, recognized for her contributions to children's literature, has received acclaim for her ability to weave intricate tales that resonate with young readers.

### Diverse Representation

The 2026 selections reflect a broad spectrum of genres and themes, drawing from the rich cultural tapestry of the Asian continent. By incorporating original IP from multiple countries, the BSM aims to foster collaboration and innovation within the region's creative industries. This year's lineup not only showcases established authors but also aims to uplift emerging voices, providing them with a platform to reach wider audiences.

### The Importance of the Busan Story Market

The Busan Story Market plays a crucial role in the global entertainment landscape, particularly as Asian narratives continue to gain traction in international markets. By focusing on original content, the BSM encourages creators to develop unique stories that can transcend cultural boundaries. This approach not only enriches the storytelling landscape but also promotes cultural exchange and understanding.
