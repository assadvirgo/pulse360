Neve Campbell has confirmed her return as Sidney Prescott for "Scream 8." This announcement follows her character's survival in "Scream 7," which was a significant box office success for the franchise.

## Key facts
- Neve Campbell will reprise her role as Sidney Prescott in "Scream 8."
- Sidney Prescott survived her latest encounter with the Ghostface killers in "Scream 7."
- "Scream 7" achieved the franchise's biggest opening ever, grossing $97.2 million worldwide.
