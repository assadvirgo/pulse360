MS NOW on-air hosts, including Rachel Maddow, have expressed disappointment that management has not yet reached a new agreement with the Writers Guild of America (WGA) East. They issued a public letter urging parent company Versant Media to secure a deal that guarantees fair wages and protections before the current agreement expires on December 3. MS NOW's president, Rebecca Kutler, responded to the hosts' complaints. 

## Key facts
- MS NOW hosts, including Rachel Maddow, issued a public letter to Versant Media.
- The letter urges management to reach a new deal with WGA East unionized employees.
- The hosts are seeking guarantees for fair wages and protections.
- The current agreement is set to expire on December 3.
- Rebecca Kutler, president of MS NOW, responded to the hosts' complaints.
