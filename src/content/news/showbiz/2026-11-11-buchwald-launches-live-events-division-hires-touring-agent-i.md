Buchwald is launching a new division focused on live events and has hired touring agent Isaiah Jones. Jones joins Buchwald after spending over nine years at UTA, where he developed a background in comedy, music touring, and live entertainment.

## Key facts
- Buchwald is expanding its representation into live events.
- Isaiah Jones has been hired as a touring agent.
- Jones previously worked at UTA for more than nine years.
- He has a background in comedy, music touring, and live entertainment.
