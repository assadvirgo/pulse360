More than a decade after its release, "Green Room" writer/director Jeremy Saulnier expressed gratitude for Patrick Stewart's role in getting the film made. This acknowledgment came during a screening of the 2015 movie, held one day after Stewart's death at age 86.

## Key facts
- Jeremy Saulnier is the writer and director of "Green Room."
- Saulnier credited Patrick Stewart with helping to ensure the film was made.
- The remarks were made during a screening of the film.
- The screening took place one day after Stewart's death.
