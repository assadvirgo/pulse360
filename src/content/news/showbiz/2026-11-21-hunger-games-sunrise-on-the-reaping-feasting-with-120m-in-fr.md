Lionsgate's latest film, "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping," has achieved a significant box office success, marking a revival for the franchise. The film has earned over $120 million in its opening weekend, indicating a strong return for the series.

## Key facts
- "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping" had the third-best opening Friday in the six-movie series, according to Hollywood Reporter.
- The film is reported to have a North American opening of over $120 million, as stated by Deadline.
- The opening weekend included a $56 million Friday, which accounted for preview earnings.
