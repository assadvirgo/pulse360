AMC Theatres CEO Adam Aron has expressed the company's desire to continue operating the Kips Bay 15 multiplex in New York City. However, he acknowledged that the decision regarding its future is not up to AMC.

## Key facts
- AMC Theatres CEO Adam Aron wrote an open letter to New York City residents.
- Aron reiterated the company's wish to keep the Kips Bay 15 multiplex open.
- He stated that the decision about the multiplex's future is not AMC's to make.
