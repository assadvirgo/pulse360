Egypt’s El Gouna Film Festival has announced the third cohort of emerging talents selected for its Rising Stars program. The program highlights filmmakers from across the Arab world.

## Key facts
- The third cohort includes Egyptian director Abdallah Dnewar.
- Saudi Arabian writer-director Razan AlSoghayer is part of the cohort.
- Palestinian filmmaker Omar Rammal is included in the program.
- Palestinian Jordanian director-producer Asmahan Bkerat is among the selected talents.
- Syrian DoP, editor, and director Rama Abdi is also part of the cohort.
