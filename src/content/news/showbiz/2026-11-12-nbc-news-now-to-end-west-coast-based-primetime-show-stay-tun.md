NBC News Now has canceled its primetime show "Stay Tuned," which was one of the few network shows produced from Los Angeles. The show's anchor, Gadi Schwartz, will continue with the network despite the cancellation.

## Key facts
- NBC News Now has ended its primetime show "Stay Tuned."
- The show was one of the few network programs originating from Los Angeles.
- Gadi Schwartz, the anchor of the show, will remain with NBC News Now.
- The cancellation affects about a dozen staff positions.
