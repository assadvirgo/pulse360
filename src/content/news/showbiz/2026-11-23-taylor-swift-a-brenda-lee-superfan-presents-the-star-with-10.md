Taylor Swift presented Brenda Lee with a plaque recognizing the diamond-selling status of Lee's song "Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree." This event took place during Swift's recent trip to Nashville.

## Key facts
- Taylor Swift has been a fan of Brenda Lee since her teens.
- The plaque honors the 10x Platinum certification of "Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree."
- Only two Christmas singles have achieved this certification.
