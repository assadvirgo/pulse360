Veteran communications executive Liz Morentin has launched a new venture called Greenvalley Media & Marketing. The company will focus on serving entertainment, media, and technology companies and their leaders.

## Key facts
- Liz Morentin has launched Greenvalley Media & Marketing.
- The new company will focus on entertainment, media, and technology sectors.
- Morentin previously served as SVP and head of global communications and brand strategy at Virgin Music Group.
- She has held top roles at Warner Bros. Records and Paradigm Talent Agency.
