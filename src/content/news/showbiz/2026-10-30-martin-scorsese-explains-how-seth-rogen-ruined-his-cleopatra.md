Martin Scorsese discussed the impact of the Sony hack on his planned Cleopatra film, attributing its disruption to the fallout from Seth Rogen's 2014 film The Interview. He expressed that the hack, which was state-sponsored by North Korea, "ruined everything" for his project.

## Key facts
- Martin Scorsese is still affected by the Sony hack that occurred over a decade ago.
- The hack was a response to Seth Rogen's film The Interview.
- Scorsese's planned Cleopatra feature was significantly impacted by the hack.
