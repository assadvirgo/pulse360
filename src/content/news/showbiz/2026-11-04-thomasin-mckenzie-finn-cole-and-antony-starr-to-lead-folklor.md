A new retelling of the Robin Hood legend titled "The Lie of Sherwood Forest" is set to launch at the upcoming AFM. The film is produced by German company augenschein and will begin production on November 9.

## Key facts
- "The Lie of Sherwood Forest" is a retelling of the Robin Hood legend.
- The film will be launched at the AFM with Logical Pictures International and Protagonist Pictures handling sales.
- Production is set to begin on November 9.
- Patrick Vollrath, known for "7500" and "Subversion," is directing the film.
