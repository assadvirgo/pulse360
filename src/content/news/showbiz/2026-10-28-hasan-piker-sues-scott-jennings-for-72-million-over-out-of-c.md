Twitch streamer Hasan Piker has filed a lawsuit against conservative commentator Scott Jennings, claiming defamation over a misrepresented quote that suggested Piker endorsed lynchings. The lawsuit seeks $72 million in damages.

## Key facts
- Hasan Piker alleges that Scott Jennings misconstrued a quote to imply he endorsed lynchings.
- Jennings played a mashup of clips from Piker on his podcast on August 11.
- Piker's lawsuit claims Jennings used a "false, fabricated, and manipulated video."
- The lawsuit seeks $72.5 million in damages.
