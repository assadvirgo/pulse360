Nearly 20 years after the last installment, David Leitch is set to direct "Ocean's 14," with filming expected to begin in 2026. Leitch mentioned that George Clooney and his producing partner Grant Heslov have provided motivation for the project.

- David Leitch is attached to direct "Ocean's 14."
