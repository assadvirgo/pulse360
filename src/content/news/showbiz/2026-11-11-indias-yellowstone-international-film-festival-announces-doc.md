The Yellowstone International Film Festival in Mumbai, India has announced its documentary lineup, which includes the Asian premiere of the Oscar contender "American Doctor." The film, directed by Poh Si Teng, focuses on three U.S. physicians who volunteered to treat Palestinian children injured in Gaza.

## Key facts
- The festival is set to take place in Mumbai, India.
- "American Doctor" is highlighted as part of the documentary lineup.
- The film examines the efforts of U.S. physicians treating injured Palestinian children.
- The screening of "American Doctor" is scheduled for Thursday.
