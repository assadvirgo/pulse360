A promotional campaign for the upcoming film "Ebenezer," directed by Ti West and starring Johnny Depp, has introduced a real Venmo account for the character Ebenezer Scrooge. The account allows users to both send and receive money, bringing the fictional miser into the digital payment space.

## Key facts
- The Venmo account is part of a promotional effort for the film "Ebenezer."
- The film features Johnny Depp in the role of Ebenezer Scrooge.
- The account enables users to interact with the character by handing out and accepting money.
