The View will travel to Italy in May 2027 for a special event celebrating its 30th anniversary. The announcement was made by Whoopi Goldberg during her birthday celebration on the show.

## Key facts
- The View will take its "Hot Topics" table to Rome.
- The event is part of the show's 30th anniversary celebration.
- Whoopi Goldberg made the announcement on Thursday's episode of the ABC talk show.
