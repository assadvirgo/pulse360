The BBC has issued an apology to viewers following an incident during the live show of "Strictly Come Dancing" at Blackpool's Tower Ballroom, where lewd noises were heard. The sounds, which appeared to be recordings of sex noises, interrupted judge Motsi Mabuse as she was commenting on a performance.

## Key facts
- The incident occurred during a live broadcast of "Strictly Come Dancing."
- Lewd noises were heard while judge Motsi Mabuse was speaking.
- The sounds seemed to be played from the audience.
- The BBC has apologized for the disruption.
