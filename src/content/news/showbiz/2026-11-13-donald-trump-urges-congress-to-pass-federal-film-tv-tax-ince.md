Donald Trump has urged Congress to pass a federal film and TV tax incentive, expressing concerns that the bill may not progress. He made this appeal on Truth Social, highlighting bipartisan support for the proposed 20% incentive.

- He expressed worries that the bill might languish.
- The proposed incentive is a 20% tax break.
