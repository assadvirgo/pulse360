Netflix has hired Steve Wilson to lead its podcast division as part of its efforts to expand in the podcasting space. Wilson, a former executive at Apple Podcasts, will oversee the company's podcast acquisitions.

## Key facts
- Steve Wilson has been contracted by Netflix to head its podcast division.
- He previously spent 15 years at Apple, where he worked in editorial, partner relations, and marketing.
- Wilson most recently led marketing for Apple Podcasts before joining Netflix.
