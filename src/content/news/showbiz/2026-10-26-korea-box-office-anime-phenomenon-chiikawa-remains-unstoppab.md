Japanese animated film "Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island" continued to dominate the South Korean box office, accounting for nearly 45% of ticket revenue during the weekend of October 23-25. This marks the film's fourth consecutive weekend at the top.

## Key facts
- "Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island" captured nearly 45% of nationwide ticket revenue.
- The film has maintained a commanding grip on the South Korean box office for four weekends.
- Data was provided by KOBIS, the tracking service operated by the Korean Film Council.
