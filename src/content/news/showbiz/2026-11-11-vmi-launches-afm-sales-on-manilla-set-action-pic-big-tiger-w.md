VMI is launching AFM sales for the action movie "Big Tiger," which is set in Manila. The film stars Luke Goss, Katherine McNamara, Byron Mann, and Alden Richards, and is directed by Keoni Waxman.

## Key facts
- "Big Tiger" is an action movie set in Manila.
- The film stars Luke Goss, Katherine McNamara, Byron Mann, and Alden Richards.
- It is directed by Keoni Waxman.
- The film is produced by Lux Angeles Studios, Birns & Sawyer, and Myriad Entertainment.
