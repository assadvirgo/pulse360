Katseye featured Camila Cabello during their performance in Los Angeles, inviting her on stage to dance to their song "Gabriela." This occurred roughly six songs into their set.

## Key facts
- Katseye performed in Los Angeles and invited Camila Cabello on stage.
- Cabello danced to the choreography of the song "Gabriela."
- Katseye consists of members Daniela Avanzini and Lara Raj, among others.
