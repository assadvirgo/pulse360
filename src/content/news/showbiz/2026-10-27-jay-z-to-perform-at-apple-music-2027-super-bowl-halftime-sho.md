Jay-Z is set to perform at the Apple Music Super Bowl LXI Halftime Show on February 14, 2027, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. He expressed his thoughts on the journey from his debut album to this significant performance.

## Key facts
- Jay-Z will perform at the Apple Music Super Bowl LXI Halftime Show.
- The event is scheduled for February 14, 2027.
- It will take place at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California.
- Jay-Z's company, Roc Nation, curates the Halftime Show.
