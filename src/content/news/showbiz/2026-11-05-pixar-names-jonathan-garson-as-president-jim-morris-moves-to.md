Pixar Animation Studios is undergoing a leadership change, with Jonathan Garson named as the new president. Jim Morris, who has served as president for 12 years, will transition to a president emeritus role.

## Key facts
- Jim Morris will move to a president emeritus role after 12 years as president and 21 years at Pixar.
- Jonathan Garson, a Disney veteran with over 30 years of experience, will succeed Morris as president.
- The leadership change is effective on January 4, 2027.
- Pete Docter will continue to serve as chief creative officer of Pixar.
- Jonas Rivera has been promoted to creative executive vice president.
