Paul Howard, the only son of Moe Howard from The Three Stooges, has died at the age of 91. His death was confirmed by a spokesperson for C3 Entertainment, the owner of The Three Stooges.

## Key facts
- Paul Howard was the last surviving son of the classic lineup of The Three Stooges.
- He died this week at the age of 91.
- He connected with fans of The Three Stooges at conventions.
- He hosted a nine-part docuseries titled ‘Hey Moe, Hey Dad!’ in 2015.
