Bill Maher humorously suggested that Selma Blair could be responsible for recent issues faced by the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) during an episode of "Real Time." This came as the FAA lifted a nationwide grounding of thousands of flights, which was attributed to communication problems affecting air traffic controllers.

## Key facts
- Bill Maher made a joke about Selma Blair being the cause of FAA issues.
- The FAA had previously grounded thousands of flights due to communication problems.
- The grounding was lifted on the same day as Maher's comments.
