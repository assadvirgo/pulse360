Netflix is proceeding with its acquisition of Radford Studio Center despite recent criticism from its current landlord on Sunset Blvd. The company had previously attempted to acquire the Warner Bros lot but lost out to another bidder earlier this year.

## Key facts
- Netflix is moving forward with the acquisition of Radford Studio Center.
- The acquisition is expected to be completed in the next several months.
- Netflix previously lost the bid for the Warner Bros lot to David Ellison.
