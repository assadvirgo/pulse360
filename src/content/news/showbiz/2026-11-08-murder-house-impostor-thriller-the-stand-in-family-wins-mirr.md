Taiwanese literary IP developer and production company Mirror Fiction announced the winners of its fifth Mirror Fiction Award at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest. The Grand Prize of NT$1 million ($32,000) was awarded to Iris Kong for her suspense novel "The Stand-In Family."

## Key facts
- The Mirror Fiction Award is in its fifth year.
- The Grand Prize winner received NT$1 million ($32,000).
- "The Stand-In Family" is a suspense novel about four strangers.
- Five projects for the 2027 slate were unveiled at the event.
