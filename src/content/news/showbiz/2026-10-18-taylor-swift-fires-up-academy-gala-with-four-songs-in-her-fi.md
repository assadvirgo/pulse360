Taylor Swift performed a four-song set with a full band at the Academy Museum's 6th Annual Gala, marking her first such performance since the Eras Tour. The event took place in front of a black-tie audience.

## Key facts
- Taylor Swift's performance featured a four-song set.
- This was her first full-band performance since the Eras Tour.
- The event was held at the Academy Museum's 6th Annual Gala.
- Conan O'Brien introduced Taylor Swift at the gala.
