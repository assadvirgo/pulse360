Media layoffs across the entertainment industry are continuing, with significant job cuts reported since mid-2024. This trend follows the impacts of the Covid-19 pandemic, dual Hollywood strikes, and recent wildfires in Los Angeles that began in January 2025, according to Deadline.

## Key facts
- Job cuts in the entertainment industry have been ongoing since mid-2024.
- The layoffs are attributed to the effects of the Covid-19 pandemic and dual Hollywood strikes.
- A series of wildfires in Los Angeles, which started in January 2025, has also contributed to the layoffs.
