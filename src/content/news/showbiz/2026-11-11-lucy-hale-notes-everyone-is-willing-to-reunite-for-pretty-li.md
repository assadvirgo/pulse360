Lucy Hale has indicated that the cast of "Pretty Little Liars" is open to reuniting for a revival of the series. Hale, who played Aria Montgomery, mentioned that everyone from the cast is "willing and would want to" reprise their roles.

- Lucy Hale starred as Aria Montgomery on "Pretty Little Liars."
- Hale noted that the cast is willing to reunite for a revival of the series.
- The series originally aired nearly a decade ago.
