## AI Tools Enhance Production Efficiencies, Yet Face Challenges, Says Weta CTO

During a recent panel discussion at the Busan International Film Festival, industry leaders including Kimball Thurston, Chief Technology Officer of Weta FX, addressed the growing role of artificial intelligence (AI) in film production. The session, part of Netflix’s Creative Asia day, also featured insights from Korean filmmaker Kang Yun-sung and Netflix’s Senior Director of Production for the Asia-Pacific region, Sung Q Lee. The conversation highlighted both the advantages and limitations of integrating AI tools into creative workflows.

### Advantages of AI in Film Production

Thurston emphasized that AI technologies are revolutionizing the production landscape by streamlining various processes. From automating repetitive tasks to enhancing visual effects, AI is enabling filmmakers to allocate resources more efficiently and focus on creative storytelling. The integration of AI can lead to significant time savings and cost reductions, allowing production teams to experiment with new ideas without the constraints traditionally associated with budget and time limitations.

Kang Yun-sung echoed these sentiments, noting that AI can assist in pre-production phases such as script analysis and casting decisions. By analyzing vast amounts of data, AI tools can provide insights that help filmmakers make informed choices, potentially increasing the success rate of projects.

### Limitations and Challenges

Despite the benefits, the panelists acknowledged that AI tools are not without their limitations. Thurston pointed out that while AI can assist in many areas, it cannot replace the nuanced understanding and emotional depth that human creatives bring to storytelling. He stressed the importance of collaboration between technology companies and creative professionals, suggesting that a partnership approach could lead to more effective and innovative solutions.

Sung Q Lee highlighted the need for a balanced approach, where AI serves as an enhancement rather than a replacement for human creativity. He noted that while AI can generate content and analyze trends, the unique perspectives and cultural insights that filmmakers provide are irreplaceable. The panelists collectively agreed that the future of filmmaking lies in leveraging AI as a tool to augment human creativity, rather than allowing it to dominate the creative process.

### The Call for Collaboration

The discussion concluded with a call for greater collaboration between tech companies and creative industries. Thurston urged technology developers to engage more deeply with filmmakers and artists to understand their needs and challenges. By fostering an environment of collaboration, both sectors can work together to harness the full potential of AI while respecting the artistry that defines film.

As the film industry continues to evolve, the insights shared during this panel at the Busan International Film Festival underscore the necessity of balancing technological advancements with the irreplaceable human touch that characterizes great storytelling. The dialogue between creatives and technologists will be crucial in shaping a future where AI enhances rather than detracts from the art of filmmaking.
