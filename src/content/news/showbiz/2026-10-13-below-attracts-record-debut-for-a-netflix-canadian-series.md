## 'Below' Sets Record for Netflix Canadian Series Debut

A new limited series titled "Below" has made a significant impact on Netflix, attracting an impressive 17 million views since its debut on October 8. This figure marks the highest viewership for a Netflix Canadian title upon its release, underscoring the growing appeal of Canadian content on the global streaming platform.

### Overview of 'Below'

"Below" tells the story of a fisherman, played by Josh Hartnett, whose tranquil life in a small Newfoundland town is disrupted by the emergence of a mysterious sea creature. The series combines elements of suspense and drama, capturing the attention of viewers with its intriguing premise and strong performances. 

The show has not only resonated with audiences in Canada but has also garnered international interest, contributing to its record-breaking debut. The series is a testament to the quality of storytelling and production that Canadian creators can offer, showcasing the unique cultural narratives that can emerge from the region.
