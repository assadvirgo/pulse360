Jamie Dornan is set to portray Aragorn in the upcoming prequel, "The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum," directed by Andy Serkis. He expressed that he is not trying to outdo Viggo Mortensen, who originally played the character.

## Key facts
- Jamie Dornan will take on the role of Aragorn in "The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum."
- The film is directed by Andy Serkis.
- Dornan mentioned feeling "pressure" regarding the role.
- He clarified that he is not attempting to outdo Viggo Mortensen's portrayal.
