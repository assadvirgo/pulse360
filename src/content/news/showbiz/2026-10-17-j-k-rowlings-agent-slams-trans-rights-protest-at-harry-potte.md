J.K. Rowling's agent, Neil Blair, criticized transgender rights activists for protesting before a gala performance of "Harry Potter and the Cursed Child" in London. The protest occurred on the red carpet as celebrities were arriving.

## Key facts
- Neil Blair condemned the protest, stating it put "others at risk."
- The protest involved activists wearing masks, hats, and goggles to conceal their identities.
- The event took place in London's West End.
