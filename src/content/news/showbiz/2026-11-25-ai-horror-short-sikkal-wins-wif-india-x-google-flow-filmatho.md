A horror short titled "Sikkal," directed by Promita Banerjee, has won the inaugural WIF India x Google Flow Filmathon. The film addresses the impact of sickle cell disease on India's tribal communities and was created using Google Flow.

## Key facts
- "Sikkal" won the first prize of INR1.2 million ($12,500).
- The second place was awarded to Syam Manohar's black comedy "Kuli," which earned INR800,000 ($8,300).
- The filmathon was organized by WIF India and Google Flow.
