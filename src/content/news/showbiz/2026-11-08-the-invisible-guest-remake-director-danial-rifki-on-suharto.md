Indonesian director Danial Rifki's remake of the Spanish thriller "The Invisible Guest" has been completed and was released at the Jakarta World Cinema. The film's release was delayed due to exclusivity clauses with HBO. It is one of three films by Rifki featured at the festival.

## Key facts
- Danial Rifki's remake of "The Invisible Guest" was completed in 2023.
- The film's release was held up by exclusivity clauses with HBO.
- The film premiered at Jakarta World Cinema.
- It is one of three films by Rifki showcased at the festival.
