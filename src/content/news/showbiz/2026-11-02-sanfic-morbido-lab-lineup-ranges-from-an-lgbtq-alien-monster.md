The Sanfic Mórbido Lab has announced its lineup for the 7th edition, featuring a variety of genre films. The event is part of Sanfic Industria and will take place in Santiago de Chile from November 2 to 8.

## Key facts
- The lineup includes Simón and Raimundo Bucher’s “L4IK4,” Roberto San Sebastián’s “The Hostess,” and Javier Attridge’s “Beneath the Flesh.”
- The Sanfic Mórbido Lab is recognized as a significant event in Latin America's genre film scene.
- The event will run from November 2 to 8 in Santiago de Chile.
