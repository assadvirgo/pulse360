Ron Howard's documentary "Avedon" focuses on the influential photographer Richard Avedon. The film has been praised for its editing and the way it presents Avedon's images.

## Key facts
- "Avedon" is described as a fantastic documentary by Ron Howard.
- The film is noted as the best documentary Howard has made to date.
- The editing of the film, done by Andrew Morreale, is highlighted for its artful technique.
- The documentary features hundreds of images shot by Richard Avedon.
- The film aims to convey how Avedon changed photography and influenced perceptions of the world.
