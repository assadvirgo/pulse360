Bill Nighy has joined the cast of the upcoming horror thriller series "Ascension" at Apple TV, alongside Caitríona Balfe. The series is inspired by the novel "Last Days" by Adam Nevill and was picked up by Apple in August.

- Bill Nighy will star in "Ascension" at Apple TV.
- Caitríona Balfe is also part of the cast.
- The series is inspired by the novel "Last Days" by Adam Nevill.
- "Ascension" was picked up by Apple in August.
