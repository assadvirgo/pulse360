“Saturday Night Live” honored the Milwaukee Brewers' World Series victory during a Weekend Update segment featuring Bobby Moynihan's Drunk Uncle character. Moynihan, who was part of “SNL” from 2009 to 2017, returned to deliver his signature blunt observations.

## Key facts
- The Milwaukee Brewers celebrated their World Series win on “Weekend Update.”
- Bobby Moynihan reprised his Drunk Uncle character during the segment.
- The Brewers' manager Pat Murphy, along with players Christian Yelich and Brice Turang, made a surprise appearance.
