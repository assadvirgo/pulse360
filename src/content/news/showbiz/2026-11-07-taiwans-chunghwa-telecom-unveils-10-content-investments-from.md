Chunghwa Telecom, Taiwan's largest telecom operator, announced 10 new investments in various content projects. The announcement was made at the "Chunghwa Telecom Content Slate and Partnership Showcase."

## Key facts
- The investments include film, TV, and music projects.
- Chunghwa Telecom also acquired stakes in two production companies.
- The company aims to support Taiwanese original intellectual property (IP).
- The initiative is designed to connect local creators with international partners.
