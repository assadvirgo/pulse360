The 20th annual LA Skins Fest concluded after a five-day celebration of Native American cinema at the TCL Chinese Theatre. The festival has served as a platform for Indigenous filmmakers to showcase their work and connect with industry professionals.

## Key facts
- The festival celebrated its 20th year.
- It took place over five days.
- Special screenings were held at the TCL Chinese Theatre.
- The festival aims to connect Indigenous filmmakers with entertainment industry professionals.
