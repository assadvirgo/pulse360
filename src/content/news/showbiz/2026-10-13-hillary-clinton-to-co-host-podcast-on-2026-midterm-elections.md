## Hillary Clinton to Co-Host Podcast on 2026 Midterm Elections

Former Secretary of State Hillary Clinton is set to enter the podcasting arena with a new limited series titled "High Stakes," which will focus on the upcoming 2026 U.S. midterm elections. The podcast will be co-hosted by journalist Kara Swisher and produced under a partnership with Vox Media, a company owned by media mogul James Murdoch.

### A New Platform for Political Discourse

"High Stakes" promises to provide listeners with insights and analysis on the political landscape as the midterm elections approach. Clinton, who has a long history in politics and public service, brings a wealth of experience and knowledge to the table. Her co-host, Kara Swisher, is a well-respected journalist known for her expertise in technology and media, making for a compelling duo that aims to engage audiences in meaningful discussions about the electoral process.

The podcast format offers a unique opportunity for both hosts to explore the complexities of the midterm elections in a more informal setting, allowing for candid conversations that may not be possible in traditional media formats. This approach could resonate with a diverse audience, particularly younger voters who increasingly consume news and information through digital platforms.

### Revenue Sharing and Production Details

As part of the agreement with Vox Media, Clinton and Swisher will share in the advertising revenue generated from the podcast episodes. This revenue-sharing model reflects a growing trend in the podcasting industry, where hosts often have a financial stake in the success of their shows. The partnership with Vox Media, known for its innovative content and strong digital presence, positions "High Stakes" to reach a broad audience.

The podcast is expected to feature a mix of interviews, expert opinions, and discussions on key issues that will shape the midterm elections. Topics could include voter turnout, campaign strategies, and the impact of social media on political discourse, among others.

### Implications for the 2026 Midterm Elections

The 2026 midterm elections are poised to be a critical juncture for U.S. politics, with all 435 seats in the House of Representatives and 34 of the 100 Senate seats up for grabs. As the political climate continues to evolve, Clinton and Swisher's podcast could play a significant role in informing and mobilizing voters.

Clinton's involvement in the podcast underscores her ongoing commitment to public engagement and political advocacy. Having previously run for president in 2016, she remains a prominent figure in the Democratic Party and continues to influence political discourse in the United States.

### Conclusion

"High Stakes" is anticipated to be a significant addition to the podcasting landscape, particularly in the context of the upcoming midterm elections. With Hillary Clinton and Kara Swisher at the helm, the series aims to provide listeners with a deeper understanding of the political dynamics at play and encourage active participation in the electoral process. As the launch date approaches, many will be watching to see how this collaboration unfolds and the impact it may have on voter engagement leading up to 2026.
