Erik von Detten, known for his roles in "The Princess Diaries" and "Brink!", is facing a lawsuit that alleges elder abuse and fraud. An elderly couple claims he convinced them to invest over $2 million of their retirement savings in overpriced gold coins.

## Key facts
- The lawsuit was filed in California.
- The elderly couple alleges they were persuaded to invest in gold coins.
- The investment was reportedly significantly overpriced.
