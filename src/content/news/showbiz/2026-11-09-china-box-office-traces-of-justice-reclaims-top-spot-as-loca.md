"Traces of Justice" has reclaimed the top position at the mainland China box office for the weekend of November 6-8, according to Variety. The film, a crime procedural drama from New Classics Pictures, earned RMB21.5 million ($3.2 million) during this period.

## Key facts
- "Traces of Justice" collected RMB21.5 million ($3.2 million) over the Nov. 6-8 weekend.
- The film has grossed a total of $103.7 million since its release on October 1.
- The data was reported by Artisan Gateway.
