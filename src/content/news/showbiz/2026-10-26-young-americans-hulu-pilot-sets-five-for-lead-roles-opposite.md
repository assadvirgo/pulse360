Hulu has cast five young leads for its D.C. drama pilot "Young Americans." The cast includes Billie Boullet, Sam Rechner, Mia Isaac, newcomer Oscar Winter Iannone, and Lauren Mei, who will join Scott Foley.

## Key facts
- Billie Boullet is known for "Man on Fire."
- Sam Rechner is part of "Scream 7."
- Mia Isaac appears in "Black Cake."
- Lauren Mei is recognized for her role in "Avatar: The Last Airbender."
- Scott Foley will portray U.S. President Judd Burchfield.
