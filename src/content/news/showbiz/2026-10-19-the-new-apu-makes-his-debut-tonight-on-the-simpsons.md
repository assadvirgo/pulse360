Apu will make his vocal return on "The Simpsons" in tonight's episode titled "The Revengening." The character, previously voiced by Hank Azaria for nearly 30 years, has not been heard since a backlash over his representation of South Asians began following a 2017 documentary.

## Key facts
- Apu has found his voice again on "The Simpsons."
- Hank Azaria voiced Apu for close to 30 years.
- Apu has been seen on the show but not heard since the backlash began.
- The episode featuring Apu's return is titled "The Revengening."
