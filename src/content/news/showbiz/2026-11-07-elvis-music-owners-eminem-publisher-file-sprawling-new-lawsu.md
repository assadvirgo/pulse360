A group of Elvis Presley music rightsholders and the publisher of many of Eminem's records have filed federal lawsuits against the AI music generator Suno. Each party has made separate allegations, contributing to the legal challenges faced by the AI company.

## Key facts
- The lawsuits were filed in federal court.
- The plaintiffs include a collection of Elvis Presley music rightsholders and the publisher of Eminem's records.
- The lawsuits add to the legal challenges faced by Suno.
