Deranged Minds Entertainment has announced a feature-length documentary about actress Danielle Harris, known for her roles in four "Halloween" films. The film, which has been in production for the last three years, will explore her life and career.

## Key facts
- The documentary focuses on Danielle Harris, an iconic scream queen.
- Harris made her film debut at age 11 as Jamie Lee Curtis' daughter.
- She has appeared in various projects, including "The Wild Thornberrys" and "Roseanne."
- The film will cover her experiences growing up on camera and personal traumas.
