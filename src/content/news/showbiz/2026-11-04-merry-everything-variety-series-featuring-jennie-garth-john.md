QVC has announced its 2026 holiday programming lineup for subscription-free streamers QVC+ and HSN+. The lineup will be anchored by a new variety series titled "Merry Everything!" featuring celebrity guest stars including Jennie Garth, John Legend, and Lori Loughlin.

## Key facts
- The series "Merry Everything!" will be hosted by Chelsea Phillips Reid.
- It is described as a joyful holiday variety series.
- The show will bring together QVC’s biggest personalities and special guests.
