The Vision Theatre Performing Arts Center in South Los Angeles is set to reopen after 15 years of renovations and a $40 million investment. The WACO Theater Center, founded by Tina Knowles, will host a grand reopening celebration in Leimert Park.

## Key facts
- The Vision Theatre has undergone extensive renovations over the past 15 years.
- The renovation project cost $40 million.
- The grand reopening celebration will be hosted by WACO Theater Center.
- Tina Knowles is the founder and artistic director of WACO Theater Center.
