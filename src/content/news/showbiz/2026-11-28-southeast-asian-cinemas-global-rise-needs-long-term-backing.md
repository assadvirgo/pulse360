The festival director of Indonesia's Jogja-Netpac Asian Film Festival, Ifa Isfansyah, emphasized the need for long-term support for Southeast Asian cinema to maintain its global rise. He highlighted the importance of integrating film into cultural strategies and praised the Philippines for its consistent talent development and international presence.

## Key facts
- The quality and international profile of Southeast Asian cinema have increased significantly.
- Ifa Isfansyah is the festival director of Indonesia's Jogja-Netpac Asian Film Festival (JAFF).
- Isfansyah advocates for countries to treat film as part of their cultural strategy.
- He specifically commended the Philippines for its talent development and international presence.
