title: SXSW Forges Partnership With Egypt’s UTH Festival Dedicated to Youth Culture

Austin’s South by Southwest Film and TV Festival has formed a partnership with Egypt’s UTH Festival, which focuses on youth culture. This agreement will allow UTH to serve as SXSW's partner in Egypt, with the goal of identifying emerging talent and creating opportunities for Egyptian filmmakers, musicians, creators, founders, and business leaders to connect with SXSW's international network.

## Key facts
- SXSW has partnered with Egypt’s UTH Festival.
- The partnership aims to identify emerging talent in Egypt.
- The agreement focuses on opportunities for filmmakers, musicians, creators, founders, and business leaders in Egypt.
