Erika Hammond has teased that the upcoming reunion for Season 16 of The Real Housewives of New York City will feature both "drama" and "resolution." In an interview, she indicated that while some issues among the cast will be addressed, she does not consider Devyn Simone a friend she wants to maintain a relationship with.

## Key facts
- Erika Hammond stated that the reunion will include "drama" and "resolution."
- She mentioned that some problems among the group will be resolved.
- Hammond expressed that she does not see a path forward with Devyn Simone, referring to her as "not a friend I want."
