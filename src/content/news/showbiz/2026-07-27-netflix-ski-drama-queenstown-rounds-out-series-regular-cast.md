## Netflix's 'Queenstown' Expands Cast with New Additions

Netflix's upcoming ski drama series, "Queenstown," has announced the addition of five new series regulars to its cast, further building anticipation for the show. Among the newly cast actors are Anna Baryshnikov, Jamie Ward, BeBe Orleans, Alex FitzAlan, and Tatum Warren-Ngata. While specific details regarding their roles remain undisclosed, the inclusion of these talented performers suggests a promising ensemble for the series.

## New Cast Members

### Anna Baryshnikov
Known for her roles in "Cape Fear" and "Idiotka," Anna Baryshnikov brings a wealth of experience to the project. Her previous work has garnered attention, and her presence in "Queenstown" is expected to add depth to the narrative.

### Jamie Ward
Jamie Ward, recognized for his performances in "The Gentlemen" and "His Dark Materials," is another notable addition. His diverse acting background positions him well to contribute to the complex dynamics that the series aims to explore.

### BeBe Orleans
BeBe Orleans, who has appeared in "Strife" and "Spiderhead," rounds out the cast with her unique talents. Her involvement is anticipated to bring fresh perspectives to the storyline.

### Alex FitzAlan
Alex FitzAlan, known for his roles in "Hunting Wives" and "My Brilliant Career," adds to the ensemble with his strong acting credentials. His experience in various genres may enhance the series' appeal.

### Tatum Warren-Ngata
Tatum Warren-Ngata, recognized for her work in "Head Girl" and "My Life is Murder," completes the new cast lineup. Her diverse range of performances suggests she will bring a dynamic presence to the show.
