Netflix is set to release "La Bola Negra," Spain's Oscar entry for Best International Feature, this weekend. The film, inspired by an unfinished play by Federico García Lorca, marks a significant milestone for Netflix. Additionally, A24 will debut the documentary "You Can See Everything," which will be shown on 855 screens.

## Key facts
- "La Bola Negra" is an award-winning film from Cannes.
- The film is inspired by an unfinished play by Federico García Lorca.
- "You Can See Everything," a documentary about Elizabeth Holmes, will debut on 855 screens.
- The number of screens for "You Can See Everything" is about double that of the film "Musk."
