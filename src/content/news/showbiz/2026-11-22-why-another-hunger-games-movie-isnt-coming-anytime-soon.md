The "Hunger Games" franchise has seen renewed success with the release of "Sunrise on the Reaping," which opened to $120 million at the box office. This figure nearly triples the opening of the 2023 prequel "The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes," which earned $44 million.

## Key facts
- "Sunrise on the Reaping" is the sixth installment in the "Hunger Games" franchise.
- The film opened to $120 million at the box office.
- This opening nearly triples the $44 million opening of the 2023 prequel "The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes."
