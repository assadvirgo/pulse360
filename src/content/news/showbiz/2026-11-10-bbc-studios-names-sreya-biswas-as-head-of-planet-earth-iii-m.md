BBC Studios has appointed Sreya Biswas as the Head of its Natural History Unit, which produces programs like Planet Earth III and Blue Planet III. She will succeed Jonny Keeling, who will transition to a different role within BBC Studios early next year.

## Key facts
- Sreya Biswas has been named Head of the Natural History Unit at BBC Studios.
- The Natural History Unit is responsible for producing Planet Earth III and Blue Planet III.
- Biswas will take over from Jonny Keeling, who will remain with BBC Studios in a different capacity.
