Toho's "Godzilla Minus Zero" topped the global weekend box office with a gross of $63 million. It outperformed "Ramayana: Rise of a Legend," which earned over $55 million worldwide, primarily from its success in India.

## Key facts
- "Godzilla Minus Zero" grossed $63 million globally.
- "Ramayana: Rise of a Legend" earned over $55 million worldwide.
- The majority of "Ramayana's" earnings came from India.
