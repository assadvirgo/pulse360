The newly-merged Banijay/All3Media entity, now known as Banijay Entertainment, has announced its senior leadership team.

## Key facts
- Marco Bassetti is the CEO of Banijay Entertainment.
- Jane Turton serves as the deputy CEO.
- Cathy Payne is the CEO of Banijay Rights.
- Fred Balmary is the chief business officer.
- Nicolas Chazarain is the chief legal officer, and Thomas Gousset is the chief finance officer.
