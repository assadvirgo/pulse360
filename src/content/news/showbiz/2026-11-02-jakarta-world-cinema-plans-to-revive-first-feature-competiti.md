Jakarta World Cinema plans to revive its First Feature Competition next year, which will be open to filmmakers globally and judged by an international jury. The competition did not accept submissions this year due to challenges in assembling an international jury.

## Key facts
- Jakarta World Cinema aims to bring back its First Feature Competition next year.
- The competition will be open to filmmakers from around the world.
- An international jury will judge the competition.
- This year's edition did not accept submissions.
- The competition was previously judged only by Indonesian judges.
