The inaugural Kathmandu Film Festival (KFF) will take place from October 30 to November 1 in Nepal. Veteran Indian actor Shabana Azmi and film critic Marco Müller are among the guests at the festival.

## Key facts
- The festival is launched by Kathmandu Metropolitan City.
- KFF will screen 40 films from 14 countries.
- The event will feature several strands, including the International Panorama and Nepal Panorama Shorts.
