The creators of "The New Stanford Prison Experiment" have discussed the finale of their project, which concludes on a more hopeful note compared to the original 1971 trial conducted by Stanford psychology professor Philip Zimbardo. The original experiment involved 24 white male college students, with one Asian American participant, who were compensated $15 a day to take part in a two-week simulated prison environment.

## Key facts
- "The New Stanford Prison Experiment" ends on a more hopeful note than the original 1971 trial.
- The original experiment included 24 white male college students and one Asian American participant.
- Participants in the original experiment were paid $15 a day.
