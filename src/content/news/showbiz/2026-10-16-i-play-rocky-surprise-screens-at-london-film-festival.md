The London Film Festival featured a surprise screening of "I Play Rocky," a biographical drama directed by Peter Farrelly about the origins of Sylvester Stallone's boxing franchise. Farrelly introduced the film and acknowledged its star, Anthony Ippolito.

## Key facts
- "I Play Rocky" was the surprise film at the London Film Festival.
- The film is directed by Peter Farrelly.
- Farrelly introduced the movie at the Royal Festival Hall.
- Anthony Ippolito stars in the film and has received praise for his performance.
