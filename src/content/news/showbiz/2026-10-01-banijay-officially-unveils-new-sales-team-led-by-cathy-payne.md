## Banijay Unveils New Sales Team Led by Cathy Payne

In a strategic move to enhance its global sales operations, Banijay Entertainment has officially announced the formation of a new sales team under the leadership of Cathy Payne, who has been appointed as the chief of the division. This restructuring comes in the wake of the company’s recent merger with All3Media, which has significantly expanded its content catalogue to over 250,000 hours.

### Leadership Structure

Cathy Payne, a seasoned executive in the entertainment industry, will spearhead the sales team, bringing a wealth of experience to the role. Under her leadership, five key executives have been appointed to support the sales strategy across various global regions. The team includes:

- **Matt Creasey**, Senior Executive Vice President, responsible for global sales excluding the EMEA (Europe, the Middle East, and Africa) region.
- **Stephen Driscoll**, Senior Executive Vice President for the EMEA region, tasked with overseeing sales initiatives and partnerships in this crucial market.
- **Rachel Job**, whose specific role within the team will further bolster Banijay's sales efforts, although details of her position were not disclosed in the announcement.

The appointments reflect Banijay's commitment to leveraging its extensive content library and enhancing its market presence worldwide.

### Strategic Goals

With the merger with All3Media, Banijay has positioned itself as a formidable player in the global media landscape. The new sales team is expected to focus on maximizing the potential of the company’s vast content offerings, which span various genres and formats. This strategic alignment aims to drive sales growth and foster long-term relationships with broadcasters and platforms around the world.

Cathy Payne expressed her enthusiasm about the new team, stating that the diverse expertise and backgrounds of her lieutenants will be instrumental in navigating the complexities of the global marketplace. She emphasized the importance of collaboration and innovation in achieving the company’s sales objectives.

### Industry Context

The global media and entertainment industry is witnessing rapid changes, driven by technological advancements and shifting consumer preferences. As streaming services continue to proliferate, traditional content distributors are adapting their strategies to remain competitive. Banijay's move to revamp its sales team reflects a broader trend within the industry, where companies are increasingly focusing on strategic partnerships and content diversification.

The newly formed sales team will not only aim to capitalize on existing relationships but also seek new opportunities in emerging markets. As the demand for high-quality content continues to rise, Banijay is well-positioned to meet the needs of various platforms and audiences.

### Conclusion

Banijay Entertainment’s unveiling of its new sales team marks a significant step in its ongoing evolution as a leading content provider. Under the guidance of Cathy Payne and her experienced team, the company is poised to leverage its extensive catalogue to drive sales and expand its global footprint. This strategic initiative underscores Banijay's commitment to innovation and excellence in the competitive landscape of the entertainment industry.
