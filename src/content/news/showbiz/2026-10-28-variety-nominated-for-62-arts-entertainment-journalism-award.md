Variety has received 62 nominations for the Los Angeles Press Club’s 19th annual National Arts & Entertainment Journalism Awards. The nominations include categories for Print Journalist of the Year and Online Journalist of the Year.

## Key facts
- Variety has 62 nominations for the National Arts & Entertainment Journalism Awards.
- The awards are organized by the Los Angeles Press Club.
- Chris Willman is nominated for Print Journalist of the Year.
- Ethan Shanfeld is nominated for Online Journalist of the Year.
- Owen Gleiberman is nominated for film critic in the criticism category.
