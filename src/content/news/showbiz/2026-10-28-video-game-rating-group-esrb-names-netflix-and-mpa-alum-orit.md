Orit Michiel has been named the next president of the Entertainment Software Rating Board (ESRB), which is responsible for assigning age and content ratings for video games and mobile apps. She will take on the role starting November 2, 2026, succeeding Patricia Vance, who is retiring after over 20 years in the position.

## Key facts
- Orit Michiel is a former employee of Netflix and the Motion Picture Association (MPA).
- The ESRB is the video game industry's self-regulatory body.
- Michiel will replace Patricia Vance as president. 
- Patricia Vance has served as president for more than 20 years.
