Champion professional wrestler Benjamin Satterley died last month at the age of 40. The Cook County Medical Examiner’s Office in Chicago has determined that the cause of death was bronchial asthma, classifying it as natural.

## Key facts
- Benjamin Satterley died last month at 40.
- The cause of death was determined to be bronchial asthma.
- The death was classified as natural by the Cook County Medical Examiner’s Office.
- The cause of death was first reported by The New York Post and confirmed by other media outlets.
