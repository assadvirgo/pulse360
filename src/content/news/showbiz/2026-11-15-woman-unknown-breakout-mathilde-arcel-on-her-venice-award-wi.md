Danish actress Mathilde Arcel has gained recognition for her performance in "Woman Unknown," where she played her first leading role in a feature film. She was awarded the Volpi Cup for best actress at the Venice Film Festival for her work in the film.

## Key facts
- Mathilde Arcel received the Volpi Cup for best actress at the Venice Film Festival.
- "Woman Unknown" is a post-World War II drama directed by May el-Toukhy.
- This role marks Arcel's first leading performance in a feature film.
