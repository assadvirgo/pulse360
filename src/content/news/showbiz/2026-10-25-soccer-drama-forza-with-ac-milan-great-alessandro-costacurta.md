The soccer drama "Forza," directed by Marco Balsamo, is set to be showcased at the Tokyo International Film Festival's TIFFCOM market. The film has been acquired for international sales by EST N8.

## Key facts
- "Forza" was shot in Bali and Milan.
- The film will have its first public screening on October 25 at Jakarta Film Week.
- The TIFFCOM market will take place from October 28 to 30.
