NFL broadcasters Joe Buck and Troy Aikman are reportedly involved in off-field drama as they prepare for the upcoming Super Bowl year. The situation is said to be linked to financial issues, with Buck recently agreeing to a contract renewal with ESPN's parent company, Disney.

- They joined ESPN in 2022 and have contributed to the revival of the program.
- The current drama is reportedly related to financial matters. 
- Buck renewed his contract with ESPN's parent company, Disney, last month.
