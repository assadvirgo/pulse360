## Tsui Hark Pays Emotional Tribute to Nansun Shi at Lumière Festival

LYON, France — Renowned Hong Kong filmmaker Tsui Hark delivered a heartfelt tribute to his late former wife and esteemed producing partner, Nansun Shi, during the Lumière Film Festival held on Sunday. The festival, which celebrates the history and artistry of cinema, served as a poignant backdrop for Hark's reflections on their shared legacy and the impact of Shi's passing in July.

In a moment filled with emotion, Hark expressed his profound sense of loss, stating, "I miss my partner, Nansun, who helped me on so many projects." His remarks resonated with many in attendance, highlighting the significant role Shi played in the Hong Kong film industry as a pioneering producer. The couple's collaborative efforts have left an indelible mark on cinema, particularly in the realm of action and fantasy films that have garnered international acclaim.

## A Legacy of Collaboration

Nansun Shi was not only a key figure in Hark's career but also a trailblazer in her own right. Her contributions to the film industry have been recognized for their innovation and creativity, helping to shape the landscape of Hong Kong cinema. Together, Hark and Shi produced a series of influential films that have captivated audiences worldwide, showcasing their unique vision and dedication to storytelling.

Hark's tribute at the festival served as a reminder of the importance of collaboration in the creative process, emphasizing how Shi's insights and support were instrumental in bringing many of their projects to fruition. The emotional weight of his words underscored the personal and professional bond they shared, which extended beyond their marriage into a fruitful partnership that spanned decades.

## Looking Ahead: New Projects and the Role of AI

In addition to honoring Shi's memory, Tsui Hark also took the opportunity to discuss his upcoming projects and the evolving landscape of filmmaking. He acknowledged the growing influence of artificial intelligence (AI) in the industry, reflecting on both its potential benefits and challenges. Hark noted that while AI can enhance certain aspects of production, it is crucial to maintain the human touch that is essential to storytelling.

As filmmakers navigate this new terrain, Hark emphasized the importance of balancing technological advancements with the artistry and emotional depth that define cinema. His insights resonate with many in the industry who are grappling with the implications of AI on creativity and audience engagement.

## Conclusion

Tsui Hark's tribute to Nansun Shi at the Lumière Film Festival not only celebrated her life and contributions but also served as a platform for discussing the future of filmmaking in an age increasingly influenced by technology. As the film community continues to mourn Shi's passing, Hark's reflections remind us of the enduring impact of their partnership and the ongoing evolution of the cinematic landscape.
