Frank Grillo expressed gratitude to former DC Studios co-heads James Gunn and Peter Safran on social media. This follows their exit after the merger of Warner Bros. and Paramount, during which Skydance parted ways with them.

- Frank Grillo took to social media to express gratitude to James Gunn and Peter Safran.
- Gunn and Safran were co-heads of DC Studios.
- Their exit occurred after the merger of Warner Bros. and Paramount.
