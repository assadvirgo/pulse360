The BBC has denied claims that James Acaster made a complaint regarding the behavior of his co-stars, Rob Beckett and Romesh Ranganathan, during the show The Celebrity Traitors. The denial follows a report from the Mail on Sunday suggesting that Acaster expressed discomfort with what was described as "macho banter."

## Key facts
- The BBC stated that it is "categorically untrue" that James Acaster complained about his co-stars.
- The Mail on Sunday reported that Acaster raised concerns with producers at Studio Lambert.
- Acaster reportedly became "uncomfortable" with the behavior of Beckett and Ranganathan.
