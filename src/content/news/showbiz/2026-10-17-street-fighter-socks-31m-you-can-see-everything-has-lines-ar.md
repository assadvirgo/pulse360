The October box office is experiencing a strong third weekend, highlighted by the performance of "Street Fighter," which has generated $31 million in its opening. In contrast, the film "Whalefall" from 20th Century Studios has underperformed with a $7.5 million opening.

## Key facts
- "Street Fighter" has earned $31 million in its opening weekend.
- The film is attracting an older male demographic, particularly those over 35.
- "Whalefall" opened to $7.5 million, significantly below expectations.
