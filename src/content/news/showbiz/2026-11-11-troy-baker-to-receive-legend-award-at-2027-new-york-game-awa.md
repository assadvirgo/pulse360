Troy Baker has been announced as the recipient of the Andrew Yoon Legend Award for the 2027 New York Game Awards. He is recognized for his extensive work as a voice actor in the gaming industry.

## Key facts
- Troy Baker will receive the Andrew Yoon Legend Award at the 2027 New York Game Awards.
- The announcement was made by the New York Videogames Critics Circle.
- Baker has voiced more than 400 roles across video games, television, and film.
