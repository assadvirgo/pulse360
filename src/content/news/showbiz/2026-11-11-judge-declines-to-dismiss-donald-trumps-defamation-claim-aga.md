A federal judge has decided not to dismiss Donald Trump's defamation claim against the BBC regarding edits made to a documentary about January 6th. The judge ruled that Trump had sufficiently pleaded actual malice.

## Key facts
- U.S. District Judge Jeffrey Kuntz rejected the BBC's efforts to dismiss the case.
- The defamation claim is related to a documentary that aired in advance of the 2024 election.
- The court dismissed another part of Trump's lawsuit that alleged the BBC had violated a Florida law.
