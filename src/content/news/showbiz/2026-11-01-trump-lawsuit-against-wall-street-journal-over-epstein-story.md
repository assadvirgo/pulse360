A U.S. District Judge has dismissed President Donald Trump's lawsuit against The Wall Street Journal regarding a story about a birthday card he allegedly sent to Jeffrey Epstein. The judge ruled that Trump did not prove the defendants made defamatory statements with actual malice.

## Key facts
- The lawsuit was dismissed with prejudice by U.S. District Judge Darrin Gayles in Miami.
- The judge ruled that Trump failed to prove actual malice in the newspaper's reporting.
- This marks the second time Trump's defamation claims against The Wall Street Journal have been dismissed.
- The lawsuit was related to a 2003 birthday letter sent to Jeffrey Epstein.
