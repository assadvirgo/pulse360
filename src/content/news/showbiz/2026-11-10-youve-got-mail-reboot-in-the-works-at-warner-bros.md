Warner Bros. is rebooting the classic 1998 rom-com "You've Got Mail," with Jason Orley set to direct. The plot details are not yet disclosed, and Orley is currently drafting a script based on an earlier version by Deborah Kaplan and Harry Elfont.

## Key facts
- Warner Bros. is rebooting "You've Got Mail."
- Jason Orley is attached to direct the reboot.
- Orley is drafting a script based on a previous take by Deborah Kaplan and Harry Elfont.
- Neal Moritz and Tania Landau will produce the film under Original Film.
