A feature documentary about California's Napa Valley wine region is in development. The film will explore the people and rivalries that contributed to the region's prominence in the wine industry.

## Key facts
- The documentary is being produced by Docsville Studios, PMG Productions, and SLAM Films.
- It is based on Tor Kenward's book "Reflections of a Vintner."
- Julien Couson is set to direct the untitled film.
