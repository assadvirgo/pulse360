U2 performed a tribute to Dolly Parton during their halftime show at the NFL's first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game. The performance featured a duet between Bono and a recording of Parton from last summer.

- U2's halftime performance took place on Wednesday night.
- The event was part of the NFL's first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game.
