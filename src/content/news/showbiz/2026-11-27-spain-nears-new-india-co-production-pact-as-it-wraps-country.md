Spain and India are nearing a new film and audiovisual co-production treaty. This development comes as Spain concluded its participation as Country of Honor at the 57th International Film Festival of India (IFFI) in Goa and its market, WAVES Film Bazaar.

## Key facts
- Spain and India are moving closer to a new co-production treaty.
- Spain recently wrapped its run as Country of Honor at the IFFI in Goa.
- The event included the WAVES Film Bazaar.
