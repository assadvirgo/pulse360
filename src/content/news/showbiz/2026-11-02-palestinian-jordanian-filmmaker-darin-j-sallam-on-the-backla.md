Palestinian-Jordanian filmmaker Darin J. Sallam expressed that she has no regrets about her debut feature film "Farha." The film, which tells the story of a Palestinian girl coming of age during the Nakba, has sparked controversy.

## Key facts
- "Farha" is Darin J. Sallam's debut feature film.
- The film focuses on a Palestinian girl during the Nakba, which involved mass displacement and dispossession of Palestinians in 1948.
- Sallam was warned that the controversy surrounding the film could derail her career.
