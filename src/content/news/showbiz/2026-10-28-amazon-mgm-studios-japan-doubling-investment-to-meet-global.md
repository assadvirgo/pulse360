Amazon MGM Studios Japan is doubling its investment to enhance its content to meet global standards, according to studio head Buddy Marini. This announcement was made during a session at the Tokyo International Film Festival TIFFCOM market.

## Key facts
- Amazon MGM Studios Japan is increasing its investment to improve content quality.
- The announcement was made by studio head Buddy Marini at a TIFFCOM market session.
- The session focused on unlocking the global potential of Japanese intellectual property.
- The event was organized by the Motion Picture Association (MPA) and UniJapan.
