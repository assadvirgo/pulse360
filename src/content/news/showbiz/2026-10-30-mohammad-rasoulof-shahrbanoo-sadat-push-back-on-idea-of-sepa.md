Iranian filmmakers Mohammad Rasoulof and Shahrbanoo Sadat expressed their opposition to the idea of a separate Oscar category for exiled filmmakers during the Tokyo International Film Festival. Their comments came as Cate Blanchett’s Displacement Film Fund premiered its first five short films in Japan.

## Key facts
- Mohammad Rasoulof and Shahrbanoo Sadat spoke at the Tokyo International Film Festival.
- They pushed back against the idea of a standalone Oscar category for exiled filmmakers.
- Cate Blanchett’s Displacement Film Fund premiered its first five short films at the festival.
- The screening included films such as "Rotation" by Maryna Er Gorbach and Sadat’s "Super Afghan Gym."
