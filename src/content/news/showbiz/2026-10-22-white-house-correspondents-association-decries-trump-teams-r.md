The White House Correspondents’ Association has criticized the Trump administration for allegedly retaliating against a journalist by blocking their access. This action reportedly followed President Trump's dissatisfaction with the journalist's questions.

## Key facts
- Jacqui Heinrich, President of the White House Correspondents’ Association, stated that the Trump administration was engaged in retaliation.
- The retaliation involved blocking a journalist's access due to the president's upset over their questions.
- Heinrich is also a senior White House correspondent for Fox News.
