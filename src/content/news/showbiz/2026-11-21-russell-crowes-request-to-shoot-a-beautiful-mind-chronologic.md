Ron Howard, the director of "A Beautiful Mind," stated that Russell Crowe's request to shoot the film chronologically added $1 million to the budget. Howard described this decision as "the right kind of investment." The film, released in 2001, depicts the life of mathematician John Forbes Nash Jr., who struggles with schizophrenia.

## Key facts
- Ron Howard directed "A Beautiful Mind."
- Russell Crowe suggested shooting the film in chronological order.
- This suggestion increased the film's budget by $1 million.
- Howard believes the decision was a worthwhile investment.
- The film follows the life of mathematician John Forbes Nash Jr.
