The cast of "Godzilla Minus Zero" received a standing ovation at the 39th Tokyo International Film Festival. This event took place two days before the film's release in Japan.

## Key facts
- The festival featured a standing ovation for the cast of "Godzilla Minus Zero."
- The film is a follow-up to Yamazaki Takashi's Oscar-winning "Godzilla Minus One."
- Kamiki Ryunosuke and Hamabe Minami are part of the cast, with Kamiki reprising his role as Shikishima Koichi.
