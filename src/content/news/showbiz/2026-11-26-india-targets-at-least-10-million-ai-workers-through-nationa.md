India plans to bring at least 10 million people into its AI workforce through the National AI Talent Mission, according to Lt. Col. Vikram Singh Bhati, director of the National Council for Vocational Education and Training (NCVET). This announcement was made during a panel discussion at the WAVES Film Bazaar.

## Key facts
- India aims to incorporate at least 10 million individuals into its AI workforce.
- The initiative is part of the National AI Talent Mission.
- The announcement was made by Lt. Col. Vikram Singh Bhati.
- The context of the announcement was a panel discussion titled "AI and the Future of Creative Skills."
