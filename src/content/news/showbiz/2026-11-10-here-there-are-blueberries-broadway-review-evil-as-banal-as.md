Here There Are Blueberries, a Pulitzer finalist docu-drama, has made its Broadway debut at the Barrymore Theatre. The play, which opened Off Broadway two years ago, focuses on a unique collection of Auschwitz photos submitted to the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum in 2007.

## Key facts
- Here There Are Blueberries is a Pulitzer finalist docu-drama.
- The play opened Off Broadway two years ago.
- It has now debuted on Broadway at the Barrymore Theatre.
- The story revolves around a cache of Auschwitz photos submitted to the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum in 2007.
