Matthew Weiner has exited his role as showrunner for the Peacock series "The Chairman," which stars Eddie Murphy. Weiner was initially brought on as a writer, executive producer, and showrunner for the high-profile drama.

## Key facts
- Matthew Weiner has departed from the series "The Chairman."
- The project is in development at Peacock.
- Eddie Murphy stars in the series.
- The series is produced by David Glasser's 101 Studios and Universal Television.
