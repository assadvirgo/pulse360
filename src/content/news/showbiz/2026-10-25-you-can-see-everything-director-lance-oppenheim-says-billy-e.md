The documentary "You Can See Everything" features a full-frontal shot of Billy Evans, the partner of Elizabeth Holmes. Director Lance Oppenheim noted that audiences were not expecting this moment, and he shared that Evans was unconcerned about being seen naked, even asking if he could be filmed while showering.

## Key facts
- The documentary is co-directed by Lance Oppenheim and Nathan Fielder.
- Audiences were surprised by the full-frontal shot of Billy Evans.
- Oppenheim stated that Evans "didn't care about being seen naked." 
- Evans reportedly asked if he could be filmed while showering.
