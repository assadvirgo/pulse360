Northern Ireland Screen has appointed Laura Livingstone as its new Chief Executive, effective January. She will succeed Richard Williams, who is stepping down after over 23 years in the role.

## Key facts
- Laura Livingstone will become the new Chief Executive of Northern Ireland Screen.
- She will take on the role in January.
- Richard Williams, the current CEO, announced his departure in June and will leave in December after over 23 years.
