Lisa Rinna, Phaedra Parks, and Dolores Catania will star in a new Off-Broadway revival of Neil Simon's farce "Rumors." The production will feature several other "Real Housewives" stars and is set to begin in January.

## Key facts
- The revival will include Lisa Rinna, Phaedra Parks, Dolores Catania, Stacey Rusch, Cynthia Bailey, Eileen Davidson, Carole Radziwill, and Candiace Dillard-Bassett.
- This production will mark the New York stage debut for all cast members.
- The revival is a reimagined version of Neil Simon's 1988 comedy farce "Rumors."
- It will take place at the Newman Mills Theater at The Robert W. Wilson MCC Theater Space.
- The cast will perform in two batches, in succession.
