Four content funds established by the Taiwan Creative Content Agency (TAICCA) and local companies showcased their investments in various projects over the past year. The projects include "Our Blues," "Midnight Diner," and "C'Est La Vie."

## Key facts
- Four content funds were established by TAICCA and local companies.
- The funds include the Chunghwa Digital Cultural and Creative Fund, Momomini Kids Content Fund, Joint Journey Creative, and Enjoy Entertainment.
- A showcase event was held to present the projects they have been investing in.
