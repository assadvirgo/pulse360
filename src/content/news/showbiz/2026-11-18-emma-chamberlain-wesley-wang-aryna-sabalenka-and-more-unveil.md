A content showcase for YouTube creators took place in Hollywood, featuring upcoming projects from various stars. The event was opened by Sean Downey of Google.

## Key facts
- The event is the second annual YouTube Creator Premieres content showcase.
- It was held at the Hollywood Roosevelt hotel.
- Featured creators include Emma Chamberlain, Wesley Wang, and Aryna Sabalenka, among others.
