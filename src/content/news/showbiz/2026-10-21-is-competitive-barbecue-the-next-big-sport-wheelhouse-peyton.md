Peyton Manning and Brent Montgomery are investing in competitive barbequing through their companies, Omaha Productions and Wheelhouse. They have invested in the BBQ World Cup & Expo, which they believe has the potential to become a significant global sports property.

## Key facts
- Peyton Manning and Brent Montgomery are involved in a new investment in competitive barbequing.
- Their companies, Omaha Productions and Wheelhouse, are backing the BBQ World Cup & Expo.
- The BBQ World Cup & Expo was founded by CEO Scott Erdmann.
