Aardman, the Bristol studio known for "Wallace & Gromit" and "Shaun the Sheep," is celebrating its 50th anniversary. The employee-owned company is focusing on handmade animation and flexible partnerships to maintain its independence.

## Key facts
- Aardman is celebrating its 50th anniversary.
- The studio is known for creating "Wallace & Gromit" and "Shaun the Sheep."
- Aardman is betting on handmade animation to sustain its independence.
- The studio is planning a Halloween-ready third feature of "Shaun the Sheep."
