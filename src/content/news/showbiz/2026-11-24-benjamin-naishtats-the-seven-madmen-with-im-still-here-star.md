Benjamín Naishtat's film "The Seven Madmen," featuring Selton Mello, has completed its production in Buenos Aires. The shoot wrapped on November 24, and the film is considered a significant contender for A-list festival selections in 2027.

## Key facts
- "The Seven Madmen" is directed by Benjamín Naishtat.
- Selton Mello stars in the film, known for his role in "I’m Still Here."
- The production took place in Buenos Aires.
- The film is described as a present-day noir thriller.
- It is loosely adapted from Argentinean literature.
