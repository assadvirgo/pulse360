Farrar, Straus & Giroux, a prominent book publisher in New York City, is celebrating its 80th anniversary. Originally a small boutique publisher, it has become a significant player in the literary world, representing numerous prestigious authors.

## Key facts
- Farrar, Straus & Giroux is the literary division of Macmillan Publishers.
- The publisher has grown from a small boutique into a major publishing house.
- It is home to Nobel laureates, Pulitzer Prize winners, and National Book Award recipients.
