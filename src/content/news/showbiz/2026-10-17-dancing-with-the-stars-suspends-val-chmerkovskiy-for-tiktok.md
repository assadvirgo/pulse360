Val Chmerkovskiy has been suspended from "Dancing with the Stars" for one week due to a TikTok post that appeared to reference Will Smith's incident at the 2022 Oscars. The producer, BBC Studios, emphasized the importance of the well-being of everyone involved in the show.

## Key facts
- Val Chmerkovskiy is suspended from "Dancing with the Stars" for one week.
- The suspension follows a TikTok message that referenced Will Smith's Oscar slap.
- BBC Studios stated that the well-being of everyone who works on the show is non-negotiable.
