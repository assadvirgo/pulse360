Adam Driver has confirmed that he will not be returning for the revival of the HBO series "Girls," which is being brought back by Lena Dunham for a limited run. He expressed his well wishes to Dunham and his co-stars.

## Key facts
- Lena Dunham is reviving "Girls" for a limited run consisting of five episodes.
- Adam Driver portrayed Adam Sackler, the romantic lead in the series, from 2012 to 2017.
- Driver stated he will not participate in the revival but wishes the cast the best of luck.
