Peggy King, a television and recording star from the 1950s, has died at the age of 96. She passed away following a battle with Alzheimer's at an assisted living facility in Wynnewood, Pennsylvania, as confirmed by her publicist.

## Key facts
- Peggy King was a regular on the “George Gobel Show” from 1954 to 1957.
- She was known by the nickname “Pretty Perky Peggy King,” given by George Gobel.
- King recorded several albums for Columbia Records.
- She appeared in the film "The Bad and the Beautiful," directed by Vincente Minnelli.
