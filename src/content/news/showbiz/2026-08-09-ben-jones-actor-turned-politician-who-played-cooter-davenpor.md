## Ben Jones, Actor Turned Politician, Passes Away at 84

Ben Jones, the beloved actor renowned for his portrayal of Cooter Davenport in the iconic television series "The Dukes of Hazzard," has passed away at the age of 84. His wife, Alma Viator, confirmed the news in a heartfelt Facebook post on Sunday afternoon, revealing that Jones died from a heart attack.

### A Legacy in Entertainment

Jones was born on August 30, 1938, in the small town of Tarboro, North Carolina. He began his career in the entertainment industry as a stage actor before transitioning to television and film. His role as Cooter Davenport, the affable mechanic and friend to the Duke cousins, made him a household name during the show's run from 1979 to 1985. The character was known for his humor, loyalty, and resourcefulness, traits that endeared him to fans of the series.

Beyond his acting career, Jones was also a successful entrepreneur. He opened a restaurant in Virginia known as Cooter's Place, which celebrated the legacy of "The Dukes of Hazzard" and served as a gathering place for fans of the show. His passion for the series and its impact on American culture was evident in his commitment to preserving its memory.

### Transition to Politics

In addition to his work in entertainment, Jones ventured into politics, serving as a Democratic member of the Virginia House of Delegates from 1983 to 1989. His political career was marked by a focus on issues such as education and economic development, reflecting his deep commitment to his community. Jones often spoke about the importance of public service and the role of government in improving the lives of citizens.
