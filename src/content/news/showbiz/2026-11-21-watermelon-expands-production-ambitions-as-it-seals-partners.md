Watermelon Pictures has announced a strategic partnership with the Film Committee at Media City Qatar to develop and co-produce films focused on stories from the Global South. This partnership signifies a significant expansion into larger-scale, mainstream production for Watermelon Pictures.

## Key facts
- Watermelon Pictures is collaborating with the Film Committee at Media City Qatar.
- The partnership aims to develop and co-produce a slate of films.
- The films will focus on stories from the Global South.
- This marks a significant expansion for Watermelon Pictures into mainstream production.
