The Marathi-language thriller "Gondhal" has unveiled its global trailer as part of India's campaign for the international feature Oscar at the 99th Academy Awards. This film marks the first feature from writer-director Santosh Davakhar.

## Key facts
- "Gondhal" is India's entry for the international feature Oscar.
- The film is a Marathi-language thriller.
- It is the first feature directed by Santosh Davakhar.
- The film was selected from a pool of 33 films in contention for the Oscar.
