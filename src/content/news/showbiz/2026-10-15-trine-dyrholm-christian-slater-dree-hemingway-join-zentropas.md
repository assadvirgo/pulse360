## Trine Dyrholm, Christian Slater, and Dree Hemingway Set to Star in Zentropa’s ‘Incidents of Intimacy’

Danish film production company Zentropa has announced a notable casting lineup for its upcoming film, *Incidents of Intimacy*. The film, directed by Jesper Just, will feature acclaimed actors Trine Dyrholm, Christian Slater, and Dree Hemingway. This project marks the second installment in Zentropa’s new collection of Dogma25 films, which seeks to revitalize the principles established in the original Dogma Manifesto of 1995.

### A New Chapter for Dogma Cinema

The Dogma Manifesto, co-created by Danish filmmakers Lars von Trier and Thomas Vinterberg, aimed to challenge traditional filmmaking conventions by emphasizing authenticity and simplicity. The initiative was first introduced at the Cannes Film Festival in 1995 and has since influenced a generation of filmmakers. The recent announcement of the Dogma25 initiative at the Cannes Film Festival in May 2023 has reignited interest in this groundbreaking approach to cinema.

Zentropa’s Dogma25 initiative seeks to reinterpret the original manifesto for a contemporary audience, blending innovative storytelling with the core tenets of the original Dogma movement. The first film in this new series has yet to be released, but *Incidents of Intimacy* promises to further explore the emotional and psychological dimensions of human relationships.

### A Compelling Narrative

While specific plot details remain under wraps, it has been revealed that Trine Dyrholm will portray a woman grappling with profound personal challenges. Dyrholm, a celebrated actress known for her roles in films such as *The Commune* and *Queen of Hearts*, is expected to bring depth and nuance to her character. 

Christian Slater, an American actor with a diverse career spanning film and television, joins the cast, bringing his unique charisma and experience to the project. Slater is recognized for his roles in iconic films like *Heathers* and *True Romance*, as well as his recent work in series such as *Mr. Robot*. Dree Hemingway, an accomplished actress and model, rounds out the cast, adding her distinctive presence to the film. Hemingway has appeared in various independent films, showcasing her versatility as an actress.

### The Impact of Dogma25

The Dogma25 initiative is not just a revival of a cinematic style; it represents a shift in the landscape of film production. By inviting a new generation of filmmakers to engage with the principles of Dogma, Zentropa aims to foster creativity and innovation in storytelling. This approach encourages filmmakers to explore themes of intimacy, vulnerability, and authenticity in their work.

As audiences increasingly seek films that resonate on a personal level, *Incidents of Intimacy* is poised to capture the essence of contemporary human experiences through its character-driven narrative. The combination of Dyrholm’s compelling performance, Slater’s seasoned talent, and Hemingway’s fresh perspective is expected to create a rich tapestry of storytelling that aligns with the ethos of the Dogma movement.

### Conclusion

*Incidents of Intimacy* is shaping up to be a significant addition to the Dogma25 collection, promising to deliver a thought-provoking exploration of intimacy and human connection. With its distinguished cast and the creative vision of Jesper Just, the film is anticipated to resonate with both critics and audiences alike. As production progresses, the film will undoubtedly attract attention within the film community and beyond, reaffirming the enduring relevance of the Dogma principles in modern cinema.
