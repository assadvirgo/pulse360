Michael Chiklis has joined the cast of the upcoming science-fiction action thriller "Bluefly," alongside Russell Crowe and Priyanka Chopra Jonas. The film is directed by Nimród Antal and is currently being shot on Australia's Gold Coast.

## Key facts
- Michael Chiklis has secured a lead role in "Bluefly."
- The film also stars Russell Crowe and Priyanka Chopra Jonas.
- "Bluefly" is directed by Nimród Antal.
- The production is taking place on Australia's Gold Coast.
- The storyline follows a grieving UN translator in the Congo.
