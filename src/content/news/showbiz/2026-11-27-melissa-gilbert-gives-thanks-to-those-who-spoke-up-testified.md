She acknowledged individuals who "spoke up, testified and wrote letters" in his favor after he faced charges related to criminal sexual contact with a minor and child abuse, according to Deadline.

## Key facts
- Melissa Gilbert gave thanks to supporters of her husband, Timothy Busfield.
