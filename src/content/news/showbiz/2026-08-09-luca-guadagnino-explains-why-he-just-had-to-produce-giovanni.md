## Luca Guadagnino Champions Giovanni Tortorici’s ‘Ketticè’ Amidst Challenges in Italian Cinema

At a recent Swiss film festival, renowned Italian filmmaker Luca Guadagnino took the opportunity to discuss his role as a producer for Giovanni Tortorici’s new coming-of-age drama, ‘Ketticè.’ Guadagnino, known for his critically acclaimed works such as ‘Call Me by Your Name’ and ‘Suspiria,’ expressed his commitment to supporting emerging voices in a landscape he described as “sterile” for Italian cinema.

### A Call for Change in Italian Film

Guadagnino's remarks highlight a growing concern within the Italian film industry about the lack of fresh narratives and innovative storytelling. He emphasized the importance of fostering new talent, particularly in a climate where traditional structures may stifle creativity. ‘Ketticè’ serves as a poignant example of this need, focusing on the experiences of young people who feel abandoned by both educational systems and adult figures.

The film features a compelling cast, including the esteemed actress Monica Bellucci, which adds significant weight to its narrative. Guadagnino noted that the inclusion of such talent not only elevates the film but also underscores the urgency of its message. He believes that ‘Ketticè’ can resonate with audiences, shedding light on the struggles faced by the youth of today.

### The Story of ‘Ketticè’

‘Ketticè’ tells the story of young individuals navigating the complexities of adolescence in a society that often overlooks their needs. The narrative delves into themes of alienation and the quest for identity, reflecting a broader commentary on the failures of institutions meant to support youth. Tortorici, who previously worked as Guadagnino’s assistant, brings a personal touch to the project, drawing from his own experiences and observations.

Guadagnino’s involvement in the film is indicative of his belief in Tortorici’s vision and storytelling capabilities. He expressed pride in mentoring the next generation of filmmakers, seeing it as a vital contribution to the evolution of Italian cinema. 

### The Role of Festivals in Film Promotion

Film festivals play a crucial role in promoting new works and facilitating discussions around pressing themes in cinema. Guadagnino’s presence at the Swiss festival not only highlights the significance of ‘Ketticè’ but also serves to amplify the voices of filmmakers who may otherwise struggle to gain recognition. He remarked on the festival’s ability to connect artists with audiences, fostering an environment where diverse stories can thrive.

### Looking Forward

As Italian cinema faces challenges in innovation and audience engagement, projects like ‘Ketticè’ offer a glimmer of hope. Guadagnino’s advocacy for new talent and fresh narratives is a call to action for the industry to embrace change. By supporting emerging filmmakers, he aims to reinvigorate the Italian film landscape, ensuring that it remains relevant and reflective of contemporary society.

In conclusion, Luca Guadagnino’s production of ‘Ketticè’ not only showcases his commitment to nurturing new talent but also highlights the urgent need for a shift in the Italian film industry. As audiences await the film’s release, it stands as a testament to the power of storytelling in addressing the complexities of youth and the societal structures that shape their experiences.
