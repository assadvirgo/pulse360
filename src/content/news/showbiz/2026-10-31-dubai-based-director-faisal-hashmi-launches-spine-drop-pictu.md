Faisal Hashmi, a Dubai-based indie director and writer, has launched a new production company called Spine Drop Pictures. The company focuses on genre films and has announced its inaugural slate, which includes three horror features.

## Key facts
- Faisal Hashmi has a 15-year career in filmmaking.
- He has previously created a collection of 10 contained, high-concept genre shorts.
- Spine Drop Pictures is focused on producing horror films.
