Production has wrapped on the film "Dead At 27," which is described as a "Gen Z music thriller." The film stars Selma Blair, Sierra McCormick, Logan Miller, and Dylan Playfair. It will also feature music from artists Iann Dior, Tommy Genesis, and Kya (Bludnymph) Hansen.

## Key facts
- "Dead At 27" is a music thriller aimed at a Gen Z audience.
- The film stars Selma Blair, Sierra McCormick, Logan Miller, and Dylan Playfair.
- Music for the film will be provided by Iann Dior, Tommy Genesis, and Kya (Bludnymph) Hansen.
- K. Asher Levin is the director of the film.
