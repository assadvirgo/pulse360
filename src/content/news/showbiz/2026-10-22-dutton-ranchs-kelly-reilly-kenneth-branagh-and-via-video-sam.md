A Paramount U.K. upfront event in London featured notable appearances from Kelly Reilly, Kenneth Branagh, and a video message from Samuel L. Jackson. The event included updates on season two of 'Dutton Ranch' and news regarding Pluto TV.

## Key facts
- Kelly Reilly and Kenneth Branagh were among the big names at the Paramount U.K. upfront event.
- A season two update for 'Dutton Ranch' was presented.
- Other attendees included Peter Sarsgaard, 'MobLand' executive producer David Glasser, and international ad sales president Lee Sears.
- David Beckham participated via video.
