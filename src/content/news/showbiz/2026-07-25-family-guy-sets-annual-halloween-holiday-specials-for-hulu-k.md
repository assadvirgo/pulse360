## 'Family Guy' Announces Annual Halloween and Holiday Specials for Hulu

In a recent announcement at Comic-Con, Rich Appel, the executive producer and showrunner of the long-running animated series "Family Guy," revealed that the show's Halloween and holiday specials will return exclusively to Hulu for a third consecutive year in 2026. This news has generated excitement among fans who look forward to the unique blend of humor and seasonal festivities that the series is known for.

### A New Guest Star

One of the highlights of the upcoming Halloween special is the casting of Kieran Culkin, best known for his role in HBO's critically acclaimed series "Succession." Culkin will lend his voice to the character of the devil, promising to bring a fresh and engaging dynamic to the episode. His involvement adds a layer of anticipation, as fans are eager to see how his portrayal will fit into the show's distinctive comedic style.

### Continuing a Tradition

"Family Guy" has established itself as a staple in animated television since its debut in 1999. The series is renowned not only for its satire and irreverent humor but also for its ability to adapt and evolve with changing audience tastes. The annual Halloween and holiday specials have become a cherished tradition for fans, offering a unique opportunity for the show to explore seasonal themes while maintaining its signature comedic approach.

The decision to continue this tradition on Hulu underscores the platform's commitment to providing exclusive content that resonates with viewers. As streaming services become increasingly competitive, original programming that appeals to established fan bases is crucial for attracting and retaining subscribers.

### What to Expect

While specific plot details for the upcoming specials remain under wraps, fans can anticipate the same blend of outrageous humor and pop culture references that "Family Guy" is known for. The series often incorporates timely social commentary and parodies, making each special a reflection of current events and trends.

As the release date approaches, additional details about the episodes, including other guest stars and plotlines, are likely to be revealed. The anticipation surrounding these specials is expected to build as more information is shared through promotional materials and social media.

### Conclusion

The return of "Family Guy's" Halloween and holiday specials to Hulu in 2026, along with the addition of Kieran Culkin as the devil, marks an exciting development for fans of the series. As the show continues to innovate and expand its reach, it remains a significant player in the landscape of animated television. With its unique blend of humor and seasonal themes, "Family Guy" is poised to deliver another memorable set of specials that will entertain audiences during the festive season.
