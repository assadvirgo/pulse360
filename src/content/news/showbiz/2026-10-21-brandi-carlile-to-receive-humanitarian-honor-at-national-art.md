Brandi Carlile will receive the Los Angeles Press Club's annual Visionary Award for Humanitarian Work at the National Arts and Entertainment Journalism Awards Gala in December. The award will be presented during a dinner and awards show scheduled for December 6 in downtown Los Angeles, according to Variety.

## Key facts
- Brandi Carlile is the recipient of the Visionary Award for Humanitarian Work.
- The award will be presented at the National Arts and Entertainment Journalism Awards Gala.
- The gala is scheduled for December 6 in downtown Los Angeles.
