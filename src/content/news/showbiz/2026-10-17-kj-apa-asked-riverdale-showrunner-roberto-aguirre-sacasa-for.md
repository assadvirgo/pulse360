KJ Apa has expressed interest in a cameo for the upcoming adaptation of "Afterlife with Archie." The actor, who portrayed Archie in "Riverdale" for seven seasons, reached out to series creator Roberto Aguirre-Sacasa regarding the possibility.

## Key facts
- KJ Apa is navigating life after "Riverdale."
- He is interested in a return for the "Afterlife with Archie" series adaptation.
- Apa portrayed Archie in "Riverdale" from 2017 to 2023.
- Roberto Aguirre-Sacasa is the series creator and chief creative officer of Archie Comics.
