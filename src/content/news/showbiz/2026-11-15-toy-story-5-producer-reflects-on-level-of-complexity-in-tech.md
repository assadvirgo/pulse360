The producer of "Toy Story 5," Lindsey Collins, discussed the evolving technology in animation and its impact on the film franchise. She emphasized the importance of balancing advancements in animation with the aesthetic of the films.

## Key facts
- Lindsey Collins is the producer of "Toy Story 5" and has worked on other Disney/Pixar films.
- The latest "Toy Story" film showcases the integration of advanced technology in animation.
- Collins highlighted the challenge of not overdeveloping the looks of animations while keeping pace with technological advancements.
