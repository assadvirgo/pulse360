## ‘Hadestown’ Parts Ways With Hades Actor Geno Segers After Online Controversy

In a significant development for Broadway's acclaimed musical "Hadestown," actor Geno Segers has been removed from the production before even stepping onto the stage. The announcement followed a wave of online controversy that emerged shortly after Segers was cast as Hades, the god of the underworld in the show.

### Background of the Controversy

Segers, who was recently announced as part of the principal cast for "Hadestown," has a diverse portfolio that includes notable stage performances in productions such as "On the Town" and "The Lion King," as well as television roles in series like "Banshee" and "Pair of Kings." However, his casting sparked backlash on social media platforms, where users raised concerns regarding past comments and actions that they deemed problematic.

The nature of the controversy has not been explicitly detailed by the production team, but it reflects a growing trend in the entertainment industry where public figures are held accountable for their past behaviors and statements. In recent years, Broadway has seen an increasing emphasis on inclusivity and sensitivity, leading to heightened scrutiny of casting decisions and the backgrounds of performers.

### Hadestown's Response

In a statement released by "Hadestown," the production expressed its commitment to maintaining a positive and inclusive environment for both its cast and audience members. The decision to part ways with Segers was described as a proactive measure to uphold the values of the show. The statement did not delve into specifics regarding the reasons for the decision, but it underscored the importance of aligning the cast with the production's ethos.

### The Impact on the Production

With Segers' departure, "Hadestown" will need to swiftly find a replacement for the role of Hades, which is pivotal to the narrative of the musical. The production, which has received critical acclaim and multiple Tony Awards, will likely face challenges in navigating the public relations aspect of this situation while also ensuring that the show continues to run smoothly.
