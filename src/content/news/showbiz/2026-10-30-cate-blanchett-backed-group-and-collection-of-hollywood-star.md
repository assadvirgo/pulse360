The Human Consent Foundation, a nonprofit supported by Cate Blanchett, along with various Hollywood companies, has filed an amicus brief in the New York Times' lawsuit against OpenAI and Microsoft. The brief argues that Hollywood has a "proven infrastructure" to license content and addresses concerns over unauthorized scraping by AI models.

## Key facts
- The Human Consent Foundation aims to protect individuals' likenesses from unauthorized use by AI.
- The amicus brief was filed in the context of the New York Times' lawsuit against OpenAI and Microsoft.
- The brief asserts that the tech companies' claims about not being able to clear rights are unfounded.
