## Jamie Lee Curtis to Receive Inaugural Award at Children’s Institute Gala

In a notable event celebrating philanthropy and the arts, acclaimed actress Jamie Lee Curtis is set to be honored with the inaugural award inspired by renowned filmmakers Rob and Michele Reiner. This recognition will take place at the upcoming Children's Institute Gala, an event designed to support the mission of the Children's Institute in providing essential services and programs for children in need.

### A Legacy of Kindness

The award, which aims to embody the values instilled by Rob and Michele Reiner, was established by their children, Jake and Romy Reiner, in collaboration with their aunt, Martine Singer, who serves as the president and CEO of the Children's Institute. In a statement reflecting on the significance of the award, Jake Reiner emphasized the importance of leading by example. "Our parents taught us what it means to be good, decent human beings. And they didn't just talk about it; they led by example," he remarked. This sentiment underscores the Reiner family's commitment to philanthropy and community service, values that resonate deeply within the mission of the Children's Institute.

### A Night of Celebration and Music

The gala is not only a platform for honoring Curtis but also promises to be an evening filled with entertainment. Acclaimed musician John Mayer is scheduled to perform, adding a musical dimension to the event that is expected to draw attention from both the entertainment industry and philanthropic circles. Mayer's participation highlights the intersection of art and charity, as he joins other notable figures in supporting a cause that aims to uplift vulnerable children.

### The Children's Institute's Mission

The Children's Institute has long been dedicated to providing comprehensive services to children facing various challenges, including mental health issues, developmental disabilities, and trauma. The organization works to create a nurturing environment where children can thrive, and events like the gala play a crucial role in raising funds and awareness for its programs.

### Jamie Lee Curtis: A Role Model

Jamie Lee Curtis, known for her versatile acting career and advocacy work, has long been an inspiration to many. Her commitment to social causes, particularly those related to children's welfare and mental health, aligns seamlessly with the ethos of the award she is set to receive. Curtis's recognition at this gala not only honors her achievements in film but also her contributions to society, making her a fitting recipient of an award that celebrates integrity and compassion.

### Conclusion

As the Children's Institute Gala approaches, anticipation builds around the celebration of Jamie Lee Curtis and the legacy of the Reiner family. This event promises to be a meaningful gathering, bringing together individuals dedicated to making a difference in the lives of children. With John Mayer's performance adding to the evening's allure, the gala stands as a testament to the power of community, compassion, and the arts in fostering positive change.
