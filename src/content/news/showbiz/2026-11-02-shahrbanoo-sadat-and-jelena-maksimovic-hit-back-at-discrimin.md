Afghan director Shahrbanoo Sadat addressed issues of discrimination in film funding during a talk at the Tokyo International Film Festival. She highlighted that a lack of funding and ignorance within institutions contribute to the disadvantages faced by women in the film industry.

## Key facts
- Shahrbanoo Sadat spoke at the Tokyo International Film Festival about women's cinema.
- She stated that discrimination in film funding begins at the development level.
- Sadat emphasized that the lack of funding keeps women at a disadvantage in the industry.
