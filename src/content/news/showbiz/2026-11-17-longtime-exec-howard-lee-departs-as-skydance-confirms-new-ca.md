Skydance has announced a new division name for its merged cable networks: Skydance Global Cable Media. The structure of this new entity, which combines Warner Bros. Discovery and Paramount Global cable networks, was revealed by Channing Dungey, head of Warner Bros. TV Group and U.S. Networks. Longtime executive Howard Lee is departing as part of this organizational change, according to Variety.

## Key facts
- Skydance's merged cable networks will be called Skydance Global Cable Media.
- The new structure combines Warner Bros. Discovery and Paramount Global cable networks.
- Channing Dungey announced the new division and its structure.
- Howard Lee, a longtime executive, is departing from the organization.
