JoBe Cerny, known for voicing the Pillsbury Doughboy, passed away on October 30 at the age of 78. He was born and raised in Cicero, Illinois, and held degrees in speech and drama as well as theater.

## Key facts
- JoBe Cerny died on October 30 at the age of 78, according to Variety.
- He voiced the Pillsbury Doughboy character in recent years.
- Cerny was born and raised in Cicero, Illinois.
- He earned a bachelor's degree in speech and drama from Valparaiso University.
- He obtained a master's degree in theater from Northwestern University.
