Jesse Eisenberg's comedy "The Debut" will open the 23rd Marrakech International Film Festival. The film features Julianne Moore as a shy housewife who discovers a passion for amateur theater.

## Key facts
- "The Debut" is set to be the opening film of the festival.
- The festival will showcase 76 films from 39 countries.
- The Marrakech International Film Festival is Morocco's premiere film festival.
