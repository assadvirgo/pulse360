Angel Studios is implementing a new content policy that prohibits sex scenes, nudity, the use of F-words, and the misuse of God's name in its films. The studio, recognized for productions like "Sound of Freedom," will also allow its paying members to vote on movies and TV shows.

## Key facts
- Angel Studios is introducing a new content policy.
- The policy bans on-screen sex, nudity, F-words, and misuse of God's name.
- The studio is known for the film "Sound of Freedom."
- Paying members will have the opportunity to vote on movies and TV shows.
