## New IP Studio Forge Films Company Launches with Viking Mythology Series

A new player in the entertainment industry has emerged as executives Antoine Disle, Orso Vesperini, and Olivier Compere have officially launched Forge Films Company. This new intellectual property (IP) studio is dedicated to producing content in the genres of science fiction, fantasy, and horror. 

### Background of the Founders

The trio behind Forge Films Company brings a wealth of experience to the table. Disle, Vesperini, and Compere have previously collaborated on projects for major networks such as Canal+, TF1, and Warner Bros. Their combined expertise in the industry positions them well to navigate the competitive landscape of content creation. 

### Focus on Genre Content

Forge Films Company aims to carve out a niche in the market by focusing on genre content that resonates with audiences seeking imaginative storytelling. The studio's commitment to sci-fi, fantasy, and horror aligns with current trends in the entertainment industry, where such genres have seen a significant rise in popularity. 

### First Project: Lost in Hel

As part of its inaugural slate, Forge Films has announced the development of a Viking mythology series titled "Lost in Hel." This project is expected to explore the rich tapestry of Norse mythology, delving into themes of heroism, fate, and the supernatural. The choice of Viking mythology reflects a growing interest in historical narratives that incorporate fantastical elements, appealing to both fans of the genre and those interested in cultural storytelling.

### Industry Reactions

The launch of Forge Films Company and its first project has garnered attention within the industry. Experts suggest that the combination of experienced executives and a focus on compelling genre narratives could position the studio for success. As audiences continue to seek innovative and engaging content, the studio's approach may resonate well with viewers looking for fresh perspectives in storytelling.

### Conclusion

With the establishment of Forge Films Company and the unveiling of "Lost in Hel," the trio of Antoine Disle, Orso Vesperini, and Olivier Compere is poised to make a significant impact in the entertainment landscape. As they embark on this new venture, the industry will be watching closely to see how their vision unfolds and how it contributes to the evolving narrative landscape of sci-fi, fantasy, and horror.
