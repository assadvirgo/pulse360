Lauren Neustadter, President of Film and Television at Hello Sunshine, is leaving the company after nearly a decade. Her departure follows a recent acquisition of the company by French group Mediawan, which partnered with Reese Witherspoon.

## Key facts
- Lauren Neustadter is stepping down from her role at Hello Sunshine at the end of the year.
- Neustadter's exit comes shortly after Hello Sunshine reduced its staff by about 50%.
- The acquisition of Hello Sunshine by Mediawan occurred just over a month ago.
