Chris Pratt will star in the sci-fi film "The Traveler," which is being produced by Paramount Pictures. This project is part of a new three-year first-look producing deal that Pratt has signed with the studio.

## Key facts
- "The Traveler" is based on a novel by Joseph Eckert.
- Zach Lipovsky and Adam Stein are set to direct the film.
- The first-look deal allows Pratt to potentially star in select projects at Paramount through his Indivisible Productions banner.
