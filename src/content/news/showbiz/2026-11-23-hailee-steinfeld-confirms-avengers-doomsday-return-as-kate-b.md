Hailee Steinfeld will reprise her role as Kate Bishop in the upcoming film "Avengers: Doomsday." The announcement was made by Marvel Studios and Disney Animation through an Instagram post featuring Steinfeld.

## Key facts
- Hailee Steinfeld confirmed her return as Kate Bishop in "Avengers: Doomsday."
- The announcement was made via an Instagram post by Marvel Studios and Disney Animation.
- Steinfeld is currently in Europe promoting Disney Animation's film "Hexed."
- The announcement served as cross-promotion for both "Avengers: Doomsday" and "Hexed."
