Philip Rosenberg, an Oscar-winning production designer known for his work on "All That Jazz," has died at the age of 91. He passed away on October 28 in West Palm Beach, Florida, as announced by his nephew.

## Key facts
- Philip Rosenberg was part of the team that won the art direction-set decoration Oscar for "All That Jazz."
- He died on October 28 in West Palm Beach, Florida.
- His death was announced by his nephew, Alan Rosenberg.
