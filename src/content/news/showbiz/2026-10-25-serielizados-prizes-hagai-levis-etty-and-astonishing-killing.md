The Serielizados Prizes were awarded in Barcelona, recognizing outstanding television series. Hagai Levi's "Etty" won best international series, while the Spanish duo Jorge and Alberto Sánchez-Cabezudo's "Killing a Bear" took home the award for best national series.

## Key facts
- Hagai Levi's "Etty" won best international series at the Serielizados Prizes.
- "Killing a Bear," created by Jorge and Alberto Sánchez-Cabezudo, won best national series.
- The awards were announced during a ceremony in Barcelona.
