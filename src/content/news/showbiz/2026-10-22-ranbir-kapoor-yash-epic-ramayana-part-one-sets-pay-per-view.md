Nitesh Tiwari’s film “Ramayana: Part One,” featuring Ranbir Kapoor and Yash, will be available for pay-per-view on JioHotstar in India following its theatrical release. The arrangement between JioHotstar and Prime Focus Studios will allow the film to be purchased on demand.

## Key facts
- "Ramayana: Part One" is directed by Nitesh Tiwari.
- The film stars Ranbir Kapoor and Yash.
- It will be available for on-demand purchase on JioHotstar after its theatrical run.
- This release marks the first pay-per-view offering on the platform.
