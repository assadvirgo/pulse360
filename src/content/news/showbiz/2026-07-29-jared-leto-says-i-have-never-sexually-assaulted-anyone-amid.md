## Jared Leto Responds to Sexual Assault Allegations in BBC Documentary

In a statement addressing the allegations, Leto asserted that the claims are "categorically false." He emphasized his innocence and expressed his commitment to defending his reputation against what he describes as unfounded accusations. The documentary has sparked significant media attention and public discourse, drawing attention to the broader issues of sexual misconduct in the entertainment industry.
