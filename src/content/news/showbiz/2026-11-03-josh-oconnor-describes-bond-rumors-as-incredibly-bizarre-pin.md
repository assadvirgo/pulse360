Josh O'Connor has commented on the rumors surrounding his potential candidacy for the role of James Bond. He described the speculation as an "incredibly bizarre pinch me moment."

## Key facts
- Josh O'Connor is rumored to be one of the finalists to succeed Daniel Craig as James Bond.
- He referred to the prospect of taking on the role as extraordinary.
- O'Connor is known for his roles in "Challengers" and "The Crown."
- He shared his thoughts during an interview with the BBC.
