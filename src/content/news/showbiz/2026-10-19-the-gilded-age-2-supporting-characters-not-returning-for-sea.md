The HBO series "The Gilded Age" will not feature two supporting characters in its upcoming fourth season. Richard Clay, George Russell's secretary, and T. Thomas Fortune, the publisher of the New York Globe, will not return.

## Key facts
- Richard Clay is portrayed by Patrick Page.
- T. Thomas Fortune is played by Sullivan Jones.
- The show's cast expands each season, leading to some characters being removed from rotation.
