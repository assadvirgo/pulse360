Sylvester Stallone discussed his long-standing rivalry with Arnold Schwarzenegger, which lasted for two decades. He stated that their competition ended when Schwarzenegger ran for governor of California and was no longer making movies.

## Key facts
- Stallone reflected on his rivalry with Schwarzenegger during the 1980s and 1990s.
- The rivalry concluded when Schwarzenegger ran for governor of California.
- Stallone mentioned that the competition ceased as Schwarzenegger was not "doing movies anymore."
