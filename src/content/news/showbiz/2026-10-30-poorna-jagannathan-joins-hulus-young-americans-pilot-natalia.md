Poorna Jagannathan has been cast as a series regular in Hulu's pilot for "Young Americans." Natalia Leite, known for her work on "The Handmaid's Tale," will direct the pilot.

## Key facts
- Poorna Jagannathan has joined the cast of Hulu's "Young Americans" pilot.
- She is known for her roles in "Deli Boys" and "Lanterns."
- Other cast members include Scott Foley, Billie Boullet, Sam Rechner, Mia Isaac, Oscar Winter, and Lauren Mei.
- Natalia Leite will direct the pilot.
