## Homer Gere Reflects on Working with Kaia Gerber in 'The Shards'

In a recent interview, Homer Gere expressed his amusement at collaborating with model and actress Kaia Gerber on the upcoming film "The Shards." Homer Gere, the son of acclaimed actor Richard Gere, and Kaia Gerber, daughter of supermodel Cindy Crawford, have both carved their paths in the entertainment industry. Given this backdrop, the opportunity for the two to work together has been described by Homer as "really funny," highlighting the unique connection they share through their families.
