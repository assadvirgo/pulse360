French-Senegalese filmmaker Alain Gomis’ film “Dao” won the Golden Spike for best film at the 71st Valladolid International Film Festival in Spain. Marine Atlan’s debut film “La Gradiva” received the Silver Spike.

## Key facts
- “Dao” is described as an expansive family saga spanning Europe and Africa.
- The film unfolds between a wedding in France and an ancestral memorial ceremony in Guinea-Bissau.
- The Valladolid International Film Festival is also known as Seminci.
