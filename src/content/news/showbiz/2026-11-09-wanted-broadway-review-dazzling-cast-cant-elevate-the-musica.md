A new Broadway musical titled "Wanted," written by Angelica Chéri and directed by Stevie Walker-Webb, has premiered. The musical is inspired by Chéri's great-great aunts, Mary and Martha Clarke, who were Black women living in post-emancipation Texas.

## Key facts
- "Wanted" is a new Broadway musical.
- The musical is written by Angelica Chéri and directed by Stevie Walker-Webb.
- It is inspired by Chéri's great-great aunts, Mary and Martha Clarke.
- The story is set in post-emancipation Texas.
