Quentin Tarantino showcased the first 17 minutes of his upcoming film, "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth," during a Q&A session at AFI Fest. This event followed a screening of his previous film, "Once Upon a Time in Hollywood."

## Key facts
- Tarantino surprised the audience at AFI Fest with the first look at the sequel.
- The screening took place after a Q&A session regarding "Once Upon a Time in Hollywood."
- This marks the first public preview of "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth."
