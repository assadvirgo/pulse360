## Key facts
- Patrick Stewart died at 86.
- He was best known for playing Captain Jean-Luc Picard in "Star Trek."
- He also portrayed Professor Charles Xavier in the "X-Men" movies.
- Stewart received four Emmy nominations, three Golden Globe nominations, and a Tony nomination during his career.
