A three-hour documentary titled "You Can See Everything" has earned $19.5 million in its second weekend of release. Meanwhile, the new comic-book adaptation "Clayface" debuted at the top of the box office with $24 million.

## Key facts
- "You Can See Everything" earned $19.5 million in its second weekend.
- "Clayface" debuted at No. 1 with $24 million from 3,458 North American theaters.
- "Clayface" is an R-rated film produced by Warner Bros. and DC.
