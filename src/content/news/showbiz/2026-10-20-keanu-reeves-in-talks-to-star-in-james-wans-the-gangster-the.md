Keanu Reeves is in discussions to star in James Wan's upcoming film "The Gangster, The Cop, The Devil" at Paramount Pictures. This project reimagines the 2019 Korean action-crime thriller, with Don Lee set to reprise his role alongside Reeves.

## Key facts
- Keanu Reeves is in talks to join the film.
- The project is directed by James Wan.
- "The Gangster, The Cop, The Devil" is a reimagining of a 2019 Korean film.
- Don Lee is attached to reprise his role in the new version.
