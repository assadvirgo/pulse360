JioStar, an Indian media company backed by Reliance Industries, reported a 10.1% increase in revenue from operations for the July–September quarter. The company generated INR68.1 billion ($710 million) despite facing a challenging advertising market.

## Key facts
- JioStar's revenue from operations grew to INR68.1 billion ($710 million) year-on-year.
- Profit after tax decreased by 13.2% to INR11.5 billion ($120 million).
- Sourav Ganguly hosted the first-ever "Bigg Boss Bangla," which launched during this quarter.
