TikTok has restored the promotional account for the documentary "Musk" after it was reportedly suspended due to an error. The documentary, directed by Alex Gibney, addresses Elon Musk and is produced by Bleecker/Universal.

## Key facts
- The TikTok account for the documentary "Musk" was initially reported as permanently suspended.
- TikTok stated that the suspension was the result of "an error."
- The account has since been restored.
