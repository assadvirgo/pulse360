Lionsgate anticipates a strong Thanksgiving box office for "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping," projecting earnings of over $85 million over the five-day holiday period. This performance could mark the second-best Thanksgiving haul for the franchise, contingent on weather conditions in the Northeast.

## Key facts
- "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping" is expected to earn over $85 million during the Thanksgiving holiday.
- This would represent the second-best Thanksgiving box office performance for the franchise.
- The success is dependent on whether extreme weather affects theater attendance in the Northeast.
