The directors of "Writing With Fire," Rintu Thomas and Sushmit Ghosh, have announced the fourth cohort of the Himalayan Story Lab, a fellowship for documentary filmmakers from the South Asian Himalayas.

## Key facts
- The fellowship is a year-long program for documentary filmmakers.
- Eight filmmakers will develop six documentaries during the 2026–27 edition.
- The stories will be drawn from regions including Kashmir, Ladakh, Uttarakhand, Assam, Manipur, and Arunachal Pradesh.
- Payal Kapadia and Shaunak Sen have been tapped as mentors for this cohort.
