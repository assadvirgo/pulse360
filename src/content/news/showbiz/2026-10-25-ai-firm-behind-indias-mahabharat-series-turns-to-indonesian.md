The AI technology behind the Indian series "Mahabharat: Ek Dharmayudh" is expanding to Indonesia. Developer Galleri5 and Collective Studios' Historyverse plan to create film and series properties based on Indonesian myths and history.

## Key facts
- Galleri5 and Collective Studios' Historyverse are collaborating on the project.
- The initiative will focus on creating content inspired by Indonesian myths and history.
- Historyverse is responsible for original IP and studio work for Collective Artists Network.
