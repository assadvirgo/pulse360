Jakarta World Cinema has expanded its festival to Malang and Bandung, marking the first time it has taken place outside the Indonesian capital. The inaugural events opened simultaneously on November 14 in both cities, which are known for their large student populations and vibrant creative scenes.

## Key facts
- The inaugural Bandung World Cinema and Malang World Cinema opened on November 14.
- Bandung's screenings are held at CGV Paris Van Java.
- Malang's screenings are taking place at a different venue.
