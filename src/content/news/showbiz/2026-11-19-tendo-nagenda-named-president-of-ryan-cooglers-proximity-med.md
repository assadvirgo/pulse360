Tendo Nagenda has been named the President of Proximity Media, a company founded by Ryan Coogler, Zinzi Coogler, and Sev Ohanian. The announcement was made by the company's heads.

## Key facts
- Tendo Nagenda is a veteran industry executive.
- He will oversee all aspects of creative development and production at Proximity Media.
- Nagenda will report directly to the founders of Proximity Media.
- His role includes advancing the company's creative strategy and delivering their slate of projects.
