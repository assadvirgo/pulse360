Timothy Busfield's sexual abuse indictment has been dismissed due to the prosecution's failure to act fairly and impartially, according to Deadline. The charges stemmed from alleged incidents involving two minor boys on the set of the TV series "The Cleaning Lady."

## Key facts
- Timothy Busfield was charged with two counts of criminal sexual contact with a minor and child abuse.
- The charges were related to alleged incidents involving two minor boys.
- The case was dismissed almost a year after the initial charges were filed.
- The District Attorney is currently evaluating options following the dismissal.
