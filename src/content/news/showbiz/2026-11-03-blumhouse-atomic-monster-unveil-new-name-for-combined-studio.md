Blumhouse and Atomic Monster have announced their new combined studio name, Blumhouse Atomic, following their merger two years ago. Both production labels will continue to operate separately, maintaining their own creative teams for film and television projects.

## Key facts
- The new name for the merged entity is Blumhouse Atomic.
- Blumhouse and Atomic Monster will continue as distinct production labels.
- Each label will have its own creative teams developing and producing projects.
