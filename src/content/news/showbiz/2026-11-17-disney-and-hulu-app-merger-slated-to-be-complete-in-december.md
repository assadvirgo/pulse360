Disney's effort to unify the Disney+ and Hulu apps is nearly complete, according to company executives. The merger is expected to be finalized in December, as Disney aims to focus its streaming services on the Disney+ platform.

## Key facts
- Disney executives announced the merger during the earnings report for the September 2026 quarter.
- The merger will affect Disney+ and Hulu bundle subscribers.
- The completion of the merger is set for December.
