The stars of the "Percy Jackson" series, Walker Scobell, Leah Sava Jeffries, and Aryan Simhadri, will attend the Lucca Comics and Games Festival in Italy. They plan to unveil unseen footage ahead of the release of Season 3 on November 20.

## Key facts
- The event will take place in late October.
- The stars aim to connect with fans at the festival.
- Unseen footage of "Percy Jackson and the Olympians" will be presented.
