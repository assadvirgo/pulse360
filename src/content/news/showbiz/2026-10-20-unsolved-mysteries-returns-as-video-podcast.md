The true-crime series "Unsolved Mysteries" is returning as a video podcast. This new format follows the conclusion of its previous audio-only podcast over three years ago and comes after Netflix released new episodes of its reboot a couple of years prior.

## Key facts
- "Unsolved Mysteries" is returning as a video podcast.
- The previous audio-only podcast ended more than three years ago.
- Netflix released new episodes of the reboot a couple of years ago.
