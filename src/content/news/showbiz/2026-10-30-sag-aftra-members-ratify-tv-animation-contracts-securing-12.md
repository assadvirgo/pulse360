SAG-AFTRA members have ratified the 2026 Television Animation Agreement and Basic Cable Animation Agreement with the Alliance of Motion Picture and Television Producers. The contracts received overwhelming support, with 94.95% of members voting in favor.

## Key facts
- The ratified agreements include a 12.6% wage increase for voice actors over four years.
- The contracts also enhance protections related to artificial intelligence.
- The vote took place nationwide among SAG-AFTRA members.
