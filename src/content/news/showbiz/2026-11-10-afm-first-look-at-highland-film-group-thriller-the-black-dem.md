Highland Film Group has unveiled the first look image of the thriller "The Black Demon: Atlantis." This film is a sequel to "The Black Demon" and features Jack Kesy in the lead role.

## Key facts
- "The Black Demon: Atlantis" is a shark survival action film.
- Jack Kesy stars as undercover DEA agent Jerry.
- The story involves Jerry arriving at an offshore fortress prison called Atlantis in the Pacific Ocean.
- The plot centers around Jerry's operative who mysteriously disappears while investigating.
