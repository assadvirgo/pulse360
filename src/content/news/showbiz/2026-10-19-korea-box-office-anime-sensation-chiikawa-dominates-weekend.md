Japanese animated film "Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island" continued to perform strongly at the South Korean box office, capturing over 42% of nationwide ticket revenue during the weekend of October 16-18.

## Key facts
- "Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island" is a Japanese animated film.
- The movie commanded more than 42% of the nationwide ticket revenue in South Korea.
- This performance occurred during its third weekend in theaters.
- Data was provided by KOBIS, the tracking service operated by the Korean Film Council.
