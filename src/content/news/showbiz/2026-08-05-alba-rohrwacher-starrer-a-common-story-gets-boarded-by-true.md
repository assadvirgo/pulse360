## True Colours Acquires World Sales for 'A Common Story' Ahead of Major Film Festivals

Italian sales company True Colours has secured the world sales rights for "A Common Story" ("Una Storia"), marking a significant step for the film as it prepares for its premieres at the prestigious Venice and Toronto International Film Festivals. This film, directed by Anna Foglietta, who is making her directorial debut, features acclaimed actress Alba Rohrwacher in a leading role.

### A Promising Directorial Debut

Anna Foglietta, known for her compelling performances in various Italian films, has garnered international recognition for her role in the hit film "Perfect Strangers." Transitioning from acting to directing, Foglietta brings her unique perspective and narrative style to "A Common Story," which explores the complexities of marriage and relationships. The film is expected to resonate with audiences, particularly given its timely themes and emotional depth.

### The Role of True Colours

True Colours, an Italian sales agency, has established itself as a key player in the global film market by representing a diverse array of Italian cinema. Their acquisition of "A Common Story" underscores their commitment to promoting Italian films on the international stage. The agency's involvement is anticipated to enhance the film's visibility and distribution potential outside of Italy, especially as it approaches its festival screenings.

### Anticipation for Festival Screenings

The Venice International Film Festival, known for showcasing innovative and artistic films, will serve as the backdrop for "A Common Story's" world premiere. Following Venice, the film is set to screen at the Toronto International Film Festival, which is renowned for its role in launching films into the awards season. Both festivals attract significant media attention and industry professionals, providing an invaluable platform for the film and its creators.

### Alba Rohrwacher's Impact

Alba Rohrwacher, a prominent figure in Italian cinema, is expected to deliver a compelling performance that will further solidify her status as a leading actress. Her previous work has earned her critical acclaim, and her participation in "A Common Story" is likely to draw interest from both audiences and distributors alike. Rohrwacher's ability to portray complex characters adds depth to the narrative, making the film a noteworthy entry in this year's festival circuit.

### Conclusion

As "A Common Story" prepares to make its debut at two of the world's most esteemed film festivals, the collaboration between Anna Foglietta and True Colours signals a promising future for Italian cinema on the international stage. With its exploration of universal themes and a talented cast, the film is poised to captivate audiences and industry insiders alike, paving the way for further opportunities in the global market. The upcoming screenings in Venice and Toronto will undoubtedly be pivotal moments for the film's trajectory.
