## Joey King and Maisie Williams Embrace Their Roles in ‘Practical Magic 2’

As the beloved Owens family returns to the screen in the highly anticipated sequel, "Practical Magic 2," new cast members Joey King and Maisie Williams have expressed a profound sense of responsibility in stepping into their roles. The film, directed by Susanne Bier, aims to capture the magic and charm of the original 1998 classic, which starred Sandra Bullock and Nicole Kidman.

### A Legacy to Uphold

In "Practical Magic 2," King and Williams portray Sally Owens' daughters, Kylie and Antonia, respectively. The original film has garnered a devoted fan base over the years, and both actresses acknowledge the challenge of living up to the expectations set by their predecessors. King, known for her roles in "The Kissing Booth" and "The Act," mentioned that the weight of this responsibility was not lost on her. "We felt a massive responsibility to honor the legacy of the film and the characters that fans have loved for so long," she stated.

Williams, who gained fame as Arya Stark in the critically acclaimed series "Game of Thrones," echoed her co-star's sentiments. "It's a unique opportunity to step into a world that has such a rich history. We want to make sure we do it justice," she remarked. The actresses are aware that they are not just taking on roles; they are becoming part of a cherished narrative that has resonated with audiences for decades.

### The Creative Vision of Susanne Bier

Under the direction of Susanne Bier, known for her work on films like "In a Better World" and the series "The Night Manager," "Practical Magic 2" aims to blend the whimsical elements of the original with a contemporary storyline. Bier's vision for the sequel includes exploring themes of family, love, and the complexities of magic in modern life. 

The collaboration between Bier and the new cast has been described as invigorating. King and Williams have both praised Bier's approach to storytelling and her ability to create an inclusive and supportive environment on set. This atmosphere has allowed them to explore their characters in depth, making the transition into the Owens family feel organic.

### Anticipation from Fans

As the release date approaches, anticipation continues to build among fans of the original film. The combination of nostalgia and the fresh perspective brought by King and Williams has sparked excitement within the community. Social media platforms are abuzz with discussions about the sequel, with many expressing their eagerness to see how the new characters will fit into the established lore of the Owens family.

In conclusion, as Joey King and Maisie Williams prepare to introduce a new generation to the world of "Practical Magic," they remain committed to honoring the legacy of the original film. Their dedication to their roles and the guidance of Susanne Bier promise to deliver a sequel that resonates with both longtime fans and newcomers alike. As the Owens women reunite, the magic of family and storytelling continues to thrive.
