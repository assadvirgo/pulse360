The 12th edition of Primer Test, part of the RECTarragona Film Festival, will feature new films from the producers of "Alcarràs" and "Romería." Notable titles include Lluis Segura's AI project "Electrónica," as well as "Mother of Sugar" and "La Unión."

## Key facts
- The event is a key industry centerpiece of RECLab, a prominent Spanish directorial talent lab.
- "Electrónica" is an AI title directed by Lluis Segura.
- "Mother of Sugar" and "La Unión" are produced by the teams behind "Alcarràs" and "Romería," respectively.
