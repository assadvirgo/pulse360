In 2021, Canadian photojournalist Bryan Dickie gained access to Myanmar following the military's overthrow of the democratically elected government. This experience led him to collaborate with Robert Mentov and Dwight Jantzi, resulting in their return to Myanmar two years later.

## Key facts
- Bryan Dickie is a Canadian photojournalist.
- He accessed Myanmar 10 months after the military coup in 2021.
- Dickie's trip inspired him to collaborate with Robert Mentov and Dwight Jantzi. 
- The trio returned to Myanmar two years after Dickie's initial visit.
