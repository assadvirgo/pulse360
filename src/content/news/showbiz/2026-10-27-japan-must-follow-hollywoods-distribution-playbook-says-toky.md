Japan's entertainment industry needs to adopt Hollywood's distribution model to achieve its content export targets, according to Shiina Yasushi, CEO of TIFFCOM. The Tokyo International Film Festival's content market is entering its 23rd edition.

## Key facts
- Shiina Yasushi is the CEO of TIFFCOM.
- The Japanese government has set a content export target of JPY20 trillion (approximately $126.6 billion).
