## Marjoe Gortner, Former Child Evangelist and Actor, Passes Away at 82

Marjoe Gortner, a multifaceted figure known for his early career as a child evangelist and later as an actor, has passed away at the age of 82. Gortner died on October 2 due to heart complications in Santa Fe, New Mexico, as confirmed by his family.

### Early Life and Evangelism

Born on January 14, 1951, Gortner was thrust into the spotlight at a young age as a child preacher. He was the son of a traveling evangelist, and by the age of four, he was already performing in front of audiences, captivating congregations with his fervent delivery. His early experiences in the evangelical circuit would later serve as the foundation for his complex relationship with fame and spirituality.

In 1972, Gortner's life took a pivotal turn when he became the subject of the Oscar-winning documentary "Marjoe." The film exposed the inner workings of the evangelical movement and Gortner's own disillusionment with it. It provided a candid look at his life, revealing both the charisma that made him a star and the struggles he faced in reconciling his upbringing with his personal beliefs.

### Transition to Acting

After his time as a child evangelist, Gortner transitioned into acting, where he found success in both film and television. He is perhaps best remembered for his role in the 1974 disaster film "Earthquake," which showcased his ability to navigate the demands of a major Hollywood production. The film, which featured an ensemble cast, became a significant box office success and cemented Gortner's place in the entertainment industry.

In the 1980s, Gortner starred in the popular primetime soap opera "Falcon Crest." His role on the show allowed him to reach a wider audience and further establish his career as a versatile actor. Gortner's performances were marked by a unique blend of charm and intensity, traits that he honed during his years in the evangelical spotlight.
