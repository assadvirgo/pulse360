The current season of 'The Celebrity Traitors' is experiencing high ratings, yet it has been described as feeling unusually flat. Observers note a lack of jeopardy in the show, suggesting that "something is missing" from this run.

## Key facts
- The ratings for 'The Celebrity Traitors' are at an all-time high.
- The trio of Maya Jama, James Acaster, and King Kenny are under the radar as the season approaches its endgame.
- Critics have pointed out a significant lack of jeopardy in the current season.
