Variety has announced the honorees for its third annual Faith and Spirituality in Entertainment Honors. The event will take place in Los Angeles on December 2.

## Key facts
- Bear Grylls, Dhar Mann, Erin Foster, Lonnie Ali, Cameron Alexander, and Sonja Warfield will be recognized.
- The event is presented by the Coalition for Faith & Media.
- The Honors celebrate individuals supporting underrepresented themes of faith and spirituality.
