Fox has announced that Adam Ciongoli, the Chief Legal and Policy Officer, will step down when his contract ends on December 1. Nicholas Trutanich has been promoted to General Counsel and will serve as the company's top lawyer.

## Key facts
- Adam Ciongoli will step down as Chief Legal and Policy Officer effective December 1.
- Nicholas Trutanich has been promoted to General Counsel.
- Trutanich is currently the EVP, Principal Deputy General Counsel.
- He will report to Fox CEO Lachlan Murdoch.
