The cast of "Upgraded 2" has been expanded with the addition of four actors who will reprise their original roles. Aimee Carrero, Rachel Matthews, Andrew Schulz, and Saoirse Monica Jackson are joining the film alongside previously announced cast members.

## Key facts
- Aimee Carrero, Rachel Matthews, Andrew Schulz, and Saoirse Monica Jackson are returning to their original roles in "Upgraded 2."
- Camila Mendes, Leslie Bibb, and Marisa Tomei are also part of the cast.
- The information was reported exclusively by Deadline.
