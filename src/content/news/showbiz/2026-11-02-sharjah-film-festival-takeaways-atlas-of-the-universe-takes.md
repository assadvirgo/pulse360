The 13th Sharjah International Film Festival for Children and Youth concluded with "Atlas of the Universe" winning the best feature film award. Additionally, "My Grandfather Is a Nihonjin" received the award for best animated feature.

## Key facts
- "Atlas of the Universe" is a Romanian-Bulgarian coming-of-age drama.
- The festival wrapped up on a Sunday.
- The jury awarded "My Grandfather Is a Nihonjin" as the best animated feature.
