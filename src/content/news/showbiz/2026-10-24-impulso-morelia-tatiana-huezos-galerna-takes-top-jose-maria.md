Tatiana Huezo's film "Galerna" has won the top José María Riba Award at the Impulso Morelia event, part of the Morelia Film Festival. The award includes a monetary prize aimed at supporting post-production and promotional activities.

## Key facts
- "Galerna" is a fiction feature from Mexico.
- The José María Riba Award is presented by Cinépolis Distribution and Profest 2026.
- The award provides $14,740 (250,000 pesos) for outstanding post-production needs and/or promotional activities.
- The Morelia Film Festival is a 10-day event that wraps up on October 25.
