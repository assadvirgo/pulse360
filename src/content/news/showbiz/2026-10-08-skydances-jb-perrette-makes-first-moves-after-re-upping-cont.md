His responsibilities extend across both Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery, positioning him at the forefront of the evolving media landscape. 

Perrette's strategic vision is expected to play a pivotal role in shaping Skydance's approach to content creation and distribution, especially as the industry continues to adapt to the demands of digital streaming and consumer preferences.
