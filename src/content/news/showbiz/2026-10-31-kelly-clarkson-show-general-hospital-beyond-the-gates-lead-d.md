The 53rd Daytime Emmys took place, with "The Kelly Clarkson Show" and "General Hospital" among the top winners. "The Kelly Clarkson Show" won five awards, while "General Hospital" secured the top daytime drama prize for the sixth consecutive year.

## Key facts
- "The Kelly Clarkson Show" won five awards at the 53rd Daytime Emmys.
- "The Kelly Clarkson Show" received the top daytime talk prize.
- "General Hospital" won the top daytime drama prize for the sixth consecutive year.
- "General Hospital" tied with another program, winning four overall awards.
