## Sean Penn’s CORE Launches $5 Million Campaign to Support Venezuela

In a significant humanitarian effort, actor and activist Sean Penn's organization, Community Organized Relief Effort (CORE), has announced a $5 million campaign aimed at aiding the people of Venezuela. This initiative, supported by the FIFA Foundation, comes in response to a series of crises that have severely impacted the nation.

### Context of the Campaign

Venezuela has faced numerous challenges in recent years, including political instability, economic collapse, and natural disasters. The recent twin shocks, which include both an earthquake and ongoing humanitarian needs, have exacerbated the situation for millions of Venezuelans. CORE, founded in the wake of the devastating 2010 Haiti earthquake, has a long-standing commitment to disaster response and recovery, making this campaign a natural extension of its mission.

### CORE’s Commitment to Humanitarian Aid

Sean Penn, who co-founded CORE, has been an outspoken advocate for humanitarian efforts worldwide. The organization has previously engaged in various relief efforts, including those in Haiti and the United States during natural disasters. The $5 million allocated for Venezuela will be directed towards providing essential services, including food, medical care, and shelter for those affected by the recent crises.

“Earthquake response and recovery have been part of CORE's DNA since our founding,” Penn stated in a recent interview. “We are committed to helping communities rebuild and recover, and Venezuela needs our support now more than ever.”

### Collaboration with the FIFA Foundation

The involvement of the FIFA Foundation in this campaign marks a notable collaboration between entertainment and sports organizations to address humanitarian needs. The FIFA Foundation has a history of supporting initiatives that promote social change and improve the lives of vulnerable populations through sports and community engagement.

This partnership aims to leverage the global reach of both organizations to raise awareness and mobilize resources for the Venezuelan people. By combining CORE’s expertise in disaster relief with FIFA’s extensive network, the campaign hopes to make a substantial impact on the ground.

### The Road Ahead

As the campaign unfolds, CORE plans to work closely with local organizations and community leaders in Venezuela to ensure that aid is delivered effectively and reaches those most in need. The focus will be on sustainable recovery efforts that empower communities and foster resilience against future challenges.

The situation in Venezuela remains dire, and international support is crucial for the country’s recovery. Initiatives like this one highlight the importance of collaboration across sectors to address complex humanitarian crises.

### Conclusion

Sean Penn’s CORE, with the support of the FIFA Foundation, is taking a proactive stance in addressing the urgent needs of the Venezuelan population. As the campaign progresses, it is expected to draw attention to the ongoing struggles faced by many in the country, emphasizing the necessity for continued global humanitarian efforts. The commitment to aid and recovery reflects a broader understanding that lasting change requires concerted action from diverse stakeholders.
