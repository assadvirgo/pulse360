Donnie Iris, a prominent figure in Pittsburgh's music scene, has died at the age of 83. He was known for his six-decade career, which included hit singles in the 1960s and 1980s, and had been battling bladder cancer for three years.

## Key facts
- Donnie Iris was regarded as Pittsburgh's biggest hometown rock hero.
- He achieved significant chart success as a member of the Jaggerz, who reached No. 2 on the Billboard Hot 100.
- Iris passed away on a Monday.
