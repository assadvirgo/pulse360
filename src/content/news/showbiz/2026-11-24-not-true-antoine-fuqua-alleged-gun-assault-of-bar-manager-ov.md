Antoine Fuqua has been accused of assault and battery by a bar manager in Los Angeles, according to a civil claim filed in the Los Angeles Superior Court. The allegations include claims that Fuqua pulled a gun on the employee and struck him in the head during an incident in February.

## Key facts
- A Hollywood assistant bar manager filed an assault and battery complaint against Antoine Fuqua.
- Fuqua's representative stated that the claims made in the filing are "not true."
- The civil claim includes accusations of intentional infliction of emotional distress.
- The incident reportedly occurred in February.
