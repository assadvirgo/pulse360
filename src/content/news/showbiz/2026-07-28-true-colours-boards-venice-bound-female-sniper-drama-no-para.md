## True Colours Acquires Venice-Bound Female Sniper Drama

True Colours, a Rome-based sales company, has announced its acquisition of the highly anticipated drama "No Paradise If You Are Killed by a Woman." Directed by the Norwegian-Kurdish filmmaker Halkawt Mustafa, the film is set to make its world premiere Out of Competition at the prestigious Venice Film Festival this September.

### Film Overview

The narrative centers around a female Peshmerga sniper, portrayed by actress Avan Jamal, who embarks on a perilous journey to Mosul with the mission of rescuing her younger sister. This gripping storyline not only highlights the complexities of war but also sheds light on the role of women in conflict zones, particularly in the context of the Kurdish struggle against ISIS.

### Director's Vision

Halkawt Mustafa, known for his poignant storytelling and unique perspective on Kurdish culture, aims to bring to the forefront the often-overlooked narratives of women in combat. His previous works have garnered critical acclaim, and "No Paradise If You Are Killed by a Woman" is expected to continue this trend, offering a fresh take on gender roles within the framework of war.

### Venice Film Festival Spotlight

The Venice Film Festival, one of the oldest and most prestigious film festivals in the world, serves as a significant platform for filmmakers to showcase their work to an international audience. By selecting "No Paradise If You Are Killed by a Woman" for its Out of Competition section, the festival acknowledges the film's artistic merit and its relevance in contemporary discussions surrounding gender and conflict.

### Industry Implications

True Colours' involvement in the film indicates a growing interest in stories that challenge traditional narratives and highlight diverse perspectives. The acquisition aligns with a broader trend in the film industry that seeks to amplify voices from underrepresented communities, particularly those of women in war-torn regions.

As the film approaches its premiere, anticipation is building among audiences and critics alike. The combination of a compelling storyline, a talented cast, and a director with a proven track record suggests that "No Paradise If You Are Killed by a Woman" will resonate with viewers and spark important conversations about the role of women in both society and warfare.

### Conclusion

With its world premiere at the Venice Film Festival, "No Paradise If You Are Killed by a Woman" is poised to make a significant impact in the film industry. True Colours' acquisition of this powerful drama reflects a commitment to promoting diverse narratives and supporting filmmakers who challenge the status quo. As the festival approaches, the film is expected to draw attention not only for its artistic qualities but also for its timely exploration of gender and conflict.
