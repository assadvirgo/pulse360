A meeting at TIFFCOM in 2023 resulted in Italian co-producers joining the animated feature "The Violinist," which won the top prize at the Annecy International Animation Film Festival this year. Director Ervin Han, co-founder and CEO of Singapore’s Robot Playground Media, shared this information during a panel at the Tokyo market.

## Key facts
- "The Violinist" is an animated feature that won the top prize at the Annecy International Animation Film Festival.
- The meeting at TIFFCOM in 2023 led to the involvement of Italian co-producers.
- Director Ervin Han is the co-founder and CEO of Singapore’s Robot Playground Media.
