Disney reported a 16% increase in quarterly revenue and a doubling of profit, surpassing Wall Street expectations. The company is forecasting $3 billion in annual cost savings by 2029, which may include layoffs.

## Key facts
- Disney's revenue rose by 16% in the latest quarter.
- The company's profit doubled during the same period.
- CEO Josh D'Amaro sees opportunities for increased efficiency.
- Disney aims for $3 billion in annual cost savings by 2029.
