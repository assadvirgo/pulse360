Warner Bros. International TV Production will now operate under Skydance TV Studios. The division, which manages around 20 production companies globally, produces international versions of formats like The Bachelor.

## Key facts
- Warner Bros. International TV Production has a new home within Skydance TV Studios.
- The division operates around 20 production companies worldwide.
- It produces international versions of formats such as The Bachelor.
- Ronald Goes is the President of the unit.
- The unit will be overseen by Warner Bros. Television.
