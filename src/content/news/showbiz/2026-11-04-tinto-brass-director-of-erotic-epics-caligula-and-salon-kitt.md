Italian director Tinto Brass, known for his erotic films including "Caligula" and "Salon Kitty," has died at the age of 93. His family announced his death to the Italian press agency Ansa.

## Key facts
- Tinto Brass was born in Milan in 1933 to a Venetian family of artists.
- He initially studied law before shifting his focus to film.
- Brass's death was announced by his wife Caterina Varzi and children Bonifacio and Beatrice Brass.
- He passed away in Rome.
