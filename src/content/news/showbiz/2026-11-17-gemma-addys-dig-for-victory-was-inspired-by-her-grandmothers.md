Gemma Addy's project "Dig For Victory" has been recognized in The Brit List 2026, which highlights emerging writers and their unproduced UK film and TV projects. The project was inspired by her grandmothers' experiences during the war.

## Key facts
- Deadline has partnered with The Brit List to profile emerging writers.
- The Brit List has been recognizing top unproduced UK film and TV projects since 2007.
- Previous projects featured on The Brit List include "The King’s Speech" and "Responsible Child."
- Gemma Addy has topped this year's Brit List with her project "Dig For Victory."
