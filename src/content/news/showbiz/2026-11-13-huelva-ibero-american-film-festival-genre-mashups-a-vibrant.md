The 52nd Huelva Ibero-American Film Festival will commence on November 13, honoring actress Paulina García. The festival will showcase works from emerging Latin American directors.

## Key facts
- The festival is set to highlight the latest films from fast-emerging Latin American directors.
- Paulina García, known for her role in "Gloria," will be honored at the event.
- The festival is described as a film event for contemporary times.
