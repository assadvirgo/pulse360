Douglas Mackinnon, known for directing "Line of Duty" and "Good Omens," is preparing a new paramedic drama titled "BurnTime," set in Edinburgh. This project is part of the expanding slate at his production company, Rugged Island Films. Mackinnon will present the series at the Drama Co-Production Forum during the MIA Market in Rome, which takes place from October 19 to 23, according to Variety.

## Key facts
- Douglas Mackinnon is directing a new paramedic drama called "BurnTime."
- The series is set in Edinburgh.
- "BurnTime" is part of the growing slate at Mackinnon's production outfit, Rugged Island Films.
- Mackinnon will present the series at the Drama Co-Production Forum at the MIA Market in Rome.
- The MIA Market runs from October 19 to 23.
