Paramount Pictures' upcoming "Call of Duty" movie will focus on the realities of war and the experience of being a warrior, according to director Peter Berg. He emphasized that the film will not portray soldiers as superheroes or resemble a comic book.

## Key facts
- The "Call of Duty" movie is based on a popular video game franchise.
- Director Peter Berg stated that the film will not feel like a comic book tentpole.
- Berg is known for directing "Friday Night Lights" and "Lone Survivor."
