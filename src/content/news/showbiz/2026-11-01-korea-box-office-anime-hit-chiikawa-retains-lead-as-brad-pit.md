Japanese animated film "Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island" maintained its position at the top of the South Korean box office for the weekend of October 30 to November 1. This marks the film's third consecutive weekend in the number one spot.

## Key facts
- "Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island" is a Japanese animated film.
- The film is distributed by Toho.
- It has held the No. 1 position at the South Korean box office for three consecutive weekends.
- The data is sourced from KOBIS, operated by the Korean Film Council.
