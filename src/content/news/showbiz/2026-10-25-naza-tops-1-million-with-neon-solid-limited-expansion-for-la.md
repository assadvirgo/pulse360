The documentary "NAZA," recently acquired by Neon, has surpassed the $1 million mark at the box office. The film, which premiered at Venice, is projected to earn $74.5k this weekend across 73 screens in its fourth week of release.

## Key facts
- "NAZA" has passed the $1 million mark in box office earnings.
- The film is directed by Yuval Abraham and Rachel Szor.
- It premiered at the Venice Film Festival.
- The film is projected to earn $74.5k this weekend on 73 screens.
- The cumulative earnings for "NAZA" stand at $1.07 million.
