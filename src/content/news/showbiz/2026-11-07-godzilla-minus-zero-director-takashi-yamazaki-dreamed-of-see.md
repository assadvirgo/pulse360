Takashi Yamazaki, the writer, director, and VFX supervisor of "Godzilla Minus One," has expressed his long-held dream of seeing his hometown destroyed by the monster King Ghidorah. He has now made that vision a reality in his latest project.

## Key facts
- "Godzilla Minus One" won the Oscar for visual effects over two years ago.
- Takashi Yamazaki is the first director to receive a VFX trophy since Stanley Kubrick for "2001: A Space Odyssey." 
- Yamazaki spoke about his experiences during a reflection on the historic night of the award ceremony.
