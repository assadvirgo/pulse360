Jane Tranter, producer of "Industry" and "Doctor Who," spoke at Rome's MIA Market about her approach to production. She emphasized the importance of resisting industry trends and focusing on stories that center women and themes of acceptance.

## Key facts
- Jane Tranter has built her career by going against prevailing industry trends.
- At the MIA Market, she advised European producers to "go in the opposite direction of everyone else’s traffic."
- Tranter highlighted the significance of telling stories about people being accepted for who they choose to be.
