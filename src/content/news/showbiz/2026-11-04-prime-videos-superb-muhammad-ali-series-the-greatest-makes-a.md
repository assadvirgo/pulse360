A new series titled "The Greatest" on Prime Video chronicles the life of Muhammad Ali, highlighting his status as a historical and mythical figure. The series features newcomer Jaalen Best in a leading role.

## Key facts
- "The Greatest" focuses on Muhammad Ali's life as an athlete, man, and activist.
- The series is part of a long history of films chronicling Ali's life, including the 2001 film "Ali" and Regina King's 2020 film "One Night in Miami."
