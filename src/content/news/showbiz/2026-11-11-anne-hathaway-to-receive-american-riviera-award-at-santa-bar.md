Anne Hathaway will receive the American Riviera Award at the 42nd annual Santa Barbara International Film Festival on February 5. The award honors actors who have made significant contributions to American cinema.

## Key facts
- Anne Hathaway will be recognized for her performance as Penelope in Christopher Nolan’s “The Odyssey.”
- The award ceremony will include an in-person tribute and conversation.
- The event is scheduled for February 5 at the Santa Barbara International Film Festival.
