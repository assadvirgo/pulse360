Kevin Kline will star alongside Meryl Streep in Netflix's limited series adaptation of "The Corrections." Kline will portray Alfred Lambert, the husband of Streep's character, Enid Lambert.

## Key facts
- Kevin Kline is set to star in Netflix's adaptation of "The Corrections."
- Meryl Streep was previously announced as part of the cast.
- Kline's character, Alfred Lambert, is described as a once-powerful patriarch facing the challenges of aging.
- The series is based on the novel by Jonathan Franzen and was originally announced with Streep attached in April.
