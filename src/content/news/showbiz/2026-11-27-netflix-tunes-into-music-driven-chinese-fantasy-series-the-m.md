Netflix has acquired the Chinese fantasy drama series The Melody Of Love, which will be available in the U.S. and internationally on November 29. The series is produced by Stellar Pictures, known for other fantasy series such as Love Between Fairy and Devil and Love Game in Eastern Fantasy.

## Key facts
- Netflix is launching The Melody Of Love on November 29.
- The series is a music-driven Chinese fantasy drama.
- It is produced by Stellar Pictures, which has created other fantasy series.
