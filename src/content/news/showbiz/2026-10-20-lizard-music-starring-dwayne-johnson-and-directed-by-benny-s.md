Amazon MGM Studios has announced the theatrical release date for the film "Lizard Music," which stars Dwayne Johnson and is directed by Benny Safdie. The movie is set to premiere on December 25, 2027.

## Key facts
- "Lizard Music" is a live-action/CGI hybrid film.
- The story follows a boy who discovers a secret late-night broadcast of lizards playing music.
- Benny Safdie wrote the script, adapting it from Daniel Pinkwater's novel.
