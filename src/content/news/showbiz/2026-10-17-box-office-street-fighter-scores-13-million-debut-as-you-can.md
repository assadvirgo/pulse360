"Street Fighter" debuted at the top of the box office, earning $13 million on its opening day. It faced competition from other new releases, including "You Can See Everything," which opened in second place.

## Key facts
- "Street Fighter" generated $13 million from 3,575 North American theaters on its opening day.
- "You Can See Everything" opened at No. 2 with $5.5 million.
- Other new releases included "Sense and Sensibility" and "Whalefall."
