The Japan IP Market will return to Cannes in 2027, continuing its partnership with the Cannes Film Market and TIFFCOM. The event is scheduled to take place on May 14-15.

## Key facts
- The Japan IP Market will be held in Cannes for a second consecutive year.
- The event is a collaboration between the Cannes Film Market and TIFFCOM, the content market of the Tokyo International Film Festival.
- The announcement was made at TIFFCOM in Tokyo.
