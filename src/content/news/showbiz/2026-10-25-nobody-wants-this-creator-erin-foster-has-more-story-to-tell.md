Erin Foster, the creator of the series "Nobody Wants This," has expressed her readiness to produce a fourth season if Netflix decides to renew the show. She indicated that she has "more story to tell" but is cautious about becoming too attached to the project.

## Key facts
- Erin Foster is the creator of the series "Nobody Wants This."
- She confirmed she has more story to tell for a potential fourth season.
- The series is a Jewish romantic-comedy.
- Foster noted she tries not to get too attached to the show.
