Netflix's series "Virgin River" has been renewed for a ninth season ahead of its eighth season premiere, making it the longest-running scripted series on the platform. The upcoming season will consist of 10 episodes.

## Key facts
- "Virgin River" has been renewed for a ninth season by Netflix.
- The renewal comes months before the premiere of Season 8, which is set for 2027.
- The show stars Alexandra Breckenridge and Martin Henderson.
- Season 9 will consist of 10 episodes.
