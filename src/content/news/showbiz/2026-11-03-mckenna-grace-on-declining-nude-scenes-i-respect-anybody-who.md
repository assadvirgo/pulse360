Mckenna Grace has discussed her decision to decline nude scenes in film, citing a powerful reason for her choice. In a recent interview, she expressed her respect for those who are comfortable with nudity but described the idea as terrifying for herself.

- Mckenna Grace is the star of "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping."
- She has openly refused to participate in nude scenes in her acting roles.
