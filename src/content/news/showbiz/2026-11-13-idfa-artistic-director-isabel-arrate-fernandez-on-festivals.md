The 39th edition of the International Documentary Film Festival Amsterdam (IDFA) is currently taking place, featuring top talents in the documentary field. The festival is noted for attracting Oscar seekers and emerging filmmakers. 

## Key facts
- IDFA is recognized as the world’s biggest nonfiction film festival.
- The festival is taking place in Amsterdam.
- Isabel Arrate Fernandez is the artistic director of IDFA. 
- The festival includes competition lineups and showcases dynamic work.
