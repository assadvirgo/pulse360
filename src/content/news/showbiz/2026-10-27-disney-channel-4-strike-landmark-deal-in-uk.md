Disney and Channel 4 have reached a landmark content deal in the UK, marking a significant development in the television landscape. This agreement will allow the Channel 4 linear network to be available on Disney+, a first for the UK.

## Key facts
- Disney and Channel 4 have struck a landmark UK content deal.
- The Channel 4 linear network will become available on Disney+.
- This deal is described as one of the biggest of its kind in UK TV history.
- Shows included in the deal are Celebrity SAS: Who Dares Wins and the upcoming series starring Bella Ramsey.
