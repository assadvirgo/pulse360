## Lollapalooza Adjusts Schedule Amid Severe Weather Conditions

The third day of the Lollapalooza music festival in Chicago has faced significant disruptions due to intense flooding in the area. Originally set to commence at 12 p.m. Central Time, the festival's schedule has been postponed to 3 p.m., impacting numerous performances planned for the day.

### Cancellations and Adjustments

As a result of the weather conditions, many of the acts scheduled to perform within the first three hours of the festival have had their sets canceled. This decision was made to ensure the safety of both the attendees and the artists, as the heavy rainfall has created challenging conditions at the venue.

However, not all performances have been entirely scrapped. The K-pop group Cortis is among the few acts that will still take the stage today, albeit with a shortened performance time. Their set has been adjusted from 50 minutes to 40 minutes, reflecting the overall changes in the festival schedule.

### Safety First

Festival organizers have prioritized safety in light of the flooding. Attendees have been advised to remain vigilant and follow any instructions provided by festival staff. The decision to delay the festival's start was made to allow for a thorough assessment of the venue and to ensure that all safety protocols are in place before the music resumes.

### Impact on Festival Experience

Lollapalooza, known for its diverse lineup and vibrant atmosphere, is a major event in the summer festival circuit. The unexpected weather disruptions have raised concerns among fans and artists alike about the overall experience. Many attendees have expressed disappointment over the cancellations but have also shown understanding of the necessity for safety measures.

As the festival continues, organizers are working diligently to adapt to the evolving situation. They are committed to providing a memorable experience while prioritizing the health and safety of everyone involved.

### Looking Ahead

With the festival now set to resume at 3 p.m., festival-goers are eagerly anticipating the performances that will take place later in the day. While the changes to the schedule may alter the lineup for some, the spirit of Lollapalooza remains strong as fans look forward to enjoying the remaining acts.

As the situation develops, updates will be provided to keep attendees informed about any further changes to the schedule or safety protocols. The resilience of the festival community is being tested, but the commitment to music and celebration continues amidst the challenges posed by the weather.
