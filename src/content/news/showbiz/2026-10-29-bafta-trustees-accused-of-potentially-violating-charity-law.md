BAFTA trustees are facing allegations of potentially violating U.K. charity and company laws by accepting complimentary tickets to the awards show. These tickets were reportedly not disclosed in the organization's financial accounts.

## Key facts
- Allegations were raised at BAFTA's annual general meeting.
- The chair of BAFTA's membership council stated that legal advice suggested trustees who approved the tickets may have violated laws.
