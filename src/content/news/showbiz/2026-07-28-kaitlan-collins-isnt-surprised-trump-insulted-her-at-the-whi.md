## Kaitlan Collins Responds to Trump's Remarks at White House Correspondents’ Dinner

In a recent appearance on "The Daily Show" with Jon Stewart, CNN anchor Kaitlan Collins addressed the remarks made by former President Donald Trump during the White House Correspondents' Dinner. Collins, who has often been at the forefront of political reporting, expressed that she was not surprised by Trump's insults, indicating that such behavior is typical in the realm of political discourse.

## Context of the Remarks

During the White House Correspondents' Dinner, which serves as an annual gathering for journalists and public figures, Trump directed several jabs at Collins, who has covered him extensively throughout his presidency. The event, known for its blend of humor and serious commentary, often sees politicians and media figures exchanging light-hearted banter. However, Trump's comments took a more personal tone, which has become a hallmark of his interactions with the press.

## Collins' Perspective

In her discussion with Stewart, Collins emphasized that Trump's approach to the media is not unexpected. Collins' remarks highlight the ongoing tension between political figures and the press, particularly in an era where media scrutiny is more intense than ever.
