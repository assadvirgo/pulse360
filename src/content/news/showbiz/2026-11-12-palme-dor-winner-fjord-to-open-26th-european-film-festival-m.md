Cristian Mungiu's Palme d'Or winner "Fjord" will open the 26th European Film Festival Malaysia (EUFF) on November 26 at TGV Pavilion Kuala Lumpur. This year, the festival will also stream selected titles for home audiences through Malaysian media and entertainment group Astro.

## Key facts
- "Fjord" is a Palme d'Or winner directed by Cristian Mungiu.
- The 26th European Film Festival Malaysia will take place from November 26 to December 13.
- The festival will feature selected films available for streaming through Astro for the first time.
