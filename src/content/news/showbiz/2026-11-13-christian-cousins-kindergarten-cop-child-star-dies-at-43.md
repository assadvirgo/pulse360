Christian Cousins, known for his role in "Kindergarten Cop," has died at the age of 43 after battling liver disease. His brother, Jonas Roeser, announced the news on Instagram on October 22.

## Key facts
- Christian Cousins was a child star who appeared in "Kindergarten Cop" alongside his twin brother Joey.
- He died after battling liver disease.
- The announcement of his passing was made by his brother, Jonas Roeser, on Instagram.
