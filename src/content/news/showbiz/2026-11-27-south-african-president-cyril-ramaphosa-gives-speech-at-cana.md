South African President Cyril Ramaphosa attended the launch of Canal+ Academy in Johannesburg, a new initiative by Canal+ Group aimed at expanding training and professional development opportunities for local professionals in the creative industries.

- The launch took place at MultiChoice City in Johannesburg.
- Canal+ Academy is a pan-African initiative.
- The program is designed for aspiring professionals in the creative industries.
