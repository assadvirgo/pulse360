Shane Gillis addressed the backlash from his "Saturday Night Live" monologue, which included controversial jokes about women's gym attire. He expressed surprise at the criticism and stated that he had promised the show there would be "no controversy."

## Key facts
- Gillis discussed the backlash on a podcast, noting that "SNL" does not approve the content of monologues.
- He mentioned that while "SNL" could shut down a monologue, comedians typically do not submit their sets for approval.
- His monologue included jokes about how women "dress like whores now" at the gym, which led to significant criticism on social media.
