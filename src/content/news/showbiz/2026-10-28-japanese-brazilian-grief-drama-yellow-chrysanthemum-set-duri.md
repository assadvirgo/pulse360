A Japanese-Brazilian film titled "Yellow Chrysanthemum" is participating in this year's Tokyo Gap-Financing Market. The film, directed by André Hayato Saito, centers on a teenager dealing with a family loss that remains unspoken.

## Key facts
- "Yellow Chrysanthemum" is the first feature from director André Hayato Saito.
- The film is set in São Paulo during July 2014, coinciding with the World Cup in Brazil.
- The story focuses on a Japanese-Brazilian teenager facing a family loss.
