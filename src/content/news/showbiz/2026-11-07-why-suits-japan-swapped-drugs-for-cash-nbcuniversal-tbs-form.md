In the Japanese version of "Suits," the original plot element involving drugs was altered to feature money instead. This change was made because drugs cannot be depicted on Japanese television, according to Linfield Ng, VP of format sales and production for Asia.

## Key facts
- The pilot of "Suits" originally features a briefcase full of marijuana.
- The Korean and Mongolian remakes retained the drug element.
- In Japan, the drugs were replaced with money for broadcast compliance.
