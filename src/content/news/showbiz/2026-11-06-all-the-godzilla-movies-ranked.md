He’s been dissolved, frozen, blown up, disintegrated, and killed, but Godzilla continues to endure thanks to his millions of fans. The character is set to return in "Godzilla Minus Zero," according to Variety. 

## Key facts
- Godzilla has faced various forms of demise, including being dissolved at the bottom of the ocean and frozen in an iceberg.
- The character has also been blown up in a volcano and disintegrated in an atomic meltdown.
- Godzilla has been killed by missiles on the Brooklyn Bridge.
- The upcoming film is titled "Godzilla Minus Zero."
