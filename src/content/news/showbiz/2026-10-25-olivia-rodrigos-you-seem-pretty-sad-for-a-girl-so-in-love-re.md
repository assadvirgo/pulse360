Olivia Rodrigo's album "You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love" has returned to the top of the Billboard 200 chart following the release of her "Expectations" remix with Chappell Roan. This marks Rodrigo's third total week at No. 1 on the chart.

## Key facts
- Olivia Rodrigo's album is titled "You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love."
- The album returned to No. 1 on the Billboard 200 chart.
- The return to the top follows the release of the "Expectations" remix with Chappell Roan.
- Rodrigo initially released the album in June.
