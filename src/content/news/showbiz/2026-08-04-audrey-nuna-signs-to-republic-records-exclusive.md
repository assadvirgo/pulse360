In a significant career development, rising star Audrey Nuna has officially signed with Republic Records. This announcement comes just ahead of the release of her highly anticipated new single, "superHuman," which is set to debut at midnight ET this Friday.

### A New Chapter for Audrey Nuna

Audrey Nuna, who has recently gained recognition as the voice of Mira in the animated series "KPop Demon Hunters," is poised to make waves in the music industry with her upcoming release. The signing with Republic Records marks a pivotal moment in her career, as the label is known for its roster of successful artists and its commitment to fostering new talent.

### Collaboration with Grammy-Winning Producer

For "superHuman," Nuna collaborated with the esteemed Grammy-winning producer Cirkut, known for his work with high-profile artists across various genres. This partnership is expected to bring a fresh and innovative sound to Nuna's music, further establishing her as a formidable presence in the contemporary music scene.
