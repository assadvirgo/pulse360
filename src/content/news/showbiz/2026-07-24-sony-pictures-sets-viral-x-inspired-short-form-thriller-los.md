## Sony Pictures Launches Short-Form Thriller 'Los Hilos del Miedo' on Prime Video Spain

This eight-part psychological suspense series is set to premiere on Prime Video in Spain, marking a significant move into the realm of short-form fiction for the company.

### A New Direction for Sony Pictures

"Los Hilos del Miedo" represents Sony Pictures’ first foray into short-form series production, reflecting a growing trend in the entertainment industry where audiences increasingly favor bite-sized content. Each episode of the series will run for approximately five minutes, making it accessible for viewers seeking quick yet engaging narratives.

The series is inspired by the works of Spanish writer Salvador Gutiérrez Solís, known for his ability to weave intricate and suspenseful tales. This adaptation aims to capture the essence of his storytelling while delivering a fresh and thrilling experience tailored for the digital streaming audience.
