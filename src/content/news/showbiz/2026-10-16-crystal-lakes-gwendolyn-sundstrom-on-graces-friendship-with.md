Gwendolyn Sundstrom plays a new character named Grace in the series "Crystal Lake," which is available for streaming on Peacock. The show revisits familiar locations from the "Friday the 13th" franchise. Sundstrom mentioned that she is learning a lot from her co-star Linda Cardellini.

## Key facts
- Gwendolyn Sundstrom portrays Grace, a childhood friend of Jason Voorhees in "Crystal Lake."
- The series is a prequel to the "Friday the 13th" franchise.
- "Crystal Lake" is currently available to stream on Peacock.
- Sundstrom noted her experience working with Linda Cardellini.
