Filmmaker James Gray shared how a piece of advice from Francis Ford Coppola influenced his career. Gray wrote a fan letter to Coppola as a teenager, and the legendary director responded with guidance that shaped Gray's approach to cinema.

## Key facts
- James Gray wrote a fan letter to Francis Ford Coppola when he was a teenager.
- Coppola replied with advice that Gray found impactful: “Be personal, James. There’s only one of you in this world.”
- This advice has shaped Gray's entire approach to cinema, according to his recollection.
