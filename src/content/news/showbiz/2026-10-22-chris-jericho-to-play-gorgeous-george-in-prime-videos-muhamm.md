Chris Jericho will portray Gorgeous George in the upcoming Prime Video limited series "The Greatest," which chronicles the life of Muhammad Ali. The series, created by Ben Watkins, will release all eight episodes on November 4.

## Key facts
- Chris Jericho is set to play Gorgeous George, a legendary wrestling pioneer.
- "The Greatest" is a historical drama focused on Muhammad Ali's life.
- The series will consist of eight episodes.
- All episodes will be available for streaming on November 4.
