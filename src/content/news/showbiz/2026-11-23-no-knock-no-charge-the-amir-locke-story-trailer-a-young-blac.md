A trailer for the documentary "No Knock – No Charge? The Amir Locke Story" has been released, focusing on the police shooting of Amir Locke in Minneapolis. The incident occurred during a no-knock warrant execution, resulting in Locke's death.

## Key facts
- The police SWAT team entered an apartment on a no-knock warrant on February 2, 2022.
- Amir Locke, a 22-year-old Black man, was shot by an officer within 10 seconds of the entry.
- The documentary highlights the family's fight for justice following Locke's death.
