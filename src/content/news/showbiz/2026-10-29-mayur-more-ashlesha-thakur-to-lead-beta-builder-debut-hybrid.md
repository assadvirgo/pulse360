Mayur More and Ashlesha Thakur will star in the first AI-driven feature film titled "Beta Builder," produced through a partnership between Abundantia Entertainment and InVideo. Principal photography for the film began today in Mumbai.

## Key facts
- "Beta Builder" is the debut hybrid AI feature film from Abundantia Entertainment and InVideo.
- Hridaye A. Nagpal is attached to direct the film.
- Principal photography for the film started today in Mumbai.
