Jack Lowden has chosen to remain discreet regarding rumors about him potentially becoming the next James Bond. The actor, known for his role in "Slow Horses," acknowledged the speculation but stated he would not contribute further to the discussion.

## Key facts
- Jack Lowden is rumored to be among the frontrunners to replace Daniel Craig as James Bond.
- He described the speculation as "very flattering."
- Lowden stated he is "not going to add to the noise" regarding the casting rumors.
- He is set to play Mr. Darcy in Netflix's adaptation of "Pride and Prejudice."
