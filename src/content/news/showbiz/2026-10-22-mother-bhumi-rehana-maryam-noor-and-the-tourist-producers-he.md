Producers of the films "Mother Bhumi," "Rehana Maryam Noor," and the drama "The Tourist" are participating in the inaugural Producers Exchange at Indonesia's JAFF Market. This event is set to take place from November 28 to 30 in Yogyakarta, coinciding with the Jogja-Netpac Asian Film Festival.

## Key facts
- The Producers Exchange is debuting at Indonesia's JAFF Market.
- The event will occur from November 28 to 30 in Yogyakarta.
- It is held alongside the Jogja-Netpac Asian Film Festival (JAFF).
- Fourteen producers have been selected for the exchange program.
