Copenhagen-based DR Sales has acquired the international distribution rights for the Danish film "Father’s House," which features actors David Dencik and Trine Dyrholm. The film has been selected for the Tallinn Black Nights Film Festival.

## Key facts
- DR Sales is the international distributor of the Oscar-winning documentary "Mr Nobody Against Putin."
- The company also distributed the Sundance winner "A New Kind of Wilderness."
- "Father’s House" stars David Dencik and Trine Dyrholm.
- The film has been selected for the Tallinn Black Nights Film Festival.
