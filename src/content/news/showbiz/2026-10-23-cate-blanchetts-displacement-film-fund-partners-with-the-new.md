Cate Blanchett's Displacement Film Fund has partnered with The New Yorker to release five short films. The films will explore themes of displacement, identity, belonging, and exile.

## Key facts
- The partnership will feature films by Mohammad Rasoulof, Shahrbanoo Sadat, Mo Harawe, Maryna Er Gorbach, and Hasan Kattan.
- The films focus on stories related to displacement.
- The films had their world premieres at the 2026 event.
