Chris Harrison has commented on the legal action taken by Warner Bros. against his new reality show, The Vow. He described the cease-and-desist issued by Warner Bros. as feeling "personal."

## Key facts
- Warner Bros. issued a cease-and-desist to The Vow earlier this month.
- The letter from Warner Bros. accuses the show of copyright and trademark infringement.
- Chris Harrison is the former host of The Bachelor and has a new dating show premiering on Nov. 11 on Fox Nation.
