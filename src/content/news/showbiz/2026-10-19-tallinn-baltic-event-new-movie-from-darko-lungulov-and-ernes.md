Filmmakers Darko Lungulov and Ernestas Jankauskas are set to pitch their works in progress at Tallinn's Baltic Event industry showcase. The event will take place on November 19 and will feature a mix of seasoned filmmakers and emerging talents.

## Key facts
- Darko Lungulov is known for Serbia’s Oscar candidate "Here and There."
- Ernestas Jankauskas has directed films including "Troll Farm," "I Am Fine, Thanks," and "Sasha Was Here."
- The showcase will include a variety of new voices in the film industry.
