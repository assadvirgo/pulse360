Nikhil Mahajan, an Indian National Film Award-winning director, is presenting his new project "Mongoose" at the Co-Production Market at WAVES Film Bazaar. The film is a Marathi-language neo-noir thriller that revolves around a cab driver and a con man who steals his taxi, set in the town of Malegaon.

## Key facts
- "Mongoose" is directed by Nikhil Mahajan, known for "Godavari" and the Netflix series "Betaal."
- The film is a neo-noir thriller in the Marathi language.
- The story follows a cab driver named Abhimanyu and a con man who steals his taxi.
- The film is set in the town of Malegaon.
- "Mongoose" is being showcased at the WAVES Film Bazaar.
