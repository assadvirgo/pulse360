TBS Holdings executives discussed their collaborations with Legendary Entertainment and Korea’s CJ ENM during a presentation at TIFFCOM. The Japanese broadcaster aims to create a new global genre of J-content through these partnerships.

## Key facts
- TBS acquired a $150 million minority stake in Legendary Entertainment in January 2026.
- TBS entered into a non-exclusive agreement with Legendary to source and develop Japanese intellectual property.
- The discussions took place during a presentation at TIFFCOM.
