Ronny Hallin, sister of Garry and Penny Marshall, has died at the age of 88. According to her daughter, she passed away peacefully at home.

## Key facts
- Ronny Hallin produced and appeared in the sitcom "Laverne & Shirley."
- She was born on January 2, 1938, in New York City.
- Hallin's death was reported by her daughter, Penny Hallin.
