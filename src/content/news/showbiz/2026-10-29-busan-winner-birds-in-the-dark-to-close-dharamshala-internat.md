Shashank Walia’s film “Birds in the Dark” will close the 15th Dharamshala International Film Festival with its India premiere. The festival is scheduled to take place from October 29 to November 1 at the Tibetan Children’s Village in Upper Dharamshala.

## Key facts
- “Birds in the Dark” is about a call center executive mistaken for a suspect by a police informer.
- The film won two prizes at the Busan International Film Festival.
- The Dharamshala International Film Festival is in its 15th year.
