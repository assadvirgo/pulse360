Chase Sui Wonders is in talks to join A24's upcoming film "Goblin," directed by David Mikalson, which is set to begin production next year. The character details for Wonders' role have not been disclosed.

- Chase Sui Wonders is in discussions to join the cast of "Goblin."
- The film is produced by A24 and directed by David Mikalson.
- Production for "Goblin" is scheduled to start next year.
- The cast includes Kenneth Branagh, who will voice the titular Goblin, along with Skyler Gisondo and Will Forte.
