The creators of "Yellowjackets," Ashley Lyle and Bart Nickerson, announced that the show will conclude with its fourth season, despite initially planning for five seasons. They expressed that it was the right time to end the series to avoid overstaying their welcome.

## Key facts
- "Yellowjackets" will end with its fourth season.
- The creators initially planned for a five-season run.
- Ashley Lyle and Bart Nickerson stated they did not want to risk overstaying their welcome.
