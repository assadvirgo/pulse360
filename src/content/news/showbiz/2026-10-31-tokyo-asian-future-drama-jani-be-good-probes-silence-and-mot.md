Indonesian filmmaker Ariani Darmawan has returned to directing with her first feature film, "Jani Be Good," which is currently being showcased in the Asian Future competition at the Tokyo International Film Festival. The film explores themes of silence and motherhood within a Chinese-Indonesian family, reflecting Darmawan's personal experiences during her time away from filmmaking.

## Key facts
- "Jani Be Good" is Ariani Darmawan's first feature film after a hiatus of over 10 years.
- The film portrays the lives of four women across three generations.
- It is part of the Asian Future competition at the Tokyo International Film Festival.
