Rachel Feldman's film "Lilly," featuring Patricia Clarkson, is set to open the third edition of the Women and the World International Film Festival in London. The festival will take place from November 5 to November 12 and emphasizes women's authorship in its programming.

## Key facts
- "Lilly" is directed by Rachel Feldman and stars Patricia Clarkson.
- The Women and the World International Film Festival will run from November 5-12 in London.
- The festival will feature the world premiere of "Laya" by Sigrid Andrea P. Bernardo.
- Other premieres include "The Nomad's" by Marina Kunarova.
