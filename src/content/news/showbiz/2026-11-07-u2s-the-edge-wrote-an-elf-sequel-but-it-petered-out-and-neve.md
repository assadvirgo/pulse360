U2's The Edge wrote a script for a sequel to the film "Elf," but it is unlikely to be produced. Andy Richter shared this information during an episode of "Obsessed: The Podcast."

## Key facts
- Andy Richter stated that The Edge penned a script for a sequel to "Elf."
- Richter mentioned that the sequel "petered out and never happened."
- Shauna Robertson, the producer of the original film, received a message regarding the script.
