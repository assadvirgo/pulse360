Quentin Tarantino has confirmed that he has written a script for his tenth feature film. However, he will not pursue it further until his newly opened play, "The Popinjay Cavalier," concludes.

## Key facts
- Tarantino has stated he will retire after his 10th feature film.
- He has completed the script for this final project.
- He will delay further work on the film until his play closes.
