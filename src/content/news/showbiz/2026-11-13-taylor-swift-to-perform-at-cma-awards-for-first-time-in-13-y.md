Taylor Swift will perform live at the 60th CMA Awards, marking her return to the stage for the first time since 2013. The Country Music Association announced her performance on Friday morning. Swift is nominated for Single of the Year with her song "I Knew It, I Knew You."

## Key facts
- Taylor Swift will perform at the CMA Awards for the first time in 13 years.
- The performance will take place at the 60th CMA Awards.
- Swift is nominated for Single of the Year for "I Knew It, I Knew You."
- The announcement was made by the Country Music Association on Friday morning.
