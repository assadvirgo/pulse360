Kevin Feige expressed that incorporating the X-Men into the Avengers franchise is a "fun way" to celebrate 25 years of Marvel's cinematic journey. He noted that it "just felt right" to bring the mutant superheroes into the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

## Key facts
- Kevin Feige is the president of Marvel Studios.
- He was an associate producer on the 2000 film X-Men.
- The film Avengers: Doomsday is set to be released next month.
