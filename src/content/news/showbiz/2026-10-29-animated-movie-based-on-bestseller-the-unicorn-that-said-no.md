The animated movie "The Unicorn That Said No" has secured distribution deals in multiple territories ahead of its premiere at the American Film Market in Los Angeles. The sales agency The Playmaker Munich facilitated these agreements.

## Key facts
- The movie has been sold to Unlimited Media for the Baltic states.
- WWE Rights has acquired distribution for Benelux.
- KMBO is set to distribute the film in France.
- Grand Entertainment is also involved in the distribution deals.
