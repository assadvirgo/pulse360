Paul McCartney expressed concerns regarding the casting of Paul Mescal in Sam Mendes' upcoming biopic about The Beatles. He noted that Mescal appears "markedly shorter" than his co-stars and reached out to Mendes about this issue.

- McCartney reached out to director Sam Mendes regarding his concerns.
- The context involves an upcoming biopic about The Beatles.
