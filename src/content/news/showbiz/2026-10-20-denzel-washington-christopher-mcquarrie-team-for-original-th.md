Amazon and United Artists have greenlit an untitled thriller featuring Denzel Washington, with Christopher McQuarrie set to direct. The film is scheduled for release in late 2027.

## Key facts
- The film is an original thriller.
- Denzel Washington will star in the film.
- Christopher McQuarrie is attached to direct.
- Scott Barkan and Gregg Zehentner wrote the script.
- The film is set to be released in theaters in late 2027.
