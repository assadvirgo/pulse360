Daniel Sullivan, a Tony-winning Broadway director, has announced his retirement after a 55-year career in theater. He was set to direct the upcoming production of David Hare's Montauk at the Manhattan Theatre Club.

## Key facts
- Daniel Sullivan directed acclaimed productions including Proof, The Heidi Chronicles, and Glengarry Glen Ross.
- His retirement announcement was unexpected.
- Sullivan was scheduled to direct Montauk starring Laura Linney in the spring.
