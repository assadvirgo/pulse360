An appeals court has ruled that "The Pitt" is similar enough to "ER" to permit a lawsuit from Michael Crichton's widow. The unanimous decision by a three-judge panel rejected an appeal from Warner Bros. Television, which contended that the two shows are unrelated.

## Key facts
- An appeals court ruled that "The Pitt" is similar enough to "ER" to allow a lawsuit.
- The decision was made by a unanimous three-judge panel.
- Warner Bros. Television had argued that the two shows are unrelated.
