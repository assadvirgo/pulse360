Keisha Lance Bottoms won the 2026 Georgia gubernatorial election, becoming the first Black woman to be elected governor of any U.S. state and Georgia's first Black governor. Tyler Perry joined her transition team following her victory.

## Key facts
- Keisha Lance Bottoms won the 2026 Georgia gubernatorial election.
- She is the first Black woman elected governor of any U.S. state.
- Bottoms previously served as the mayor of Atlanta and a member of the Atlanta City Council.
