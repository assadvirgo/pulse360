  the Rise of Vertical Dramas

Acclaimed filmmaker Peter Chan Ho-sun stated that he will likely use AI in his filmmaking but will not use AI-generated content. He expressed confidence that two-hour feature films will continue to thrive alongside the rise of short-form and vertical dramas.

## Key facts
- Peter Chan Ho-sun is known for directing films such as “Comrades: Almost a Love Story” and “The Warlords.”
- Chan indicated that he will probably use AI in his filmmaking.
- He ruled out the use of AI-generated content (AIGC).
- Chan believes that feature films will maintain their relevance against shorter formats during his lifetime.
