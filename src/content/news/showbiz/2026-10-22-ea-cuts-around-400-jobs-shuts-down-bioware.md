Electronic Arts (EA) is laying off approximately 400 employees, which represents about 3% of its workforce, and is shutting down video game developer BioWare. The layoffs include around 50 positions at BioWare as EA shifts its focus to more profitable projects.

## Key facts
- EA is cutting about 3% of its staff.
- The company is laying off around 400 people.
- Approximately 50 of the layoffs will occur at BioWare.
- EA is shifting focus to more lucrative projects.
