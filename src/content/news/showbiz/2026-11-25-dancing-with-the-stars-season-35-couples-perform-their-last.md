The finale of Season 35 of "Dancing With the Stars" features five remaining celebrities competing for the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy. The contestants are Tatyana Ali, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins, and Connor Wood.

## Key facts
- The five finalists are Tatyana Ali, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins, and Connor Wood.
- Each finalist will perform three routines: an instant dance, a freestyle, and judges' choice.
- The season finale is taking place on November 24.
