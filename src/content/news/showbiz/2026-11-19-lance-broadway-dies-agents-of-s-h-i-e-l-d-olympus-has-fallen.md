Lance Broadway, a former MLB pitcher turned actor, has died unexpectedly at the age of 43. He was found dead in a Dallas, TX apartment complex stairwell.

## Key facts
- Broadway appeared in Marvel's "Agents Of S.H.I.E.L.D.," "Lucifer," and the film "Olympus Has Fallen."
- He was found dead on November 17, according to the Dallas County Medical Examiner.
