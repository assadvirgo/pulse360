A federal judge has sided with ABC in a First Amendment lawsuit against the FCC, halting the agency's early license renewal proceeding. The lawsuit claims that the FCC's order to review ABC's broadcast licenses was a retaliatory act by the Trump administration.

## Key facts
- A U.S. District Judge issued a preliminary injunction in favor of ABC.
- The lawsuit alleges that the FCC's actions were intended to chill ABC's content, including news coverage and comedy.
- The case centers on claims of retaliation by the Trump administration against ABC.
