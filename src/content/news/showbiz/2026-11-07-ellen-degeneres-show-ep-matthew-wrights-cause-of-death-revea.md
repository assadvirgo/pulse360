A cause of death has been determined for Matthew Wright, an executive producer of "The Ellen DeGeneres Show," who died at age 52. His death certificate lists cardiopulmonary arrest as the cause, with hypertension as the underlying cause and contributing conditions including obstructive sleep apnea and class II obesity.

## Key facts
- Matthew Wright died at age 52.
- The cause of death is listed as cardiopulmonary arrest.
- Hypertension is noted as the underlying cause.
- Contributing conditions include obstructive sleep apnea and class II obesity.
