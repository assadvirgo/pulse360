Abramorama has acquired the North American theatrical distribution rights to the documentary "The Choice," directed by John David Coles. The film is set to be released on January 15 at the Quad in New York City, followed by a national rollout.

## Key facts
- Abramorama has acquired North American theatrical distribution rights to "The Choice."
- The film is directed by John David Coles.
- "The Choice" will be released on January 15 at the Quad in NYC.
- The documentary won the Brizzolara Family Foundation Award for Films of Conflict & Resolution at the 2026 Hamptons International Film Festival.
