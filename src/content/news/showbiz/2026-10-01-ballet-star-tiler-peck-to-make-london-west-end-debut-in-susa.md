## Ballet Star Tiler Peck to Make London West End Debut in ‘Little Dancer’

Tiler Peck, a principal dancer with the New York City Ballet, is set to make her highly anticipated debut in London's West End. The celebrated ballerina will star in the musical "Little Dancer," which is inspired by the life of the young girl who posed for Edgar Degas' renowned sculpture. The production is scheduled to open on February 15, 2027, at the Theatre Royal Haymarket.

### A Unique Collaboration

"Little Dancer" features a compelling score by Lynn Ahrens and Stephen Flaherty, the creative duo behind other successful musicals such as "Ragtime" and "Seussical." The show delves into the story of Marie van Goethem, a young ballet dancer in Paris during the late 19th century, who becomes the muse for Degas. The narrative not only highlights her journey in the world of ballet but also explores themes of ambition, artistry, and the struggles faced by aspiring performers.

The production is directed and choreographed by Susan Stroman, a Tony Award-winning artist known for her innovative work in musical theatre. Stroman's involvement promises to bring a unique blend of dance and storytelling to the stage, making "Little Dancer" a significant addition to the West End's theatrical offerings.

### Tiler Peck's Journey

Peck's transition from the New York stage to London's West End marks a significant milestone in her illustrious career. As a principal dancer with the New York City Ballet, she has garnered acclaim for her performances in a variety of classical and contemporary works. Her ability to embody complex characters through movement makes her an ideal choice for the role of Marie.

In a statement, Peck expressed her excitement about the opportunity, saying, "I am thrilled to be part of 'Little Dancer' and to bring this beautiful story to life on the West End stage. The collaboration with Susan Stroman and the talented creative team is an honor, and I look forward to sharing this journey with the audience."

### Anticipation for the Production

The announcement of "Little Dancer" has generated considerable buzz within the theatre community. The limited run at the Theatre Royal Haymarket is expected to attract a diverse audience, from ballet enthusiasts to theatre lovers eager to witness a fresh interpretation of a classic story.
