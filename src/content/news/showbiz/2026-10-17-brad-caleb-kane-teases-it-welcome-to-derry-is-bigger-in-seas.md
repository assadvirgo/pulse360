Brad Caleb Kane, the showrunner for Season 2 of "It: Welcome to Derry," has indicated that the upcoming season will present a new version of the character Pennywise. Kane mentioned that this portrayal will be different from what fans have seen before.

## Key facts
- Brad Caleb Kane is taking the reins as showrunner for Season 2 of "It: Welcome to Derry."
- Kane promises a new depiction of Pennywise, played by Bill Skarsgård.
- The character will be shown in a way that has not been seen before.
