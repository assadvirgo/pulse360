New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani addressed the postponement of the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade during a press briefing. He expressed disappointment that many New Yorkers and millions globally were looking forward to the event.

## Key facts
- The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade was postponed due to a major storm.
- Mayor Mamdani emphasized the significance of the parade for New Yorkers and others worldwide.
- He made a lighthearted remark about inflation, stating, "It’s the only inflation we support in New York City."
