## Variety Announces Key Speakers for Entertainment and Technology Summit

Variety, a leading publication in the entertainment industry, has revealed an impressive lineup of speakers for its upcoming Entertainment and Technology Summit. Scheduled to take place on September 17 in Los Angeles, the event will feature notable figures from both the entertainment and technology sectors, including Emmy-winning producer and actor Bill Hader, renowned director and producer James Wan, and popular actor and producer Nick Kroll. 

### A Diverse Panel of Experts

The summit aims to bridge the gap between entertainment and technology, showcasing how these two fields intersect and influence one another. Bill Hader, recognized for his work on "Saturday Night Live" and "Barry," will share insights from his extensive experience in the industry. His unique perspective as both a producer and actor is expected to resonate with attendees, offering a glimpse into the creative processes behind successful television and film projects.

James Wan, a celebrated filmmaker known for his work on blockbuster franchises such as "The Conjuring" and "Aquaman," will also take the stage. Wan's ability to blend technology with storytelling will provide valuable insights into the evolving landscape of cinema, particularly in how technology enhances the viewer experience.

Nick Kroll, another prominent figure in the entertainment world, is known for his contributions to animated series and live-action comedy. His participation will add a comedic element to the discussions, highlighting the role of humor in engaging audiences across various platforms.

### Leaders from the Technology Sector

In addition to these entertainment luminaries, the summit will feature key figures from the technology industry. Devon Pritchard, president and COO of Nintendo of America, will bring a unique perspective on the intersection of gaming and entertainment. As gaming continues to grow in popularity, Pritchard's insights will be invaluable to understanding how interactive entertainment shapes audience engagement.

Keith Le Goy, chairman of Sony Pictures Television, will also contribute to the discussions. His extensive experience in television production and distribution will provide a comprehensive view of the current trends in media consumption and the impact of technology on content delivery.

### An Event for Industry Professionals

The Entertainment and Technology Summit is designed for industry professionals seeking to explore the latest trends and innovations in both fields. With a focus on collaboration, the event will foster discussions on how technology can be leveraged to enhance storytelling and audience engagement. 

Presented by EY, the summit promises to be a thought-provoking gathering that not only highlights the achievements of its speakers but also encourages dialogue about the future of entertainment and technology. 

As the event approaches, anticipation builds for the insights and discussions that will emerge from this gathering of influential voices. The convergence of entertainment and technology is more relevant than ever, and the summit aims to illuminate the pathways forward for both industries. 

For those interested in attending, further information regarding registration and the full agenda will be available on Variety's official website.
