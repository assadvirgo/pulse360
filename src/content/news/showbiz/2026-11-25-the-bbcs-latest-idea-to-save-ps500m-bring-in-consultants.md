The BBC plans to save £500 million by bringing in consultants, according to Deadline. The consultancy firm AlixPartners, known for assisting companies in financial distress, will be involved in this initiative.

## Key facts
- The BBC's plan aims to save £500 million ($654 million).
- Consultants brought in may bill more than £1,000 an hour.
- AlixPartners specializes in helping turnaround companies in financial distress.
- BBC director-general Matt Brittin has turned to AlixPartners for this initiative.
