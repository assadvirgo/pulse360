Paramount+ has ordered a six-episode limited drama series titled "Mushroom Murders," which will star and be executive produced by Melanie Lynskey. The series is based on the infamous Leongatha mushroom murders in Australia, with Lynskey portraying Erin Patterson, who was convicted in the case.

## Key facts
- "Mushroom Murders" is a limited drama series consisting of six episodes.
- The series is produced by Paramount+ and is set in Australia.
- Melanie Lynskey, known for her roles in "Yellowjackets" and "Don’t Say Good Luck," stars in the series.
- Lynskey also serves as an executive producer for the project.
- The series is created by Anya Beyersdorf.
