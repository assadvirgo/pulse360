During a discussion at Turin's View Conference, Cristóbal Valenzuela, co-CEO of the generative AI startup Runway, stated that AI could "absolutely" replace human actors. He indicated that this replacement is already occurring in some capacity.

## Key facts
- Cristóbal Valenzuela spoke during a fireside chat titled "Every Pixel Will Be Generated."
- He described AI as "a new medium."
- The event took place at the View Conference in Turin, Italy.
