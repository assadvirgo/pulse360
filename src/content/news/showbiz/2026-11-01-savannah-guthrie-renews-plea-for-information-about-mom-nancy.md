Savannah Guthrie has renewed her plea for information regarding her mother Nancy's disappearance, which occurred nine months ago. She emphasized that her family cannot find closure until they learn what happened to her mother and reminded followers that the FBI reward for information leading to Nancy's location is still available, according to Deadline. 

## Key facts
- Savannah Guthrie is the co-anchor of the Today show.
- Nancy Guthrie has been missing for nine months.
- Savannah stated that her family "cannot move forward" until they know what happened to Nancy.
- The FBI reward for information about Nancy's whereabouts is still available.
