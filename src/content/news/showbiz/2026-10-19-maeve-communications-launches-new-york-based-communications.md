Maeve Communications has launched a new publicity and strategic communications company based in New York. Co-founded by Laura Puig and Michelle Ciciyasvili, the company will focus on entertainment, media, and talent services.

## Key facts
- Maeve Communications is co-founded by Laura Puig and Michelle Ciciyasvili.
- The company is based in New York.
- It will offer project-based consultation and executive coaching services.
- The idea to start the company began in 2019 while the founders were working together.
