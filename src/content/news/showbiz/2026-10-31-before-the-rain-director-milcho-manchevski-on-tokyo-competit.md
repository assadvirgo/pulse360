Milcho Manchevski, a North Macedonian director, is competing at the Tokyo International Film Festival with his film "Sister Brother Manhole Cover." The project faced delays after Manchevski accused his country's film agency of corruption.

## Key facts
- Manchevski is known for his 1994 debut "Before the Rain," which won Venice’s Golden Lion and received an Oscar nomination.
- "Sister Brother Manhole Cover" is described as a dark comedy.
- The film was stalled for years due to allegations of corruption against the North Macedonian film agency.
