Walter Koenig, known for his role as Chekov in the original "Star Trek" series, has died at the age of 90. His passing was confirmed by his representatives, who noted his significant impact on generations through his performances.

## Key facts
- Walter Koenig played Chekov in the original "Star Trek" series and seven "Star Trek" feature films.
- He died in Los Angeles, according to Variety.
- His death was reported to be due to natural causes, as stated by a representative.
- Koenig also appeared in another acclaimed sci-fi program, "Babylon 5."
