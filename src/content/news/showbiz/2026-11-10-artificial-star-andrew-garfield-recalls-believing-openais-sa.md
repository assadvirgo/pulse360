Andrew Garfield discussed his initial admiration for OpenAI co-founder Sam Altman during a Q&A for his film "Artificial." He recalled believing Altman was "genuinely great" and could potentially be "my future best friend."

## Key facts
- Andrew Garfield portrays Sam Altman in the film "Artificial."
- Garfield's portrayal of Altman is described as less than flattering.
- He admired Altman after seeing him testify in front of Congress.
