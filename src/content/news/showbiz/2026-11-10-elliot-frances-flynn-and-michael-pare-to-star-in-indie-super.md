Elliot Frances Flynn and Michael Paré will star in the indie supernatural horror film "Not Herself." The film recently completed production in New York.

## Key facts
- Elliot Frances Flynn is known for her role in "Peacemaker."
- Michael Paré is recognized for his work in "Streets of Fire."
- The film is co-written and co-directed by Jon Russell Cring and Tracy Nichole Cring.
- "Not Herself" centers on a guilt-ridden young woman named Izmarie, played by Flynn.
