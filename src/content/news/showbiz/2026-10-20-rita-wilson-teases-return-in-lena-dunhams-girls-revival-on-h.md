Rita Wilson has teased her return in the upcoming revival of Lena Dunham's "Girls" on HBO. The revival is set to consist of five episodes in 2027, and Wilson indicated she would reprise her role as Marnie Michael's mother.

## Key facts
- Rita Wilson confirmed the revival of "Girls" for five episodes in 2027.
- Wilson expressed her willingness to return, stating she "would do anything" for Lena Dunham.
- She mentioned that she is hearing she will reprise her role, suggesting confidence in the revival plans.
