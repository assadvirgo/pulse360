Indonesian studio MD Entertainment announced plans for a sequel to its 2025 horror film "Pabrik Gula," set to be released in early 2028. The company also revealed it has three Imax films scheduled for 2027.

## Key facts
- MD Entertainment will release a sequel to "Pabrik Gula" in early 2028.
- The sequel follows the success of the original film, which was a horror hit in 2025.
- MD Entertainment has three Imax films planned for release in 2027.
- The announcement was made by Astrid Wienardi at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest (TCCF) in Taipei.
