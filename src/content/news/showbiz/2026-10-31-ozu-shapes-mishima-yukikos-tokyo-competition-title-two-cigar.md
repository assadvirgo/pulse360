Mishima Yukiko's latest film, "Two Cigarettes, Three Nights," is competing at the 39th Tokyo International Film Festival. The film marks a departure from her typical dark relationship dramas.

## Key facts
- Mishima Yukiko is known for her dark relationship dramas, including the award-winning "Dear Etranger."
- "Two Cigarettes, Three Nights" is based on a 2014 novel by Chihaya Akane.
- The film stars Matsuoka Mayu as Kanna.
