Holland Taylor discussed her experience on "Two and a Half Men," stating that she was "never paid very well" during her time on the show. She mentioned that despite receiving four Emmy nominations, she did not receive a raise.

## Key facts
- Holland Taylor portrayed Evelyn Harper on "Two and a Half Men" for six years.
- She revealed that her decision not to renew her contract was influenced by her pay.
- Taylor received four Emmy nominations while on the show.
