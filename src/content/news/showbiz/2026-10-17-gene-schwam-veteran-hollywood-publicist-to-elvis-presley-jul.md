Veteran Hollywood publicist Gene Schwam has died at the age of 96 in Los Angeles after a brief illness. Schwam represented notable talents including Elvis Presley, Julie Andrews, and Barbara Eden.

## Key facts
- Gene Schwam co-founded Hanson & Schwam Public Relations in 1955.
- He established an independent publicity agency that lasted for decades.
- Schwam represented top talent such as Blake Edwards and Lenny Bruce.
