Egyptian filmmaker Muhammad Mustapha's documentary "Big Boys Don’t Cry" explores masculinity through the lives of Cairo bodybuilders and nightclub bouncers. The film had its world premiere in the Feature Documentary Competition at Egypt's El Gouna Film Festival.

## Key facts
- "Big Boys Don’t Cry" follows a group of Cairo bodybuilders and nightclub bouncers.
- The documentary examines how men understand and perform masculinity.
- The film premiered at the El Gouna Film Festival.
