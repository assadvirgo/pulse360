Jon Stewart criticized Michigan congresswoman Haley Stevens for her endorsement of Republican Mike Rogers over Democrat Abdul El-Sayed just before Election Day. During an episode of "The Daily Show," Stewart examined the reasons behind Stevens' last-minute decision.

- Jon Stewart addressed Haley Stevens' endorsement of Mike Rogers on "The Daily Show."
- Stevens switched her support from Democrat Abdul El-Sayed to Republican Mike Rogers.
