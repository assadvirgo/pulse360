Paapa Essiedu, who plays Severus Snape in the upcoming HBO adaptation of Harry Potter, is addressing controversy surrounding his casting. This comes in light of criticisms related to J.K. Rowling's past comments on trans rights.

## Key facts
- Essiedu is set to star in the Harry Potter series debuting this Christmas.
- He has responded to criticisms regarding his involvement in the project.
- The controversy is linked to Rowling's previous social media campaign perceived as transphobic.
