The Tallinn Black Nights Film Festival (PÖFF) continues to show solidarity with Ukraine during its 30th edition, which features films made in response to the ongoing war. This support has been a consistent theme for the festival since Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022.

## Key facts
- The Tallinn Black Nights Film Festival is currently in its 30th edition.
- The festival has been showing solidarity with Ukraine since the 2022 invasion by Russia.
- Films featured at the festival are made despite, and because of, the war in Ukraine.
