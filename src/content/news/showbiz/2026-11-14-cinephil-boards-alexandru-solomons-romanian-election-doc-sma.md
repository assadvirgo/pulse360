Cinephil has acquired Alexandru Solomon’s documentary "Small Expectations" ahead of its world premiere at the International Documentary Film Festival Amsterdam. The film provides a first-person investigation into the lead-up to Romania's 2025 presidential election.

## Key facts
- Cinephil has boarded the documentary "Small Expectations."
- The film is directed by Alexandru Solomon.
- It will premiere in the Frontlight sidebar of the International Documentary Film Festival Amsterdam.
- The documentary chronicles the chaotic lead-up to Romania’s 2025 presidential election.
- The election features moderate independent Nicușor Dan against far-right forces.
