## Whitney Houston Honored with New Barbie Doll Inspired by Iconic Music Video

Mattel has announced the release of a new Barbie Signature doll that pays tribute to the late pop icon Whitney Houston. The doll is inspired by Houston's vibrant look from her 1987 music video for "I Wanna Dance With Somebody (Who Loves Me)," a song that remains one of her most beloved hits. This collectible doll is set to launch on August 8 and will be available at Target stores and online.

## Celebrating Whitney Houston's Legacy

Whitney Houston, who passed away in 2012, is celebrated for her powerful voice and significant contributions to the music industry. Her rendition of "I Wanna Dance With Somebody" not only topped charts but also became an anthem of joy and celebration in the late 1980s. The song's infectious energy and Houston's charismatic performance have left an indelible mark on pop culture.

The new Barbie doll captures the essence of Houston's iconic style, featuring a colorful outfit reminiscent of the one she wore in the music video. This initiative by Mattel not only honors Houston's legacy but also aims to inspire new generations by showcasing her influence in the entertainment industry.

## A Collector's Item

Priced at $60, the Whitney Houston Barbie doll is positioned as a collectible item, appealing to both fans of the singer and Barbie enthusiasts. The doll's design reflects the vibrant colors and dynamic fashion of the 1980s, making it a nostalgic piece for those who remember Houston's impact during that era. 

Mattel's decision to create a doll based on Houston's likeness aligns with a broader trend of honoring influential figures through collectible toys. The company has previously released dolls inspired by other cultural icons, further solidifying its commitment to celebrating diversity and representation.

## Availability and Anticipation

The Whitney Houston Barbie doll will be available at Target stores and online starting August 8, 2023. Given Houston's enduring popularity and the nostalgic appeal of her music, there is considerable anticipation surrounding the doll's release. Fans and collectors alike are expected to respond positively, eager to add this unique piece to their collections.

As the launch date approaches, social media platforms are buzzing with excitement, and many fans are sharing their favorite memories associated with Houston's music.
