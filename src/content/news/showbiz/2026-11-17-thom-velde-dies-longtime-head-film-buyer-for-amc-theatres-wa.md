Thomas "Thom" Velde, a longtime head film buyer for AMC Theatres, has died at the age of 83. He passed away on October 31 due to cancer in Rancho Mirage, California.

## Key facts
- Thom Velde served as head film buyer for AMC Theatres and oversaw its Canadian operations.
- He spent more than 25 years at AMC Theatres.
- Velde was involved in negotiating film agreements during his tenure.
