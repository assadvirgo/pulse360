Disney+ has announced a landmark content deal with U.K. network Channel 4, which it describes as one of its "most significant" arrangements globally. This deal will allow the Channel 4 service to launch on Disney+ in the U.K. and Ireland in early 2027.

## Key facts
- Disney+ has struck a deal with Channel 4 to bring local content to its platform.
- The deal is described as a first-of-its-kind arrangement.
- The Channel 4 service is set to launch on Disney+ in the U.K. and Ireland in early 2027.
