Indonesian filmmaker Ravi Bharwani's Netflix thriller "Secrets" is set to premiere at the 21st Jogja-Netpac Asian Film Festival on November 29, 2023. This marks Bharwani's first feature film in eight years, and it will begin streaming on Netflix on December 3, 2023.

## Key facts
- "Secrets" is directed by Indonesian filmmaker Ravi Bharwani.
- The film will world premiere at the Jogja-Netpac Asian Film Festival.
- The festival premiere is scheduled for November 29, 2023.
- "Secrets" will start streaming on Netflix on December 3, 2023.
- Sha Ine Febriyanti stars as Parni, a former sex worker.
