Antonio Banderas has been cast alongside Nicolas Cage in the upcoming fifth season of HBO's "True Detective." Production for the new season is scheduled to begin in 2027.

## Key facts
- Antonio Banderas and Nicolas Cage will headline "True Detective" Season 5.
- The new season is set to begin production in 2027.
- Issa López, who was the showrunner for the fourth season, will also lead Season 5.
