Indian auteur Girish Kasaravalli reflected on his 50-year career in Kannada-language cinema during a masterclass at the 57th International Film Festival of India (IFFI) in Goa. The session was titled "The Moral Universe of Parallel Cinema: 50 Years of Girish Kasaravalli."

## Key facts
- The masterclass focused on the moral contradictions of society.
- Kasaravalli discussed the relationship between cinematic form and social issues.
- The event took place at IFFI, an international film festival held in Goa.
