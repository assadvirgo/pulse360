Former "Malcolm in the Middle" star Frankie Muniz was injured in a crash while practicing for the Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic exhibition race. Muniz was set to drive for Team Reaume in its No. 33 Ford.

## Key facts
- Frankie Muniz is a former star of "Malcolm in the Middle."
- He is currently a NASCAR Truck Series driver.
- The incident occurred during practice for the Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic exhibition race.
- Muniz was scheduled to compete alongside co-driver Austin Cindric.
