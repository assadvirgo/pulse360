A virtual film festival is focusing on disability by highlighting ableism as the true horror rather than disability itself. Ariel Baska, who transitioned from a 15-year teaching career to the film industry, shared their experiences of facing barriers due to their deaf-blindness.

## Key facts
- Ariel Baska encountered unexpected obstacles when entering the film industry.
- Baska noted that many opportunities were inaccessible due to physical barriers.
- The festival aims to shift the narrative around disability in horror films.
