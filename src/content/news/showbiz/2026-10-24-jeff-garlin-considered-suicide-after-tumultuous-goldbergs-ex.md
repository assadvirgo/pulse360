title: Jeff Garlin Considered Suicide After Tumultuous ‘Goldbergs’ Exit

Jeff Garlin, who left the ABC comedy "The Goldbergs" following an HR investigation into complaints about his behavior, revealed he considered suicide during this tumultuous period. He reached out to his "Curb Your Enthusiasm" co-star Susie Essman to inform her of his plans.

## Key facts
- Jeff Garlin left "The Goldbergs" after an HR investigation.
- The investigation was prompted by multiple complaints about his behavior on set.
- Garlin reached out to Susie Essman regarding his mental state.
