The 57th International Film Festival of India opened in Goa, featuring a program titled "Stories Without Borders" directed by Ashutosh Gowariker. Goa's Chief Minister Pramod Sawant emphasized the need for a film city in the state.

## Key facts

- Ashutosh Gowariker is the festival director for the first time.
- The program is titled "Stories Without Borders."
