Liz Daly Byrne, a model and philanthropist, has died at the age of 81. She was married to PMC vice chairman Gerry Byrne for over 55 years. Byrne had been battling cancer and recently learned that her illness had returned.

## Key facts
- Liz Daly Byrne was a model and philanthropist.
- She was married to Gerry Byrne, vice chairman of PMC, for more than 55 years.
- Byrne had faced a battle with cancer three years ago.
- She recently learned that her cancer had returned.
- She suffered from recurring complications due to severe injuries from a bicycle accident.
