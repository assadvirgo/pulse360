Buena Vista Social Club, the Tony-winning musical, will close on Broadway on January 17, 2027, after a run of 96 weeks and 767 performances. Producers announced the closing date, noting that the production has not yet recouped its reported $17 million investment but is "on schedule" to do so.

## Key facts
- Buena Vista Social Club will close on Broadway on January 17, 2027.
- The musical has had a run of 96 weeks and 767 performances.
- The production has not recouped its reported $17 million investment.
- The show is set to move to London's West End in 2027.
