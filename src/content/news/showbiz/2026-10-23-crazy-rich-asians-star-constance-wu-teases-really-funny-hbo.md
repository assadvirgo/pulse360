Constance Wu is reuniting with the Young family for the HBO sequel series of "Crazy Rich Asians." She described the script as "really funny" and confirmed that filming is taking place in New York City.

## Key facts
- Constance Wu is reuniting with the Young family for the HBO sequel series of "Crazy Rich Asians."
- The script is co-written by Adele Lim, who also worked on the 2018 film.
- Filming for the one-hour drama series is taking place in New York City.
