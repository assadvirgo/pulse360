OneTwoThree Media has acquired worldwide sales rights to the mystery-horror film "One Of Us," which stars Emmy winner Kit Connor. The film is the second feature from writer-director Stefan van de Graaff.

## Key facts
- "One Of Us" begins at a funeral where a family member dies every hour on the hour.
- The film is directed by Stefan van de Graaff, known for his previous work on "Simmer."
- Kit Connor, an Emmy winner, stars in the film.
