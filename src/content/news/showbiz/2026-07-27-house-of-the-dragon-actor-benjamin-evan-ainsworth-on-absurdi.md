## ‘House of the Dragon’ Actor Benjamin Evan Ainsworth Discusses Daeron Targaryen’s Evolution and Recent Plot Developments

In a recent interview, Benjamin Evan Ainsworth, who portrays Daeron Targaryen in the acclaimed series "House of the Dragon," shared insights into his character's journey and the unfolding narrative of the show. As Season 3 progresses, Ainsworth reflects on the complexities of Daeron's character, the implications of an assassination attempt, and the dynamics of power within the Targaryen family.

### The Absurdity of Assassination Attempts

In the latest episode, viewers witnessed a shocking assassination attempt that left audiences questioning the safety and stability of the Targaryen dynasty. Ainsworth described the absurdity of such events within the context of the show, emphasizing how they serve to heighten the tension and drama among the characters. "It's a reflection of the political landscape in Westeros, where power struggles often lead to desperate measures," he explained.

The assassination attempt not only highlights the dangers faced by the royal family but also serves as a catalyst for Daeron's character development. As the youngest son of Alicent Hightower and Viserys Targaryen, Daeron is navigating a world filled with intrigue and peril, forcing him to confront the realities of his lineage.

### Daeron’s Growing Power

As the series progresses, Daeron's character is evolving, showcasing a blend of innocence and burgeoning strength. Ainsworth noted that this transformation is crucial for the narrative, as Daeron begins to understand the responsibilities that come with being a Targaryen. "He’s not just a child anymore; he’s starting to realize the weight of his family’s legacy," Ainsworth remarked.

This growth is particularly significant in light of the power struggles within the Targaryen family. Daeron's interactions with his siblings and the political machinations surrounding them reveal a complex web of alliances and rivalries. Ainsworth hinted at upcoming developments that will further test Daeron's character, suggesting that viewers can expect to see him take a more active role in the unfolding drama.

### The Hair Dye Controversy

Another intriguing aspect of Daeron's character is his distinctive appearance, particularly his hair color, which has sparked discussions among fans. Ainsworth humorously addressed the speculation surrounding whether Ormund, a character in the series, is dyeing Daeron's hair. "It's a fun topic among fans, and while I can’t give too much away, it certainly adds an interesting layer to the character," he said with a smile.

This playful banter reflects the show's ability to engage its audience on multiple levels, blending serious themes with light-hearted moments. Ainsworth's enthusiasm for his role and the series as a whole is evident, indicating a strong connection between the actor and the character he portrays.

### Conclusion

As "House of the Dragon" continues to captivate audiences with its intricate storytelling and character development, Benjamin Evan Ainsworth's portrayal of Daeron Targaryen adds depth to the narrative. With the stakes higher than ever following the recent assassination attempt, viewers are left eagerly anticipating how Daeron will navigate the treacherous waters of Westeros. As the season unfolds, Ainsworth's insights promise to keep fans engaged in the evolving saga of the Targaryen dynasty.
