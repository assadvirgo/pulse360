Qatar's Film Committee and Watermelon Pictures have formed a strategic partnership to co-produce a series of films. The collaboration aims to highlight filmmakers and stories from the Global South, focusing on culturally authentic storytelling with international appeal.

## Key facts
- Qatar's Film Committee and Watermelon Pictures announced their partnership to develop and co-produce films.
- The partnership emphasizes stories from the Global South and aims for broad international appeal.
- The initiative is intended to champion visionary filmmakers and compelling narratives.
