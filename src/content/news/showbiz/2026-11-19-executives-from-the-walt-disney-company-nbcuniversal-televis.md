Variety will host its annual Entertainment Summit at CES on January 7 at Aria Las Vegas. The event will feature executives from major brands including the Walt Disney Company, NBCUniversal Television, Blumhouse Atomic, Waymo, and Chipotle.

## Key facts
- The Entertainment Summit is part of CES and will be open to all CES registrants at no additional cost.
- The summit will include keynotes and panels featuring industry executives.
- The event is scheduled for January 7 at Aria Las Vegas.
