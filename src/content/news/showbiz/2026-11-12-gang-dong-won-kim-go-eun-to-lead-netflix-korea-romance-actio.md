Gang Dong-won and Kim Go-eun will star in the Netflix Korea romance-action period drama titled "The Twilight Between Us." The series is directed by Kim Won-suk and written by Park Ji-eun.

## Key facts
- "The Twilight Between Us" is an action-romance period drama for Netflix.
- The story revolves around heirs to two feuding houses who are forced into marriage by royal decree.
- Kim Won-suk, known for "When Life Gives You Tangerines," is directing the series.
- The script is written by Park Ji-eun, recognized for "Queen on Tears."
