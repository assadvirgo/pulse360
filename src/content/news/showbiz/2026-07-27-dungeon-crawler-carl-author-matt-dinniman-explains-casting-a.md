## Author Matt Dinniman Discusses Audiobook Casting Choices for "Dungeon Crawler Carl"

In an era where fandoms are rapidly evolving and expanding, the "Dungeon Crawler Carl" series by author Matt Dinniman is garnering significant attention. Originally self-published in 2019, this unique blend of fantasy and gaming elements has captured the imaginations of readers and listeners alike. As the series continues to grow, Dinniman recently shared insights regarding the casting of audiobook narrator Jeff Hays in the role of Princess Donut, a character that has resonated deeply with fans.

## The Rise of "Dungeon Crawler Carl"

"Dungeon Crawler Carl" follows the journey of Carl, a Coast Guard veteran who finds himself thrust into a perilous, video game-inspired dungeon after an alien invasion takes over Earth. The narrative combines elements of adventure, humor, and an exploration of gaming culture, making it appealing to both readers and gamers. Since its inception, the series has developed a dedicated following, positioning it as a potential contender in the realm of popular fandoms, alongside established franchises like Marvel and DC.

## Fan Engagement and Casting Decisions

In a recent interview, Dinniman emphasized the importance of fan feedback in the creative process, particularly in casting decisions for the audiobook adaptation. He stated, "We are listening to the fans," indicating a commitment to ensuring that the characters resonate with the audience. The decision to cast Jeff Hays as Princess Donut was influenced by the overwhelming support and enthusiasm from fans who expressed their desire for a narrator who could bring the character to life in a compelling manner.

Hays is known for his versatile voice acting and has previously lent his talents to various audiobook projects, making him a fitting choice for the role. Dinniman's acknowledgment of fan input reflects a broader trend in the entertainment industry, where creators are increasingly recognizing the value of audience engagement in shaping their work.

## The Impact of Audiobooks on Fandom

The rise of audiobooks has transformed how stories are consumed, allowing for a more immersive experience that appeals to a diverse range of listeners. For "Dungeon Crawler Carl," the audiobook format not only broadens its reach but also enhances the storytelling experience through skilled narration. Dinniman's collaboration with Hays is expected to elevate the narrative, drawing in both existing fans and new listeners who may be intrigued by the series.

As the "Dungeon Crawler Carl" series continues to expand, it remains to be seen how the combination of engaging storytelling, fan involvement, and skilled narration will influence its trajectory in the competitive landscape of modern fandoms. Dinniman's approach serves as a reminder that in today's entertainment landscape, the voices of fans can significantly shape the creative process, leading to a richer and more inclusive experience for all.

## Conclusion

With its unique premise and growing popularity, "Dungeon Crawler Carl" is poised to become a significant player in the realm of fantasy literature and audiobooks. As Matt Dinniman continues to engage with his audience and adapt to their preferences, the series may well carve out its own niche within the expansive world of fandoms. The collaboration with Jeff Hays as Princess Donut exemplifies a thoughtful approach to storytelling that prioritizes listener engagement, setting a promising foundation for the future of the series.
