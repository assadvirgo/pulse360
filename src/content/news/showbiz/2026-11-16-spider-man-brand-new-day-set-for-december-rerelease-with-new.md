A re-release of "Spider-Man: Brand New Day" is scheduled for December 4, featuring previously unseen footage. The new cut will be titled "Spider-Man: [Brand New] Brand New Day."

## Key facts
- The re-release will include new scenes featuring Rosario Dawson's character, Night Nurse.
- The film is the highest-grossing motion picture in the U.S. and the third-highest worldwide.
- The re-release is timed for the awards season.
