A man was arrested on the set of "Miami Vice '85" in Brooklyn after brandishing a knife. The incident occurred on Friday night, leading to charges of menacing and criminal possession of a weapon.

## Key facts
- The man arrested was identified as 23-year-old Alex Perez of Brooklyn.
- The New York Police Department confirmed the arrest took place around 11 p.m.
- No injuries were reported during the incident.
