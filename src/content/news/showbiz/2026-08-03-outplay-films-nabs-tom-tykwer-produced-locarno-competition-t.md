## Outplay Films Acquires Tom Tykwer-Produced Title ‘I Rarely Wake Up Dreaming’

In a significant development in the film industry, Outplay Films, a French production company, has secured the world sales rights to the critically acclaimed film “I Rarely Wake Up Dreaming.” The film, which has garnered attention at the Locarno Film Festival, is produced by renowned filmmaker Tom Tykwer and Uwe Schott through their Berlin-based company, X Filme Creative Pool.

### Overview of the Film

“I Rarely Wake Up Dreaming” is directed by Isabelle Stever, a filmmaker known for her nuanced storytelling and character-driven narratives. The film explores the lives of Olya and Oleh, a young queer couple from Ukraine, navigating their relationship amid the backdrop of societal challenges and personal struggles. Written by Anna Melikova, the film promises to offer an intimate portrayal of love and resilience in the face of adversity.

### Significance of the Acquisition

The acquisition by Outplay Films marks a strategic move for the company as it aims to expand its portfolio with compelling international titles. The film's selection for the main competition at the prestigious Locarno Film Festival underscores its artistic merit and potential appeal to global audiences. By securing the rights to this title, Outplay Films positions itself to capitalize on the growing interest in LGBTQ+ narratives, particularly those emerging from Eastern Europe.

### Tom Tykwer’s Influence

Tom Tykwer, best known for his work on films such as “Run Lola Run” and “Cloud Atlas,” brings a wealth of experience to the project. His involvement as a producer is expected to enhance the film's visibility and credibility within the competitive film market. Tykwer's reputation for innovative storytelling and strong visual aesthetics could play a pivotal role in attracting distributors and audiences alike.

### The Context of Ukrainian Cinema

The film arrives at a time when Ukrainian cinema is gaining international recognition, particularly for its representation of LGBTQ+ stories. The ongoing socio-political climate in Ukraine has prompted filmmakers to explore themes of identity, love, and resistance, resonating with audiences worldwide. “I Rarely Wake Up Dreaming” is poised to contribute to this growing narrative, offering a unique perspective on the experiences of queer individuals in Ukraine.
