Martin Sheen, Alexis Bledel, and Tate Donovan are set to star in the feature sports drama "Raising Cole," produced by Santa Rita Film Co. and Crowbar Media. The film tells the true story of Cole Pittman, a standout defensive lineman from the University of Texas, whose life ended tragically in February 2001.

## Key facts
- Martin Sheen, Alexis Bledel, and Tate Donovan are headlining the film.
- The film is produced by Santa Rita Film Co. and Crowbar Media.
- "Raising Cole" tells the true story of Cole Pittman, a University of Texas defensive lineman.
- Pittman's life was cut short in February 2001.
