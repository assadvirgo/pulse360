Helene Hahn Lloyd, a former executive vice president at Disney and a founding executive of DreamWorks, has died at the age of 78. She passed away peacefully in her sleep on October 24 in Park City, Utah.

## Key facts
- Helene Hahn Lloyd was part of the executive team that built DreamWorks SKG.
- She played a significant role in Disney's renaissance during the 1980s and 1990s.
- Lloyd was born Helene Bulakoff in Westbury, New York, and graduated from Hofstra University.
- After leaving DreamWorks in 2004, she moved to Park City, Utah, where she lived with her husband.
