## Busan's Asian Project Market Reveals 30 Feature Projects for 2026 Edition

The Busan Asian Project Market (APM) has announced the selection of 30 feature projects for its 2026 edition, showcasing a diverse array of talent from across Asia. Notable filmmakers such as Anthony Chen, Mouly Surya, Reza Rahadian, and Fran Borgia are among those featured in this year's lineup, underscoring the region's vibrant cinematic landscape.

### Record Submissions Highlight Growing Interest

This year, the APM set a new record for submissions, receiving an impressive 604 entries from 48 countries. This marks the third consecutive year that the event has achieved an all-time high in submissions, reflecting a growing interest in Asian cinema on a global scale. The increasing number of entries not only highlights the talent present in the region but also the international community's recognition of Asian filmmakers.

### Prominent Filmmakers in the Spotlight

Anthony Chen, known for his critically acclaimed works, brings a unique perspective to the APM. His involvement in the project market is expected to draw attention to new narratives and innovative storytelling techniques. Similarly, Mouly Surya, a prominent figure in Indonesian cinema, has garnered international acclaim for her films that often explore complex social themes. 

Reza Rahadian, one of Indonesia's leading actors and filmmakers, is also part of this distinguished lineup. His participation is likely to enhance the visibility of Indonesian cinema within the broader Asian context. Fran Borgia, who has made significant contributions to the film industry, rounds out the group of notable filmmakers, promising to bring fresh ideas and perspectives to the market.

### A Platform for Emerging Talent

The APM serves as a crucial platform for emerging filmmakers to showcase their projects and connect with potential investors and collaborators. By facilitating networking opportunities, the market aims to foster the growth of new talent in the industry. The selected projects will not only highlight established filmmakers but also provide a stage for up-and-coming artists to present their work to a wider audience.

### Looking Ahead

As the 2026 edition approaches, anticipation builds around the selected projects and the potential they hold for the future of Asian cinema. The APM continues to play a vital role in promoting the region's film industry, encouraging collaboration and innovation among filmmakers. With the increasing number of submissions and the participation of renowned talents, the APM is poised to further solidify its status as a key event in the global film calendar.

In conclusion, the Busan Asian Project Market's announcement of the 30 feature projects for 2026 not only highlights the remarkable talent within Asia but also reflects the growing recognition of the region's cinematic contributions on the world stage. As filmmakers like Anthony Chen, Mouly Surya, Reza Rahadian, and Fran Borgia take center stage, the future of Asian cinema looks promising.
