The documentary "Diamond Diplomacy," directed by Yuriko Gamo Romer, is set to have its first international screening at the Tokyo Film Festival this week. The film has previously been showcased at various U.S. festivals, including Mill Valley and Hot Springs.

## Key facts
- "Diamond Diplomacy" is a feature documentary by filmmaker Yuriko Gamo Romer.
- The film has completed a year on the U.S. festival circuit.
- It will have its first international screening at the Tokyo Film Festival.
- The screening is described as a unique homecoming for Gamo Romer.
