Gerald R. Molen, an Academy Award-winning producer known for his work on Steven Spielberg's "Schindler's List," has died at the age of 91. His death was announced by his great-niece, Rachel Pullan, on Sunday.

## Key facts
- Gerald R. Molen was a producer at Steven Spielberg's Amblin Entertainment.
- He passed away on Sunday, as announced by his niece.
- Molen was 91 years old at the time of his death.
