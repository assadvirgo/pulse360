AMC Kips Bay 15 movie theater in New York City will close on January 3. AMC CEO Adam Aron expressed the company's desire to remain in the location, but indicated that NYU Langone, which plans to expand its medical facility, wants the theater to close.

## Key facts
- AMC Kips Bay 15 will close on January 3.
- AMC CEO Adam Aron stated that the company wants to stay in the location.
- NYU Langone plans to grow its medical facility and wants the theater to close.
