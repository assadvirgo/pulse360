Disney+ has announced the upcoming release of its latest Japanese original series, "Night Tales Club." The supernatural thriller follows a businessman named Hojo, who becomes obsessed with solving life's biggest mysteries, leading to bizarre horrors.

## Key facts
- "Night Tales Club" is a supernatural thriller series produced by Disney+.
- The series follows a businessman named Hojo who is obsessed with the inexplicable.
- A teaser trailer for the show has been released.
- The six-part series is set to launch in January 2027.
