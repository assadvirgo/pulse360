The second season of the animated series "Blue Eye Samurai" will premiere on Netflix on January 19, 2027. This release comes more than three years after the first season aired in November 2023. Netflix has also announced that the series will have a third and final season.

## Key facts
- "Blue Eye Samurai" Season 2 will launch on January 19, 2027, according to Netflix.
- The first season of the series aired in November 2023.
- Netflix has renewed "Blue Eye Samurai" for a third and final season.
