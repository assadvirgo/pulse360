Denis Villeneuve expressed his disappointment regarding Disney's decision to release "Avengers: Doomsday" on the same weekend as "Dune: Part Three." He noted that the release date of December 18 poses a challenge for his film, as his previous two "Dune" films faced difficulties due to the pandemic.

## Key facts
- Villeneuve is "deeply disappointed" by the scheduling of "Avengers: Doomsday."
- The two films are set to release on December 18.
- Villeneuve's earlier "Dune" films contended with challenges related to the pandemic.
