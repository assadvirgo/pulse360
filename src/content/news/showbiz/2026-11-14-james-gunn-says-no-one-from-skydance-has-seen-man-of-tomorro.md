James Gunn addressed rumors regarding the Superman sequel, "Man of Tomorrow," stating that no one from Skydance has seen the film. This clarification comes after his exit as co-head of DC Studios and amid changes at Skydance following the Paramount and Warner Bros. merger.

## Key facts
- James Gunn is clearing up rumors after his departure from DC Studios.
- He stated that "no one from Skydance has seen" the film "Man of Tomorrow."
- The context includes changes at Skydance due to the Paramount and Warner Bros. merger.
- Many rumors have circulated following Gunn and Peter Safran's announcement of their exit from DC Studios.
