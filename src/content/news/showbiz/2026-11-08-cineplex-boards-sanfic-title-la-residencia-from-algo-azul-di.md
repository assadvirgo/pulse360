Latin American distributor Cineplex has acquired the North and South American sales rights to "La Residencia," directed by Mariel García Spooner. This film is a follow-up to her previous work, "Algo Azul." "La Residencia" recently participated in the Sanfic Industria's WIP Iberoamericana section, which showcases films from Latin America, Spain, and Portugal that are in post-production.

## Key facts
- Cineplex has acquired North and South American sales rights to "La Residencia."
- The film is directed by Mariel García Spooner.
- "La Residencia" is a follow-up to the hit film "Algo Azul."
- The film participated in the Sanfic Industria's WIP Iberoamericana section.
