Hayden Panettiere's death has been ruled accidental, and the investigation by the Greenville, South Carolina police department has concluded. The police department announced the findings on Wednesday.

## Key facts
- The Greenville Police Department has officially closed its investigation into Hayden Panettiere's death.
- The death has been ruled accidental based on the Medical Examiner's determination and the findings of the investigation.
- The police department confirmed the conclusion of the investigation in a statement to media outlets.
