Miley Cyrus performed her first 'Bass Persuades' concert at the Hollywood Bowl. During the show, she expressed feeling drained after a lengthy trek back to the main stage.

## Key facts
- Miley Cyrus descended from a B-stage in the stands at the Hollywood Bowl.
- She appeared visibly sapped after a 200-foot trek back to the main stage.
- Cyrus acknowledged the audience's energy, stating they gave her energy but also took some away.
