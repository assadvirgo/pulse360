Pathé has unveiled a 4K restoration of Sofia Coppola's film "Marie Antoinette" along with three works by Louis Daquin. This announcement coincides with the honoring of Jérôme Seydoux and Sophie Seydoux at the Lumière Film Festival's International Classic Film Market.

## Key facts
- Pathé has restored Sofia Coppola's "Marie Antoinette" in 4K.
- The restoration also includes three works by Louis Daquin.
- Jérôme Seydoux and Sophie Seydoux are being honored at the Lumière Film Festival's International Classic Film Market.
