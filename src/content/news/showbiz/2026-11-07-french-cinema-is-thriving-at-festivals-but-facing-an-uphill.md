French cinema is experiencing success at film festivals and in awards competitions, but it faces challenges in U.S. theaters. Distributors are finding it increasingly difficult to attract American audiences to purchase tickets for these films.

## Key facts
- French cinema is thriving on the festival circuit and in the awards race.
- There are challenges in getting French films into U.S. theaters.
- Persuading American audiences to buy tickets for French films has become more difficult.
