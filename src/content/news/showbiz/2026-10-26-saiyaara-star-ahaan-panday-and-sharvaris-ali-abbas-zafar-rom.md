Ali Abbas Zafar's romantic action-drama featuring Ahaan Panday and Sharvari has been titled "Beparwah," as announced by Yash Raj Films. The film is set to be released in theaters worldwide on March 26, 2027.

- The title "Beparwah" was revealed by Yash Raj Films.
