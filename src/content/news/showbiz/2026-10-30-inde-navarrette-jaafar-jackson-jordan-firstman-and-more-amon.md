The Santa Barbara International Film Festival has announced the recipients of the 2027 Virtuosos Award, recognizing talent in film. The award highlights individuals whose performances have garnered significant attention in the cinematic landscape.

## Key facts
- The 2027 Virtuosos Award recipients include Carrie Coon, Mariana di Girolamo, Rosalind Eleazar, Jordan Firstman, Anthony Ippolito, Jaafar Jackson, and Inde Navarrette.
- The award acknowledges talent whose work has placed them at the center of the conversation around noteworthy cinematic performances.
