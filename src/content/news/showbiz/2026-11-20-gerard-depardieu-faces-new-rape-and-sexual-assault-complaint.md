French actor Gérard Depardieu is facing a new complaint alleging rape and sexual assault during the filming of Guillaume Nicloux’s “Thalasso” in 2018. The complaint also names Nicloux and writer Michel Houellebecq, who starred alongside Depardieu.

## Key facts
- The complaint was reported by Mediapart.
- The alleged incidents occurred during the filming of “Thalasso” in 2018.
- The complaint names both director Guillaume Nicloux and writer Michel Houellebecq.
