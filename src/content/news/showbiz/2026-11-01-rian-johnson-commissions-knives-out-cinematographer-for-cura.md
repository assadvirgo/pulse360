Rian Johnson has partnered with cinematographer Steve Yedlin to create a new iPhone app called Chromagraph Camera. The app aims to replicate the aesthetic they have developed over nearly 30 years of collaboration.

## Key facts
- Rian Johnson and Steve Yedlin have worked together for nearly 30 years.
- The Chromagraph Camera app is designed to use the same image processing techniques as their films.
- Johnson announced the launch of the app this week.
