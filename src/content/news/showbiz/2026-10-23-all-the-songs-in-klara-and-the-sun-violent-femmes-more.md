Taika Waititi's film "Klara and the Sun," based on Kazuo Ishiguro's book, features Jenna Ortega as the main character, a robotic Artificial Friend named Klara. The story revolves around Klara's desire for a charge or owner to take her from the Friend Store.

## Key facts
- The film is directed by Taika Waititi.
- Jenna Ortega stars as Klara, the robotic Artificial Friend.
- The narrative follows Klara's wish to be claimed from the Friend Store.
