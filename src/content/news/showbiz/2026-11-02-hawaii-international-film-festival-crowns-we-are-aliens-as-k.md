The 46th Hawai’i International Film Festival awarded the Kau Ka Hōkū (Shooting Star) Award to the animated feature "We Are Aliens," directed by Kadowaki Kōhei. The awards were presented at a reception held at the Halekulani Hotel in Waikīkī on November 1.

## Key facts
- "We Are Aliens" is an animated feature directed by Japanese director Kadowaki Kōhei.
- The film won the Kau Ka Hōkū (Shooting Star) Award at the Hawai’i International Film Festival.
- The awards reception took place at the Halekulani Hotel in Waikīkī.
- The event was held on November 1.
