The VFX supervisor for "The Angry Birds Movie 3" discussed the character Red's redesign and the automation of character effects. DNEG Animation had to rebuild much of Red to bring the franchise back to theaters.

## Key facts
- DNEG Animation reconstructed Red's fur, facial rig, and expressions for the film.
- The studio developed a system that automated character-effects work on approximately 75% of the film.
