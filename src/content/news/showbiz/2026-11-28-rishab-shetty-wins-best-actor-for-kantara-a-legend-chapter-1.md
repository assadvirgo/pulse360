Rishab Shetty won the Best Actor award for his performance in "Kantara: A Legend – Chapter 1" at the 57th International Film Festival of India (IFFI). The festival concluded in Goa, where the Portuguese film "Aqui," directed by Tiago Guedes, received the Golden Peacock for Best Film.

## Key facts
- Rishab Shetty won Best Actor for "Kantara: A Legend – Chapter 1."
- Tiago Guedes' film "Aqui" won the Golden Peacock for Best Film.
- The IFFI took place in Goa and concluded on November 28.
