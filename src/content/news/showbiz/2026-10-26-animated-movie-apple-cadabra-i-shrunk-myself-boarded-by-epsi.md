Epsilon Film has acquired worldwide rights to the animated movie "Apple Cadabra – I Shrunk Myself," which is based on the successful German children's book series "Petronella Applesauce." The company plans to launch international sales for the film at the upcoming American Film Market in Los Angeles.

## Key facts
- Epsilon Film has acquired worldwide rights to "Apple Cadabra – I Shrunk Myself."
- The movie is based on the children's book series "Petronella Applesauce."
- Epsilon Film will launch international sales at the American Film Market, scheduled for Nov. 10-15 in Los Angeles.
