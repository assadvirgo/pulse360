Fox Entertainment Studios has signed a first-look development and producing deal with Cottage Industry Entertainment, founded by Lisa Hamilton Daly and Alysia Cotter Thomas. The deal includes a slate of scripted television series and feature films, including an adaptation of the Danielle Steel novel "The Whittiers."

## Key facts
- Fox Entertainment Studios has a new first-look deal with Cottage Industry Entertainment.
- Cottage Industry Entertainment was launched by Lisa Hamilton Daly and Alysia Cotter Thomas.
- The deal involves scripted television series and feature films.
- "The Whittiers," based on a novel by Danielle Steel, is among the projects in development.
