Danny Boyle discussed his filmography and the common themes in his movies while receiving the Deadline Legend Award. He reflected on his belief that he never made the same film twice, highlighting his diverse works across various genres.

## Key facts
- Danny Boyle has directed films such as Shallow Grave, Trainspotting, 127 Hours, The Beach, 28 Days Later, and Slumdog Millionaire.
- Slumdog Millionaire won the Best Picture Oscar in 2009, and Boyle received the Best Director award for it.
