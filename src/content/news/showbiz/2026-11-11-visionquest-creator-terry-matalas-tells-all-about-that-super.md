The creator of 'VisionQuest,' Terry Matalas, discussed the show's finale and a post-credits surprise in an interview with Hollywood Reporter. He also paid tribute to the late Patrick Stewart during the conversation.

## Key facts
- Terry Matalas is the creator of 'VisionQuest.'
- He previously served as the showrunner for 'Picard.'
- Matalas described the finale as part of the WandaVision trilogy.
- He emphasized that "all endings need to touch on the beginning."
