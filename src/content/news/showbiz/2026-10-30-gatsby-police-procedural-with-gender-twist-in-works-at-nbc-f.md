NBC is developing a new series titled "Gatsby," which offers a partially gender-swapped police procedural take on the literary classic "The Great Gatsby." The project comes from writers and executive producers Jake Coburn and Jason Tracey, in collaboration with Universal Television.

## Key facts
- The series is described as a modern-day police procedural.
- "Gatsby" is a fresh spin on the classic novel "The Great Gatsby."
- The project is being developed by NBC and Universal Television.
