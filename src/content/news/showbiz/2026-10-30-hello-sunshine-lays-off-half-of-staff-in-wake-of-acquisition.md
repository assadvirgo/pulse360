Hello Sunshine is laying off half of its staff following its recent acquisition by co-founder Reese Witherspoon and France-based Mediawan. The company, known for producing film and TV titles and maintaining a Witherspoon-branded book club, is letting go of about 30 workers.

## Key facts
- Hello Sunshine is re-acquired by Reese Witherspoon and Mediawan.
- The company is laying off half of its staff.
- Approximately 30 workers are being let go.
- The layoffs were described as not unexpected.
