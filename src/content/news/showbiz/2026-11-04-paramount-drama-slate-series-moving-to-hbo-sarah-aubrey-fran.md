Paramount's acquisition of Warner Bros Discovery into Skydance is leading to changes in its drama slate, with series moving to HBO. The content operations of Paramount+ and HBO/HBO Max are now combined under Casey Bloys.

## Key facts
- Paramount's acquisition of Warner Bros Discovery into Skydance is ongoing.
- The drama slate is being reorganized, with some series moving to HBO.
- Casey Bloys is now Co-Chair and Chief Content Officer of Skydance DTC, overseeing the combined content operations of Paramount+ and HBO/HBO Max.
