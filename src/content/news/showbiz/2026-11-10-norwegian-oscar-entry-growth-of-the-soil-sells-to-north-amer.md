Beta Cinema has secured multiple distribution deals for the Norwegian film "Growth of the Soil," which is the country's entry for best international feature film at the 99th Academy Awards. The film, directed by Hans Petter Moland, premiered in Toronto and had its European premiere at San Sebastián.

## Key facts
- "Growth of the Soil" is Norway's entry for the best international feature film at the 99th Academy Awards.
- Beta Cinema has closed major distribution deals for the film in North America, the U.K., Germany, and Spain.
- The film premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival and won two major awards at the San Sebastián International Film Festival.
