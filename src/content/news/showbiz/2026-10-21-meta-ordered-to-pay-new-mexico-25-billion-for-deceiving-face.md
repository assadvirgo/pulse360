A judge in New Mexico has ordered Meta to pay $25 billion in civil penalties for deceiving Facebook users about privacy. This ruling is part of the state's landmark Cambridge Analytica case against Facebook for violations of New Mexico’s Unfair Practices Act.

## Key facts
- The ruling was made by state District Court Judge Francis J. Mathew.
- The case involved allegations of Meta lying to users about access to their data.
- This penalty is noted as the largest of its kind.
