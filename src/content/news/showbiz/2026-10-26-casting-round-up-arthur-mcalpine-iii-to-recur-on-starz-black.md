Arthur McAlpine III has been cast in a recurring role in the upcoming Black Rodeo drama series from Starz. The series is an eight-episode family drama produced by Kirk A. Moore, Mark Johnson, and Counterpart Studios.

## Key facts
- Arthur McAlpine III is known for his roles in "Watson" and "War Dawgz."
- The series is described as being infused with hip hop swagger.
- Kirk A. Moore serves as the showrunner and executive producer.
- Mark Johnson, an executive producer of "Breaking Bad," is also involved in the project.
- The series is currently untitled.
