Jessica Marie Garcia will star in and produce a new vampire comedy film titled "Randy Mandy and the Curse of the Plaza Theatre." The film is directed by Kristina Arjona and produced by Dawn of the Jerks Pictures, set in Atlanta's historic Plaza Theatre.

## Key facts
- Jessica Marie Garcia will play the role of Mandy Alejandra Pike, a British Latina vampire.
- The film is titled "Randy Mandy and the Curse of the Plaza Theatre."
- The project is produced by Dawn of the Jerks Pictures.
- Kristina Arjona is the director of the film.
- The setting of the film is Atlanta's historic Plaza Theatre.
