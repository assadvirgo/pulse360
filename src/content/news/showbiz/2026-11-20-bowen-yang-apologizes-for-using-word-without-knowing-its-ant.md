Bowen Yang has apologized for using the word "Zio" during a recent episode of Las Culturistas, stating he was unaware of its antisemitic history. His apology comes amid accusations of antisemitism related to his comments about Regina Spektor.

## Key facts
- Bowen Yang issued an apology regarding his use of the word "Zio."
- He stated he was unaware of the antisemitic history of the term.
- The comment was made in reference to Regina Spektor removing a pro-Palestine protester from her concert.
