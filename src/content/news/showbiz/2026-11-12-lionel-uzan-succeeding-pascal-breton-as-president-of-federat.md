Lionel Uzan is succeeding Pascal Breton as the president of Federation Studios, according to Deadline. Montefiore Investments has increased its stake to become the majority shareholder in the company.

## Key facts
- Lionel Uzan is taking over as president of Federation Studios.
- Pascal Breton is stepping back from his role.
- Montefiore Investments has become the majority shareholder of Federation Studios.
- Federation Studios has been seeking a significant cash injection of hundreds of millions of euros for the past couple of years.
