Ivy Lion Productions has secured development capital for IP acquisitions and has changed its management from Zero Gravity to the Gotham Group. Lamar Richardson, who runs Ivy Lion, became the youngest Black lead producer to win a Tony Award at the age of 33 for the Best Revival of Musical for "Ragtime."

## Key facts
- Ivy Lion Productions has secured development capital for IP acquisitions.
- The company switched management from Zero Gravity to the Gotham Group.
- Lamar Richardson is the youngest Black lead producer to win a Tony Award at age 33.
- Richardson won the Tony for Best Revival of Musical for "Ragtime."
