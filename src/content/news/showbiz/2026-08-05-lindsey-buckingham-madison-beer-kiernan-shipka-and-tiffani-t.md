## Variety Announces Presenters for Power of Young Hollywood Event

Variety has unveiled the lineup of presenters for its highly anticipated annual Power of Young Hollywood event, which is set to take place on August 5 in Los Angeles. This year, the event is presented by Disney+, underscoring the collaboration between the entertainment industry and emerging talent.

### Notable Presenters

Among the distinguished presenters are singer-songwriter Madison Beer and legendary musician Lindsey Buckingham. Madison Beer will present the Triple Threat Award to Canadian singer Tate McRae, recognizing her multifaceted talent in music, dance, and acting. Meanwhile, Lindsey Buckingham, best known as the guitarist and vocalist of Fleetwood Mac, will honor Sombr with the Songwriter of the Year award, highlighting the importance of songwriting in the music industry.

### Celebrating Emerging Talent

The Power of Young Hollywood event aims to celebrate the achievements of young artists who are making significant contributions to the entertainment landscape. This year’s awards will also feature Tyriq Withers, who will receive the New Hollywood Award, acknowledging his rising influence in the industry.

### A Platform for Recognition

The event serves as a platform to recognize not only established figures but also the next generation of talent. By spotlighting young artists, Variety aims to inspire and encourage further innovation within the entertainment sector. The Power of Young Hollywood has become a staple in recognizing the contributions of youth in film, television, and music, fostering a sense of community among young creatives.

### Conclusion

As the event approaches, excitement builds around the recognition of these talented individuals. With Madison Beer and Lindsey Buckingham among the presenters, attendees can expect an evening filled with celebration, inspiration, and a focus on the future of entertainment. The Power of Young Hollywood continues to play a vital role in acknowledging the accomplishments of young artists and their impact on the industry.
