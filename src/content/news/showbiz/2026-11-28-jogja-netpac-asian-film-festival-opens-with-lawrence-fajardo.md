The 21st Jogja-Netpac Asian Film Festival (JAFF) commenced on November 28 at the Empire XXI cinema in Yogyakarta, Indonesia. The festival opened with the thriller "Silenced Nights," directed by Filipino filmmaker Lawrence Fajardo.

## Key facts
- The festival's theme for this edition is "Influx."
- The opening ceremony took place at the Empire XXI cinema in Yogyakarta, Indonesia.
- The event began with a gentle drizzle that stopped shortly after the ceremony commenced.
