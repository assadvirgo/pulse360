The newly merged Paramount-Warner Bros. (Skydance) achieved its second consecutive No. 1 weekend at the global box office with the release of DC's "Clayface," which earned $43 million worldwide.

## Key facts
- "Clayface" opened with a worldwide box office total of $43 million.
- This marks the second back-to-back No. 1 weekend win for the Paramount-Warner Bros. merger.
- The success raises questions about the potential for a continued winning streak for Skydance.
