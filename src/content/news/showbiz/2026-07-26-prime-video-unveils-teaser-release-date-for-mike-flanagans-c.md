## Prime Video Announces Release Date for Mike Flanagan’s ‘Carrie’ Series

In a much-anticipated reveal, Prime Video has officially announced the release date for its upcoming series adaptation of Stephen King's iconic novel, ‘Carrie.’ During the ‘Carrie’ panel at San Diego Comic-Con, showrunner Mike Flanagan confirmed that all eight episodes of the series will premiere on October 7, 2023.

## A New Take on a Classic

Stephen King's ‘Carrie’ has long been a staple of horror literature, first published in 1974 and later adapted into a successful film in 1976, directed by Brian De Palma. The story revolves around a high school girl, Carrie White, who discovers she possesses telekinetic powers, leading to a dramatic and tragic climax at her prom. Flanagan’s adaptation is expected to explore the psychological and supernatural elements of the narrative while offering a fresh perspective on the characters and themes that have resonated with audiences for decades.

## Mike Flanagan's Vision

Mike Flanagan is known for his work on popular horror series such as ‘The Haunting of Hill House’ and ‘Midnight Mass,’ both of which have garnered critical acclaim for their storytelling and character development. Flanagan has expressed a deep appreciation for King's work and aims to honor the source material while also providing a contemporary interpretation that speaks to today’s viewers.

In an interview, Flanagan mentioned, “Carrie is a story about the pain of adolescence, the struggle for acceptance, and the consequences of bullying. We want to delve into these themes in a way that resonates with a new generation.” His commitment to character-driven narratives suggests that this adaptation will not only focus on horror elements but also on the emotional journeys of its characters.

## Anticipation Builds

The announcement at Comic-Con has generated significant excitement among fans of both King’s work and Flanagan’s previous projects. The combination of a beloved story and a talented director has set high expectations for the series. Prime Video has also released a teaser, which, while brief, hints at the atmospheric tension and emotional depth that viewers can expect.

As the release date approaches, fans are eagerly awaiting additional promotional material and insights into the casting and production details. The series is expected to attract both long-time fans of the original story and new viewers who are discovering the tale for the first time.

## Conclusion

With its premiere set for October 7, 2023, Prime Video's ‘Carrie’ series stands to be a significant addition to the realm of horror television. Mike Flanagan's adaptation promises to blend the psychological intricacies of adolescence with the supernatural elements that have made Stephen King's work enduringly popular. As anticipation builds, viewers will be keen to see how this new interpretation unfolds, potentially setting a new standard for adaptations of classic literature in the horror genre.
