Joseph Mazzello will star in the supernatural thriller "A Grim Life," which is set in Los Angeles' Cecil Hotel. The film has been picked up for international sales by Hong Kong's Cappu Films at the Tokyo International Film Festival's TIFFCOM market.

## Key facts
- Joseph Mazzello leads the cast of "A Grim Life."
- The film is a supernatural thriller set in the Cecil Hotel in Los Angeles.
- Cappu Films from Hong Kong has acquired international sales rights.
- The announcement was made at the Tokyo International Film Festival's TIFFCOM market.
- Francesco Lucente is the writer and director of the film.
