French filmmaker Julie Bertuccelli discussed her experiences at the IFFI Masterclass, revealing that her adaptation of Henning Mankell's novel "Italian Shoes" was taken from her by a Canadian producer. She also announced plans to shoot her next feature film in India.

## Key facts
- Julie Bertuccelli is a French filmmaker known for her film "The Tree."
- She stated that her adaptation of "Italian Shoes" fell apart after a Canadian producer took the project from her.
- Bertuccelli plans to shoot her next feature film in India.
