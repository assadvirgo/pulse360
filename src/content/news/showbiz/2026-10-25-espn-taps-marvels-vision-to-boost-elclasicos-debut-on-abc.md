ESPN is utilizing Marvel's character The Vision to enhance the broadcast of ElClásico on ABC. This collaboration coincides with the promotion of The Vision's new streaming series on Disney+.

## Key facts
- The Vision is a Marvel superhero.
- The character is being used to promote the telecast of ElClásico.
- ElClásico features a soccer match between Barcelona and Real Madrid.
- The event is scheduled for Sunday.
