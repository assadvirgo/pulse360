Barbara Cane, BMI’s Vice President of Worldwide Creative, has announced her retirement after a five-decade career with the company. She will leave her current role at the end of this year but will continue to work in an advisory capacity with CEO Mike O’Neill.

## Key facts
- Barbara Cane has been with BMI for five decades.
- She will retire from her role at the end of this year.
- Cane will serve in an advisory capacity after her retirement.
