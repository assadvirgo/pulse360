Adam Driver has voiced his disappointment with Disney's decision to cancel his "Star Wars" sequel movie, "The Hunt for Ben Solo." He had been developing the project with director Steven Soderbergh for two-and-a-half years.

## Key facts
- Adam Driver expressed dissatisfaction with Disney's decision to kill "The Hunt for Ben Solo."
- The project was in development for two-and-a-half years with director Steven Soderbergh.
- Driver made these comments during an interview while promoting his film "Paper Tiger."
