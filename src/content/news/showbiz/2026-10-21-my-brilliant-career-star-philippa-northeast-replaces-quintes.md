Philippa Northeast will replace Quintessa Swindell in the upcoming Netflix series "Feral," according to Variety. The series is based on Shirley Conran’s 1987 novel "Savages," which follows four women who must learn to survive in the jungle after a work trip goes awry.

## Key facts
- Quintessa Swindell has exited the Netflix series "Feral."
- Philippa Northeast will take over Swindell's role.
- "Feral" is based on the novel "Savages" by Shirley Conran.
- The story involves four women navigating survival in the jungle.
