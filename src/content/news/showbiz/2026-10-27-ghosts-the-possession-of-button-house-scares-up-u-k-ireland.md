Lionsgate U.K.'s horror-comedy "Ghosts: The Possession of Button House" topped the U.K. and Ireland box office during its opening weekend, earning £2.4 million ($3.2 million). The film outperformed Warner Bros.' DC horror thriller "Clayface," which opened in second place with $2.6 million.

## Key facts
- "Ghosts: The Possession of Button House" debuted at the top of the U.K. and Ireland box office.
- The film collected £2.4 million ($3.2 million) in its opening weekend.
- Warner Bros.' "Clayface" opened in second position with $2.6 million.
- Amazon MGM Studios' "Ali G: Who Iz I?" secured third place.
