Paul Greengrass revealed that he was offered the chance to direct a James Bond film after his work on "Bourne Supremacy." He made this statement during an appearance on The Rest Is Classified podcast. Greengrass is known for directing several films in the Jason Bourne series.

## Key facts
- Paul Greengrass directed "Bourne Supremacy."
- He was offered the opportunity to direct a James Bond film after this project.
- Greengrass discussed this offer on The Rest Is Classified podcast.
