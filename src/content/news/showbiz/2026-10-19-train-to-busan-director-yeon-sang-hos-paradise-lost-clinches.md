"Paradise Lost," a new psychological thriller by director Yeon Sang-ho, has secured sales in 118 countries. The film is set to have its U.S. premiere at the 46th Hawai‘i International Film Festival, where it will be featured in the Spotlight on Korea section on November 1 and 4.

## Key facts
- "Paradise Lost" is directed by Yeon Sang-ho, known for "Train to Busan."
- The film has been sold to 118 countries.
- It will premiere in the U.S. at the Hawai‘i International Film Festival.
- The film will be showcased in the Spotlight on Korea section.
