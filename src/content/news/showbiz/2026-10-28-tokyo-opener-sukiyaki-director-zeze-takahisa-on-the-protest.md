The 39th Tokyo International Film Festival opened with Zeze Takahisa's film "Sukiyaki," which explores the global impact of the song that became a hit in 1963. The film examines the song's journey and its significance beyond just the music.

## Key facts
- "Sukiyaki" is the opening film of the 39th Tokyo International Film Festival.
- The film tells the story of how the title tune became a worldwide hit in 1963.
- The song has been recorded in foreign-language versions that have also topped charts.
- The film delves into themes beyond the song itself.
