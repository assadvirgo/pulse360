A new TV series centered on the character Poison Ivy is in development at HBO, with Lauren LeFranc, the showrunner of "The Penguin," attached as a producer. This project is confirmed to be separate from Matt Reeves' Batman universe.

## Key facts
- A Poison Ivy TV show is in development at HBO.
- Lauren LeFranc, known for her work on "The Penguin," is producing the series.
- The series will not be part of Matt Reeves' Batman universe, according to reports.
