Katseye has announced the return of Sophia Laforteza to the group after a brief hiatus. Members of the group expressed their excitement and support for Sophia during the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show.

## Key facts
- Katseye is welcoming back Sophia Laforteza after her hiatus.
- Group members Lara Raj, Daniela Avanzini, Megan Skiendiel, and Yoonchae Jeung showed their support for Sophia.
- Raj stated that the group is happy about Sophia's return.
