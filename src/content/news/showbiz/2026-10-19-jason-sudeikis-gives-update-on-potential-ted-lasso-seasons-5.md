Jason Sudeikis discussed the potential for additional seasons of the series "Ted Lasso." He initially believed the show would conclude with Season 3 but has since expressed interest in continuing with Seasons 5 and 6.

## Key facts
- Jason Sudeikis is a co-creator of "Ted Lasso."
- He previously thought the series would end after Season 3.
- Sudeikis indicated he "would like to" pursue more seasons during a podcast appearance.
