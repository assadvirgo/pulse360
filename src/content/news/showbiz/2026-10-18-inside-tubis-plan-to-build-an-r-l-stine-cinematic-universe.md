title: Inside Tubi’s Plan to Build an R.L. Stine Cinematic Universe

Tubi is planning to develop a cinematic universe based on the works of children's horror author R.L. Stine. The initiative involves filmmaker Jem Garrard and Tubi's VP of Original Scripted Content, Josh Van Houdt, focusing on projects such as 'Pumpkinhead' and its sequel, as well as 'Afraid of Clowns' and 'The Babysitter.'

## Key facts
- Tubi is collaborating with R.L. Stine and filmmaker Jem Garrard.
- The projects include 'Pumpkinhead' and its sequel.
- Future projects also include 'Afraid of Clowns' and 'The Babysitter.'
