The Tokyo Broadcasting System (TBS) Group presented its initiatives at TIFFCOM, the content market of the Tokyo International Film Festival, aimed at promoting Japanese content globally. The session focused on TBS's efforts to cultivate international interest in Japanese media, positioning it as a potential counterpart to K-dramas.

## Key facts
- TBS highlighted its strategies to enhance the global presence of Japanese content.
- The session took place during TIFFCOM, part of the Tokyo International Film Festival.
- TBS aims to inspire global affection for Japan through its content initiatives.
