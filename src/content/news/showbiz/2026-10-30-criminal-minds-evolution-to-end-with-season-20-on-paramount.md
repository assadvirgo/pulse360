The series "Criminal Minds: Evolution" will conclude with its upcoming 20th season on Paramount+. This decision marks the first official cancellation for the new leadership team at Paramount+.

## Key facts
- "Criminal Minds: Evolution" is ending its run on Paramount+.
- The 20th season will be the series' last on the platform.
- The show has been titled "Criminal Minds: Evolution" since its move to the streamer in 2022, according to Hollywood Reporter.
