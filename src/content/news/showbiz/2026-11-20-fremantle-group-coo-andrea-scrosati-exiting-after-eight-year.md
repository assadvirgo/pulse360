Andrea Scrosati is set to leave Fremantle Group after eight years to pursue a new entrepreneurial venture. He will continue in his roles as COO and CEO of Continental Europe until the end of January 2027.

## Key facts
- Andrea Scrosati is exiting Fremantle Group after eight years.
- He will remain in his position until the end of January 2027.
- Scrosati oversaw Fremantle's international expansion into feature films and TV drama. 
- His tenure included the acquisition of Element Pictures and Lux Vide.
