Renate Reinsve discussed her experience working on Cristian Mungiu’s film "Fjord," which won an award at the Cannes Film Festival. She expressed that she was aware she was entering a "completely different world" with this project.

## Key facts
- Renate Reinsve won the best actress trophy at the Cannes Film Festival in 2021 for her role in Joachim Trier’s "The Worst Person in the World."
- "Fjord" is directed by Cristian Mungiu, who is known for his work at the Cannes Film Festival.
- Reinsve has previously collaborated with Trier on other projects.
