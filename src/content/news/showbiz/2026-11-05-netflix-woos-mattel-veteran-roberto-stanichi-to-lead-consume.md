Netflix has hired Roberto Stanichi as its vice president of consumer products and experiences. Stanichi, who has nearly 20 years of experience at Mattel, will oversee Netflix's global consumer products and licensing business as well as its global experiences division.

## Key facts
- Roberto Stanichi was previously the chief marketing and brand officer at Mattel.
- His hiring is part of Netflix's strategy to enhance its off-screen presence.
- Stanichi will manage Netflix's global consumer products and licensing business, including Netflix House activations.
