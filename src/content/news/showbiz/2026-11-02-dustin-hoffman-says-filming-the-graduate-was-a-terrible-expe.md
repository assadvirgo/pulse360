Dustin Hoffman has described his experience filming "The Graduate" as a "terrible experience." In a recent essay for The New Yorker, he revealed that director Mike Nichols instructed Katharine Ross to slap him 27 times for a scene that ultimately was not used in the film.

## Key facts
- Dustin Hoffman starred as Ben Braddock in "The Graduate."
- Hoffman described the filming experience as "terrible."
- The film was released in 1967 and significantly impacted Hoffman's career.
- Director Mike Nichols directed the film.
- Katharine Ross was involved in a scene where she slapped Hoffman multiple times.
