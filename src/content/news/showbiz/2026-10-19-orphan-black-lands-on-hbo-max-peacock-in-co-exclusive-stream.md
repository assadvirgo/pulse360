Boat Rocker Studios has secured new U.S. streaming deals for the series "Orphan Black." The show is now available to stream on both HBO Max and Peacock.

## Key facts
- "Orphan Black" is a critically acclaimed sci-fi drama series.
- All five seasons of "Orphan Black" are available on HBO Max and Peacock.
- The series stars Emmy-winning Tatiana Maslany as Sarah Manning.
