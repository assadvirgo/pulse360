Mahershala Ali discussed his feelings about his two Oscars, stating he felt a sense of accomplishment but not fulfillment. He also spoke about his experiences in London, including his roles and the impact of police brutality.

## Key facts
- Mahershala Ali has two Oscars, which he placed in a cabinet.
- He expressed a sense of accomplishment regarding his awards.
- Ali discussed his experiences playing characters who "haven’t been seen."
- He mentioned nearly going crazy while working on 'True Detective.'
- Ali reflected on how 'Green Book' made him feel like a "golden boy," followed by vitriol.
