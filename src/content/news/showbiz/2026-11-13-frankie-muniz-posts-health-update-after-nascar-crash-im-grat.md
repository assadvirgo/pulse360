Frankie Muniz provided a health update following a NASCAR crash that occurred during a warmup session. He expressed gratitude for being able to walk after what he described as "a pretty bad wreck."

## Key facts
- Frankie Muniz was involved in a crash during a NASCAR warmup.
- He was driving for Team Air Force in its No. 30 Ford.
- The incident took place while he was preparing for the Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic exhibition.
- Muniz was sent to the hospital following the crash.
