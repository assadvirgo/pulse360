Sergei Loznitsa's film "Imperium" has won the Best Film Award at the 70th BFI London Film Festival. The award was announced on the final day of the festival, which concluded recently.

## Key facts
- "Imperium" revisits the Soviet Union of the early 1970s using footage shot by Italian cameramen, according to Variety.
- The competition jury for the festival was led by filmmaker Sam Pollard, as reported by Deadline.
- The winners were unveiled on the last day of the London Film Festival, according to Deadline.
