The Hollywood Reporter has won a Daytime Emmy Award for its 'Off Script' roundtable series, marking its third consecutive nomination. The series has received a total of six nominations over the past nine years.

## Key facts
- The 'Off Script' roundtable series has been nominated three times consecutively.
- The series has received a total of six nominations in nine years.
- The 53rd annual Daytime Emmy Awards will take place on October 30.
- The awards will be available only via the National Academy of Television Arts & Sciences' YouTube page.
