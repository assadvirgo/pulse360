Sacha Baron Cohen's film "Ali G: Who Iz I?" features the character Ali G as the antihero in an absurdist narrative. The film is compared to other "Saturday Night Live" movies, but it has been described as dawdling and cumbersome, with fewer relevant moments than expected.

## Key facts
- "Who Iz I?" presents Ali G as the doofus antihero of his own story.
- The film is likened to "Saturday Night Live" movies such as "MacGruber" and "A Night at the Roxbury."
- It contains scattered funny moments but is criticized for being less relevant than it believes.
