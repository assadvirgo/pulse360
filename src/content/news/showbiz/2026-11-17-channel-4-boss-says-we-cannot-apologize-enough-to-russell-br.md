The CEO of Channel 4 expressed deep regret regarding the handling of complaints made by a woman about Russell Brand. Priya Dogra stated that the broadcaster did not address the complaints properly, which the woman, referred to as Bella, found to be "almost unforgivable."

## Key facts
- Channel 4's CEO, Priya Dogra, stated, "we cannot apologize enough" to the woman who made complaints about Russell Brand.
- The woman, known as Bella, felt her complaints were not dealt with properly by the broadcaster.
- Dogra described the situation as "almost unforgivable."
