My Chemical Romance has achieved its first-ever No. 1 on the Billboard 200 chart with "The Black Parade," which has risen from No. 139 to the top position. This milestone comes 20 years after the album's original release, following a deluxe reissue.

## Key facts
- My Chemical Romance's "The Black Parade" has reached No. 1 on the Billboard 200 chart.
- The album surged from No. 139 to the top of the chart.
- The achievement follows a deluxe reissue of "The Black Parade."
- The album garnered 78,000 equivalent album units.
