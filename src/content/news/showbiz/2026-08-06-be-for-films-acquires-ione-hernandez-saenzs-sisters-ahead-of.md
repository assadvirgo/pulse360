## Be For Films Acquires International Rights to Ione Hernández Sáenz’s ‘Sisters’

Brussels-based Be For Films has announced the acquisition of international sales rights to the highly anticipated debut feature film “Sisters” (Hermanas) by Spanish director Ione Hernández Sáenz. This acquisition comes just ahead of the film's premiere at the Toronto International Film Festival, where it will be showcased in the prestigious Discovery Section.

### Overview of ‘Sisters’

“Sisters” tells the poignant story of two sisters, Chelo and Marta, whose lives are poised for transformation due to an unexpected event during a pivotal summer. The film is expected to resonate with audiences through its exploration of familial bonds, personal growth, and the complexities of sisterhood. 

Hernández Sáenz, who is making her feature debut with this project, has garnered attention for her unique storytelling style and ability to capture the emotional nuances of her characters. The film's narrative promises to delve into the dynamics of sisterly relationships, highlighting both the challenges and joys that come with them.

### Significance of the Acquisition

The acquisition by Be For Films is a strategic move that underscores the growing interest in international cinema, particularly from emerging filmmakers. The company, known for its commitment to promoting diverse and innovative films, aims to leverage its global distribution network to bring “Sisters” to audiences worldwide.

The Toronto International Film Festival is renowned for showcasing new talent and providing a platform for films that may not yet have widespread recognition. By premiering in the Discovery Section, “Sisters” will have the opportunity to reach a wide array of industry professionals, critics, and film enthusiasts, potentially paving the way for future success.

### Implications for Ione Hernández Sáenz

For Ione Hernández Sáenz, the acquisition marks a significant milestone in her career as a filmmaker. The recognition from Be For Films not only validates her work but also enhances her visibility in the international film community. As she steps into the spotlight with her debut feature, the anticipation surrounding “Sisters” may open doors for future projects and collaborations.

### Conclusion

As the film gears up for its premiere at the Toronto International Film Festival, the acquisition by Be For Films positions “Sisters” as a noteworthy entry in this year’s lineup. With its compelling narrative and the promise of a fresh perspective from Hernández Sáenz, the film is poised to capture the hearts of audiences and critics alike. The coming weeks will reveal how “Sisters” performs in the festival circuit and its potential impact on the global stage of cinema.
