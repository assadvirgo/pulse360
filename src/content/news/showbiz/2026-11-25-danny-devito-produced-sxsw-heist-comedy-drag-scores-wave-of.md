The heist comedy "Drag," produced by Danny DeVito and starring Lizzy Caplan and John Stamos, has secured multiple international sales following the American Film Market.

## Key facts
- "Drag" is a SXSW heist comedy.
- The film is produced by Danny DeVito.
- Blue Finch Films, a U.K. sales and distribution company, is handling the title.
- International deals have been closed in Germany with Indeed Film, in France with VOD Factory, and in Spain with A Contracorriente.
