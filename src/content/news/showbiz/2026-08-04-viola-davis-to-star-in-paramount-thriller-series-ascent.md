## Viola Davis to Star in Paramount+ Thriller Series ‘Ascent’

Viola Davis, the acclaimed actress and producer, is set to take on a leading role in the upcoming thriller series "Ascent," which has been officially greenlit by Paramount+. This announcement marks an exciting addition to Davis's already impressive portfolio, as she steps into the character of Catriona Vail, an elite corporate fixer navigating the treacherous waters of global corporate crises and damage control.

### A New Role for a Versatile Actress

Davis, who has received numerous accolades for her performances in film, television, and theater, will portray a character that embodies both intelligence and tenacity. Catriona Vail is described as a corporate fixer whose skills are both ruthless and effective, suggesting that the series will delve into the morally complex world of corporate maneuvering. The character's ability to navigate crises is expected to provide a thrilling narrative that explores the lengths to which individuals will go to protect their interests and those of their clients.

### The Premise of ‘Ascent’

The official logline for "Ascent" hints at a gripping storyline that will likely resonate with contemporary audiences. Set against the backdrop of corporate power struggles, the series will explore themes of ethics, loyalty, and the consequences of ambition. As global corporations face increasing scrutiny and challenges, the show aims to reflect the real-world complexities of corporate governance and the often murky waters of corporate responsibility.

### Production and Creative Team

While specific details regarding the production team have yet to be disclosed, the involvement of Davis as both a star and a producer suggests that the series will benefit from her extensive experience in the industry. Davis has a reputation for choosing projects that challenge societal norms and provoke thought, which aligns well with the themes suggested by the premise of "Ascent."

### The Growing Trend of Corporate Thrillers

The announcement of "Ascent" comes at a time when corporate thrillers are gaining traction in popular media. With audiences increasingly fascinated by the inner workings of powerful corporations and the ethical dilemmas they face, the series is poised to tap into this interest. Shows and films that explore corporate intrigue often draw viewers in with their suspenseful plots and complex characters, making "Ascent" a timely addition to the genre.

As Viola Davis prepares to bring Catriona Vail to life, anticipation builds for "Ascent."
