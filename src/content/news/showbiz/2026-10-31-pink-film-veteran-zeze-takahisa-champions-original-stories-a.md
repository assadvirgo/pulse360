The Tokyo International Film Festival hosted a masterclass featuring director Zeze Takahisa, known for his film "Sukiyaki." The event took place at Tokyo Midtown Hibiya and attracted a full audience.

## Key facts
- The masterclass was part of the festival's Tokyo Talk program.
- Zeze Takahisa discussed his early "pink" films and indie projects.
- The event included a question-and-answer session about his recent work.
