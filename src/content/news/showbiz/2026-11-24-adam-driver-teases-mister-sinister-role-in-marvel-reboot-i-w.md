Adam Driver is set to join the Marvel Cinematic Universe in the upcoming X-Men reboot. He will portray the villain Mister Sinister, as confirmed during his appearance on Late Night with Seth Meyers.

## Key facts
- Adam Driver is joining the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
- He will play the character Mister Sinister in the X-Men reboot.
- The reboot is directed by Jake Schreier.
- Driver expressed that he was a big fan of X-Men before taking on the role.
