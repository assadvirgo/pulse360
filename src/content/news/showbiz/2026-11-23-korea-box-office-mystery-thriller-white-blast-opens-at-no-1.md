Showbox's mystery thriller "White Blast" debuted at No. 1 in the South Korean box office over the weekend of November 20-22, ending the five-week reign of the Japanese anime "Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island," according to Variety.

## Key facts
- "White Blast" is a mystery thriller released by Showbox.
- The film opened at the top of the South Korean box office.
- It ended the five-week chart-topping run of "Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island."
- The data was provided by KOBIS, operated by the Korean Film Council.
