The documentary "You Can See Everything," directed by Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, has begun its Oscar campaign for several categories, including Best Picture and Supporting Actress. The film follows the founder of Theranos during the 34 days leading up to her imprisonment and continues beyond that period. 

## Key facts
- "You Can See Everything" is currently playing in theaters.
- The film is produced by A24.
- It documents the final days of the Theranos founder before her imprisonment.
