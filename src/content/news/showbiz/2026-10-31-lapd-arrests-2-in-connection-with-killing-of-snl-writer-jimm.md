The LAPD has arrested two individuals in connection with the murder of Christina Downer, the sister of "Saturday Night Live" writer Jimmy Fowlie. The arrests occurred nearly a year after Downer went missing.

## Key facts
- Delorean Bijon Brandon, 41, and Kaylee Nicole Markowski, 34, were taken into custody on Thursday.
- Brandon is believed to be Downer's boyfriend.
- The arrests took place in Highland Park and Tarzana, California.
- Detectives suspect that both Brandon and Markowski were involved in Downer's murder.
