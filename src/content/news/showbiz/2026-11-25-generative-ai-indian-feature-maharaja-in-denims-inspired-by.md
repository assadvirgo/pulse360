A feature-length generative AI film titled "Maharaja in Denims," inspired by the life of 19th-century Sikh ruler Maharaja Ranjit Singh, is nearing completion. The film is based on a novel by author Khushwant Singh and is expected to be released by April 2027.

## Key facts
- "Maharaja in Denims" is based on a novel by Khushwant Singh.
- The film depicts a young man reliving the life of Maharaja Ranjit Singh.
- The production team reportedly had to create the film three times.
- The planned release date for the film is April 2027.
