Prime Video has announced the release date for Season 3 of "Beast Games." The new season, titled "Beast Games: World Champions," will premiere on December 16.

## Key facts
- Season 3 of "Beast Games" is titled "Beast Games: World Champions."
- The premiere date is set for December 16.
- The season will launch with three episodes.
- Following the premiere, two additional episodes will be released weekly.
- The season finale is scheduled for January 13.
