As production begins on the highly anticipated Netflix series "This Summer Will Be Different," based on Carley Fortune's novel, the streaming giant has announced the addition of six new actors to its cast. ### New Cast Members Introduced

The newly announced cast members include Jordan Rodrigues, who will portray the character Miles Lam, and Jude Wilson, among others.
