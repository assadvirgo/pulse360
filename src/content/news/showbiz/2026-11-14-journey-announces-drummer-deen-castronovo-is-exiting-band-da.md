Journey has announced that drummer Deen Castronovo is leaving the band due to health issues. This decision follows two instances in which he had to exit mid-concert in just over a month, with the most recent occurrence happening during a show in Boston.

## Key facts
- Deen Castronovo is exiting Journey due to health issues.
- He had to leave two shows mid-concert in the past month.
- The latest incident occurred during a performance in Boston.
- Castronovo was replaced by his drum tech during these shows.
