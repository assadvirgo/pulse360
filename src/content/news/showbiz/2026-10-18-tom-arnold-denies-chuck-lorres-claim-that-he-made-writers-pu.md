Tom Arnold has denied a claim made by Chuck Lorre in his memoir regarding the sitcom "Roseanne." Lorre alleged that writers were instructed to put Arnold's name on scripts to suggest he had written them.

## Key facts
- Chuck Lorre's memoir includes a claim that writers on "Roseanne" were told to attribute scripts to Tom Arnold.
- Tom Arnold responded to Lorre's claim, stating, "Never."
- The controversy arises as Lorre's memoir continues to circulate.
