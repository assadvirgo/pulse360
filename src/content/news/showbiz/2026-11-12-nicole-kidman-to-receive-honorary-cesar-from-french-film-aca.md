title: Nicole Kidman to Receive Honorary Cesar From French Film Academy

Nicole Kidman will receive an honorary Cesar at France's Cesar Awards. The presentation is scheduled for the 52nd ceremony on February 25, 2027, at the Olympia in Paris, as announced by the Académie des Arts et Techniques du Cinéma and broadcaster Canal+.

## Key facts
- Nicole Kidman will receive an honorary Cesar.
- The award presentation will take place at the 52nd Cesar Awards.
- The ceremony is set for February 25, 2027.
- The event will be held at the Olympia in Paris.
- The announcement was made by the French film academy and Canal+.
