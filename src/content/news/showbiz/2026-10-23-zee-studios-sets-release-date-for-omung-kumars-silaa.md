Zee Studios has announced the release date for Omung Kumar's film 'Silaa'. The movie, featuring Harshvardhan Rane and Sadia Khateeb, is set to launch on February 5, 2027.

## Key facts
- 'Silaa' is directed by Omung Kumar.
- The film stars Harshvardhan Rane and Sadia Khateeb.
- It is described as a story that combines intense romance, gripping mystery, and high-octane action.
- The film is set against the landscapes of Kashmir and Vietnam.
