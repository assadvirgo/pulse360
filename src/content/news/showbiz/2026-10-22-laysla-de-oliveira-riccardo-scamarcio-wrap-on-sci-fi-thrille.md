Laysla De Oliveira and Riccardo Scamarcio have completed filming on the indie sci-fi thriller "Telephone," directed by Anthony Scott Burns. The project was shot in Sudbury, Ontario, and features Gale Anne Hurd as one of the executive producers.

## Key facts
- Laysla De Oliveira stars in "Telephone," alongside Riccardo Scamarcio.
- The film is directed by Anthony Scott Burns.
- It was filmed in Sudbury, Ontario.
- Gale Anne Hurd is among the executive producers of the film.
