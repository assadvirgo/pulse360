Taika Waititi has responded to negative reviews of his film "Klara and the Sun," calling critics "sad lost souls." He defended the film on social media, noting its audience score on Rotten Tomatoes.

## Key facts
- "Klara and the Sun" is set in a dystopian future and stars Jenna Ortega and Amy Adams, according to Hollywood Reporter.
- Waititi described critics as "irrelevant" and stated he hasn't read a review in 15 years, according to Variety.
- The film has a Rotten Tomatoes audience score of 78%, while the critic score is 28%, marking Waititi's lowest yet.
