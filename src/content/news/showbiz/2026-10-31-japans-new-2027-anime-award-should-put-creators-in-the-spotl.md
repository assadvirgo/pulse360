A new anime award is set to be introduced in Japan in 2027, aimed at highlighting the contributions of creators in the anime industry. Industry leaders discussed this initiative at TIFFCOM, part of the Tokyo International Film Festival.

## Key facts
- The new award is planned for 2027.
- Industry leaders believe the award will spotlight anime creators.
- The discussion took place at TIFFCOM, the market of the Tokyo International Film Festival.
- The session was moderated by Fujitsu Ryota, a programming advisor for the festival’s animation section.
