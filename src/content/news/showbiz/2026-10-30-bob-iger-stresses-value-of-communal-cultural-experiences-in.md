Bob Iger was honored at the PAC NYC Icons of Culture Gala, where he emphasized the importance of communal cultural experiences in a world characterized by disruption and polarization. The event featured performances and appearances by various celebrities.

## Key facts
- Bob Iger was celebrated at the PAC NYC Icons of Culture Gala.
- The event included performances by Benj Pasek and Justin Paul, who performed a new song from the 'Greatest Showman' stage show.
- Celebrities in attendance included Mickey Mouse, Nick Jonas, Ariana DeBose, and ABC News stars Robin Roberts and David Muir.
