In nearly a century of Oscars, no documentary has been nominated for Best Picture, according to Variety. The outlet argues that while documentaries can win their own Oscars and significantly influence cultural discussions, they have been consistently excluded from the top category.

## Key facts
- No documentary has been nominated for Best Picture in nearly a century of the Oscars.
- Documentaries can win their own Oscars and reshape cultural conversations.
- The exclusion of nonfiction feature films from the top category is highlighted as an issue.
