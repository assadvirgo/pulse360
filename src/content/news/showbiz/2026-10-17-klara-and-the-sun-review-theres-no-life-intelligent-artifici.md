The review of "Klara and the Sun" by Taika Waititi indicates that the film fails to capture the essence of the complex novel it adapts. The review suggests that the approach of using a child's perspective in a comedic manner does not succeed as it did in Waititi's previous work, "Jojo Rabbit."

## Key facts
- The film is described as a "cockeyed child's-eye comedy."
- "Jojo Rabbit," another film by Waititi, won an Oscar and grossed nearly $100 million.
- The review implies that "Klara and the Sun" does not achieve the same artistic success as "Jojo Rabbit."
