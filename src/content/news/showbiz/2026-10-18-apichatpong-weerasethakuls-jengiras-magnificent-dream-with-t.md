Apichatpong Weerasethakul's film "Jengira's Magnificent Dream" has been joined by the Mexican production company Piano, known for its work on "Triangle of Sadness." Producer Julio Chavezmontes confirmed the collaboration, which includes actress Tilda Swinton.

## Key facts
- Piano is the production and distribution company behind "Triangle of Sadness."
- Julio Chavezmontes confirmed that Piano has boarded "Jengira's Magnificent Dream."
- Tilda Swinton is attached to the project.
- The collaboration aligns with Piano's commitment to international filmmaking.
