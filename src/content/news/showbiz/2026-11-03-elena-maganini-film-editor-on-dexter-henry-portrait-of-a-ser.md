Elena Maganini, a film and television editor known for her work on "Dexter" and "Henry, Portrait of a Serial Killer," has died at the age of 70. She passed away on October 21 in Burbank due to complications from breast cancer.

## Key facts
- Maganini won an Emmy for editing the pilot episode of "Dexter."
- She edited several films for director John McNaughton.
- She was born in Chicago and graduated from the University of Illinois.
- Maganini studied filmmaking at Columbia College.
