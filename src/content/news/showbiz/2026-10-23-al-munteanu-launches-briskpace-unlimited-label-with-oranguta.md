Al Munteanu has launched a new label called BriskPace Unlimited under his German company BriskPace Studios. The label's first original series is titled "The Light and Hard Times of Lucky Briscoe," which is a true-crime comedy about an imprisoned orangutan with political ambitions.

## Key facts
- BriskPace Unlimited is a new-formats label launched by Al Munteanu.
- The label's first series is "The Light and Hard Times of Lucky Briscoe."
- The series is a true-crime comedy about an imprisoned orangutan.
- The project is co-produced with AiMation.
