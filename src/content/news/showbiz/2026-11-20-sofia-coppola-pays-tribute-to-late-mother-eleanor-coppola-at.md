Sofia Coppola paid tribute to her late mother, Eleanor Coppola, at the Paris premiere of the documentary “Making Marie Antoinette.” The documentary, directed by Sofia Coppola, focuses on the making of her daughter’s 2006 film.

## Key facts
- Sofia Coppola was unable to attend the premiere due to illness.
- She sent a note honoring her mother during the event.
- The documentary details the making of the 2006 film "Marie Antoinette," which starred Kirsten Dunst.
