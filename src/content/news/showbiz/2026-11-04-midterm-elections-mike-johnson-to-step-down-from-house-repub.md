House Speaker Mike Johnson will step down from his position as Republican leader as Democrats are set to take control of the House in January. Hakeem Jeffries is expected to become the next speaker after serving as minority leader for four years.

## Key facts
- Mike Johnson will not continue as Republican leader when Democrats take control of the House.
- Hakeem Jeffries is in line to become the next speaker.
- Democrats are projected to have a majority of around 75 seats.
