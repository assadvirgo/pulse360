Bob Buziak, the former president of RCA Records and Sony's TriStar Music Group, has died at the age of 83. His death was confirmed by a representative, but no cause has been announced.

## Key facts
- Bob Buziak died on October 19.
- He was 83 years old at the time of his death.
- Buziak was known for reviving RCA's fortunes in the 1980s with hit albums by various artists.
