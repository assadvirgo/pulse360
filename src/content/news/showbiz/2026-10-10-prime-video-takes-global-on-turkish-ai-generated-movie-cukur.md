## Prime Video Acquires Global Rights to Turkish AI-Generated Film "Çukur: Vartolu"

In a significant move within the entertainment industry, Prime Video has secured the global distribution rights to the Turkish film "Çukur: Vartolu." This film is a spin-off from the acclaimed series "Çukur," which has gained international recognition under the title "The Pit." The project is a product of Ay Yapim, a leading Turkish production company known for its innovative storytelling and high production values.

## The Rise of AI in Film Production

"Çukur: Vartolu" is notable not only for its connection to a successful television series but also for its use of artificial intelligence in its production. The film is produced by Ay Yapim's AI division, AI Yapim, which recently made headlines with the launch of the AI-generated historical drama "Castle Walls." This development marks a notable trend in the film industry, where AI technology is increasingly being utilized to enhance storytelling and streamline production processes.
