Marvel is developing a spy thriller series titled "Zodiac" for Disney+, with Amit Cohen and Ron Leshem set to write the show.

## Key facts
- The series "Zodiac" is in the works at Disney+.
- Amit Cohen and Ron Leshem have been appointed as the writers for the series.
- The announcement of the show was made on October 8.
- Details about the story remain limited, but it will involve a pair of spies chasing targets.
