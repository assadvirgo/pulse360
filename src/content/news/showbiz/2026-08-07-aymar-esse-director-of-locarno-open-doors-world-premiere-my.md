## Aymar Esse's 'My Father’s Kora' to Premiere at Locarno Film Festival

The upcoming film "My Father’s Kora" ("La Kora de Mon Père"), directed by Aymar Esse, is set to make its world premiere on August 9 at the prestigious Locarno Film Festival. This film is part of the festival's Open Doors section, which has been dedicated to showcasing cinematic works from 42 African countries since its inception in 2025.

## A Story of Identity and Exploration

"My Father’s Kora" is a collaborative production involving Benin, Switzerland, and Germany. The narrative centers around Souleymane Everston Adowo Freudenreich, an Afro-German trans man who embarks on a journey to Dakar, Senegal. This film seeks to delve into the complexities of queer identity within the context of Senegal, a country where LGBTQ+ issues remain sensitive and often stigmatized.

Esse's direction aims to highlight the struggles faced by individuals navigating their identities in environments that may not be accepting. By focusing on a character who embodies the intersection of multiple identities—Afro-German and trans—"My Father’s Kora" not only tells a personal story but also serves as a broader commentary on the societal challenges encountered by queer individuals in Africa.

## The Significance of the Locarno Film Festival

The Locarno Film Festival is renowned for its commitment to promoting diverse voices in cinema, particularly from regions that are often underrepresented in mainstream film. The Open Doors section is specifically designed to provide a platform for filmmakers from Africa, fostering dialogue and understanding through storytelling. Esse's film is poised to contribute to this important conversation, offering insights into the lived experiences of marginalized communities.

## Aymar Esse's Vision

Aymar Esse, known for his thoughtful approach to storytelling, has expressed his desire to create a film that resonates with audiences both in Senegal and internationally. He emphasizes the importance of representation in cinema, particularly for queer narratives that challenge societal norms. Esse's work is characterized by a commitment to authenticity, aiming to depict the realities of his characters with sensitivity and depth.

## Conclusion

As "My Father’s Kora" prepares for its debut at the Locarno Film Festival, anticipation builds around its potential to spark conversations about queer identity in Senegal and beyond. The film not only represents a significant artistic endeavor but also serves as a vital exploration of the challenges faced by individuals who navigate the complexities of their identities in a world that often imposes rigid boundaries. Through this premiere, Aymar Esse hopes to inspire empathy and understanding, shedding light on the diverse narratives that shape the human experience.
