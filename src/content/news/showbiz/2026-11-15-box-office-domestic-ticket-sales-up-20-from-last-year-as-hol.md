Domestic ticket sales have increased by 20% compared to last year, marking a significant rebound for movie theater owners. This growth is noted ahead of the releases of a "Hunger Games" prequel, a third "Dune," and another "Avengers" installment.

## Key facts
- Domestic ticket sales are 20% higher than the same point last year.
- Revenues have reached $8.76 billion through November.
- The increase in sales occurs before the release of major upcoming films.
