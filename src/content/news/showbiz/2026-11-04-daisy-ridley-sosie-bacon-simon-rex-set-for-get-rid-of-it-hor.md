Daisy Ridley, Sosie Bacon, and Simon Rex have been cast in the horror film "Get Rid of It," directed by Ry Russo-Young. The screenplay is written by Gemma Holdway, and the film is produced by David Lancaster.

## Key facts
- Ry Russo-Young, known for directing "Before I Fall," will direct "Get Rid of It."
- The film's screenplay is penned by Gemma Holdway.
- Daisy Ridley, Sosie Bacon, and Simon Rex are set to star in the film.
- David Lancaster, an Oscar-nominated producer, is involved in the project.
