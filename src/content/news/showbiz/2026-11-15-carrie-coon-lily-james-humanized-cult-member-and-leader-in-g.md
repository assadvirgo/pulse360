Carrie Coon and Lily James portray characters in the cult thriller "The Liberation," directed by Guy Nattiv. The film is inspired by Nattiv's own grandmother, and Coon plays a character named Rita, who seeks belonging in a cult under the mentorship of Ricki King, played by James.

## Key facts
- Carrie Coon and Lily James star in the film "The Liberation."
- The film is a cult thriller directed by Guy Nattiv.
- Nattiv based the film on his own grandmother.
- Coon's character, Rita, is described as an unhappy woman looking for a sense of belonging.
- Rita finds this sense of belonging under the mentorship of Ricki King, played by James.
