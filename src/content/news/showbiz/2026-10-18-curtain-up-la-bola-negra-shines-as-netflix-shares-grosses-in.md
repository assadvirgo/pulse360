Netflix's Spanish drama "La Bola Negra" debuted with box office grosses of $277.5k across four screens, marking the company's first official box office figures. The film, which won the TIFF audience award, earned $108.3k on Friday and $84.6k on Saturday.

## Key facts
- "La Bola Negra" is a Spanish drama released by Netflix.
- The film grossed $277.5k on its opening weekend.
- It was shown on four screens.
- The film earned $108.3k on Friday and $84.6k on Saturday.
- This marks Netflix's first official box office grosses.
