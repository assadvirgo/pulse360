## Sterlin Harjo Discusses Inspirations Behind Season 2 of ‘The Lowdown’

As the second season of the acclaimed series “The Lowdown” premieres, creator Sterlin Harjo shares insights into the creative process and influences that shaped the latest installment. The series, which follows citizen journalist Lee Raybon, portrayed by Ethan Hawke, delves into the complexities of truth and media in contemporary society.

### A Shift in Lee Raybon's Journey

In the opening episode of Season 2, Lee Raybon finds himself grappling with an unexpected dilemma: success. The character is set to receive a local media award for his investigative work surrounding the death of Dale Washberg, a storyline that was central to the first season. However, rather than bask in the accolades, Raybon is depicted as uncomfortable with the recognition, reflecting a deeper commentary on the nature of journalism and personal integrity.

Harjo emphasizes that this internal conflict is designed to resonate with audiences who may question the values of success in the media landscape. “Lee's journey is not just about uncovering truths; it’s about what those truths mean for him personally,” Harjo explains. This thematic exploration sets the stage for a season that promises to challenge both the protagonist and viewers alike.

### Cinematic Influences

When asked about the films and media that inspired the storytelling in Season 2, Harjo cites a variety of influences ranging from classic cinema to contemporary documentaries. He notes that the raw, unfiltered nature of certain films has shaped his vision for “The Lowdown.” “I’ve always been drawn to stories that feel authentic and grounded,” he remarks. This commitment to realism is evident in the series’ narrative style, which blends elements of drama and dark humor.

Harjo's approach to storytelling also reflects a desire to create a dialogue about the role of media in society today. By drawing on cinematic techniques that emphasize character development and moral ambiguity, he aims to engage viewers in a conversation about the responsibilities of journalists and the impact of their work.

### The Addition of Tommy Lee Jones

A significant highlight of Season 2 is the introduction of veteran actor Tommy Lee Jones, who joins the cast in a pivotal role. Harjo reveals that it was a combination of the script's depth and the character's complexity that convinced Jones to come on board. “Tommy is an icon, and having him in our world adds a layer of gravitas that is hard to replicate,” Harjo states.

The collaboration with Jones not only elevates the series but also aligns with Harjo's vision of assembling a diverse and talented cast. The dynamic between Jones and Hawke is anticipated to bring a new dimension to the narrative, further enriching the exploration of truth and morality in journalism.

### Looking Ahead

As “The Lowdown” continues to unfold, audiences can expect a season filled with thought-provoking narratives and character-driven storytelling. Harjo's commitment to exploring the intricacies of truth-telling in a media-saturated world is poised to resonate with viewers, making this season a compelling watch.

With its blend of humor, drama, and social commentary, “The Lowdown” Season 2 promises to be a significant contribution to the ongoing conversation about the role of journalism in shaping public perception and accountability. As Lee Raybon navigates his new reality, viewers are invited to reflect on their own understanding of truth in an ever-evolving media landscape.
