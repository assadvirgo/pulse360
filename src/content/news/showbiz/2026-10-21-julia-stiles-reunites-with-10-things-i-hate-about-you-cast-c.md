Julia Stiles honored the late Heath Ledger during a recent episode of "Dancing With the Stars." She performed a contemporary routine with her partner Ezra Sosa, dedicating the dance to Ledger, who starred in the 1999 film "10 Things I Hate About You."

## Key facts
- Julia Stiles performed a dance to "Can't Take My Eyes Off You" on "Dancing With the Stars."
- The performance was dedicated to Heath Ledger, who played a significant role in "10 Things I Hate About You."
- Stiles reunited with much of the cast from the film during the episode.
