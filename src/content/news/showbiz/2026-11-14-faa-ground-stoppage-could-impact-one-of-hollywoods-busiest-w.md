The FAA's nationwide ground stoppage has been lifted, but it may still impact events in the entertainment industry. Organizers are assessing the situation as they prepare for one of Hollywood's busiest weekends.

## Key facts
- The FAA's nationwide ground stoppage has been lifted.
- The stoppage occurred just before a busy weekend for the entertainment industry.
- Organizers are evaluating the potential effects on attendee travel to events.
