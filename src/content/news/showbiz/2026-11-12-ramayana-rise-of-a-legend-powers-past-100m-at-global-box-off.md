The film "Ramayana: Rise of a Legend" has surpassed $100 million at the global box office. The movie, directed by Nitesh Tiwari, has generated $67.1 million from India.

## Key facts
- "Ramayana: Rise of a Legend" is a Diwali event movie produced by Prime Focus Studio and Sony.
- The film tells the story of Prince Rama from childhood to his 14-year exile.
- The global box office total for the film has exceeded $100 million.
