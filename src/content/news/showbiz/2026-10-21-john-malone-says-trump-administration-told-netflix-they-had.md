Media mogul John Malone stated that the Trump administration informed Netflix that their proposed acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery would be blocked. Following this information, Netflix dropped out of the bidding, which allowed Paramount to proceed with its purchase.

## Key facts
- John Malone claims the Trump administration told Netflix their merger with Warner Bros. Discovery would not receive regulatory approval.
- As a result of this information, Netflix withdrew from the bidding process.
- Malone made these comments at the Paley Center for Media’s International Council Summit.
