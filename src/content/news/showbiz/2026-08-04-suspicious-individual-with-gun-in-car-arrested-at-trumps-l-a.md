## Suspicious Individual Arrested at Trump’s Golf Club Ahead of Fundraiser

Authorities in Los Angeles County have confirmed the arrest of a man at the Trump National Golf Club in Rancho Palos Verdes, California, just ahead of a scheduled appearance by former President Donald Trump. The individual was reportedly observed taking photographs and videos at the venue, which raised concerns regarding potential security threats.

### Details of the Arrest

The incident occurred over the weekend, with the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department stating that the suspect was seen "appearing to monitor security-planning activities" at the golf club. The man was apprehended on Saturday, a day before Trump is set to speak at a Republican National Committee fundraiser at the same location.

While specific details about the individual have not been disclosed, law enforcement officials have described the situation as concerning, particularly given the high-profile nature of the event and the former president's presence.
