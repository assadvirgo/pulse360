## PiperPlay Takes on Sales for 'The City of the Living'

PiperPlay, a prominent player in the film sales industry, has announced that it has boarded sales for the highly anticipated true crime drama, *The City of the Living* (Italian: *La città dei vivi*). This film, directed by Edoardo Gabbriellini, is set to make its world premiere in the Orizzonti competition at the Venice Film Festival, followed by its North American debut at the Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF).

## A True Crime Narrative Inspired by Literature

*The City of the Living* is loosely based on the award-winning and best-selling work of Italian author Nicola Lagioia. The film promises to delve into the intricacies of true crime, exploring the darker aspects of human nature and society. Gabbriellini's adaptation aims to capture the essence of Lagioia's narrative while bringing a fresh cinematic perspective to the story.

## Festival Screenings and Expectations

The Venice Film Festival, renowned for showcasing innovative and artistic films, will provide a significant platform for *The City of the Living* as it competes in the Orizzonti section. This category is dedicated to films that offer new perspectives and original storytelling, making it an ideal fit for Gabbriellini's work. Following Venice, the film will transition to TIFF, where it will be featured as part of the Centrepiece selection, further amplifying its visibility on the international stage.

## Trailer Release and Audience Anticipation

In conjunction with the sales announcement, PiperPlay has unveiled the trailer for *The City of the Living*. The trailer is expected to generate excitement among audiences and industry professionals alike, providing a glimpse into the film's narrative style and thematic depth. The visual and auditory elements showcased in the trailer suggest a compelling blend of suspense and emotional resonance, characteristic of well-crafted true crime dramas.

## Industry Impact and Future Prospects

The collaboration between PiperPlay and Gabbriellini marks a significant moment in the film's journey, as the sales company is poised to attract interest from international distributors and audiences. With the film's dual premiere at two prestigious festivals, it is positioned to gain critical acclaim and commercial success.
