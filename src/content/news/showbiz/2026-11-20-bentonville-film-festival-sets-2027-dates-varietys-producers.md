The Bentonville Film Festival has announced its dates for 2027, scheduled to take place from June 14 to June 20. This will mark the festival's 13th year and will continue its partnership with Variety’s 10 Producers to Watch for the third consecutive year.

## Key facts
- The Bentonville Film Festival was founded in 2015 by Geena Davis.
- The festival focuses on authentic storytelling through film screenings, industry conversations, and community experiences.
- This year marks the festival's 13th iteration.
