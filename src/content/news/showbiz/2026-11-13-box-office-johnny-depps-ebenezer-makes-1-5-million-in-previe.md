Johnny Depp's new film "Ebenezer" has earned $1.5 million in box office previews. This marks Depp's return to a major Hollywood movie since 2018's "Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald."

## Key facts
- "Ebenezer" is a darkly comedic take on "A Christmas Carol."
- The film is directed by Ti West.
- Johnny Depp stars in the film, marking his return to the big screen in a major role.
