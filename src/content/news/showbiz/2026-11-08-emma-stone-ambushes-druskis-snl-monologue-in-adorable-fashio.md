When Druski was announced as a future host of "Saturday Night Live," it marked a significant move for the sketch-comedy show. Emma Stone joined Druski during his hosting debut, adding to the excitement surrounding the episode.

## Key facts
- Druski is set to host "Saturday Night Live," marking a notable decision for the show.
- He is the first exclusively digital creator to host, without prior crossover success.
- Emma Stone will co-star with Druski in the upcoming Universal Pictures rom-com "The Catch," scheduled for release on May 21, 2027.
