Skydance Corp. has established its finance leadership team following the merger of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery. The new company was formed by David Ellison, who is also the co-CEO alongside Ynon Kreiz. 

## Key facts
- Skydance Corp. was created from the merger of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery.
- David Ellison and Ynon Kreiz are co-CEOs of Skydance.
- The senior leadership team was announced a day before the merger deal officially closed.
