Mark Tildesley, a notable British production designer, has died at the age of 63. He passed away on November 24 following a long illness. Tildesley was known for his work on major film titles and had a career spanning over 30 years.

## Key facts
- Tildesley worked on the James Bond film "No Time to Die."
- His credits also include "28 Days Later," "The Constant Gardener," and "The Two Popes."
- He collaborated with director Sam Mendes on upcoming Beatles movies.
- Tildesley was regarded as one of the industry's most respected production designers.
