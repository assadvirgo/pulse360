Weta FX is partnering with the Taiwan Creative Content Agency (TAICCA) to establish Taiwan as a hub for AI-driven screen production in Asia. The formal launch of this initiative is scheduled for November 6 at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest.

## Key facts
- Weta FX is a visual effects house from New Zealand known for its work on "The Lord of the Rings" and "Avatar."
- The partnership aims to enhance Taiwan's capabilities in AI screen production.
- The initiative will be officially launched at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest.
