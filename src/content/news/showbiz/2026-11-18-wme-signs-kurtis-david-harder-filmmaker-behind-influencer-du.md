Kurtis David Harder, a Canadian filmmaker known for the "Influencer" films, has signed with WME. This move aims to expand his efforts in genre filmmaking and beyond.

## Key facts
- Kurtis David Harder is the filmmaker behind the "Influencer" duology.
- His film "Influencer" has been named among the best horror films of 2023.
- The plot of "Influencer" centers on a social media star named Madison, who travels alone in Thailand.
