Don Toliver has announced the release of his sixth album titled "Nitrous," set to come out on December 4. The album will be released via Cactus Jack/Donnway & Co/Atlantic Records.

## Key facts
- Don Toliver received five Grammy nominations prior to the album announcement.
- "Nitrous" was recorded, produced, and engineered entirely by Don Toliver himself.
- The announcement was made on social media, accompanied by a clip.
