Russell Brand has claimed he is "not guilty" following his sentencing to 10 years and six months in prison for rape and sexual assault. His wife, Laura Gallacher, shared his message in a YouTube video, expressing that he is "okay" and "praying" while incarcerated.

## Key facts
- Russell Brand was convicted of rape and sexual assault in a London criminal trial.
- He received a prison sentence of 10 years and six months.
- Laura Gallacher spoke about the conviction and shared Brand's written remarks in a YouTube video.
- Brand's message included the assertion that "something strange is happening."
