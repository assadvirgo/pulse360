Jennifer Love Hewitt and Ryan Phillippe recently reunited for an upcoming crossover episode of the series 9-1-1. Hewitt stated that they had not seen each other since their time working together on the show I Know What You Did Last Summer.

## Key facts
- Jennifer Love Hewitt plays the role of Maddie Kendall in 9-1-1.
- The reunion is part of a crossover with 9-1-1: Nashville.
- Hewitt remarked that Phillippe "hadn't changed at all" since they last met.
