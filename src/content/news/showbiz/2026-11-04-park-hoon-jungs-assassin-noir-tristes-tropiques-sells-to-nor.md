"Tristes Tropiques," directed by Park Hoon-jung, has been sold to multiple territories, including North America, France, and Spain. The film is set for a theatrical release in Korea on November 25.

## Key facts
- "Tristes Tropiques" is the latest film from director Park Hoon-jung, known for "New World" and "The Witch."
- Well Go USA has acquired the film for North America.
- Swift Distribution has secured rights for French-speaking territories.
- Alfa Pictures will distribute the film in Spain and Andorra.
- The sales were facilitated by Korean sales outfit Finecut.
