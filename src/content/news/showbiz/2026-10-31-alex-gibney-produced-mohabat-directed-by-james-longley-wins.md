James Longley's film "Mohabat," produced by Alex Gibney, won the Opus Bonum section at the Ji.hlava Documentary Film Festival. The film tells the story of a 12-year-old boy named Mohabat, who is taken to a city orphanage after his father dies in a drone strike.

## Key facts
- "Mohabat" won the Opus Bonum section at the Ji.hlava Documentary Film Festival.
- The film is directed by James Longley and produced by Alex Gibney and Mohammad Sharif Hotak.
- The story follows a 12-year-old boy named Mohabat after his father dies in a drone strike.
- Mohabat is left without parental care and taken to a city orphanage.
