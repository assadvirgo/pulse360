Neil Young has filed a $10 million lawsuit against Donald J. Trump's political action committee, Trump 47 Committee Inc., for copyright infringement related to the use of his song "Rockin' in the Free World." The lawsuit was filed on Monday and concerns the song's use as walkup music at midterm election rallies.

## Key facts
- Neil Young's lawsuit claims copyright infringement.
- The lawsuit is for $10 million.
- The song "Rockin' in the Free World" was used at Trump's midterm election rallies.
- This legal action follows a prior settlement over the same song six years ago.
