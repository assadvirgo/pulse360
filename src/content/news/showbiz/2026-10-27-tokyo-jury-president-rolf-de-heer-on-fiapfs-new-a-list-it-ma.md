FIAPF has officially designated A-list status to 17 film festivals, including the Tokyo International Film Festival. Rolf de Heer, the president of the jury at the Tokyo festival, stated that this decision is unlikely to significantly impact the event.

## Key facts
- FIAPF has made A-list status official for 17 film festivals, including Tokyo.
- Rolf de Heer is the president of the jury at the Tokyo International Film Festival.
- De Heer commented that the A-list designation may matter to some people but not to others.
