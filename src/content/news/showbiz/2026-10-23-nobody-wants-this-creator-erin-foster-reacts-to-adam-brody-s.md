Erin Foster, creator of the series "Nobody Wants This," has responded to Adam Brody's comments regarding the Israel-Gaza conflict. Brody expressed his support for a "free Palestine" in a recent interview, which Foster addressed while promoting the show's return to Netflix for its third season.

## Key facts
- Adam Brody stated in an interview with GQ that he views the situation as a genocide and supports a free Palestine.
- Erin Foster acknowledged that she disagrees with Brody's views but believes he is entitled to his opinion.
- "Nobody Wants This" is returning to Netflix for its third season.
