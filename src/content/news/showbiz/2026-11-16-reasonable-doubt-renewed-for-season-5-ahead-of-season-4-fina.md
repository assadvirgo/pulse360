"Reasonable Doubt" has been renewed for Season 5 at Hulu and Onyx Collective, according to Variety. This announcement comes just ahead of the legal drama's Season 4 finale, which is scheduled to air on November 24. With the release of its fifth season, "Reasonable Doubt" will be one of the longest-running scripted shows on Hulu.

## Key facts
- "Reasonable Doubt" has been renewed for Season 5.
- The renewal was confirmed by Variety.
- The Season 4 finale is scheduled to air on November 24.
- The show will be among the longest-running scripted series on Hulu.
