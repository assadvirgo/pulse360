## 'Black Panther 3': David Jonsson to Star as T’Challa’s Son, Sequel Set for 2028

In a highly anticipated announcement during this year's Comic-Con, Marvel Studios revealed that actor David Jonsson will portray a grown version of T'Challa's son in the upcoming sequel, "Black Panther 3." The film is set to be released in 2028, continuing the legacy of the beloved franchise that has captivated audiences worldwide.

### A New Chapter for the Black Panther Franchise

The announcement was made during a Marvel Studios panel that drew significant attention and excitement from fans. The "Black Panther" series, which began with the groundbreaking film released in 2018, has been praised for its cultural significance and representation. Following the untimely passing of Chadwick Boseman, who portrayed T'Challa, the franchise has faced the challenge of continuing the story while honoring his legacy.

Jonsson's casting marks a pivotal moment for the series, as it introduces a new generation of characters while maintaining a connection to the original storyline. The decision to focus on T'Challa's son reflects the narrative's evolution and the potential for exploring themes of legacy, responsibility, and identity.
