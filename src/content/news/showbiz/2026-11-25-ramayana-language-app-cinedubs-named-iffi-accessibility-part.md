The mobile app Cinedubs, which allows users to hear "Ramayana" in various languages, has been named the accessibility partner for the International Film Festival of India (IFFI) 2026 in Goa. The app has reached over 100,000 moviegoers across 42 countries.

## Key facts
- Cinedubs has been named the accessibility partner for IFFI 2026.
- The app has enabled more than 100,000 moviegoers to experience "Ramayana" in their chosen language.
- Cinedubs provides audio description and closed-caption technology.
