The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) halted all U.S. flights on Friday for approximately four hours, impacting many Hollywood insiders. This occurred during a critical time with events such as the American Film Market and the Governors Awards scheduled.

## Key facts
- The FAA issued a flight stoppage at around 1 p.m. PT on Friday.
- The order was lifted approximately four hours later.
- The stoppage affected dealmakers, talent agents, and Hollywood stars.
- Key events scheduled during the stoppage included the American Film Market and the Governors Awards.
