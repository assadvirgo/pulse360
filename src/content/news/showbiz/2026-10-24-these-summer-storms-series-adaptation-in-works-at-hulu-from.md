A new series adaptation titled "These Summer Storms" is in development at Hulu, based on Sarah MacLean's upcoming 2025 novel. The project is being developed by Melanie Marnich and Erin & Sara Foster, who are also involved in the Netflix comedy series "Nobody Wants This."

## Key facts
- "These Summer Storms" is based on a novel by Sarah MacLean set to be released in 2025.
- The series is in development at Hulu through 20th Television.
- Erin and Sara Foster are currently working on their Netflix comedy series "Nobody Wants This," which is launching its third season.
