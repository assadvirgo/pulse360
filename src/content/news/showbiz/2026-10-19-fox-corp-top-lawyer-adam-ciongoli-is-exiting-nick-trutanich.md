Fox Corp. announced that Adam Ciongoli, the chief legal and policy officer, will not renew his contract and will step down from his role effective December 1. Nicholas Trutanich, currently the executive vice president and principal deputy general counsel, will take over as general counsel.

## Key facts
- Adam Ciongoli will serve as a special adviser to Fox through June 2028.
- Nicholas Trutanich has been promoted to general counsel.
- Ciongoli joined Fox in 2023 after his tenure at Campbell Soup.
