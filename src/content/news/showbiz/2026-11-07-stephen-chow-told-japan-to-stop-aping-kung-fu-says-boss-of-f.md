Hong Kong star-director Stephen Chow advised Japanese filmmakers to stop imitating kung fu and to create action films based on their own martial arts. This statement was made by K2 Pictures founder Kii Muneyuki during the Taiwan Creative Content Fest in Taipei.

## Key facts
- Stephen Chow suggested that Japanese filmmakers should utilize their own martial arts, such as karate, judo, jujitsu, and kendo.
- Kii Muneyuki shared Chow's comments at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest.
- The event took place in Taipei.
