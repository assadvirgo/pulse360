Richard Lloyd, a guitarist known for his work with the influential band Television, has died at the age of 75. He passed away at his home in Chattanooga, Tennessee, and the cause of death was bile duct cancer, according to the New York Times.

- Richard Lloyd was a vital element in the 1970s-era band Television.
