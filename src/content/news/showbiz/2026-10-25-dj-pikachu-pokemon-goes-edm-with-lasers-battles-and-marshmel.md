Pokémon celebrated its 30th anniversary with an EDM concert at the Intuit Dome in Los Angeles. The event featured DJs Marshmello and Alison Wonderland, combining elements from the Pokémon franchise with an energetic electronic music atmosphere.

## Key facts
- The concert was titled Pokémon Night Out.
- It included lasers, pyrotechnics, and visuals typical of EDM events.
- The event took place on a Saturday night.
