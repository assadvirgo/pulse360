Robert Ben Garant, co-creator of "Reno 911!", is launching a new comedy series based on the satirical tabloid Weekly World News. He has raised $2.5 million to film a full season independently in California.

## Key facts
- Robert Ben Garant is venturing outside the Hollywood system for his next series.
- The series is based on the satirical tabloid Weekly World News.
- Garant will serve as co-showrunner and executive producer alongside Cathy Shim.
