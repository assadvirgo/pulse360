title: ‘Sinners’ Music Exhibition Opens at California African American Museum

An exhibition titled “Sinners: Sounds That Summon” has opened at the California African American Museum. The exhibition is dedicated to the music of Ryan Coogler’s Oscar-winning film “Sinners” and will be on view from October 20 to March 14.

## Key facts
- The exhibition is presented in collaboration with Warner Bros. Archives.
- It is the first museum exhibition devoted to the music of a film.
