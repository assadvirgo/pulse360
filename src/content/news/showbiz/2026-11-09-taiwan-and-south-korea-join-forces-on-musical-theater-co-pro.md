title: Taiwan and South Korea Join Forces on Musical Theater Co-Productions

Taiwan and South Korea have signed a memorandum of understanding to enhance collaboration in musical theater. The agreement was made between the Taiwan Creative Content Agency (TAICCA) and the Korean Association of Musical Producers (KAMP) during the Taiwan Creative Content Fest (TCCF) in Taipei.

## Key facts
- TAICCA and KAMP signed the memorandum to expand musical theater collaboration.
- The agreement aims to open new opportunities across Asia.
- The signing took place at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest (TCCF) in Taipei.
