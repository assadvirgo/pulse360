Sir Patrick Stewart has passed away at the age of 86, prompting tributes from friends and colleagues. Ian McKellen, who worked alongside Stewart in the X-Men films, shared his admiration for Stewart's character and legacy.

## Key facts
- Sir Patrick Stewart has died at age 86, according to Deadline.
- Ian McKellen described Stewart as "a man to rely on, to admire and in my case, love."
- McKellen and Stewart starred together in the original X-Men franchise, portraying Magneto and Professor X, respectively, as reported by Hollywood Reporter.
