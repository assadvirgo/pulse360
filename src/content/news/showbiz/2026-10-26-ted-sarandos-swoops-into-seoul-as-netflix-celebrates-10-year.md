Ted Sarandos, co-CEO of Netflix, participated in a two-day fan festival in Seoul to celebrate the company's tenth anniversary in Korea. He joined stars from popular Korean series such as 'Squid Game' and 'KPop Demon Hunters' while previewing upcoming titles.

## Key facts
- Ted Sarandos attended a fan festival in Seoul to mark Netflix's tenth anniversary in Korea.
- The event featured stars from 'Squid Game,' 'KPop Demon Hunters,' and other Korean hits.
- Upcoming titles previewed included 'Possible Love,' 'Tantara,' and 'Solo Leveling.'
