A deal has been made for the North American distribution rights of the film "Snapshot." The agreement was reached between UK and France-based sales firm Alief and U.S. distributor Dark Star Pictures.

## Key facts
- "Snapshot" is a UK found-footage horror film.
- The film debuted at the Edinburgh Film Festival earlier this year.
- The deal was reported by Deadline.
