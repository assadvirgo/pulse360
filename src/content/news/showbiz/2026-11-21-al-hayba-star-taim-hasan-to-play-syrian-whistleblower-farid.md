Syrian actor Taim Hassan will star in the Neon-backed biopic "Caesar," which focuses on military officer Farid Al-Madhhan. The film depicts Al-Madhhan's efforts to expose torture and human rights abuses in Syrian prisons during Bashar Al Assad's presidency. Ameer Fakher Eldin has been announced as the director of the drama.

## Key facts
- Taim Hassan is known for his role in "Al Hayba."
- The biopic "Caesar" is backed by Neon.
- The film centers on Farid Al-Madhhan, who exposed human rights abuses in Syria.
- Ameer Fakher Eldin will direct the film.
