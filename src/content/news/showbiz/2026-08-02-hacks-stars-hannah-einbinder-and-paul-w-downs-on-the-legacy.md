## ‘Hacks’ Stars Reflect on Legacy and Future Spin-offs

In a recent interview, Hannah Einbinder and Paul W. Downs, stars of the critically acclaimed series "Hacks," shared their insights on the show's legacy and the potential for future spin-offs. Their conversation, which took place during promotional activities for Einbinder's new film "Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma," highlighted the impact of their work on "Hacks" and the characters that have resonated most with audiences.

### A New Perspective

Hannah Einbinder, who portrayed the character Ava in "Hacks," noted that her collaboration with Gillian Anderson for the new film has influenced her perspective on the end of "Hacks." During her discussions with Anderson, who is known for her iconic role in "The X-Files," Einbinder reflected on the lasting significance of their series. "Doing all this press with Gillian for the movie, she talks about 'X-Files' constantly," Einbinder shared on Variety's Awards Circuit Podcast. This connection to a show with a strong legacy has prompted her to think deeply about the cultural footprint of "Hacks."

### The Legacy of ‘Hacks’

"Hacks," which premiered in 2021, has garnered significant acclaim for its sharp writing and compelling performances. The series follows the complex relationship between a legendary Las Vegas comedian, played by Jean Smart, and a young writer, Ava, portrayed by Einbinder. The show has been praised for its exploration of generational differences, the comedy industry, and the nuances of personal and professional relationships.

As the series concluded, both Einbinder and Downs expressed a sense of pride in what they accomplished. Downs, who played the character of Damien, emphasized the importance of the show's themes and the way it has resonated with viewers. "The legacy of 'Hacks' is one of authenticity and vulnerability," he stated. "We tackled real issues while still making people laugh."

### Spin-off Potential

During the interview, the question of which character might deserve a spin-off was also raised. Einbinder and Downs discussed the various supporting characters who have captured the audience's attention. While they did not settle on a definitive choice, the conversation hinted at the rich possibilities within the universe of "Hacks." 

The duo acknowledged that characters like the show's quirky producers or even some of the Las Vegas locals could lead to intriguing storylines. "There are so many characters with untold stories," Downs remarked. "It would be exciting to explore their lives further."

### Looking Ahead

As both actors transition to new projects, including Einbinder's collaboration with Anderson, they remain committed to the legacy of "Hacks." The series has not only showcased their talents but also opened doors for future opportunities in the entertainment industry. 

In conclusion, while "Hacks" has wrapped up its narrative, the discussions surrounding its impact and potential spin-offs continue to thrive. The show's legacy, marked by its humor and depth, will likely influence future comedic endeavors and inspire new stories that resonate with audiences for years to come.
