The European Film Academy has announced the nominations for the 39th European Film Awards, which will take place in Greece in January 2027. "Fatherland," directed by Paweł Pawlikowski, leads with 11 nominations, while "La Bola Negra," directed by Javier Calvo and Javier Ambrossi, follows closely with ten nominations.

## Key facts
- "Fatherland" is centered on the relationship between writer Thomas Mann and his daughter Erika.
- "La Bola Negra" is described as a time-spanning epic based on a work of literature.
- Sandra Hüller received a dual best actress nomination for her roles in "Fatherland" and "Rose."
- Other acting nominees include Renate Reinsve, Sebastian Stan, Penelope Cruz, Javier Bardem, and Andrew Scott.
