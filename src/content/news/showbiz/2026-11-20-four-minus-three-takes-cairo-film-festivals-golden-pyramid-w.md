Austrian filmmaker Adrian Goiginger's film "Four Minus Three" won the Golden Pyramid for best film at the 47th Cairo International Film Festival. The festival concluded its 10-day run in Cairo, with "Four Minus Three" also receiving two additional awards.

## Key facts
- "Four Minus Three" won three awards at the Cairo International Film Festival.
- The film is an Austrian-German drama about a professional clown.
- The festival took place in Cairo and lasted for 10 days.
