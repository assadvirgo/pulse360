## Mary Rivera, Beloved Actress from 'Spider-Man: No Way Home,' Passes Away at 82

Mary Rivera, an actress best known for her role as the grandmother of Ned Leeds in the blockbuster film "Spider-Man: No Way Home," has passed away at the age of 82. Rivera's family confirmed her death, stating that she suffered a stroke prior to her passing on April 15, 2023, in Honolulu, Hawaii.

### Early Life and Career

Born on June 2, 1943, in Iloilo, Philippines, Rivera's journey in the entertainment industry began long before her notable appearance in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Her career spanned several decades, during which she showcased her talent across various mediums, including television and film. Rivera's warm presence and ability to connect with audiences made her a cherished figure in the industry.

### Role in 'Spider-Man: No Way Home'

Rivera's portrayal of Ned's grandmother in "Spider-Man: No Way Home" brought her into the spotlight for a new generation of viewers. The film, released in December 2021, was a critical and commercial success, and Rivera's character added a layer of familial warmth to the narrative. Her performance resonated with audiences, contributing to the film's overall charm and emotional depth.

### Legacy and Impact

Beyond her role in "Spider-Man: No Way Home," Rivera's contributions to the arts have left a lasting impact on her community and fans alike. She was known for her dedication to her craft and her ability to bring authenticity to her roles. Rivera's passing is felt deeply by those who knew her personally and professionally, as well as by fans who admired her work.

Her family, in their statement, expressed their grief and highlighted the love and support Rivera provided throughout her life. They noted her passion for acting and her commitment to her family, which remained a central theme in both her personal and professional life.
