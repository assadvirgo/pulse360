Bassam Tariq discussed his experience with the scrapped "Blade" reboot during a recent interview. He reflected on the challenges of casting and building the project without a proper script.

## Key facts
- Bassam Tariq described the process of casting and building the "Blade" reboot as "funny and strange."
- He noted that working without a proper script is "very dangerous."
- Tariq expressed concern that if the script does not align with what has been established, it could damage credibility.
