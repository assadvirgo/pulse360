Jeremy Saulnier, the writer and director of "Green Room," paid tribute to Patrick Stewart during a 10th-anniversary screening of the film in Los Angeles. Saulnier credited Stewart with rescuing the film when they were close to shutting down production. Stewart passed away one day prior to the screening.
