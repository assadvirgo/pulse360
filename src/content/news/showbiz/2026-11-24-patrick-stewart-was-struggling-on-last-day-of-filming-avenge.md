Marvel Studios is preparing to honor the late Patrick Stewart in the upcoming film "Avengers: Doomsday," which marks his final on-screen appearance. Stewart's last day of filming was noted to be challenging due to his health struggles.

## Key facts
- Patrick Stewart's role in "Avengers: Doomsday" is his final on-screen appearance after his death in October.
- Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige mentioned that Stewart gave a speech to the crew on his last day of filming.
- Marvel plans to include a special tribute to Stewart in the film.
- "Avengers: Doomsday" is set for release on December 18.
