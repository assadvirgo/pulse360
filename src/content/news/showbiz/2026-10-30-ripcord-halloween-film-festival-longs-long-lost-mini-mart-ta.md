A short film titled "Long’s Long Lost & Mini Mart" won the grand prize of $100,000 at the Ripcord Halloween Film Festival. This amount is noted as the largest ever awarded at a short film festival.

## Key facts
- The film was directed by Julian Doan.
- The Ripcord Halloween Film Festival awarded the prize.
- The $100,000 prize is the largest amount ever given at a short film festival.
- Jurors for the festival included Tim Miller and Jean Lauer.
