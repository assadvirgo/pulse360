Milcho Manchevski's film "Sister Brother Manhole Cover" is competing in the main competition at the Tokyo International Film Festival. The director discusses the recurring theme of mothers in his stories and shares insights on creating his first full documentary despite initial reservations about the genre.

- Milcho Manchevski is the director of "Sister Brother Manhole Cover."
- The film is part of the main competition at the Tokyo International Film Festival.
- Manchevski reflects on the presence of mothers in his narratives.
- He has made his first full documentary, despite having misgivings about the genre.
