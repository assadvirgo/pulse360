## ‘SNL U.K.’ Cold Open Features Royal Family Reunion

In a humorous take on the British royal family, the latest episode of "SNL U.K." opened with a contentious reunion between Prince William, Kate Middleton, Prince Harry, and Meghan Markle. The sketch, which aired this past weekend, took place at the iconic Sandringham Estate, known for its historical significance to the monarchy.

### A Comedic Reunion

The cold open featured Ayoade Bamgboye portraying Meghan Markle, who nostalgically remarked, “This place brings back so many memories,” as she and Prince Harry, played by Jack Shep, navigated their complex family dynamics. The sketch cleverly highlighted the ongoing tensions within the royal family while infusing it with comedic elements that have become a hallmark of the "SNL" franchise.

The portrayal of the royal family was not without its sharp wit. The inclusion of former Prince Andrew added another layer of humor, as the characters speculated on the family's future and their potential return to royal duties. The line, “They must want us to be royals again!” served as a tongue-in-cheek commentary on the public's fascination with the monarchy and the ongoing discussions surrounding its relevance in modern society.
