Spanish actor Karla Sofia Gascon stated that the controversy surrounding her Oscar nomination for "Emilia Pérez" stemmed from individuals who did not want to see someone like her win. She emphasized that the backlash was driven by a significant number of people.

## Key facts
- Karla Sofia Gascon is the first transgender actor to receive an Oscar nomination.
- The controversy during her awards campaign was linked to her identity.
- Gascon believes the backlash was fueled by those opposed to her winning.
