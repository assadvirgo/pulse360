Tony Romo has joined the sports betting app Betr as its first "Chief Predictions Officer." In this role, he will produce original content and provide sports predictions. His debut is scheduled for a Thanksgiving Day campaign.

## Key facts
- Romo will produce original content for the app.
- He will deliver sports predictions as part of his role.
- Betr was co-founded by Jake Paul.
- Romo's debut is set for a Thanksgiving Day campaign.
