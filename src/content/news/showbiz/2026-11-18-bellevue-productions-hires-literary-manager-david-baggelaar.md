Bellevue Productions has announced the hiring of literary manager David Baggelaar. He joins the company after more than a decade at Benderspink and its successor, Good Fear.

## Key facts
- Bellevue Productions is led by President John Zaozirny.
- David Baggelaar brings a roster of film and television writers, directors, and IP creators.
