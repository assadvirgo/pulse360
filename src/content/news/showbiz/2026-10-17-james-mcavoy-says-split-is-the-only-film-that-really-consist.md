James McAvoy stated that the only film from which he consistently receives residuals is M. Night Shyamalan's 2016 horror film "Split." He mentioned that he has starred in various films but noted that "Split" is unique in providing ongoing payments.

## Key facts
- James McAvoy has starred in films including "Speak No Evil," "Atonement," and "X-Men: First Class."
- He revealed that "Split" is the only film that consistently pays him residuals.
- McAvoy mentioned receiving occasional checks for varying amounts from "Split."
