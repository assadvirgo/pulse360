Cristian Mungiu’s Palme d’Or winner “Fjord” opened the fifth edition of Jakarta World Cinema at CGV Grand Indonesia. The film is Romania’s submission for the international feature Oscar at the 2027 Academy Awards.

## Key facts
- “Fjord” is a Palme d’Or winner directed by Cristian Mungiu.
- The film opened the fifth edition of Jakarta World Cinema.
- The event took place at CGV Grand Indonesia.
- Romania’s ambassador to Indonesia, Dan Adrian Bălănescu, expressed pride in the film.
- The film is Romania's submission for the international feature Oscar at the 2027 Academy Awards, aiming to resonate with local audiences.
