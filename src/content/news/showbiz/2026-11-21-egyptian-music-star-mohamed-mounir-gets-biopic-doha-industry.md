The life and legacy of Egyptian artist Mohamed Mounir will be the subject of an upcoming biopic. The Film Committee at Media City Qatar is leading the project and has acquired the rights to document Mounir's life story.

## Key facts
- The biopic will explore the life and legacy of Mohamed Mounir.
- The Film Committee at Media City Qatar is spearheading the project.
- Rights to document Mounir's life story have been acquired by the Film Committee.
- The project will include partnerships with a leading regional studio and other partners.
