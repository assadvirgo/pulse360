A distribution deal has been established between French classic film distributor Carlotta Films and U.S. restoration company Artcraft Pictures. The 1927 silent film "It," featuring Clara Bow, is the leading title in this agreement.

## Key facts
- Carlotta Films is a French classic film distributor.
- Artcraft Pictures is a U.S. restoration company.
- The deal focuses on the distribution of Hollywood classics in France.
- "It" is a breakout movie of silent film star Clara Bow.
