Ray Tintori, a music video director and filmmaker known for his work with bands such as Arcade Fire and MGMT, has died in New York. The New York City Office of Chief Medical Examiner is investigating the death, and the cause is currently unknown.

## Key facts
- Ray Tintori directed music videos for Arcade Fire and MGMT.
- He worked on the film "Beasts of the Southern Wild."
- His death is under investigation by the New York City Office of Chief Medical Examiner.
