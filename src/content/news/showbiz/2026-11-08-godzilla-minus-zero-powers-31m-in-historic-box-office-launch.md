The box office debut of "Godzilla Minus Zero" has generated $31 million, marking a strong start for the sequel to "Godzilla Minus One." In contrast, "The Cat in the Hat" underperformed with a $20 million launch.

## Key facts
- "Godzilla Minus Zero" earned $31.6 million from 3,254 theaters.
- The film is noted as a significant success for a foreign-language epic in North America.
- "The Cat in the Hat" debuted with $20 million from 4,003 locations.
- Other films, "Ramayana" and "Wild Horse Nine," also premiered alongside these titles.
