A new film titled "Jimmy" explores the life of Oscar-winning actor James Stewart, as told by his daughter, Kelly Stewart Harcourt. Harcourt, who is also an executive producer of the film, initially thought the concept might be dull due to her father's lack of scandals typically found in biopics.

## Key facts
- The film is about James Stewart, an Oscar-winning actor.
- Kelly Stewart Harcourt is the daughter of James Stewart and an executive producer of the film.
- Harcourt expressed concerns that the film might be boring due to her father's uneventful life.
