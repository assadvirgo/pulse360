C.V. Rao, the chief technology officer at Annapurna Studios in Hyderabad, has been named a Fellow of the Society of Motion Picture and Television Engineers (SMPTE). He is recognized for his contributions to the production and post-production processes of the "Baahubali" films and "RRR." Rao is the first technologist from South India to receive this honor.

## Key facts
- C.V. Rao is the chief technology officer at Annapurna Studios.
- He has contributed to the production and post-production of "Baahubali" and "RRR."
- Rao is the first technologist from South India to be named a Fellow of SMPTE.
