C.W. Morgan, known for his role in the "Scream" franchise, has had his cause of death revealed by the Los Angeles County Department of Medical Examiner. He passed away at the age of 72 on August 30.

## Key facts
- C.W. Morgan's cause of death was determined to be atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease.
- He played Hank Loomis, the father of one of the franchise's Ghostface killers.
- Morgan was also the ex-husband of another character from "Scream 2."
