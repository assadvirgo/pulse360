Paramount has acquired the film rights to the sci-fi book series "Red Rising" by Pierce Brown. Francis Lawrence will direct the adaptation of the first installment.

## Key facts
- Paramount has acquired the film rights to the "Red Rising" series.
- Francis Lawrence is set to direct the adaptation.
- Justin Rhodes will write the script for the film.
