In the new film "Whalefall," production designer Ramsey Avery created the creature's stomach using mattress foam and silicone bits. The film features a storyline where a son, Jay, searches for his father's remains off the coast of California while encountering a giant squid and an 80-foot, 60-ton sperm whale. 

## Key facts
- "Whalefall" is currently in theaters.
- The film's main character, Jay, is portrayed by Austin Abrams.
- Jay's father is played by Josh Brolin.
- The plot involves Jay diving deeper into the ocean during his search.
