Actor Danny Trejo has recently addressed the controversy surrounding his participation in the film "Pawn Shop," produced by the Daily Wire, a media company co-founded by conservative commentator Ben Shapiro. Trejo's involvement in the project has drawn criticism due to its perceived pro-ICE (Immigration and Customs Enforcement) messaging, prompting the actor to clarify his position on immigration issues.

In a statement released to the media, Trejo emphasized his identity as a proud Mexican and reiterated his long-standing support for the immigrant community. This clarification comes as a response to the backlash he has faced from various groups and individuals who view the film as endorsing policies they oppose.
