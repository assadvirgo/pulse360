YouTube is investigating a video featuring Russell Brand's prison message after the UK government requested its removal. The video, posted on Brand's YouTube channel, includes a message delivered by his wife, Laura Brand, in which he asserts his innocence regarding allegations of rape.

## Key facts
- YouTube is conducting an investigation into Russell Brand's prison message video.
- The UK government has called for the removal of the video from the platform.
- The video features a message from Brand's wife, Laura Brand, on his YouTube channel.
- In the video, Brand maintains his innocence of rape allegations.
