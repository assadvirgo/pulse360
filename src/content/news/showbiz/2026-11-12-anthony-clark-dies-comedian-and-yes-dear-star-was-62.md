Anthony Clark, a comedian known for his role in the CBS sitcom "Yes, Dear," has died at the age of 62. His co-star Mike O'Malley confirmed the news, remembering Clark as "a hilarious guy."

## Key facts
- Anthony Clark started his career as a stand-up comedian.
- He was the star of the CBS show "Yes, Dear."
- Clark also appeared in the comedy shows "Boston Common" and "Soul Man."
