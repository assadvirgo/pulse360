HBO Max's "Rooster" has announced several guest stars for its upcoming second season. The new additions include O-T Fagbenle, Sutton Foster, Lamorne Morris, Zavior Phillips, and Michael Urie.

## Key facts
- O-T Fagbenle is known for "The Handmaid’s Tale" and "Presumed Innocent."
- Sutton Foster has appeared in "Younger" and "The Music Man."
- Lamorne Morris is recognized for his roles in "Fargo" and "Spider-Noir."
- Zavior Phillips is known for "Bad Monkey."
- Michael Urie has starred in "Shrinking" and "Oh, Mary!"
