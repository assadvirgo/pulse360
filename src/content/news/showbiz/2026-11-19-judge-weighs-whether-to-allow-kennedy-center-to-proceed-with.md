A federal judge is set to rule on whether the Kennedy Center can move forward with its plan to close for two years for renovations. Concerns have been raised regarding the potential for the demolition of the 55-year-old arts complex.

## Key facts
- A federal judge indicated he will issue a ruling on the Kennedy Center's closure plan.
- The closure is intended for renovations lasting two years.
- There are fears that President Donald Trump may seek to demolish the arts complex.
