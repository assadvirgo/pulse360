## EJAE Honored at Gyopo’s Chuseok Benefit: A Celebration of Heritage and Achievement

In a heartfelt ceremony held during the Chuseok festivities, EJAE, the star of the popular series "KPop Demon Hunters," was recognized for her outstanding contributions to the music industry. The event, hosted by Gyopo, celebrated not only the significance of the harvest festival but also the achievements of artists who have made a mark in the entertainment sector.

### Celebrating Cultural Heritage

Chuseok, often referred to as Korean Thanksgiving, is a time when families come together to honor their ancestors and celebrate the bountiful harvest. This year, the Gyopo organization took the opportunity to highlight the importance of cultural heritage in the arts. EJAE, who has recently gained acclaim for her songwriting in the Netflix animated film, was one of the key figures honored at the event.

EJAE expressed her gratitude during her acceptance speech, stating, “I’m so proud to celebrate where we come from.” Her words resonated with many attendees, as they reflected a collective appreciation for the rich traditions that shape Korean identity. The event served as a reminder of the importance of cultural roots in the ever-evolving landscape of global entertainment.
