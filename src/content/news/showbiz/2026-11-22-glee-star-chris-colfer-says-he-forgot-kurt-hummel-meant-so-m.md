Chris Colfer discussed the significance of his character, Kurt Hummel, from "Glee," reflecting on the impact it had on viewers. He mentioned that he had forgotten how much the character meant to people due to the negative experiences he faced during the show's run.

## Key facts
- Chris Colfer portrayed Kurt Hummel on "Glee."
- He recently reunited on stage with co-star Darren Criss, who played Blaine Anderson.
- The two performed "Baby It’s Cold Outside" for the audience.
- Colfer expressed that he overlooked the character's importance because of the homophobia and hatred he experienced during the show.
