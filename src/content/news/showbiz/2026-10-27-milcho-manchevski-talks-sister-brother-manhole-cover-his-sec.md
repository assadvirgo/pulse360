Macedonian filmmaker Milcho Manchevski has released his second feature of the year, titled "Sister Brother Manhole Cover," which is screening in competition at the Tokyo Film Festival. The film follows siblings Malina and Viktor living on the outskirts of post-Communist Macedonia.

## Key facts
- "Sister Brother Manhole Cover" is directed and written by Milcho Manchevski.
- The film is competing at the Tokyo Film Festival.
- The story centers on siblings Malina and Viktor.
