## Fred Tsui Appointed Executive Director of Hong Kong International Film Festival Society

The Hong Kong International Film Festival Society (HKIFFS) has announced the appointment of Fred Tsui as its new executive director. Tsui, who is the founder and CEO of Moebius Entertainment, will officially take on the role on September 23, succeeding Albert Lee.

### Background of Fred Tsui

Fred Tsui brings a wealth of experience in the film and entertainment industry to his new position. As the founder of Moebius Entertainment, he has been instrumental in promoting innovative film projects and fostering new talent within the industry. His leadership at Moebius has been marked by a commitment to quality storytelling and a deep understanding of the evolving landscape of cinema, making him a fitting choice for the executive director role at HKIFFS.

### Responsibilities and Vision

In his new capacity, Tsui will report to Dr. Wilfred Wong, the chair of HKIFFS, and will work closely with the board to advance the society's mission. His responsibilities will include overseeing the planning and execution of the annual Hong Kong International Film Festival, which is renowned for showcasing a diverse array of films from around the globe. Tsui's vision for the festival includes enhancing its profile on the international stage and further engaging with local filmmakers and audiences.

### The Importance of HKIFFS

The Hong Kong International Film Festival is one of Asia's oldest and most prestigious film festivals, having been established in 1977. It serves as a vital platform for filmmakers to present their work and connect with audiences. The festival not only highlights local talent but also features international films, fostering cultural exchange and dialogue within the global film community.

### Looking Ahead

Tsui's appointment comes at a pivotal time for the film industry, which continues to navigate the challenges posed by the global pandemic and changing audience behaviors. His leadership is expected to bring fresh ideas and renewed energy to the festival, as well as to strengthen HKIFFS's role in promoting Hong Kong as a vibrant hub for cinema.

As the film industry evolves, the HKIFFS remains committed to supporting filmmakers and enriching the cultural landscape of Hong Kong. Under Tsui's guidance, the society aims to continue its tradition of excellence while embracing new opportunities for growth and innovation.

In summary, Fred Tsui's appointment as executive director marks a significant step for the Hong Kong International Film Festival Society as it seeks to enhance its influence and relevance in the ever-changing world of cinema.
