Selma Blair has pleaded not guilty after being charged with second-degree assault for allegedly assaulting two flight attendants on a Delta flight from Los Angeles to New York. Her spokesperson stated that Blair did not intend to harm anyone.

- Selma Blair was charged with second-degree assault following an incident on a Delta flight.
- The actress allegedly assaulted two flight attendants during the flight.

- The incident occurred earlier this week as Blair was traveling from L.A. to New York.
