Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick will speak at SXSW London 2027, as announced by the event organizers. He is expected to discuss the development of the Grand Theft Auto VI franchise.

## Key facts
- Strauss Zelnick will talk about building the Grand Theft Auto VI franchise.
- He is among the first-wave of speakers announced for SXSW London.
- SXSW London will return to Shoreditch, east London, for its third year in May 2027.
