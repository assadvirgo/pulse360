California lawmakers began a discussion about removing the cap on the state's $750 million incentive for film and TV production. The discussion took place during an Assembly committee hearing in North Hollywood, where lawmakers acknowledged potential budget hurdles.

## Key facts
- Lawmakers are considering removing the cap on the film and TV production incentive.
- The current incentive is valued at $750 million.
- The discussion occurred at an Assembly committee hearing in North Hollywood.
- Testimony was heard from union leaders and industry advocates.
