Tim Robbins and Giancarlo Esposito will guest star in the upcoming Monsterverse series at Apple TV, which is led by Wyatt Russell. The series is centered on Young Lee Shaw and is produced by Legendary.

## Key facts
- Tim Robbins and Giancarlo Esposito are set for guest star roles in the Monsterverse series at Apple TV, according to Variety.
- Wyatt Russell is the series lead and executive producer, reprising his role from the series Monarch: Legacy of Monsters, as reported by Deadline.
- Other cast members include Millie Brady, Pilou Asbaek, Corey Stoll, Ralph Ineson, Nyasha Hatendi, Connie Nielsen, and Stana Katic.
