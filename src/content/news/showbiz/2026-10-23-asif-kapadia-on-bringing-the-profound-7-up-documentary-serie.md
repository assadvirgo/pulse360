Asif Kapadia has discussed his work on the "7 Up" documentary series, which has been ongoing for more than 60 years. He describes the project as a "dream job," focusing on the lives of ordinary people.

## Key facts
- Asif Kapadia is an Academy Award-winning documentary filmmaker.
- He has previously told stories of larger-than-life figures, including a Formula 1 racing legend and a troubled singer.
- The "7 Up" series features a group of ordinary people now in their seventies.
