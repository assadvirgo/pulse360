## Kirti Kulhari's Production Debut 'Kinaare' to Premiere at Dharamshala International Film Festival

The 15th Dharamshala International Film Festival (DIFF) is set to showcase a blend of innovative storytelling and artistic expression, with Kirti Kulhari's debut production, "Kinaare" ("Shores"), making its premiere at this year's event. The festival will take place from October 29 to November 1, 2023, at the Tibetan Children's Village in Upper Dharamshala, India. Following the in-person screenings, an online edition will be available from November 2 to November 9.

### Opening Film: 'Not a Hero'

The festival will kick off with the screening of "Not a Hero," a film directed by Rima Das, who has garnered international acclaim, including recognition at the Berlin International Film Festival. This opening feature sets the tone for a festival that aims to highlight diverse narratives and voices from across the globe.

### Kirti Kulhari's Journey as a Producer

Kirti Kulhari, known for her versatile acting skills in Indian cinema, steps into the role of producer with "Kinaare." This film marks a significant milestone in her career, showcasing her commitment to storytelling beyond acting. Kulhari has expressed her enthusiasm for this new venture, emphasizing the importance of creating meaningful content that resonates with audiences.

### The Significance of DIFF

The Dharamshala International Film Festival has established itself as a prominent platform for independent filmmakers, providing a space for innovative cinema to flourish. The festival not only showcases films but also encourages discussions and interactions between filmmakers and audiences, fostering a deeper understanding of the art of filmmaking.

### A Focus on Diverse Voices

This year's lineup promises a rich tapestry of films that reflect various cultures and perspectives. The inclusion of works like "Kinaare" and "Not a Hero" underscores the festival's commitment to promoting narratives that challenge conventional storytelling and highlight underrepresented voices in the film industry.

### Conclusion

As the festival approaches, anticipation builds for the premiere of "Kinaare" and the opening film "Not a Hero." The 15th Dharamshala International Film Festival stands as a celebration of creativity, innovation, and the power of cinema to connect people across borders. With a diverse array of films scheduled for screening, attendees can expect an enriching experience that showcases the best of independent filmmaking.
