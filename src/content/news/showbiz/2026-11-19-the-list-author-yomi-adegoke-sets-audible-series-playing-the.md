Author Yomi Adegoke has created a new audio series titled "Playing The Game," which will be available on Audible starting December 3. The series is characterized as a satirical romantic comedy centered around a major dating show in Britain.

## Key facts
- "Playing The Game" is authored by Yomi Adegoke, known for "The List."
- The series will launch on Audible on December 3.
- It is described as a satirical romantic comedy.
- The setting is Britain's biggest dating show, focusing on the theme of finding love while planning to fake it.
- Aimée Kelly is among the cast members.
