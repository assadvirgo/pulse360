Egypt’s El Gouna Film Festival concluded its ninth edition after nine days of screenings and industry activities. The festival awarded its top prize to Mohammed Hammad’s psychological drama “Safe Exit,” which focuses on a young Coptic security guard in Cairo dealing with trauma from religious violence.

## Key facts
- El Gouna Film Festival wrapped its ninth edition on Friday.
- The festival featured nine days of screenings and industry activities.
- The top prize was awarded to “Safe Exit” by Mohammed Hammad. 
- The film explores themes of trauma related to religious violence.
