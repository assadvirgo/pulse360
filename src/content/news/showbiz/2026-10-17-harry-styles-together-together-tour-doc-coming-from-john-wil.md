Harry Styles is set to release a documentary about his Madison Square Garden residency, directed by John Wilson. The project is being produced by Universal Pictures Content Group.

## Key facts
- John Wilson, known for "The History of Concrete" and HBO’s "How To with John Wilson," is directing the documentary.
- Wilson has been filming Harry Styles at Madison Square Garden since August.
- The documentary will provide behind-the-scenes access to Styles' historic residency.
