The entertainment sector has surpassed the automotive industry to become the world's second-largest business sector, according to a report by consultancy Interbrand. The cumulative value of entertainment companies' brands is now estimated at $332.5 billion, reflecting a year-on-year increase of 12.4%.

## Key facts
- The entertainment sector's brand value reached $332.5 billion.
- This marks a 12.4% increase year-on-year.
- The sector has overtaken the automotive industry in value.
- Netflix's brand value grew by 22% according to the report.
