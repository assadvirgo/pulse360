Chuko and Arie Esiri, a Nigerian directing duo, have gained recognition for their adaptation of "Mrs. Dalloway," titled "Clarissa." The film, which features a star-studded cast and is set in Lagos, received acclaim at the Cannes Film Festival.

## Key facts
- "Clarissa" is a reinterpretation of Virginia Woolf's "Mrs. Dalloway."
- The film premiered in the Directors' Fortnight section at the Cannes Film Festival.
- It has embarked on a lengthy festival run following its premiere.
