Amazon MGM Studios is developing a series adaptation of the novel "The Courier" by Melinda Leigh. The project is based on a concept from Martin Gero, who will serve as showrunner, writer, and executive producer.

## Key facts
- Amazon MGM Studios is working on a series adaptation of "The Courier."
- The novel is written by bestselling author Melinda Leigh.
- Martin Gero, creator of "Blindspot," is involved as showrunner, writer, and executive producer.
- Gero's production company, Quinn's House, is producing the series alongside Premeditated Productions.
