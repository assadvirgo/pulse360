## Taylor Swift Surprises Dakota Johnson on 'SNL' for Birthday Celebration

The unexpected collaboration not only showcased the strong friendship between the two stars but also added an extra layer of excitement to the show's lineup.

### A Special Birthday Tribute

Dakota Johnson, who was hosting the episode, is set to celebrate her 37th birthday on Sunday. Swift's appearance can be seen as a thoughtful gesture, possibly intended as an early birthday present for the actress. The two have a history of collaboration, with Johnson appearing in Swift's music video for "Patient Zero," which further solidifies their camaraderie in the entertainment industry.

During the monologue, Swift and Johnson engaged in light-hearted banter, bringing laughter to the audience and showcasing their chemistry.
