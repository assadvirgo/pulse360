Korean sales company Finecut has acquired international rights to the martial arts action film "The Hit List," directed by Kim Hong-sun. The project brings together Kim with his crew from "Gangs Of London," including cinematographer Lars Cox and gaffer Kieran Waites.

## Key facts
- Finecut has picked up international rights to "The Hit List."
- The film is directed by Kim Hong-sun, known for "Project Wolf Hunting" and "Gangs Of London."
- Cinematographer Lars Cox and gaffer Kieran Waites, who worked on "Gangs Of London," are also involved in this project.
