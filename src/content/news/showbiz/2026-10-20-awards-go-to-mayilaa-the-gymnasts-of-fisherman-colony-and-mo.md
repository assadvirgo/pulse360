The Tasveer Film Festival in Seattle has concluded its 21st edition, announcing awards in various categories. The film "Mayilaa," directed by Semmalar Annam, received the Jury Award for Best Feature Narrative.

## Key facts
- The Tasveer Film Festival is based in Seattle.
- "Mayilaa" won the Jury Award for Best Feature Narrative.
- The film is directed by Semmalar Annam.
- "Mayilaa" features actors P. Melody Dorcas, Geetha Kailasam, Shudar Kodi, and Sathya Maruthani.
