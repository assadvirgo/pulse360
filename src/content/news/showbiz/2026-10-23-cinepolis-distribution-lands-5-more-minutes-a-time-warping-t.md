Cinépolis Distribution has acquired the Latin American rights to the Spanish film "5 More Minutes" ("5 Minutos Más"), which stars Javier Cámara. This marks a rare move for the company, as it typically secures only Mexican rights to films.

## Key facts
- Cinépolis has acquired Latin American rights to "5 More Minutes."
- The film stars Javier Cámara, who won the Best Supporting Actor award at the San Sebastian Film Festival.
- Cinépolis usually acquires only Mexican rights to films about 90% of the time.
