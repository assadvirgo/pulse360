Teodora Ana Mihai has begun the international launch of her feature film "Heysel 85." The film recently had its Asian premiere at the Tokyo Film Festival after debuting in Berlin almost eight months ago.

## Key facts
- Teodora Ana Mihai is a Romanian-Belgian filmmaker.
- "Heysel 85" is her latest feature film.
- The film had its Asian premiere at the Tokyo Film Festival.
- Mihai co-wrote the screenplay for the film with Lode.
