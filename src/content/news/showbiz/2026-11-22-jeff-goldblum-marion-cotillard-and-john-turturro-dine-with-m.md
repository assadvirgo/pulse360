Jeff Goldblum, Marion Cotillard, and John Turturro attended a dinner hosted by Morocco's HRH Prince Moulay Rachid during the Marrakech Film Festival. The event took place at the El Badi Palace, notable for its architecture.

## Key facts
- The dinner was held on Saturday night.
- It was hosted by HRH Prince Moulay Rachid and HRH Princess Lalla Oum Keltoum.
- The venue for the dinner was the 16th-century El Badi Palace.
