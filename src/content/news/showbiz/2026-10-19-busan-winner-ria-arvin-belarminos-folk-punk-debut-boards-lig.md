"Ria," the debut feature by Filipino filmmaker Arvin Belarmino, has been acquired by international sales company Lights On after its world premiere at the Busan International Film Festival. The film, which centers on a young woman from the folk-punk scene fighting against a property developer to save her home, won the Heart of Eurasia Award.

## Key facts
- "Ria" is Arvin Belarmino's debut feature film.
- The film premiered at the Busan International Film Festival.
- It won the Heart of Eurasia Award at the festival.
- Lights On has acquired the film for international sales.
