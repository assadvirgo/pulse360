Jessica Edwards has been appointed as the new executive director of the Woodstock Film Festival, as announced by the organization. This announcement came as the 27th annual festival concluded. Edwards will succeed Meira Blaustein, who is a co-founder of the festival.

## Key facts
- Jessica Edwards has been named the new executive director of the Woodstock Film Festival.
- The announcement was made as the 27th annual festival came to a close.
- Edwards will take over the role from Meira Blaustein, the festival's co-founder.
