Taiwanese technology company imCore Corp. launched Core Studio, an AI filmmaking platform, at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest (TCCF) in Taipei. The platform aims to cover the full production process, including script development, storyboarding, image generation, editing, and color grading.

## Key facts
- Core Studio was launched by imCore Corp. at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest.
- The platform is designed to handle the entire filmmaking process.
- It includes features for script development, storyboarding, image generation, editing, and color grading.
- The development team has roots in animation and visual effects.
