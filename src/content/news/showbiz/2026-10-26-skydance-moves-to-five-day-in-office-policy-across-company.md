Skydance has implemented a new policy requiring all staff to work in the office five days a week. This decision was made by co-CEOs David Ellison and Ynon Kreitz, who previously observed the benefits of such an arrangement at Paramount.

## Key facts
- Skydance's co-CEOs are David Ellison and Ynon Kreitz.
- The company has moved staff from Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery to a five-day in-office workweek.
- The co-CEOs believe this policy is in the best interest of the company and its employees.
