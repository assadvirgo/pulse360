“Gintama: Yoshiwara in Flames,” the latest iteration of the “Gintama” story, previewed at Lucca Comics and Games, Europe’s largest Comic-Con, ahead of its theatrical release in Italy. Director Naoya Andô and producer Ryo Nakamura discussed their continuation of the saga.

## Key facts
- The film is a continuation of the "Gintama" story.
- "Gintama" originated as a manga.
- The preview took place at Lucca Comics and Games. 
- The film is set for a theatrical release in Italy.
