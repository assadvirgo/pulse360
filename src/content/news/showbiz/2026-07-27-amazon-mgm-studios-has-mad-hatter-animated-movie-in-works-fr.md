### Project Overview

While specific plot details remain undisclosed, the film aims to explore the whimsical and eccentric world of the Mad Hatter, a character renowned for his peculiar behavior and memorable tea parties. The Mad Hatter is one of the most recognizable figures from Carroll's narrative, which has inspired numerous adaptations across various media since its publication in 1865.
