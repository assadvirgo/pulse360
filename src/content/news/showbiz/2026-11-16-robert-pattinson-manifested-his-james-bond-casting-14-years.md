Robert Pattinson has been confirmed as the next James Bond, succeeding Daniel Craig, who ended his tenure with the film 'No Time to Die' in 2021. Pattinson expressed that being cast in this iconic role is a "dream come true."

## Key facts
- Robert Pattinson will take over the role of James Bond.
- He succeeds Daniel Craig, who concluded his role in 2021.
- Pattinson described the casting as an "honour of a lifetime."
- He expressed gratitude to the team at Amazon MGM Studios for the opportunity.
