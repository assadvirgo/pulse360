Author Karin Slaughter is considering a sequel in the form of a short story to her 2017 novel "The Good Daughter." This follows her adaptation of the novel into a series for Peacock, where she also served as showrunner.

## Key facts
- Karin Slaughter adapted her novel "The Good Daughter" into a Peacock series.
- She is contemplating a short story sequel focusing on the characters Charlie and Sam.
- Slaughter has expressed that she has "actually been thinking about" this follow-up.
