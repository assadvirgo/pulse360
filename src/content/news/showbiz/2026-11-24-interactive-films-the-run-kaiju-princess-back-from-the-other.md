Moviegoers in Taiwan will have the opportunity to influence the endings of three interactive films using their smartphones. The films, produced by Next Interactive Studio and showcased by Showtime Cinemas, will begin their release on November 27.

## Key facts
- The interactive films include "The Run," "Kaiju Princess," and "Back From the Other World."
- Audiences will be able to vote on the endings by scanning a QR code displayed on the screen.
- The rollout of these titles will occur one after another in Taiwan.
