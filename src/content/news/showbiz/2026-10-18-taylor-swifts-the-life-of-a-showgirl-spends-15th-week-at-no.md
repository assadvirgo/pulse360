Taylor Swift's album "The Life of a Showgirl" has maintained its position at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 chart for a 15th non-consecutive week. The album gained an additional 87,000 equivalent album units this week, bolstered by its "Encore" edition, which includes four new tracks.

## Key facts
- "The Life of a Showgirl" is at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 for the 15th non-consecutive week, according to Variety.
- The album earned 87,000 equivalent album units this week.
- The "Encore" edition features four new tracks, including the Hot 100-topping "Patient Zero."
