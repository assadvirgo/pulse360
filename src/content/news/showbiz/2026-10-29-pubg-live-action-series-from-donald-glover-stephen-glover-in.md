Netflix is developing a live-action series based on the video game "PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds" (PUBG), with Donald Glover attached to star. Glover will also write, executive produce, and direct the series alongside his brother Stephen Glover.

## Key facts
- The live-action series is based on the battle royale video game "PUBG."
- Donald Glover will star in, write, executive produce, and direct the series.
- Stephen Glover will co-write and executive produce the series.
- Michael Schaefer of Neon TV is also involved as an executive producer.
- The series is being developed by Netflix.
