Jack Thorne is adapting Matt Haig's novel "The Humans" for a stage production in London's West End. The play will feature Matt Smith, known for his role in "House of the Dragon," portraying an alien inhabiting the body of a Cambridge academic.

## Key facts
- The stage adaptation is titled "The Humans."
- Matt Smith will play the role of an alien inside a Cambridge academic's body.
- The play is adapted from Matt Haig's novel by Jack Thorne, who wrote "Adolescence."
- Jeremy Herrin will direct the limited run.
- The world premiere is scheduled for next year in London’s West End.
