Nithya Raman is projected to win the Los Angeles mayoral race, defeating Karen Bass. Raman, a city councilmember, ran a progressive campaign against the city's Democratic Party establishment.

## Key facts
- Nithya Raman represents an industry-heavy district in Los Angeles.
- Raman's campaign was described as an insurgent progressive effort.
- She is set to unseat incumbent Karen Bass, who has faced challenges during her term.
- The race was previously considered a statistical tie before Raman took the lead.
