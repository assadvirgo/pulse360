Michael Forest, a prolific character actor known for his roles in Roger Corman's B movies and as Apollo in "Star Trek," has died at the age of 97. He also lent his voice to numerous anime projects and guest-starred in various television series.

## Key facts
- Michael Forest was a longtime character actor.
- He appeared in B movies produced by Roger Corman.
- Forest guest-starred in TV series including "The Adventures of Rin Tin Tin," "The Twilight Zone," and "The Dick Van Dyke Show."
- He was known for voicing characters in numerous anime projects.
