Josephine Decker, director of "Chasing Summer," has expressed her feelings about the isolation in filmmaking and her desire to foster community among filmmakers. She envisions creating a residency for movies that do not rely on scripts, inspired by her experience making her short film "A Stable Marriage."

## Key facts
- Josephine Decker has a plan to create a residency for movies without scripts.
- She made her short film "A Stable Marriage" with minimal written content, using improvisation.
- Decker describes the process of making the short as fun and refreshing.
