The musical adaptation of "The Notebook" will debut in London's West End at the newly renamed Judi Dench Theatre, which was formerly known as the Shaftesbury Theatre. The show is based on Nicholas Sparks' bestselling novel and the popular film featuring Rachel McAdams and Ryan Gosling.

## Key facts
- "The Notebook" musical will be the first production at the Judi Dench Theatre.
- The theatre was previously named the Shaftesbury Theatre.
- The musical features music and lyrics by Ingrid Michaelson.
