Barbara Barrie, an Oscar-, Emmy-, and Tony-nominated actress, has died at the age of 95 in Manhattan. Her daughter confirmed that Barrie's death was medically assisted following a terminal cancer diagnosis.

## Key facts
- Barbara Barrie starred in the original production of "Company" and in the film "Breaking Away."
- She received a Tony nomination for her role in "Company" and an Oscar nomination for "Breaking Away."
- Barrie was widely recognized for her role as the wife of Hal Linden's police captain on the TV show "Barney Miller."
- She died at her home in Manhattan.
