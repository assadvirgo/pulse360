"Dancing With the Stars" has decided to bench Val Chmerkovskiy following an inappropriate joke he made about judge Carrie Ann Inaba. BBC Studios stated that the well-being of everyone involved with the show is "non-negotiable."

- Val Chmerkovskiy has been benched by "Dancing With the Stars."
- The decision follows a joke he made about judge Carrie Ann Inaba.
- BBC Studios emphasized that the well-being of staff is non-negotiable.
- The social media post by Chmerkovskiy was deemed inappropriate and not reflective of the show's values.
