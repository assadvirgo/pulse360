Production has begun on "Hooters: The Movie," which is filming in the Tampa Bay, Florida area. The film features Owen Patrick Joyner, Kyle Clark, and Audrey Trullinger in leading roles.

## Key facts
- "Hooters: The Movie" is currently in production.
- Filming is taking place in the Tampa Bay, Florida area.
- Owen Patrick Joyner and Kyle Clark star as lifelong friends Ted and Lenny.
- The movie tells the origin story of the Hooters chicken wing chain.
