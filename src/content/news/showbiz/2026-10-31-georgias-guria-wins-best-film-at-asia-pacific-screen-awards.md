Georgian filmmaker Levan Koguashvili's film "Guria" won the best film award at the 19th Asia Pacific Screen Awards, which took place on October 30 in Queensland, Australia. Koguashvili shared the prize with producers Olena Yershova and Boris Frumin. The film was produced across seven countries: Georgia, Switzerland, Luxembourg, Bulgaria, Türkiye, Norway, and Saudi Arabia.

## Key facts
- "Guria" was named best film at the Asia Pacific Screen Awards.
- The awards ceremony was held on October 30 at The Star Gold Coast in Queensland, Australia.
- Levan Koguashvili shared the best film prize with producers Olena Yershova and Boris Frumin.
- The film was made in collaboration with seven countries.
