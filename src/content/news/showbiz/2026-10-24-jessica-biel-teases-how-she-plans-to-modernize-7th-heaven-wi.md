Jessica Biel is working on a reboot of the show "7th Heaven" nearly 20 years after its original run. She will serve as an executive producer for the new iteration, which is being developed at CBS Studios.

## Key facts
- Jessica Biel is bringing a reboot of "7th Heaven" to a new generation.
- The reboot is in development at CBS Studios.
- Biel is executive producing the project through her Iron Ocean banner.
