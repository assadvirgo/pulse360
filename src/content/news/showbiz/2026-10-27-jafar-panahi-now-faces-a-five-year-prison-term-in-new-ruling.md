Jafar Panahi, an Iranian filmmaker, has been sentenced to five years and one month in prison by a Tehran Revolutionary Court. This new ruling adds to his previous one-year prison sentence.

## Key facts
- Jafar Panahi is a Cannes Palme d’Or-winning filmmaker.
- He was already facing a one-year prison sentence prior to this new ruling.
- The new sentence totals five years and one month in jail.
- The information was reported by the Emtedad News Agency, an Iranian reformist media outlet.
