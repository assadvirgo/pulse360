Hollywood director Stephen Herek is set to direct the romantic comedy "Emma's Dilemmas," which will be filmed in Italy and New York City. The film will focus on Emma Tate, a young artist from Brooklyn dealing with modern romance.

## Key facts
- Stephen Herek, known for films like "Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure" and "101 Dalmatians," is attached to direct.
- "Emma's Dilemmas" is scheduled to shoot late next year.
- The film will be produced by former Prodigy Media and Italy's Panorama Films.
