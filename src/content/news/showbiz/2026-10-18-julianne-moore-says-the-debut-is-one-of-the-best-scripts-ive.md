The London Film Festival concluded with the screening of "The Debut," a musical comedy directed by Jesse Eisenberg and starring Julianne Moore. Both Eisenberg and Moore expressed admiration for each other during the event held at the Royal Festival Hall.

## Key facts
- "The Debut" closed the London Film Festival on a high note.
- Jesse Eisenberg directed the film and starred alongside Julianne Moore.
- The screening took place at the Royal Festival Hall.
- Eisenberg referred to Moore as his favorite person during the introduction.
