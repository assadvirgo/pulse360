Hubert de la Bouillerie, an editor known for his work on films such as "The Witches of Eastwick" and "Tango & Cash," has died at the age of 75 in Los Angeles. He passed away on August 8.

## Key facts
- Hubert de la Bouillerie edited films including "The Witches of Eastwick" and "Tango & Cash."
- He was involved in additional editing for the 1970 documentary "Woodstock."
- His other credits include "Highlander II: The Quickening," "Mo’ Money," "Doin' Time," "Blank Check," and "Celtic Pride."
