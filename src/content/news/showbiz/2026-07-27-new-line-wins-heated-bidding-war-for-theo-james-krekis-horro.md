## New Line Cinema Acquires Theo James Krekis' Horror Project Amid Competitive Bidding

In a significant development in the film industry, New Line Cinema has secured the rights to the horror package titled "Jealous People Are Ugly People," directed by the Greek/British filmmaker Theo James Krekis. This acquisition follows a competitive bidding war that reportedly involved at least ten interested parties, underscoring the project's high demand and potential impact in the horror genre.

### Overview of the Project

"Jealous People Are Ugly People" is an adaptation of Krekis' short film of the same name, which has garnered attention for its intriguing premise and artistic direction. While specific details regarding the logline remain undisclosed, the project is anticipated to explore themes of jealousy and its psychological ramifications, a subject that resonates with audiences in contemporary society.
