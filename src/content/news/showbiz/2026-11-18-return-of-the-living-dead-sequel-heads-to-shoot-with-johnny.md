A new installment of the cult horror franchise "Return of the Living Dead" is set to begin shooting. The film will feature a cast that includes Johnny Knoxville, Ruby Snowber, Blake Richardson, Doug Jones, and Sean Gunn.

## Key facts
- Johnny Knoxville, Ruby Snowber, Blake Richardson, Doug Jones, and Sean Gunn are set to star in the film.
- The project is written and directed by Steve Wolsh.
- Mason Novick is involved in the production.
