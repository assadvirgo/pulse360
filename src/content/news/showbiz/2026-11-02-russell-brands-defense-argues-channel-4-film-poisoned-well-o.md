Russell Brand's defense argued that a 2023 Channel 4 documentary about him "poisoned the well of justice deliberately" and "fundamentally corrupted" his accusers. This claim was made during the closing defense argument by Brand's lawyer, Ian Winter.

## Key facts
- The defense's argument was presented in response to the Channel 4 documentary.
- The documentary is said to have negatively impacted the perception of Brand's accusers.
- Ian Winter, Brand's lawyer, referenced a phrase from the mid-19th century in his argument.
