Impossible Dream Entertainment is collaborating with Wall to Wall to develop a new series titled "The Invited." This supernatural mystery thriller is inspired by the ghost story novels of bestselling author Wendy Webb. The series will not be based on any single novel but will draw from Webb's body of work.

## Key facts
- Impossible Dream Entertainment is known for producing Oscar-winning films such as "Get Out" and "BlacKkKlansman."
- Wall to Wall is a UK-based production company.
- Helen Black and Jeff Howard are scripting the series.
