The Tokyo International Film Festival's Women’s Empowerment section will focus on individuals who have been marginalized or displaced. This year marks the third iteration of the section, which features a lineup of seven films.

## Key facts
- The Women’s Empowerment section is part of the Tokyo International Film Festival.
- The theme centers on people who have been pushed to move or left without a sense of belonging.
- The section is overseen by programmer Shimizu Yu.
- This year’s lineup includes seven films, with a notable entry being Manuela Martelli’s work.
