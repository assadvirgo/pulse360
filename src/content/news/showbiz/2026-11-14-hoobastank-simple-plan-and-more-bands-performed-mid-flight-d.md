Southwest Airlines revived its “Live at 35” program during a nationwide grounding of air travel, featuring performances from several bands mid-flight. Artists including Hoobastank, The Used, Simple Plan, and Lolo performed on a flight from Los Angeles to Orlando.

## Key facts
- The performances took place on a Southwest flight on November 13.
- The event occurred shortly before all planes were grounded by the FAA.
- The bands were en route to the Vans Warped Tour.
