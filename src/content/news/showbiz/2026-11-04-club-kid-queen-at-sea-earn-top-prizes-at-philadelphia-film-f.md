Lance Hammer's "Queen at Sea" and Jordan Firstman's "Club Kid" received top honors at the Philadelphia Film Festival. The festival, now in its 35th edition, achieved the highest attendance in its history.

## Key facts
- "Queen at Sea" won the narrative jury prize.
- "Club Kid" won the narrative audience award.
- The festival marked its 35th edition this year.
- This edition of the festival set an all-time attendance record.
