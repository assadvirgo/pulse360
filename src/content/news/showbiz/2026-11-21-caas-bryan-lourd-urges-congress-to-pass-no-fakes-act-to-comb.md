Creative Arts Agency CEO Bryan Lourd has called on Congress to pass the NO FAKES Act, which aims to regulate unauthorized AI deepfakes. Lourd expressed his views in a New York Times Opinion piece, emphasizing the importance of individual rights regarding the use of one's likeness.

## Key facts
- Bryan Lourd is the CEO of Creative Arts Agency.
- He urged Congress to pass the NO FAKES Act.
- The bill aims to regulate unauthorized AI deepfakes.
- Lourd stated that individuals should have the right to refuse unwelcome uses of their likeness.
