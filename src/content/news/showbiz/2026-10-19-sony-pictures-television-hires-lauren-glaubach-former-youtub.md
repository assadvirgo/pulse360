Sony Pictures Television has appointed Lauren Glaubach as executive vice president of distribution for digital and social platforms. This is a newly created role aimed at enhancing the company's presence on YouTube and other digital platforms.

## Key facts
- Lauren Glaubach previously worked at YouTube and Pinterest.
- Glaubach will report to Jason Spivak and Paul Littmann, who are presidents of distribution and networks at Sony Pictures Entertainment.
- Her role will focus on expanding Sony Pictures Entertainment's reach across digital platforms for both film and television content.
