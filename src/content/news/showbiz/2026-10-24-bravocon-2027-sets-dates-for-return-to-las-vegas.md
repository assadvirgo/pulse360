Bravo has announced the dates for BravoCon 2027, which will take place in Las Vegas. The event is scheduled for October 22-24, 2027, at the Caesars Forum.

## Key facts
- BravoCon 2027 will be held from October 22-24, 2027.
- The event will take place at the Caesars Forum in Las Vegas.
- This will be the 5th BravoCon experience.
