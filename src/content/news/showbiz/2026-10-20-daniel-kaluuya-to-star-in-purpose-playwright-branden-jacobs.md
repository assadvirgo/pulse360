Daniel Kaluuya will make his New York stage debut in a new play titled "Grass," written by Branden Jacobs-Jenkins. The play follows a mother and son on a surreal road trip through the American South.

## Key facts
- "Grass" is a new play by Branden Jacobs-Jenkins, a Tony Award- and Pulitzer Prize-winner.
- The play features a mother and son on a road trip marked by encounters with historical and allegorical figures.
- The production is described as "equal parts fantasia, memory."
