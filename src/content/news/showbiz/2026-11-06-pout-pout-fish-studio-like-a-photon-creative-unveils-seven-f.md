Like a Photon Creative, an Australian animation studio known for "The Pout-Pout Fish," has announced a slate of seven new animated features. The studio has granted international distribution rights for the entire slate to Sola Media. Production financing is provided by Elevate Production Finance and Alceon Entertainment Partners, while Maslow Entertainment will handle distribution in Australia and New Zealand.
