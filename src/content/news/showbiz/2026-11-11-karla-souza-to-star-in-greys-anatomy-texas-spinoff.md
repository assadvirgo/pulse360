Karla Souza has been cast as a lead in the new Grey's Anatomy spinoff set in West Texas. She is the first actor to join the series, which is produced by Shonda Rhimes and others.

## Key facts
- Karla Souza is known for her role in 'How to Get Away with Murder.'
- The spinoff is produced by 20th Television.
- The series is set in West Texas.
