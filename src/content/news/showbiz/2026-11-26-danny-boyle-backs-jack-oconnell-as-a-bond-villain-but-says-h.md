Danny Boyle has expressed his support for Jack O'Connell as a potential Bond villain in the next James Bond film. Boyle, who previously was set to direct "No Time to Die," noted that O'Connell would have also been a great choice for the role of James Bond.

## Key facts
- Danny Boyle supports Jack O'Connell as a potential Bond villain.
- Boyle was previously set to direct "No Time to Die."
- The lead role of James Bond has now been cast.
