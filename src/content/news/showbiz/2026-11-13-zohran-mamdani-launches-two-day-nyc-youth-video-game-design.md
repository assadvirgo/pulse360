New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani is launching a youth video game design competition this weekend. The event, called the NYC Game Jam, will take place in the city where "Grand Theft Auto 6" developer Rockstar Games is headquartered.

## Key facts
- The NYC Game Jam is a two-day competition.
- It is aimed at young New Yorkers.
- The competition is set to launch on Saturday, ahead of the game's release on November 19.
