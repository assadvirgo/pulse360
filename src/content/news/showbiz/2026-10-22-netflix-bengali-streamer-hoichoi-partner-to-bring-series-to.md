Netflix has partnered with Bengali streamer Hoichoi to provide Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu-language versions of every series Hoichoi launches over the next two years. This partnership was announced in Mumbai, and new titles will be available on Netflix simultaneously with their Bengali debut on Hoichoi.

## Key facts
- Hoichoi will launch series with Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu versions on Netflix.
- The partnership will last for the next two years.
- New titles will stream on Netflix at the same time as their Bengali debut on Hoichoi.
