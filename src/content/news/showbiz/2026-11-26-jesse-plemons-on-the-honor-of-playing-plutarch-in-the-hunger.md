Oscar nominee Jesse Plemons has been cast as Plutarch Heavensbee in the upcoming film "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping." Plemons was the first person cast in the film, which serves as a prequel focusing on Haymitch Abernathy.

## Key facts
- Jesse Plemons plays Plutarch Heavensbee, a Capitol insider.
- Plutarch becomes a key figure in the rebellion in the latter half of Suzanne Collins' original trilogy.
- "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping" is a prequel movie.
