The Hollywood Reporter and the Animation Is Film Festival are collaborating on a live animation roundtable event in Los Angeles. The event will feature several directors and industry figures.

## Key facts
- The event is scheduled for Sunday in Los Angeles.
- Participants include Bill Hader, Leah Nelson, Fawn Veerasunthorn, Phuong Mai Nguyen, Louis Clichy, and Joel Crawford.
- The roundtable will focus on animation.
