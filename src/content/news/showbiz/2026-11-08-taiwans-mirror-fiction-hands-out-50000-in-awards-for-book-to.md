Mirror Fiction, a Taiwanese IP development and production company, awarded over $50,000 in cash prizes to two authors for their book-to-screen adaptations. This event took place during the fifth edition of its biennial awards at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest (TCCF).

## Key facts
- Mirror Fiction is a Taiwanese IP development and production outfit.
- The awards were aimed at helping authors publish their works and adapt them for screen.
- The total cash prizes awarded exceeded $50,000.
- The announcement occurred during the Taiwan Creative Content Fest (TCCF).
- This was the fifth edition of the biennial awards.
