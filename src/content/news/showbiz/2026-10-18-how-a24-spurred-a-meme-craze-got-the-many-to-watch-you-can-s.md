A24's documentary co-directed by Nathan Fielder, featuring Elizabeth Holmes, has achieved significant success, opening to $15 million. The film has garnered attention from Gen Z, leading to over 2,000 sellouts and long lines.

## Key facts
- The documentary follows controversial Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes.
- A24's marketing strategy has resonated with Gen Z audiences.
- The film opened to a milestone of $15 million.
- There were over 2,000 sellouts for the documentary.
- Audiences experienced long lines for the 3-hour film.
