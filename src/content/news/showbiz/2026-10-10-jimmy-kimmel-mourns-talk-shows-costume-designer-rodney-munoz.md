## Jimmy Kimmel Pays Tribute to Late Costume Designer Rodney Muñoz

The entertainment community is in mourning following the death of Rodney Muñoz, the esteemed costume designer for "Jimmy Kimmel Live," who passed away at the age of 60. The news of Muñoz's passing was shared by a close friend, with the date of his death reported as October 2.

### A Legacy of Creativity

Rodney Muñoz made a significant impact on the world of television through his work on "Jimmy Kimmel Live," where he served as the costume designer since the show's inception in 2002. His creative vision and dedication to his craft earned him recognition, including an Emmy Award, solidifying his status as a talented figure in the industry.

Muñoz's designs were not just costumes; they were an integral part of the show's identity, enhancing the comedic and thematic elements presented on stage. His ability to blend humor with fashion allowed the show's hosts and guests to shine in their roles, contributing to the overall success of the program.

Kimmel's statement resonates with many who worked alongside Muñoz, highlighting not only his professional achievements but also his warm personality and the kindness he extended to colleagues and friends alike.
