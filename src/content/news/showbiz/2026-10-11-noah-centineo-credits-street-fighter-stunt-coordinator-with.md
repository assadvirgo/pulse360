## Noah Centineo Acknowledges Stunt Coordinator's Role in Bringing 'Street Fighter' to Life

As anticipation builds for the release of the highly awaited film adaptation of the iconic video game "Street Fighter," actor Noah Centineo has expressed his appreciation for the work of stunt coordinator John Valera. Centineo, who portrays the character Ken Masters in the Paramount production, emphasized Valera's significant contribution to authentically translating the game's distinct combat styles to the big screen.

## A Focus on Authenticity

In a recent interview, Centineo highlighted Valera's meticulous approach to choreography, which he believes is crucial for capturing the essence of "Street Fighter." The film, set to premiere on October 16 in IMAX, aims to resonate with both long-time fans of the franchise and newcomers alike. Valera's expertise in martial arts and stunt coordination has been pivotal in ensuring that the fight sequences not only reflect the game's "specific fighting style" but also provide an engaging cinematic experience.

## The Legacy of 'Street Fighter'

"Street Fighter," originally released as a video game by Capcom in 1987, has maintained a dedicated fan base for decades. The franchise is renowned for its diverse roster of characters, each with unique fighting techniques and backgrounds. As the film adaptation seeks to honor this legacy, the choreography of fight scenes becomes a central element in bridging the gap between the interactive world of gaming and the narrative-driven format of cinema.

## Centineo's Commitment to the Role

Centineo's portrayal of Ken Masters is part of a broader effort to revitalize the "Street Fighter" series for a new generation. The actor has undergone extensive training to embody the character authentically, aligning his performance with the physicality and style that fans expect. His collaboration with Valera has allowed him to immerse himself in the character's fighting techniques, which he describes as "insane fights" that promise to captivate audiences.

## Looking Ahead

As the release date approaches, both Centineo and Valera are optimistic about the film's reception. The combination of Valera's stunt coordination and Centineo's dedication to his role is expected to create a dynamic viewing experience. Fans of the franchise are eagerly awaiting the film, hoping it will successfully capture the spirit of the original game while introducing its rich universe to a wider audience.

With the film's premiere just around the corner, the collaboration between Centineo and Valera stands as a testament to the importance of authenticity in adaptations of beloved properties. As viewers prepare to experience the action-packed world of "Street Fighter," the contributions of dedicated professionals behind the scenes will undoubtedly play a crucial role in shaping the film's success.
