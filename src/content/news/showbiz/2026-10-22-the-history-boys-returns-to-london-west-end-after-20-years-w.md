Alan Bennett's play "The History Boys" is returning to London's West End after a 20-year absence. Nicholas Hytner, who directed the original production in 2004, will reprise his role for this new staging.

## Key facts
- "The History Boys" is set in 1980s Britain.
- The play follows eight pupils at a Sheffield boys' grammar school.
- The original production won six Tony Awards on Broadway.
