Japan's film industry is exploring new financing models beyond the traditional production committee system. This system, where multiple companies finance a film and share its rights, has been beneficial in spreading risk but has limitations, such as capping budgets and complicating overseas investment.

## Key facts
- Japan's production committee system involves joint financing of films by a group of companies.
- This system helps spread risk across the industry.
- Limitations of the system include capping budgets and making it difficult for overseas investors to participate.
- The decision-making process can be slowed down due to the need for consensus among rights holders.
