A New Mexico judge has dismissed an indictment against actor Timothy Busfield due to prosecutorial misconduct. The judge found that prosecutors did not act in a fair and impartial manner during the grand jury proceedings.

## Key facts
- A New Mexico judge dismissed the indictment against Timothy Busfield.
- The judge stated that prosecutors failed to act in an impartial manner during the grand jury proceedings.
- Busfield was indicted on charges of groping a 7-year-old boy on the set of "The Cleaning Lady," a Fox show. 
- Refiling of the charges remains a possibility.
