Shondaland has hired Amy Joy Rivkin as vice president of production and Karyn Smith-Forge as vice president of scripted series. Both appointments are part of the company's expansion.

## Key facts
- Amy Joy Rivkin has an impressive track record in steering complex, premium productions.
- Karyn Smith-Forge has vast experience across scripted genres.
- Rivkin joins Shondaland from Media Res, where she worked on The Morning Show.
- Prior to Media Res, Rivkin was part of Netflix's Original Series Drama group.
- Shondaland is known for producing Netflix series, including Bridgerton.
