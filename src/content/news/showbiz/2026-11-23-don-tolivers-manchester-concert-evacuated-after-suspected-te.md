Don Toliver's concert at Co-op Live in Manchester, England was evacuated after a substance believed to be tear gas was released into the crowd. The incident occurred towards the end of the concert, according to a statement from Greater Manchester Police.

## Key facts
- Don Toliver's concert was held at Co-op Live in Manchester, England.
- The concert ended in an evacuation due to the release of a substance believed to be tear gas.
- Greater Manchester Police confirmed the incident in a statement.
