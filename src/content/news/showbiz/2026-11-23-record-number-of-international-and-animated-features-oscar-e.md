title: Record Number of International and Animated Features Oscar-Eligible This Season
---

A record number of films are eligible for the Oscars this season, according to the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences. The announcement includes 104 international features, 38 animated features, and 190 documentary features competing for shortlists.

## Key facts
- 104 international features are in the running for Oscar shortlists.
- 38 animated features are also eligible for the Oscars.
- 190 documentary features are competing for shortlists.
