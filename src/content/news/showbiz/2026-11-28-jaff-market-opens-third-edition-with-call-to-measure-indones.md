The JAFF Market, Indonesia's largest film market, has opened its third edition at the Jogja Expo Center in Yogyakarta. Market director Linda Gozali emphasized the importance of measuring the growth of the local cinema industry by quality, not just volume.

## Key facts
- The JAFF Market is billed as Indonesia's biggest film market.
- The third edition of the market is taking place at the Jogja Expo Center in Yogyakarta.
- The event runs for three days alongside the Jogja-Netpac Asian Film Festival (JAFF).
- Linda Gozali is the market director.
