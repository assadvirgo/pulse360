Chris Pratt expressed his desire for James Gunn to direct a fourth "Guardians of the Galaxy" film following Gunn's recent departure from DC Studios. Pratt believes it would be "awesome" to reunite with Gunn for the project.

## Key facts
- Chris Pratt stated he would love to work with James Gunn again on a fourth "Guardians of the Galaxy" film.
- Gunn and co-chief Peter Safran were ousted from DC Studios on November 9.
- Pratt has not spoken with Gunn since the news of his exit from DC Studios.
