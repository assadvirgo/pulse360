The Writers Guild of America East is assisting its members in making their work less accessible to artificial intelligence models. The union has started providing free access to a software called Cloak, which makes documents "unreadable" by AI while maintaining their formatting.

## Key facts
- The Writers Guild of America East is offering support to its members regarding AI.
- The union provides free access to a software named Cloak.
- Cloak renders documents "unreadable" by AI models while preserving formatting.
