Microdrama platform FlareFlow is set to launch in Thailand on November 2 through AIS Play, the entertainment service of Advanced Info Service (AIS). The launch is part of a deal with Singapore-based distributor Rock Networks.

## Key facts
- FlareFlow is owned by COL Group.
- The subscription cost for FlareFlow will be THB39 ($1.15) per month.
- AIS Play is the platform through which FlareFlow will be available in Thailand.
