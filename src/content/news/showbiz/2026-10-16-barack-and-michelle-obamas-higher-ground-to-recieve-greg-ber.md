## Barack and Michelle Obama’s Higher Ground to Receive Greg Berlanti Social Impact Award

Higher Ground, the media company established by former President Barack Obama and former First Lady Michelle Obama, has been selected to receive the prestigious Greg Berlanti Social Impact Award. This recognition is part of the sixth annual Teens & Screens Summit, organized by the Center for Scholars & Storytellers at UCLA, and will take place on October 22 at UCLA’s Luskin Center.

### Acknowledging Social Impact

The Greg Berlanti Social Impact Award is designed to honor individuals and organizations that have made significant contributions to social change through their work in media and storytelling. The award is named after Greg Berlanti, a prominent television producer and director known for his commitment to social issues and advocacy through entertainment.

In a statement regarding the award, the Center for Scholars & Storytellers highlighted that Higher Ground's initiatives exemplify the power of storytelling to foster understanding and inspire action on critical societal issues. The organization has been recognized for its commitment to producing content that addresses themes of inclusion, empowerment, and social justice.

### Higher Ground’s Mission and Achievements

Founded in 2018, Higher Ground aims to create compelling narratives that resonate with diverse audiences. The company has produced a variety of projects across different media, including documentaries, films, and podcasts. Notable works include the Netflix documentary "American Factory," which won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature in 2020. This film explores the cultural clashes that occur when a Chinese billionaire reopens a factory in post-industrial Ohio.

Higher Ground’s projects often highlight stories that might otherwise go untold, focusing on the experiences of marginalized communities and individuals. The Obamas have expressed a strong belief in the transformative power of storytelling, emphasizing its ability to bridge divides and foster empathy among viewers.

### The Significance of the Award Presentation

The presentation of the Greg Berlanti Social Impact Award will take place during a summit that brings together young people, educators, and industry leaders to discuss the intersection of media, technology, and social issues. The Teens & Screens Summit aims to empower the next generation of storytellers, encouraging them to use their voices to effect positive change.

Greg Berlanti himself will present the award to representatives from Higher Ground, marking a moment of recognition not only for the Obamas but also for the broader mission of using media as a tool for social impact. Berlanti's own body of work, which includes popular television series that tackle various social themes, aligns closely with the objectives of Higher Ground.

### Looking Ahead

As Higher Ground continues to develop new projects, the recognition from the Center for Scholars & Storytellers serves as a reminder of the important role that media plays in shaping public discourse and promoting social change. The upcoming award presentation is expected to draw attention to the ongoing efforts of the Obamas and their team to leverage storytelling for the greater good.

The Teens & Screens Summit will provide a platform for discussions around the future of media and its potential to address pressing social issues, making it a significant event for both the entertainment industry and the communities it serves.
