Studiocanal's genre label Sixth Dimension is developing a feature adaptation of the multiplayer horror game "The Headliners." The game was released by Kafi Games in 2025 and was developed by Lucas Bouyoux and Bruno Grégoire.

- Studiocanal's Sixth Dimension is in early development on the adaptation.
- "The Headliners" is a multiplayer horror game.
