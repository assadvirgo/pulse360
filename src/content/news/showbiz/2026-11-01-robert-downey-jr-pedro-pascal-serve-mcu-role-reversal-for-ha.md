Robert Downey Jr. and Pedro Pascal participated in a Halloween costume swap, trading their Marvel Cinematic Universe roles. Downey dressed as Pascal's character, Mr. Fantastic, while Pascal donned the costume of Downey's character, Dr. Doom, in anticipation of the upcoming film "Avengers: Doomsday."

## Key facts
- Robert Downey Jr. and Pedro Pascal swapped their MCU roles for Halloween.
- Downey dressed as Mr. Fantastic, a character played by Pascal.
- Pascal dressed as Dr. Doom, a character played by Downey.
- The costume swap occurred ahead of the movie's premiere on December 18.
