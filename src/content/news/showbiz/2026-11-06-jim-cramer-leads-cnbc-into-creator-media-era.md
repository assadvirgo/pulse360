CNBC is increasing its investment in Jim Cramer, who leads the network's "Mad Money" and co-anchors "Squawk on the Street." Cramer will expand his investing club by launching a premium tier in 2027, which will provide subscribers with new access to live shows and workshops.

## Key facts
- CNBC is investing more heavily in Jim Cramer.
- Cramer leads "Mad Money" and co-anchors "Squawk on the Street."
- He will launch a premium tier for his investing club in 2027.
- The premium tier will offer subscribers access to live shows and workshops.
