Sudanese filmmaker Mohamed Kordofani is preparing to showcase his new film "About Love & September Laws" at Rome's MIA Market, scheduled for October 19-23. This follows the success of his debut feature "Goodbye Julia," which premiered at Cannes.

## Key facts
- Mohamed Kordofani is the director of "Goodbye Julia," which premiered in Cannes’ Un Certain Regard sidebar.
- Kordofani has attracted multiple partners for his follow-up film "About Love & September Laws."
- The film will be presented at the MIA Market in Rome. 
- Kordofani launched his production outfit, Klozium Studios, in 2020.
