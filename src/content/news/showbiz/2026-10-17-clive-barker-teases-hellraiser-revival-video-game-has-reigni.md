Clive Barker has expressed interest in reigniting ideas for the Hellraiser film franchise following the release of the Hellraiser: Revival video game. He is ready to explore themes of pain and pleasure again in the franchise.

## Key facts
- Clive Barker is the writer/director of the original 1987 Hellraiser movie.
- The recent release of the Hellraiser: Revival video game has inspired Barker to revisit the film franchise.
- Barker mentioned he is planning to keep his involvement in the franchise.
