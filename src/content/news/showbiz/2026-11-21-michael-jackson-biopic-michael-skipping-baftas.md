The biopic "Michael," about Michael Jackson, will not be submitted for the BAFTAs. This decision was confirmed by Deadline, indicating that producers GK Films and distributor Universal will not participate in the awards.

## Key facts
- The film "Michael" is a record-breaking box office success.
- It is directed by Antoine Fuqua.
- The film has grossed over a billion dollars.
- Producers GK Films and distributor Universal will not submit the film for BAFTA consideration.
