The Tony Awards will continue to be broadcast on CBS through 2032 under a new five-year deal. This agreement was announced on Wednesday and involves the American Theatre Wing and The Broadway League.

## Key facts
- CBS has secured a new broadcast agreement with the American Theatre Wing and The Broadway League.
- The Tony Awards have been broadcast on CBS since 1978.
- The new deal extends the broadcast of the awards show for an additional five years.
