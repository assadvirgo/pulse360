Stephanie March will return to "Law & Order: Special Victims Unit" as assistant district attorney Alexandra Cabot in the ninth episode of Season 28. March has portrayed this character in over 100 episodes since she joined the main cast in Season 2.

## Key facts
- Stephanie March will reprise her role as Alexandra Cabot in the ninth episode of "Law & Order: SVU" Season 28, according to Variety.
- March was part of the main cast from Season 2 to Season 5 of the series.
- Her last appearance on the show was in Season 19, episode 19, titled "Sunk Cost Fallacy," according to Deadline.
