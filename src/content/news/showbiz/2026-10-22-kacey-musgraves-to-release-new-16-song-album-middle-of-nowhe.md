Kacey Musgraves is set to release a new 16-song album titled “Middle of Nowhere Vol. 2: Tex Mex Tapes” on January 15, 2027. This album will feature all-new material and will be released via Lost Highway Records, just eight months after the release of Vol. 1.

## Key facts
- The album is titled “Middle of Nowhere Vol. 2: Tex Mex Tapes.”
- It will contain 16 songs of all-new material.
- The release date is January 15, 2027.
- The album will be released via Lost Highway Records.
- It continues the themes established in Vol. 1.
