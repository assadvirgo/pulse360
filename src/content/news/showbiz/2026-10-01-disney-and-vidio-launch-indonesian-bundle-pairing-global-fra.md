## Disney+ and Vidio Join Forces to Launch Comprehensive Streaming Bundle in Indonesia

In a significant development for the Indonesian streaming landscape, Disney+ has announced its first partnership with local streaming service Vidio. This collaboration aims to provide subscribers with a unique package that combines global entertainment franchises with local content and live sports.

### The New Subscription Offering

The newly launched subscription service, titled "Vidio Ultimate Disney+ All Screen," is designed to cater to a diverse audience by integrating popular global franchises such as Marvel, "Star Wars," and various Korean series with local programming and live sports events. This innovative bundle allows subscribers to enjoy a wide range of content under a single plan, enhancing the viewing experience for Indonesian audiences.

### Content Highlights

The partnership brings together an impressive array of content. Subscribers will have access to Disney+ Standard, which includes blockbuster films and series from the Disney universe, alongside Vidio Ultimate All Screen, which features local Indonesian originals and a variety of live sports. Notably, the bundle includes coverage of major sporting events such as the Premier League and UEFA Champions League soccer, appealing to sports enthusiasts in the region.

### Strategic Implications

This collaboration marks a strategic move for both Disney+ and Vidio as they seek to expand their reach in Indonesia's competitive streaming market. By combining global content with local offerings, the partnership aims to attract a broader audience, catering to both fans of international franchises and those interested in local productions. 

The integration of live sports is particularly significant, as it addresses a growing demand for sports content among Indonesian viewers. Live sports have proven to be a key driver of subscriptions in the streaming industry, and this partnership positions both platforms to capitalize on that trend.

### Market Context

Indonesia, with its rapidly growing digital landscape, presents a lucrative opportunity for streaming services. The country has seen a surge in internet penetration and smartphone usage, leading to an increase in demand for online content. By partnering with Vidio, Disney+ is not only entering a new market but also leveraging Vidio's established presence and understanding of local consumer preferences.

### Conclusion

The launch of the "Vidio Ultimate Disney+ All Screen" bundle represents a significant step in the evolution of Indonesia's streaming market. By combining global franchises with local content and live sports, Disney+ and Vidio are poised to deliver a comprehensive entertainment experience that meets the diverse needs of Indonesian viewers. As the competition in the streaming sector intensifies, this partnership could set a new standard for how content is packaged and delivered in the region.
