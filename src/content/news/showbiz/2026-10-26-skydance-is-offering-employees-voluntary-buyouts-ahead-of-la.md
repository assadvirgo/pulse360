Skydance is offering certain employees a voluntary buyout as part of its integration efforts with Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery. This program is described as a "one-time offer" and was communicated to staff in a memo from chairman and CEO David Ellison and co-CEO Ynon Kreiz.

## Key facts
- Skydance is integrating the businesses of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery.
- The company is offering a voluntary buyout to certain employees.
- The buyout is characterized as a "one-time offer."
- The announcement was made in a memo to staff by David Ellison and Ynon Kreiz.
