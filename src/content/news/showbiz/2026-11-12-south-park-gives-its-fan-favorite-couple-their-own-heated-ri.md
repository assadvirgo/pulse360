The animated series "South Park" features characters Tweek and Craig in a new episode titled "We Be Clubbin’." The episode addresses issues related to pay-to-play youth athletics and critiques parents who boast about their children's participation.

## Key facts
- Tweek and Craig are fan-favorite characters from "South Park."
- The episode "We Be Clubbin’" critiques pay-to-play youth athletics.
- It also addresses parents who brag about their children's involvement in these activities. 
- The episode includes a commentary on being swindled by a "fat con man" who exploits anxieties.
