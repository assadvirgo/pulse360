## Composer Simon Franglen Discusses the Impact of AI on Film Music at Ghent's WSA Film Music Days

At the recent World Soundtrack Awards (WSA) Film Music Days in Ghent, Belgium, composer Simon Franglen shared his insights on the evolving landscape of film music, particularly in relation to artificial intelligence (AI). Franglen, known for his work on the highly anticipated "Avatar: Fire and Ash," emphasized the irreplaceable human element in music creation during a panel discussion that resonated with many in the industry.

### The Human Element in Music Creation

Franglen highlighted the unique experience of collaborating with a large ensemble of musicians, noting the emotional depth that such interactions bring to the creative process. "We had hundreds of musicians in a room, pouring their emotion out in that precise second," he remarked. This sentiment underscores a growing concern among artists regarding the potential of AI to replicate the nuances and emotional authenticity that human musicians contribute to their work.

The composer’s reflections come at a time when the film industry is increasingly exploring the use of AI technologies in various capacities, including music composition. While AI can generate melodies and harmonies, Franglen and other panelists argued that the emotional connection and spontaneity of live performances cannot be replicated by algorithms.

### Industry Response to AI Integration

The discussion at the WSA Film Music Days is part of a broader dialogue within the creative community about the implications of AI on artistic integrity and job security. Many composers and musicians are voicing their concerns about the potential for AI to overshadow human creativity, which they argue is essential for the authenticity of film music.

Franglen's remarks reflect a sentiment shared by many artists who believe that while technology can enhance the creative process, it should not replace the human touch that defines art. The panel emphasized the importance of defending artistic expression in the face of technological advancements.

### The Future of Film Music

As the film industry continues to evolve, the integration of AI is likely to become more prevalent. However, the consensus among industry professionals at the WSA Film Music Days suggests a cautious approach. Many believe that a balance must be struck between utilizing technology and preserving the core values of artistic creation.

The conversation around AI in film music is not merely about the tools themselves but also about the broader implications for artists and their livelihoods. The need to advocate for the value of human creativity is paramount as the industry navigates these changes.

### Conclusion

The discussions at the WSA Film Music Days serve as a reminder of the enduring importance of human emotion and creativity in the arts. As composers like Simon Franglen continue to champion the irreplaceable qualities of live music, the industry is called to reflect on how to embrace technological advancements without compromising the essence of artistic expression. The future of film music may depend on this delicate balance, ensuring that while technology can assist, it does not overshadow the artistry that defines the medium.
