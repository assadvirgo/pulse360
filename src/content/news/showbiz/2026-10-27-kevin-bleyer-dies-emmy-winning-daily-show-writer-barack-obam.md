Kevin Bleyer, an Emmy- and Peabody Award-winning television writer, died in a car accident on October 25 in Millrift, Pennsylvania. He was known for his work on The Daily Show and Politically Incorrect with Bill Maher, as well as for contributing speeches for President Barack Obama.

## Key facts
- Kevin Bleyer won both an Emmy and a Peabody Award.
- He worked on The Daily Show and Politically Incorrect with Bill Maher.
- Bleyer contributed speeches for President Barack Obama, including addresses to the annual White House Correspondents' Dinner.
- He died in a car accident on a rural road in Millrift, Pennsylvania.
