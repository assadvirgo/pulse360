Jorrybell Agoto, the star of the film Filipiñana, has died at the age of 32 after battling lymphoma, according to her family. Epicmedia Productions, the company behind Filipiñana, shared a tribute on Instagram expressing their love for Agoto.

## Key facts
- Jorrybell Agoto died on November 17.
- She was the star of Rafael Manuel’s award-winning debut feature Filipiñana.
- Agoto's family confirmed her death was due to lymphoma.
- Epicmedia Productions posted a tribute on Instagram.
