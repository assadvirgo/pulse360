As casting rumors for the next James Bond intensify, actors Robert Pattinson and Nicholas Hoult have reportedly participated in screen tests for the role. They join a list of contenders following Daniel Craig's tenure as the iconic spy.

## Key facts
- Robert Pattinson and Nicholas Hoult have screen-tested for the role of James Bond.
- They are among several actors, including Jack Lowden, Josh O’Connor, and Callum Turner, who have also auditioned.
- The casting process follows Daniel Craig's 15-year run as Bond.
