Mahershala Ali stars in the film "Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother," directed by Bassam Tariq. The movie features a complex narrative centered around religion and follows Ali's character, Latif, a Muslim contract killer who grapples with his faith after the death of his wife.

## Key facts
- "Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother" is an action movie directed by Bassam Tariq.
- Mahershala Ali plays the role of Latif, a staunch Muslim and father of three.
- Latif works as a contract killer and faces challenges in reconciling his faith following his wife's death.
