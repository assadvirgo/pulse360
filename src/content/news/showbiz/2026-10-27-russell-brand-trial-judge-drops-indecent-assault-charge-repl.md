A judge has dismissed an indecent assault charge against Russell Brand, replacing it with a count of attempted indecent assault. This decision was made during the trial at Southwark Crown Court, where the judge cited "insufficient evidence" to support the original charge.

## Key facts
- The judge overseeing the trial is Mr Justice Bennathan.
- The indecent assault charge was dismissed and replaced with a count of attempted indecent assault.
- The decision was made midway through the trial.
- The prosecution is concluding its case at Southwark Crown Court.
