Iain Armitage, the star of "Young Sheldon," expressed that he was the cast member who was "the most happy about the show ending." He mentioned that he was not "going home and crying all day" when the seven-season series concluded in 2024, according to Variety. 

## Key facts
- Iain Armitage is an actor on the CBS sitcom "Young Sheldon."
- The series is set to end after seven seasons in 2024.
- Armitage stated he was the most happy about the show's conclusion. 
- He indicated he was not upset about the ending of the show.
