Alex Edelman, an Emmy-winning comedian, expressed disappointment over the Kennedy Center becoming politicized, which he believes negatively impacts comedians. He feels that this shift represents a loss for performers in the comedy community.

## Key facts
- Alex Edelman is an Emmy-winning comedian and actor.
- He won an Emmy for his 2024 HBO/Max special "Just For Us."
- Edelman also received a special Tony for the original Broadway staging.
- He laments the politicization of the Kennedy Center and its implications for comics.
