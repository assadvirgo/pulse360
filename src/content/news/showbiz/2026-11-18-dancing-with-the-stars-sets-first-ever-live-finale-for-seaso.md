For the first time, "Dancing With the Stars" will air its finale live. The three-hour finale is scheduled for November 24 and will be broadcast on ABC and Disney+.

## Key facts
- The finale will air from 8:00-11:00 p.m. ET.
- It will also be available in different time zones: 7:00-10:00 p.m. CT, 6:00-9:00 p.m. MT, and 5:00-8:00 p.m. PT.
- The finale will re-air from 8:00-11:00 p.m. PT.
