The film "La Bola Negra," directed by Spanish duo Los Javis, has won the audience award at the 2026 BFI London Film Festival. The film features notable actors including Penélope Cruz, Glenn Close, and Miguel Bernardeau.

## Key facts
- "La Bola Negra" is a queer historical drama.
- The film was directed by Javier Calvo and Javier Ambrossi, known as Los Javis.
- It has also received a nomination for best international independent film.
- Grace Harper Brighouse's documentary "Dependence" won the audience award for best British discovery at the same festival.
