The film "Clayface," produced by Peter Safran and James Gunn, has reportedly earned over $3.5 million in Thursday previews. The film is part of DC's strategy to enter the lower-budget genre market. Domestic weekend forecasts for "Clayface" range between $20 million and $25 million.

## Key facts
- "Clayface" earned an estimated $3.5 million in Thursday previews.
- The film is part of DC's lower-budget genre movie initiative.
- Domestic weekend forecasts for "Clayface" are between $20 million and $25 million.
- A successful opening could mark the second consecutive No. 1 opener for the newly combined Paramount-Warner Bros.
