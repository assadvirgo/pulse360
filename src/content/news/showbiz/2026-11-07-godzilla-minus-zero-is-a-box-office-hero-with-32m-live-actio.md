'Godzilla Minus Zero' has achieved a record-breaking $32 million opening weekend, marking the highest for a live-action foreign-language film. The film's success is significantly attributed to its performance in IMAX and premium large formats (PLFs), which accounted for 74% of the weekend gross.

## Key facts
- 'Godzilla Minus Zero' opened with a gross of $32 million.
- The film earned $14.5 million on its opening Friday.
- 74% of the weekend gross came from IMAX and PLFs.
- This opening surpasses the first weekend gross of Universal's 'The Odyssey', which had 52% of its $123.5 million opening from IMAX and PLFs.
