Olivier winner George Maguire has been cast as Captain Jack Sparrow in the London stage production "Disney’s Pirates of the Caribbean: The Immersive Adventure," produced by Secret Cinema and TodayTix. The show has also extended its run in London.

## Key facts
- George Maguire will play Captain Jack Sparrow.
- Phil Daniels will portray Captain Barbossa.
- Lily Blunsom is cast as Elizabeth Swann.
- Harrison Wilde will play Will Turner.
- Gleanne Purcell-Brown is Anamaria.
