Politico has corrected a report regarding Susie Wiles, Donald Trump's Chief of Staff, and her involvement in the departure of Katie Zacharia as White House press secretary. Following this correction, an attorney for Politico cautioned the Trump administration against using the correction as a basis for "unconstitutional retaliation."

## Key facts
- Politico issued a correction to a previous report about Susie Wiles' role in Katie Zacharia's exit.
- The correction came after a threat from a Trump official.
- Politico's attorney warned against potential unconstitutional retaliation from the Trump administration.
