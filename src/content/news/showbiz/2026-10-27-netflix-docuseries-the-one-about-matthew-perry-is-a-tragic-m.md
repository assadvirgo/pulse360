The Netflix docuseries "The One About Matthew Perry" presents a portrayal of the late actor's struggles with addiction. The three-part series features interviews with Perry's sister and various friends, but notably lacks participation from his "Friends" castmates or other prominent figures in his life.

## Key facts
- The docuseries is titled "The One About Matthew Perry."
- It consists of three parts and is directed by Mary Robertson.
- Interviews include Perry's sister Mia, friends, former girlfriends, and colleagues.
- The series does not feature interviews with Perry's "Friends" castmates or other notable collaborators.
