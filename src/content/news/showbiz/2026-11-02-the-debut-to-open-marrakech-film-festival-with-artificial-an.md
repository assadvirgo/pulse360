Jesse Eisenberg's film "The Debut" will open the 23rd Marrakech International Film Festival. The film features Julianne Moore as a shy housewife who becomes a passionate Method actor after being cast in a community theater musical.

## Key facts
- "The Debut" is directed by Jesse Eisenberg.
- Julianne Moore stars as the lead character.
- The film will be part of the out-of-competition gala at the festival.
