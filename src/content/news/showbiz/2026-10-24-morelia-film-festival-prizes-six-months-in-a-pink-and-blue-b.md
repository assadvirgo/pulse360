Bruno Santamaría Razo’s film "Six Months in a Pink and Blue Building" has won the top Ojo award at the 24th Morelia Film Festival. The festival is set to conclude on October 25.

## Key facts
- "Six Months in a Pink and Blue Building" is Bruno Santamaría Razo’s fiction debut.
- The film is described as a “lush, poetic film rooted in memories.”
- It is the first Mexican feature to win the top Ojo award at the festival.
