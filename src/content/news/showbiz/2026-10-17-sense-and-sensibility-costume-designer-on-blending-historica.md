The costume designer for "Sense and Sensibility," Grace Snell, is focusing on blending historical accuracy with modern styling in her work for the film adaptation of Jane Austen's 1811 novel. The film features Daisy Edgar-Jones and Esmé Creed-Miles as the Dashwood sisters, Elinor and Marianne.

## Key facts
- Grace Snell is the costume designer for the new adaptation of "Sense and Sensibility."
- The film is directed by Georgia Oakley.
- Daisy Edgar-Jones and Esmé Creed-Miles play the roles of Elinor and Marianne Dashwood, respectively.
- Snell conceptualizes the costumes in distinct chapters, particularly focusing on the differences between country and city styles.
