Russell Brand's appearance on The Wendy Williams Show in October 2017 has been referenced by the prosecution during his rape trial in London. This occurred during closing remarks at Southwark Crown Court, where Brand faces multiple charges.

## Key facts
- Russell Brand is on trial for three charges of rape, three counts of sexual assault, and one of attempted sexual assault.
- The prosecution used Brand's comments from his 2017 appearance on The Wendy Williams Show in their closing arguments.
- The trial is taking place at Southwark Crown Court in London.
