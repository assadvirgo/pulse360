Danny Boyle confirmed that the redacted tabloid headlines from the British version of his Netflix drama "Ink" will be shown in full in the U.S. theatrical and streaming versions. This announcement was made during the Deadline Legend Awards event.

## Key facts
- Danny Boyle is the director of the Netflix drama "Ink."
- The film focuses on the founding of the UK tabloid, The Sun.
- Redacted headlines will be included in the U.S. versions of the film.
