Sean Astin, President of SAG-AFTRA, announced the formation of the President's Healthcare Task Force to address the challenges many union members face in qualifying for health insurance. The initiative aims to confront what Astin describes as a "broken healthcare system," as a substantial majority of members struggle to meet the union's minimum eligibility requirements for insurance.

## Key facts
- SAG-AFTRA members are facing difficulties in qualifying for health insurance.
- Sean Astin announced the President's Healthcare Task Force to tackle the issue.
- The task force aims to address the problems within the healthcare system affecting union members.
