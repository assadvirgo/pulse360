## Alia Shawkat Reflects on Reunion with Kristen Stewart in 'The Wrong Girls'

In a recent screening of the upcoming film *The Wrong Girls*, actress Alia Shawkat shared her thoughts on reuniting with Kristen Stewart, with whom she previously starred in the 2010 film *The Runaways*. The two actresses, who portrayed members of the iconic all-girl rock band, have come a long way since their early roles, and Shawkat's reflections provide insight into their evolving careers and the dynamics of their friendship.

### A Journey from 'The Runaways' to 'The Wrong Girls'

*The Runaways*, which was released over a decade ago, showcased the rise of the influential all-girl rock band in the 1970s. In this film, Shawkat and Stewart played pivotal roles, but Shawkat recalls a challenging experience during filming. "We were children on that set," she remarked, highlighting the pressures they faced as young actors. She noted that during the production, she felt constrained, stating she "literally was not allowed to speak." This sentiment underscores the often rigid environments that young actors must navigate in the film industry.

Fast forward to *The Wrong Girls*, a Neon stoner comedy written and directed by Dylan Meyer, where the two actresses reunite in a more collaborative and supportive setting. The film, set to premiere in theaters on August 14, marks a significant shift in their professional relationship. Shawkat expressed excitement about the opportunity to work alongside Stewart again, emphasizing the growth they have both experienced since their earlier collaboration.

### The Evolution of Their Careers

The reunion in *The Wrong Girls* not only reflects their personal friendship but also highlights the changing landscape of Hollywood, particularly for women in film. Both Shawkat and Stewart have carved out successful careers, taking on diverse roles that challenge traditional narratives. Shawkat has gained recognition for her work in television and independent films, while Stewart has become a prominent figure in both mainstream and arthouse cinema.

Their collaboration in *The Wrong Girls* is indicative of a broader trend in the industry, where female-led projects are increasingly gaining visibility and support. The film's comedic approach allows both actresses to explore new facets of their talents, further solidifying their status as versatile performers.

As the release date for *The Wrong Girls* approaches, anticipation is building among fans of both actresses.
