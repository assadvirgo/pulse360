Japanese filmmaker Ishikawa Hiroshi is set to release his first feature film in 13 years, titled "the blue we touch" (original title: "Awo no Jo"). The film is currently in post-production and is scheduled for release in Japan in 2027.

## Key facts
- Ishikawa Hiroshi is known for previous works including "Tokyo.sora," "Su-ki-da," and "Petal Dance."
- "the blue we touch" is being produced by Bitters End.
- The project was unveiled ahead of the Tokyo Film Festival.
