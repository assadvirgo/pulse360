Fox's reboot of "Baywatch" is set to premiere on January 6 at 9 p.m. The series continues the story of Hobie Buchannon, who is portrayed as a grown-up version of the character from the original 1990s series.

## Key facts
- The reboot of "Baywatch" will air on Fox.
- It premieres on January 6 at 9 p.m.
- Stephen Amell stars as Hobie Buchannon, now a Baywatch Captain.
