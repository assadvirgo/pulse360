The showrunner of the FX drama "The Drop," Malcolm Spellman, has outlined a multi-season plan for the series, which is a spinoff of "Snowfall." He indicated that the first season serves as just a "dash of what's coming."

## Key facts
- Malcolm Spellman is the showrunner of "The Drop."
- The series focuses on the rise of West Coast hip-hop.
- Record labels will continue to have "all the leverage" in the storyline.
- The future of the romance between central characters Wanda and Leon will be explored.
- The series ultimately centers around themes of family.
