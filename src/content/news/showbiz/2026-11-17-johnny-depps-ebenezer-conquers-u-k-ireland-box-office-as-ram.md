Johnny Depp's film "Ebenezer," directed by Ti West, has debuted at the top of the U.K. and Ireland box office, earning £3.9 million ($5.2 million). The film features Depp as Ebenezer Scrooge and includes performances by Rupert Grint, Andrea Riseborough, and Ian McKellen.

## Key facts
- "Ebenezer" is a gothic reimagining of the classic story.
- The film collected £3.9 million ($5.2 million) in its opening weekend.
- It is directed by Ti West.
