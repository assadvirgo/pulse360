In 2001, three films significantly impacted Hindi-language cinema: "Dil Chahta Hai," "Lagaan," and "Chandni Bar." These films are being revisited as they mark their 25th anniversary.

## Key facts
- "Dil Chahta Hai," directed by Farhan Akhtar, introduced a new screen language focused on friendship and young adulthood.
- "Lagaan," directed by Ashutosh Gowariker, transformed a village cricket match into an epic narrative about resistance.
- "Chandni Bar," directed by Madhur Bhandarkar, explored the largely unseen world of its setting.
