Elon Musk's Grok has released the first episode of an AI-generated series based on "The Odyssey." This follows Musk's earlier promise of a full-length, historically accurate film created entirely by AI.

## Key facts
- The first episode is 7 minutes long and was shared on X.
- The release comes months after Musk's announcement regarding the AI tool Grok Imagine.
- The company described the project as a "creative collaboration."
