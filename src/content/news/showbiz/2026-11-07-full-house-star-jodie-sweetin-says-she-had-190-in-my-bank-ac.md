Jodie Sweetin, known for her role as Stephanie Tanner on "Full House," discussed her financial difficulties prior to filming the reboot "Fuller House." She revealed that she had only $190 in her bank account before the show began.

## Key facts
- Jodie Sweetin gained fame from the sitcom "Full House."
- She faced financial struggles before the 2016 reboot "Fuller House."

- She mentioned borrowing money during the first week of filming.
