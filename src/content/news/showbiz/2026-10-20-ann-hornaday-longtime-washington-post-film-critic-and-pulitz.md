Ann Hornaday, a longtime film critic for The Washington Post, has died at the age of 66. She passed away at her home in Baltimore after battling glioblastoma, a form of brain cancer.

## Key facts
- Ann Hornaday was the film critic at The Washington Post for over 20 years.
- She joined The Washington Post in 2002 and was with the publication until 2025.
- Hornaday was a finalist for the Pulitzer Prize.
