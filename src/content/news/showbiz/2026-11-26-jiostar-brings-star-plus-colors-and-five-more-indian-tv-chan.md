JioStar has launched seven Indian television channels on the live TV service Fubo in the U.S. This includes popular Hindi-language channels such as Star Plus and Colors.

## Key facts
- Seven JioStar television channels are now available on Fubo in the U.S.
- The channels include Star Plus, Colors, Star Bharat, Colors Rishtey, Colors Cineplex, MTV India, and News18 English.
- The channels primarily feature Hindi-language entertainment and English-language news content.
