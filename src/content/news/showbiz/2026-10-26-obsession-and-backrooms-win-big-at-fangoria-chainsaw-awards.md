The Fangoria Chainsaw Awards celebrated achievements in horror film, television, and gaming, with "Obsession" winning the most awards this year. The event premiered on Shudder and was hosted by Devon Sawa.

## Key facts
- "Obsession" won five awards, including Best Director for Curry Barker and Best Lead Performance for Inde Navarrette.
- The awards are fan-voted.
- The ceremony premiered on Shudder.
