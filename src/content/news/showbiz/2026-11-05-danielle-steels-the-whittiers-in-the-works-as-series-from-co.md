Cottage Industry Entertainment, founded by former Hallmark Media executive Lisa Hamilton Daly and Warner Bros. and Legendary executive Alysia Cotter Thomas, has announced a series adaptation of Danielle Steel's book "The Whittiers." This project is part of their first slate under a first-look deal with Fox Entertainment Studios.

## Key facts
- Cottage Industry Entertainment was launched last year by Lisa Hamilton Daly and Alysia Cotter Thomas.
- The company has a first-look deal with Fox Entertainment Studios.
- "The Whittiers" is a book by Danielle Steel that will be adapted into a series.
