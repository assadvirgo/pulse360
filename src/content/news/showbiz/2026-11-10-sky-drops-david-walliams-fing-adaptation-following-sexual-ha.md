Sky has dropped the upcoming fantasy feature "Fing!" following sexual harassment allegations against David Walliams. Walliams wrote the children's book on which the film is based, co-wrote the screenplay, and co-starred in the film.

## Key facts
- "Fing!" was set to be distributed in the U.K.
- The film is based on a children's book written by David Walliams.
- Walliams co-wrote the screenplay and co-starred in the film alongside Taika Waititi and Mia Wasikowska.
- The film premiered at Sundance.
