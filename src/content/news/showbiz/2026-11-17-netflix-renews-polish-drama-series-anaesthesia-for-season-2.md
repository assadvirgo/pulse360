Netflix has renewed the Polish drama series "Anaesthesia" for a second season. The show has performed well, reaching the Top 10 list in Poland shortly after its launch and maintaining a position there.

- Netflix has ordered a second season of "Anaesthesia" (Znieczulenie).
- The series quickly hit the Top 10 list in Poland after its launch.
- "Anaesthesia" is currently fifth on Netflix's global chart.
