Irma P. Hall, known for her roles in "Soul Food" and "The Ladykillers," has died at the age of 91, according to her family. Her passing was confirmed on Tuesday.

## Key facts
- Irma P. Hall played Josephine “Big Mama” Joseph in the 1997 film "Soul Food."
- She also portrayed the character Minerva in "Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil" in the same year.
- Hall was a Dallas schoolteacher for nearly three decades.
- Her family announced her death, but a cause was not specified.
