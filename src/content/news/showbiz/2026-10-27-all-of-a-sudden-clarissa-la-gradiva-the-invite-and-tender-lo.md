The Gotham Film & Media Institute announced the nominations for the 36th edition of The Gothams, with five films leading the nominations.

## Key facts
- "All of a Sudden," "Clarissa," "La Gradiva," "The Invite," and "Tender Loving Care" each received four nominations.
- A total of 42 feature films and 25 performances were recognized across 11 award categories.
