Disney has officially greenlit a series adaptation of "Aquamarine," which will air on both Disney Channel and Disney+. The live-action series was initially announced with a pilot order in June.

## Key facts
- The series adaptation of "Aquamarine" has been picked up by Disney.
- It will be available on both Disney Channel and Disney+.
- The series stars Kenzi Richardson, Briella Guiza, Theodore Dalton, and Annika Reese.
- Emma Roberts and Jake McDorman are also involved in the project.
