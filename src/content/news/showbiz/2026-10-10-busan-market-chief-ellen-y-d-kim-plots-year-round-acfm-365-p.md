## Busan Market Chief Proposes Year-Round ACFM 365 Platform Amid Funding Concerns

Ellen Y.D. Kim, the director of the Asian Contents & Film Market (ACFM), has unveiled an ambitious vision for the future of the market, suggesting a transformation into a year-round platform known as ACFM 365. In an interview with Variety, Kim emphasized the need for the market to expand beyond its traditional four-day event held annually in Busan, South Korea.

### Vision for ACFM 365

The proposed ACFM 365 aims to establish a permanent database and matchmaking service that would facilitate ongoing connections between content creators, distributors, and industry professionals throughout the year. Kim envisions this initiative as a means to enhance collaboration and innovation within the Asian content and film sectors, allowing stakeholders to engage continuously rather than being limited to the annual event.

"ACFM 365 would provide a vital resource for the industry, ensuring that opportunities for networking and collaboration do not end when the market closes," Kim stated. This initiative reflects a growing recognition of the importance of sustained engagement in the rapidly evolving content landscape, particularly in Asia, where demand for diverse and high-quality content continues to surge.

### Funding Challenges

Despite the ambitious plans for ACFM 365, Kim expressed concerns regarding the stagnation of public funding for the market. She noted that while the ACFM has grown significantly in recent years, financial support from public sources has not kept pace with its expansion. This discrepancy poses challenges for the market's sustainability and its ability to implement new initiatives.

"The growth of ACFM has been remarkable, but we need to ensure that we have the necessary resources to support our vision for the future," Kim remarked. She highlighted the importance of securing additional funding to develop the ACFM 365 platform and to maintain the market's relevance in a competitive global environment.

### The Importance of ACFM

The ACFM plays a crucial role in the Asian film and content industry, serving as a hub for networking, deal-making, and showcasing new projects. It attracts a diverse array of participants, including filmmakers, producers, and distributors from across the region and beyond. The market's focus on Asian content is particularly significant as the global appetite for such material continues to grow.

By proposing a year-round platform, Kim aims to capitalize on this momentum, fostering a more dynamic ecosystem for content creation and distribution. The ACFM 365 initiative could not only enhance the market's offerings but also strengthen the position of Asian content in the global marketplace.

### Conclusion

As the ACFM prepares for its next iteration, Ellen Y.D. Kim's vision for a year-round platform underscores the need for innovation and adaptability in the face of evolving industry demands. However, the challenge of securing adequate public funding remains a critical concern that must be addressed to realize this ambitious plan. The success of ACFM 365 could redefine the landscape of the Asian content and film market, positioning it for sustained growth and influence in the years to come.
