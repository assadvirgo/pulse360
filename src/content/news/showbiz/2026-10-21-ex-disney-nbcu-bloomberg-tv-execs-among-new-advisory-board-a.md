Former executives from Disney, NBCUniversal, and Bloomberg TV have joined a new advisory board at the storyboarding and previsualization platform Pzaz. This move is part of Pzaz's plan to expand internationally across North America, EMEA, and Asia Pacific.

## Key facts
- Former Disney, NBCUniversal, and Bloomberg TV executives are now part of Pzaz's advisory board.
- The new board includes Paul Robinson, Rose Stacey, Lindsey Oliver, Jonathan Levi, and Weerada Sucharitkul.
- Pzaz aims to expand its operations internationally across North America, EMEA, and Asia Pacific.
