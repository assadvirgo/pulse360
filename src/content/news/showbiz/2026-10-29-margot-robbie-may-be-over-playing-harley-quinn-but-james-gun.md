James Gunn has indicated that Harley Quinn will "almost certainly" return in the expanding DC Universe. However, Margot Robbie, who portrayed the character in three DCEU films, may not reprise her role.

## Key facts
- James Gunn stated that Harley Quinn will "almost certainly" return in the DC Universe.
- Margot Robbie may not return to play Harley Quinn.
- Gunn made these comments on "The Suicide Squad: Is It Canon? The Official Podcast."
