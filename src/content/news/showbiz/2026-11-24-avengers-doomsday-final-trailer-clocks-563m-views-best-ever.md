The final trailer for "Avengers: Doomsday" has garnered 563 million views, marking the best performance ever for Disney and the second most viewed trailer of all time, according to Deadline. The trailer's significant traffic suggests a strong potential for record-breaking box office openings for the film. 

## Key facts
- The final trailer for "Avengers: Doomsday" received 563 million views.
- This view count is the highest ever for a Disney film.
- It is the second most viewed trailer in history.
- The film is directed by the Russo Brothers.
