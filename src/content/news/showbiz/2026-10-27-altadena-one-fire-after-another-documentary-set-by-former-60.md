A new documentary titled "Altadena: One Fire After Another" is being produced by climate journalist Bill McKibben and former "60 Minutes" producer David Gelber. The film will focus on the aftermath of the 2025 Eaton Fire in Los Angeles and will depict the experiences of Altadena residents as they work to rebuild their homes and preserve their community.
