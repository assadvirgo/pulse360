Remington Scott, known for his work on the motion capture of Gollum in "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers" and for creating digital humans for "Spider-Man 2," will lead Eros Imagination. This new venture is a creative technology and experiences business established by Eros Innovation, an AI company.

## Key facts
- Remington Scott is a Hollywood technologist.
- He led the motion capture for Gollum in "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers."
- He built the first digital humans using Light Stage technology for "Spider-Man 2."
- Eros Imagination is a new business set up by Eros Innovation.
