Kenneth Atchity, a producer known for "The Meg" and a literary agent, has died at the age of 82. His career included producing over 30 films and writing more than 20 books.

## Key facts
- Atchity produced more than 30 films throughout his career.
- He wrote over 20 books and negotiated various publishing and entertainment deals.
- He published hundreds of titles through his own imprint, Story Merchant Books.
- He worked on films including "Joe Somebody," "Hysteria," and "Life or Something Like It."
