Perspective Films has announced its inaugural slate of documentary films, according to Variety. The initiative, led by independent producer Jess Devaney, features five documentaries covering various subjects.

## Key facts
- Perspective Films is a new documentary co-production initiative.
- The initiative is led by independent producer Jess Devaney.
- The first five documentaries will address topics including federal immigration enforcement in Minneapolis, the war in Gaza, and the invention of C-SPAN.
