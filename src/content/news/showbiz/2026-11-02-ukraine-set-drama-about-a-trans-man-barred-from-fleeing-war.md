German director and screenwriter Isabelle Stever's film "I Rarely Wake Up Dreaming" has won the Tokyo International Film Festival's Ethical Film Award. The award, established in 2023 with the support of Sumitomo Corporation, recognizes films that address issues such as the environment, poverty, and discrimination.

## Key facts
- "I Rarely Wake Up Dreaming" is a drama set in Ukraine.
- The film focuses on a trans man who is barred from fleeing the war.
- The Ethical Film Award was created in 2023.
- A jury selects the winner from three nominees.
