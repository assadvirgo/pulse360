Luc Besson commented on the possibility of making the next 'Terminator' film, suggesting he could do so only if the character expressed emotional sentiments, such as missing his mother. He also rejected the notion that he reinvented action cinema, attributing his creative freedom to a lack of experience and the absence of studio constraints.

## Key facts
- Luc Besson joked about making the next 'Terminator' film.
- He stated that the Terminator would need to express missing his mother.
- Besson rejected the idea that he reinvented action cinema.
- He attributed his creative freedom to having less experience and fewer studio constraints.
