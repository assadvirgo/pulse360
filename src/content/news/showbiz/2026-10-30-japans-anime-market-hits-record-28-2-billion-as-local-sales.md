Japan's anime market reached a record of JPY4.45 trillion ($28.2 billion) in 2025, marking a 15.8% increase from the previous year. Over half of the total spending comes from overseas consumers, according to the Association of Japanese Animations.

## Key facts
- The anime market in Japan hit a record of JPY4.45 trillion ($28.2 billion) in 2025.
- This figure represents a 15.8% increase compared to the previous year.
- Overseas consumers account for well over half of the total spending on anime.
- The preliminary figures were presented at TIFFCOM by Hasegawa Masahiro, editor-in-chief of the Association of Japanese Animations.
