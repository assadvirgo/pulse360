Zyri, known for voicing the titular character in "Steven Universe" and for their role as Brian Corbett on "The Goldbergs," has come out as transgender. In a Substack post, Zyri discussed the challenges faced as a child star and their journey in understanding their gender identity.

## Key facts
- Zyri voiced the main character in "Steven Universe."
- Zyri played Brian Corbett on "The Goldbergs."
- Zyri announced their transgender identity in a Substack post.
- The post addressed the challenges of being a child star and dealing with gender dysphoria.
