Myrsini Aristidou, a Cypriot filmmaker, has begun an Oscar campaign for her debut feature film, "Hold Onto Me," which won the audience prize at the Sundance Film Festival. The film is notable for being selected to represent Cyprus at the Oscars.

## Key facts
- Myrsini Aristidou's film "Hold Onto Me" won the audience prize in the World Cinema Dramatic competition at Sundance.
- The film is set to represent Cyprus in the upcoming Oscars.
- Aristidou describes her team as feeling like underdogs in the Oscar race.
