Park Min-young will star in the upcoming Korean office romance series "Nine to Six," which will be available on Prime Video. The series also features Yook Sung-jae and Go Soo in leading roles.

## Key facts
- "Nine to Six" is a 12-episode series.
- Park Min-young plays the character Kang Yi-ji, a senior manager on the legal team at Chronic Motors.
- Yook Sung-jae, a member of the K-pop group BtoB, co-stars in the series.
- Go Soo is also part of the main cast.
