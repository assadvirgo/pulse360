Disney will feature an animated "Alterna-Cast" for the 2027 Super Bowl, including C-3PO from the "Star Wars" franchise. The golden droid will guide viewers through ESPN's coverage of Super Bowl LXI.

## Key facts
- C-3PO will be part of an animated telecast for the 2027 Super Bowl.
- The animated "Alterna-Cast" will be available on Disney Channel, XD, and Disney+.
- The event will be part of ESPN's coverage of Super Bowl LXI.
