Matsumura Shingo's new film "Between Us" is featured in the Tokyo International Film Festival's Nippon Cinema Now section. The film explores the emotional wounds between childhood best friends Makoto and Kasumi, who drift apart after an incident during a family camping trip and reunite six years later.

## Key facts
- "Between Us" is directed by Matsumura Shingo.
- The film is part of the Tokyo International Film Festival's Nippon Cinema Now section.
- It follows the story of childhood best friends Makoto and Kasumi.
- The friends drift apart after an incident on a family camping trip.
- They reunite six years later.
