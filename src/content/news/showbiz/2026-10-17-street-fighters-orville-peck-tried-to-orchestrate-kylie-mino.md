Orville Peck, who stars as Vega in the new "Street Fighter" movie, attempted to arrange a cameo for Kylie Minogue, who famously portrayed Cammy in the original game. Peck mentioned that he could easily reach out to Minogue, indicating his close relationship with the pop icon.

## Key facts
- Kylie Minogue played Cammy in the original "Street Fighter" game over 30 years ago.
- Orville Peck is a friend and collaborator of Minogue.
- Peck revealed his efforts to orchestrate Minogue's cameo in the latest film adaptation.
