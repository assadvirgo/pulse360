Former NCT member Lucas Wong has made his acting debut in the Taiwanese drama series "A Time," which was unveiled by director Tsao Jui-yuan at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest. The series is based on Taiwan's campus folk music movement from the 1970s.

## Key facts
- "A Time" is directed by Tsao Jui-yuan, known for previous works like "Crystal Boys" and "A Touch of Green."
- The project took eight years to develop.
- The drama explores themes of friendship and love over four decades.
