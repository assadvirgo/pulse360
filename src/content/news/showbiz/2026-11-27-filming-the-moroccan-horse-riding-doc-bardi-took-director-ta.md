Director Tala Hadid spent over four years filming the documentary "Bardi," which focuses on the Moroccan equestrian tradition of Tbourida. The project took her into communities where this centuries-old ritual is practiced.

## Key facts
- "Bardi" explores the Tbourida, an equestrian ritual rooted in warfare.
- The ritual involves horsemen charging in a single line and firing rifles in alignment.
- Tala Hadid was embedded in Moroccan communities for over four years during the filming.
