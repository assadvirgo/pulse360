Courtney B. Vance has taken over the role of Zeus in the upcoming Season 3 of "Percy Jackson and the Olympians," following the death of Lance Reddick. Vance expressed his intention to honor Reddick's legacy in the role.

## Key facts
- Courtney B. Vance is returning for Season 3 of "Percy Jackson and the Olympians."
- Vance has taken over the role of Zeus from the late Lance Reddick.
- Vance remembered Reddick as "a great, great man."
