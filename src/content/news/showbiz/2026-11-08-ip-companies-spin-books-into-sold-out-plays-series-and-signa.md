Taiwanese companies discussed the potential of original stories to evolve into various formats, including stage plays, series, fragrances, and travel itineraries, during a session at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest (TCCF) in Taipei. The focus was on cross-sector intellectual property and its role in fostering industry growth.

## Key facts
- The session took place at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest (TCCF) in Taipei.
- Companies highlighted the transformation of original stories from books into multiple formats.
- The discussion emphasized the collaborative nature of developing intellectual property for mutual financial benefit.
