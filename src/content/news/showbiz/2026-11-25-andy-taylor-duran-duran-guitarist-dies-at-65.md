Guitarist Andy Taylor, known for his role in the band Duran Duran, has died at the age of 65. He passed away at his home from prostate cancer while surrounded by family.

## Key facts
- Andy Taylor was a guitarist for Duran Duran during the band's commercial peak in the early 1980s, according to Variety.
- He was also a founding member of the supergroup The Power Station, as reported by Hollywood Reporter.
- Taylor's wife, Tracey, shared a statement on behalf of their family regarding his passing.
