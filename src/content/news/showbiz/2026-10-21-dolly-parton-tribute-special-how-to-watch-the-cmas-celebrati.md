A tribute special honoring Dolly Parton will feature several prominent country music artists. The event, titled "CMA Presents I Will Always Love You: A Celebration of Dolly Parton," is set to air on October 21.

## Key facts
- The tribute will include performances by Reba McEntire, Carrie Underwood, Lainey Wilson, and Kelsea Ballerini.
- The special is a two-hour event celebrating Dolly Parton's life and legacy.
- It is organized by the Country Music Association (CMA).
