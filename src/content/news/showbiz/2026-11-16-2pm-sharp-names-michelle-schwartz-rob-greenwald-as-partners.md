2PM Sharp has promoted Michelle Schwartz and Rob Greenwald to Partners of the strategic communications agency. This decision follows the firm's significant growth in the filmmaker/content and comedian sectors since its launch in 2024.

## Key facts
- 2PM Sharp was co-founded by Lindsay Galin and Jeff Raymond.
- Michelle Schwartz and Rob Greenwald are longtime colleagues at 2PM Sharp.
- The agency has experienced outstanding growth in specific sectors of its business.
