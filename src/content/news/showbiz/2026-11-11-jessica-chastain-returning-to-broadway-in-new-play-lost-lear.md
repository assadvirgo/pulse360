Jessica Chastain will return to Broadway in a new play titled "Lost Lear," co-starring Corey Stoll and Michael Urie. The play, written by Dan Colley, is set to begin a 16-week run at the Booth Theatre on March 29, 2027.

## Key facts
- Jessica Chastain is an Oscar winner and Tony nominee.
- "Lost Lear" is described as a play that puts a meta spin on "King Lear."
- Chastain's last Broadway appearance was in the 2023 revival of "A Doll's House."
- This will be her first time appearing in a new show on Broadway.
- The play will also feature Michael Urie and Corey Stoll.
