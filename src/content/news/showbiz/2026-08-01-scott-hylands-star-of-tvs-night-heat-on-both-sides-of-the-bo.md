Scott Hylands, a distinguished actor known for his compelling performances on both Canadian and American television, has died at the age of 83. His career spanned several decades, during which he became a familiar face in the entertainment industry, particularly for his role in the popular cop show "Night Heat."

Hylands' most recognized role came with the police drama "Night Heat," which aired from 1985 to 1989. Hylands played the role of Detective Frank C. McCabe, a character that resonated with viewers and contributed to the show's popularity.

"Night Heat" was notable for its gritty portrayal of urban crime and its focus on character development, elements that Hylands embodied through his performance.
