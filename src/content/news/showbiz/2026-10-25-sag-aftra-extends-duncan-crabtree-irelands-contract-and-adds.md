The SAG-AFTRA board has voted to extend Duncan Crabtree-Ireland's contract as national executive director and to add CEO to his title. The contract extension will keep him in the position until June 20, 2032.

## Key facts
- The contract extension was approved with a 98.34% vote in favor.
- Crabtree-Ireland's original contract was set to expire on June 21, 2028.
- The financial terms of the new agreement were not disclosed.
