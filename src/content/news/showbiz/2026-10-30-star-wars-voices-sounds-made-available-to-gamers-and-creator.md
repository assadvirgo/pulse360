Voicemod, a company based in Spain, has announced a licensing deal with Disney to make audio clips from Star Wars characters available to users. The Mandalorian and Grogu will be the first characters featured, with plans to include Marvel characters in the future.

## Key facts
- Voicemod is a maker of sound boards and voice-changing technology.
- The audio clips will be available to gamers and creators.
- The Mandalorian and Grogu are the initial characters included in the offering.
- Marvel characters are expected to be part of the offering in the future.
