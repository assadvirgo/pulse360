French animation filmmaker Sylvain Chomet has responded to accusations that generative AI was used in his animated short for Dior, which features a voice cast including Robert Pattinson and Rihanna. Chomet asserts that "everything was drawn by hand."

## Key facts
- Sylvain Chomet is a four-time Oscar nominee known for "The Triplets of Belleville" and "The Illusionist."
- The animated short for Dior features a voice cast that includes Robert Pattinson, Rihanna, Josh O’Connor, LaKeith Stanfield, Louis Garrel, and Camille Cottin.
- Chomet emphasizes that the animation was entirely hand-drawn.
