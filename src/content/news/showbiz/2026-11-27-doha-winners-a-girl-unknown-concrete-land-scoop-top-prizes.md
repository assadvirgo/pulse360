Chinese director Zou Jing’s film A Girl Unknown won the Best Narrative prize at the Doha Film Festival. The festival concluded with the announcement of the winners, highlighting the film's narrative centered on a young girl found abandoned as a baby. 

## Key facts
- A Girl Unknown won the $75,000 Best Narrative prize at the Doha Film Festival.
- The film is Zou Jing's first feature and is set in China.
- The festival recently concluded its second edition with a revamped format.
