BET announced the creation of the Chaka Khan Humanitarian Award to honor musicians and cultural figures for their contributions to humanitarianism, community service, and social justice. The award will be presented annually starting in 2027 at the Soul Train Awards.

## Key facts
- The Chaka Khan Humanitarian Award was announced by BET on Friday.
- It aims to recognize outstanding contributions to humanitarianism, community service, and social justice.
- The award will be presented annually at the Soul Train Awards beginning in 2027.
