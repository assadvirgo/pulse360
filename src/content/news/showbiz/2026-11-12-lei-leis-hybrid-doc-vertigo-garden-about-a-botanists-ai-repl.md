Boutique sales company Asian Shadows has acquired "Vertigo Garden," a new feature from Chinese filmmaker and animation artist Lei Lei. The film is set to have its world premiere in the Envision Competition at the International Documentary Film Festival Amsterdam (IDFA). This marks Lei's second feature, and IDFA has nominated it for the Beeld en Geluid IDFA ReFrame Award.

## Key facts
- "Vertigo Garden" is a hybrid documentary about a botanist's AI-replicated memories.
- The film has been acquired by Asian Shadows.
- It will premiere in the Envision Competition at IDFA.
- "Vertigo Garden" is Lei Lei's second feature.
- The film has been nominated for the Beeld en Geluid IDFA ReFrame Award.
