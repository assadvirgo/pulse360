Director Ron Howard has clarified that a sequel to "How the Grinch Stole Christmas" featuring Jim Carrey will only proceed if it has the potential to be great. He emphasized the need for caution regarding the project. 

## Key facts
- Ron Howard stated that he and Jim Carrey will not move forward with a sequel unless they believe it can be great.
- The comments come months after the sequel was announced.
- Howard highlighted the cautious approach they are taking regarding the film's development.
