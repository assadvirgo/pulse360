Morocco's city of Marrakech has gained attention recently following the appointment of its longtime mayor, Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri, as the country's first female prime minister. The city, known for its historic ochre-colored walls, is preparing for an upcoming festival that will celebrate both established cinema artists and newcomers.

## Key facts
- Marrakech is Morocco's fourth biggest city.
- Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri was appointed as the country's first female prime minister at the end of September.
- The city is referred to as the Red City due to the color of its historic center's walls.
