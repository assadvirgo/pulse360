## Regina Hall Joins Dwayne Johnson in 'Lizard Music'

In an exciting development for the film industry, actress Regina Hall has officially joined the cast of the upcoming live-action/CGI hybrid film, **Lizard Music**. The project is being produced by Amazon MGM Studios and United Artists and is directed by Benny Safdie, known for his unique storytelling and visual style.

### A Star-Studded Ensemble

Regina Hall will be sharing the screen with Dwayne Johnson, who is not only starring in the film but also taking on the role of producer. The ensemble cast further includes Theo Leber, Alicia Silverstone, and Tim Heidecker, promising a diverse range of performances that will likely appeal to a broad audience.

### Plot Overview

**Lizard Music** centers around a young boy, played by Theo Leber, who finds himself left to his own devices. The narrative unfolds as he stumbles upon a fantastical world, blending elements of live-action and CGI to create an engaging visual experience. While specific plot details remain under wraps, the premise hints at a whimsical adventure that could resonate with both children and adults.
