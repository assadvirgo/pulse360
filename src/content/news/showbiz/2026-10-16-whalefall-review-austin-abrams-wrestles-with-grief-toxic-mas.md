Austin Abrams stars in "Whalefall," a survival/body horror film based on Daniel Kraus' 2023 novel. The film, written and directed by Brian Duffield, explores themes of grief and toxic masculinity as Abrams' character, Jay, dives in the Pacific Ocean to find the remains of his late father.

## Key facts
- "Whalefall" features Austin Abrams in the lead role.
- The film is based on a novel by Daniel Kraus.
- Brian Duffield is the writer and director of the film.
- The story involves Jay diving to find his father's remains after his father's suicide.
