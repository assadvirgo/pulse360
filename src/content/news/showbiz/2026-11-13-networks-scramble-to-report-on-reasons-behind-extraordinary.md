A nationwide ground stop of all U.S. flights was ordered by the FAA due to "extraordinary circumstances" and "reasons of safety." The order was prompted by a communication issue affecting all Air Route Control Centers.

## Key facts
- The FAA implemented a nationwide ground stop on Friday.
- The order was due to "extraordinary circumstances" and "reasons of safety."
- Secretary of Transportation Sean Duffy stated that the ground stop followed a communication issue impacting all Air Route Control Centers.
- FAA technicians are currently investigating the issue.
