## Leslie Iwerks Explores Disney's Legacy in "Disney Worldbuilders"

Filmmaker Leslie Iwerks, known for her insightful documentaries that reveal the intricacies of Disney's creative processes, is set to premiere her latest work, "Disney Worldbuilders," on Disney+. This documentary continues her exploration of the magic behind the Disney brand, focusing on the visionaries who are shaping its future.

### A Legacy of Storytelling

Iwerks has built a reputation for her in-depth storytelling, having previously directed acclaimed documentaries such as "The Pixar Story," "Disneyland Handcrafted," and "The Imagineering Story." Each of these films has provided audiences with a unique perspective on the artistic and technical efforts that go into creating the beloved Disney parks and films. With "Disney Worldbuilders," Iwerks aims to highlight the contributions of contemporary creators who are carrying forward the legacy of Walt Disney.

### Spotlight on Modern Visionaries

In "Disney Worldbuilders," Iwerks features notable figures such as Jennifer Lee, James Cameron, and Jared Bush. Each of these individuals has played a significant role in the evolution of Disney's storytelling and technological advancements. 

Jennifer Lee, who co-directed the blockbuster "Frozen," has been instrumental in shaping Disney's approach to animated storytelling, emphasizing character development and emotional depth. Her work reflects a commitment to inclusivity and representation, aligning with Disney's ongoing efforts to resonate with diverse audiences.

James Cameron, renowned for his groundbreaking work in film technology, has collaborated with Disney on various projects, including the "Avatar" franchise. His innovative techniques in visual effects have set new standards in the industry, showcasing the potential of immersive storytelling.

Jared Bush, a director and writer known for his work on "Zootopia," brings a fresh perspective to Disney's narrative style. His focus on social themes and moral lessons has contributed to the studio's reputation for creating films that are both entertaining and thought-provoking.

### Continuing Walt Disney's Vision

Iwerks emphasizes that the documentary is not just about individual achievements but rather about how these creators embody and extend Walt Disney's original vision. Walt Disney was a pioneer in blending storytelling with technology, and Iwerks believes that the current generation of filmmakers and storytellers is continuing that tradition. 

"Disney Worldbuilders" aims to inspire viewers by showcasing the passion and creativity that drive these modern-day visionaries. Iwerks hopes that the documentary will encourage audiences to appreciate the hard work and dedication that goes into crafting the Disney experience, both in parks and films.

### Conclusion

As "Disney Worldbuilders" prepares for its premiere, Leslie Iwerks invites viewers to delve into the world of Disney through the eyes of those who are shaping its future. By exploring the legacies of influential figures like Jennifer Lee, James Cameron, and Jared Bush, the documentary serves as a reminder of the enduring impact of Walt Disney's vision and the ongoing evolution of storytelling in the entertainment industry. The film promises to be a celebration of creativity, innovation, and the magic that continues to define Disney.
