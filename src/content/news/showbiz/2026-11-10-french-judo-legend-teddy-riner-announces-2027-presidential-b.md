French judo superstar Teddy Riner has announced his candidacy for the French presidency in 2027. Riner, a five-time Olympic gold medalist and multiple world champion, made the announcement in a speech shared on social media.

## Key facts
- Teddy Riner is a five-time Olympic gold medalist and multiple world champion in judo.
- He has never held political office.

- His announcement was made in a fiery speech posted on social media.
