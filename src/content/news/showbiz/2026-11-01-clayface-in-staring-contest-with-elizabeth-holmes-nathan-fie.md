Warner Bros' film "Clayface" is competing for the top spot at the box office over the Halloween weekend, reportedly earning $10.4 million. However, A24's "You Can See Everything" claims a slightly higher total of $10.6 million. 

## Key facts
- "Clayface" earned $10.4 million over the weekend.
- "You Can See Everything" reported earnings of $10.6 million.
- "Clayface" made $4.2 million on Halloween.
- Nathan Fielder and Elizabeth Holmes' film collected $3.58 million from moviegoers.
- The duo's film is projected to earn $3.45 million on Sunday.
