## Toby Emmerich Joins FilmNation's Infrared as Consultant

FilmNation Entertainment has announced that Toby Emmerich, the former chair of Warner Bros. Motion Pictures, will be consulting for its production label, Infrared. This strategic move is aimed at enhancing the development of genre films with significant franchise potential.

### A New Chapter for Infrared

Infrared, a label under FilmNation, has carved out a niche for itself by focusing on genre films that can be expanded into larger franchises. The label has already begun to make waves in the industry with its upcoming projects, including "Painter," a film written by Derek Kolstad and featuring notable actors Amber Midthunder and Walton Goggins. Emmerich's extensive experience in the film industry is expected to bolster Infrared's efforts in producing compelling content that resonates with audiences.

### Emmerich's Industry Expertise

Toby Emmerich has a long-standing reputation in Hollywood, having played a pivotal role in shaping the direction of major film projects during his tenure at Warner Bros. His leadership was instrumental in the success of various franchises, including the DC Extended Universe and the "Harry Potter" series. With over two decades of experience in the industry, Emmerich brings a wealth of knowledge in film development, production, and marketing to his new consulting role.

### The Vision for Infrared

FilmNation's decision to bring Emmerich on board reflects a broader trend in the industry where studios are increasingly focused on creating content with franchise potential. The demand for films that can generate sequels, spin-offs, and related merchandise has never been higher. Emmerich's insights into audience preferences and market trends will be invaluable as Infrared seeks to navigate this competitive landscape.

### Upcoming Projects and Future Prospects

The label's forthcoming projects, including "Painter," are expected to showcase a blend of originality and commercial viability. By leveraging Emmerich's expertise, FilmNation aims to refine its approach to storytelling and production, ensuring that its films not only capture the imagination of viewers but also have the potential for long-term success.

As the film industry continues to evolve, the collaboration between FilmNation and Emmerich signals a commitment to innovation and quality in genre filmmaking. This partnership may very well influence the direction of future projects, setting a new standard for what audiences can expect from genre films in the years to come.

Toby Emmerich's consulting role at FilmNation's Infrared marks a significant development in the studio's efforts to produce genre films with franchise potential. With a strong slate of upcoming projects and Emmerich's industry insights, Infrared is poised to make a notable impact in the film landscape. As the collaboration unfolds, industry watchers will be keen to see how this partnership shapes the future of genre filmmaking.
