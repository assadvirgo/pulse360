Dafne Keen, known for her role in "Logan," shared her experiences of being offered cocaine at Hollywood parties when she was 11 years old. She also spoke about Hugh Jackman's protective role during the filming of the movie.

## Key facts
- Dafne Keen revealed on the "Inside of You" podcast that she was offered cocaine at Hollywood parties as a young teenager, according to Variety.
- Keen, now 21, gained recognition for her role as Laura/X-23 in the 2017 film "Logan."
- She stated that Hugh Jackman ensured that no one abused her working hours on set and that she was shielded from any behind-the-scenes tensions, according to the Hollywood Reporter.
