"6TM," directed by Karim Akalay, won the New Visions Award at the Ji.hlava Documentary Film Festival. The film is a Belgian family comedy that incorporates elements of magic realism and focuses on community resilience.

## Key facts
- "6TM" is directed by Karim Akalay and produced by Walid Bekhti.
- The film won the New Visions Award for the most promising European project at the Ji.hlava Documentary Film Festival.
- It is described as a Belgian family comedy in three chapters, rooted in magic realism and community resilience.
