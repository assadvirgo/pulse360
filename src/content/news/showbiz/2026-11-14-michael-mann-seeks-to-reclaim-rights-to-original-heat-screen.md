Michael Mann is involved in a legal dispute with New Regency as he seeks to reclaim the copyright to his original screenplay for the film "Heat." New Regency, which produced the 1995 film, filed a lawsuit arguing that it still owns the rights.

## Key facts
- Michael Mann is set to begin shooting "Heat 2" in January.
- He is in a legal dispute with New Regency over the copyright to the original "Heat" screenplay.
- New Regency produced the original "Heat," which was released in 1995.
- The company filed a lawsuit arguing it still owns the screenplay rights.
