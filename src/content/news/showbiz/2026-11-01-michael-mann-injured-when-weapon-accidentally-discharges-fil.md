Film director Michael Mann was injured when a weapon accidentally discharged while holstered at a gun range. He sustained a superficial injury and was treated before being released.

## Key facts
- The incident occurred on Saturday at a gun range.
- Michael Mann was treated for a superficial injury.
- He is scheduled to direct "Heat 2" in January.
- The incident will not impact the start of the film's production, according to a source close to Mann.
