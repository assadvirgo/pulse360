Banjong Pisanthanakun, the Thai director, has confirmed that his psychological horror film "Inherit" will have a sequel, with star Davika Hoorne returning. The original film was a Centrepiece selection at the Toronto Film Festival and premiered in September. It is also closing the Jakarta World Cinema festival.

## Key facts
- Banjong Pisanthanakun is the director of "Inherit."
- Davika Hoorne will return for the sequel.
- "Inherit" premiered at the Toronto Film Festival in September.
- The film is closing the Jakarta World Cinema festival.
