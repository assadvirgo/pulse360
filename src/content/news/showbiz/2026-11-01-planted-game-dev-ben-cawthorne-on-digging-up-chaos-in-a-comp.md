Cloth Cat Games has developed a new game titled "Planted!" that combines gardening with competitive gameplay. The game features an unusual premise where players can engage in combat by launching harvested vegetables, such as potatoes, at each other.

## Key facts
- "Planted!" is described as a quirky combat game.
- The game turns gardening into a competitive experience.
- Players can launch freshly harvested vegetables at friends as part of the gameplay.
