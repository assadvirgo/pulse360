SAG-AFTRA, the American Federation of Music, and the Writers Guild of America East and West have requested a federal judge to reject a fair use exemption for training AI models. This request is part of the ongoing copyright infringement case involving OpenAI and various news outlets and writers.

## Key facts
- The unions filed an amicus brief in the case.
- The brief was submitted on Thursday.
- The case involves copyright infringement claims against OpenAI.
