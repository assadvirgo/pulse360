Daily Capital, a new fund for Taiwan-Japan film and television co-productions, launched at the Tokyo International Film Festival market on the opening day of TIFFCOM. The fund aims to raise NT$100 million (approximately $3.1 million) and plans to invest in at least five projects across film, television, and animation.

## Key facts
- Daily Capital launched at the "Taiwan Originals: Project Showcase" event during TIFFCOM.
- The fund focuses on Taiwan-Japan co-productions.
- It aims to raise NT$100 million (about $3.1 million).
- Daily Capital plans to invest in at least five projects in various media formats.
