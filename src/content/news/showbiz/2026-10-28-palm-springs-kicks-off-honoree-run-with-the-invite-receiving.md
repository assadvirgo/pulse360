A24's film "The Invite" will receive the Vanguard Award at the Palm Springs International Film Awards. This award is part of the opening-night gala for the 2027 Palm Springs Film Festival, scheduled for January 7.

## Key facts
- "The Invite" has been selected for the Vanguard Award at the Palm Springs International Film Awards.
- The award will be presented during the festival's opening-night gala on January 7.
- Confirmed attendees for the ceremony include Olivia Wilde, Seth Rogen, Edward Norton, and Penélope Cruz.
