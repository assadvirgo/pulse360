Indonesia and Kyrgyzstan have collaborated on their first film, titled "The Land of Jinn." The supernatural horror feature was presented at the Tokyo Gap-Financing Market.

## Key facts
- "The Land of Jinn" is a horror film directed by Kevin Rahardjo.
- The film is the first co-production between Indonesia and Kyrgyzstan.
- The story follows a young Indonesian woman who travels to Kyrgyzstan in search of a mystical healer.
