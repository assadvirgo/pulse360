The Netflix series "The Altruists" has been reviewed by Variety, which states that it fails to adequately portray the story of Sam Bankman-Fried, the founder of the collapsed cryptocurrency exchange FTX. The review notes that the collapse of FTX in 2022 was a significant event, leading to Bankman-Fried's imprisonment along with many of his associates.

## Key facts
- The series is titled "The Altruists."
- FTX collapsed in 2022, leading to significant repercussions for its founder and his inner circle.
- Sam Bankman-Fried was imprisoned following the collapse of FTX.
- The review suggests that the series does not effectively capture the essence of Bankman-Fried's story.
