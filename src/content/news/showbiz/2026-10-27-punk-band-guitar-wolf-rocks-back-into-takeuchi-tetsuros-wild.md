Guitar Wolf, a punk band known for its international touring, is set to headline the film "Wild Zero Overdrive," directed by Takeuchi Tetsuro. This project is part of the Tokyo Gap-Financing Market and serves as a follow-up to Takeuchi's 1999 film "Wild Zero." Takeuchi plans to use the original film as a template while updating it for contemporary audiences.
