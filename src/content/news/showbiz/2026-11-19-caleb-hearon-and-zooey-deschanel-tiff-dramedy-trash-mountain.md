Magnolia Pictures has acquired the North American rights to the dramedy film "Trash Mountain," which stars Caleb Hearon and Zooey Deschanel. The film, co-written by Hearon and Ruby Caster, is directed by Kris Rey and produced by Colin Trevorrow. A theatrical release is planned for next year, according to Variety.

## Key facts
- Magnolia Pictures acquired North American rights to "Trash Mountain."
- The film stars Caleb Hearon and Zooey Deschanel.
- Hearon co-wrote the script with Ruby Caster.
- Kris Rey directs the film.
- Colin Trevorrow serves as producer.
