Indian filmmaker Sanjay Gupta has died following a fall at his residence in Mumbai. He was 59 years old.

## Key facts
- Sanjay Gupta was known for directing films such as "Kaante," "Shootout at Wadala," and "Kaabil."
- He died on October 30, according to the Press Trust of India.
- Gupta was a longtime friend of director Milap Zaveri.
