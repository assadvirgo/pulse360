Indian superstar Rajinikanth’s film "Jailer 2" ranked as the second-biggest film globally over the weekend of October 16-18, grossing $31.2 million. The film was outperformed by "Street Fighter," which earned $51.1 million.

## Key facts
- "Jailer 2" grossed $31.2 million during the weekend.
- "Street Fighter" was the top film, earning $51.1 million.
- The film opened in India on October 15.
- "Jailer 2" grossed an estimated INR224 crore ($23.5 million) in India.
