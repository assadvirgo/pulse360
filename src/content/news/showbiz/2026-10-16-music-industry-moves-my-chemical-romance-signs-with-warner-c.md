Warner Chappell Music has signed an exclusive worldwide administration agreement with the rock band My Chemical Romance. This partnership includes the global publishing administration of the band's entire catalog as well as future works.

## Key facts
- Warner Chappell Music is the music publishing arm of Warner Music Group.
- The agreement covers My Chemical Romance's entire catalog, including albums like "Three Cheers for Sweet Revenge" and "The Black Parade."
- The partnership also includes future works by the band.
