## Lee Chang-dong’s ‘Possible Love’ Sets Theatrical Release Dates Ahead of Netflix Debut

Acclaimed South Korean filmmaker Lee Chang-dong is set to unveil his latest work, "Possible Love," with a series of theatrical releases scheduled across various countries. The film will make its debut in Korean theaters on September 23, 2023, marking a significant moment for both the director and the South Korean film industry.

### International Release Schedule

Following its initial release in South Korea, "Possible Love" will be shown in select theaters in Australia on October 22, 2023. The film will then expand its reach to audiences in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, and Japan on October 23, 2023. This staggered release strategy aims to maximize the film's visibility and audience engagement before it transitions to streaming platforms.

### Global Streaming on Netflix

After its theatrical run, "Possible Love" is set to be available for global streaming on Netflix starting November 6, 2023. This move reflects the growing trend of filmmakers leveraging streaming platforms to reach wider audiences, particularly in the wake of the COVID-19 pandemic, which has significantly altered film distribution dynamics.

### Festival Premieres

"Possible Love" will have its world premiere at the prestigious Venice Film Festival, a notable event that showcases international cinema and often serves as a launching pad for films seeking critical acclaim. Following its Venice debut, the film will also have its North American premiere, further solidifying its status as a significant entry in the current film festival circuit.

### Anticipation and Expectations

Lee Chang-dong, known for his masterful storytelling and deep exploration of human emotions, has garnered a dedicated following both in South Korea and internationally. His previous works, such as "Burning" and "Poetry," have received numerous accolades and critical praise, setting high expectations for "Possible Love." The film is anticipated to delve into themes of love, loss, and the complexities of human relationships, hallmarks of Lee's cinematic style.

As the release dates approach, anticipation is building among film enthusiasts and critics alike. The combination of a theatrical release followed by a streaming debut is expected to attract a diverse audience, allowing the film to resonate with viewers across different demographics.

### Conclusion

With its carefully planned release strategy and the reputation of its director, "Possible Love" is poised to make a significant impact in the film industry. The film’s journey from festival screenings to theatrical showings and finally to Netflix exemplifies the evolving landscape of film distribution, where accessibility and audience engagement are paramount. As Lee Chang-dong continues to explore the depths of human experience, "Possible Love" promises to be a noteworthy addition to his illustrious body of work.
