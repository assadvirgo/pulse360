Andrew Garfield stated that he does not recall waving at Sam Altman while filming the movie "Artificial." Altman, the CEO of OpenAI, claimed he waved at Garfield during a shoot outside his San Francisco home, but Garfield expressed confusion about the incident.

## Key facts
- Andrew Garfield is preparing for his role as Sam Altman in the film "Artificial."
- Sam Altman claimed he waved at Garfield during filming outside his home in San Francisco.
- Garfield responded that he "wasn't there" and expressed confusion about Altman's account.
