Cher is responding to Michael Douglas' comments regarding her 1988 Oscar win for "Moonstruck." A representative for Cher stated that "Cher adores Michael Douglas."

## Key facts
- Michael Douglas made a viral comment about Cher's Oscar win for "Moonstruck."
- Cher won the Academy Award for best actress at the 60th Academy Awards in April 1988.
- Douglas starred in "Fatal Attraction," which was released the same year.
