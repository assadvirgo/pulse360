Elon Musk has invested in Skydance, as confirmed by David Ellison, the chairman and CEO of the company. Ellison expressed that having Musk as a shareholder is an honor.

## Key facts
- David Ellison confirmed Elon Musk's investment in Skydance.
- Skydance was formed from the merger of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery.
- Musk invested about $1 billion in Skydance post-merger, according to reports from CNBC and Semafor.
