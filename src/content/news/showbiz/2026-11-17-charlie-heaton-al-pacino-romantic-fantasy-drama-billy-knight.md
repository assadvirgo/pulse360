Magenta Edge Films has acquired the North American rights to the romantic fantasy drama "Billy Knight," which features Charlie Heaton and Al Pacino. The film marks the feature directorial debut of Alec Griffen Roth, who also wrote the screenplay. A nationwide theatrical release is planned for January 8, 2027.

## Key facts
- Magenta Edge Films acquired North American rights to "Billy Knight."
- The film stars Charlie Heaton, Al Pacino, Diana Silvers, and Patrick Schwarzenegger.
- Alec Griffen Roth makes his feature directorial debut with this film.
- Roth also wrote the screenplay for "Billy Knight."
- A nationwide theatrical release is scheduled for January 8, 2027.
