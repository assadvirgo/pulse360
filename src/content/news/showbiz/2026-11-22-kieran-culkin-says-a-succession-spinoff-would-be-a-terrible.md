Kieran Culkin has expressed his disapproval of a potential spinoff of the HBO series "Succession." During an appearance on the "Heart Breakfast" radio show, he stated that he believes a spinoff would be a "terrible idea."

## Key facts
- Kieran Culkin is currently starring in "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping."
- He discussed the possibility of a "Succession" spinoff during an interview on the "Heart Breakfast" radio show.
- Culkin questioned what the premise of a spinoff would be, humorously referencing "Cousin Greg: The Sitcom."
