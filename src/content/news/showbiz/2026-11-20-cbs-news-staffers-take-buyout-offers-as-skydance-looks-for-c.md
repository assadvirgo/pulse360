CBS News staffers have taken buyout offers as part of a cost-saving initiative by Skydance. This decision follows Skydance's recent acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery, which aims to achieve $6 billion in savings.

## Key facts
- Skydance set a buyout deadline for employees, including those at CBS News.
- The buyout offers were made after Skydance's purchase of Warner Bros. Discovery.
- The merger places CBS News and CNN under the same corporate umbrella.
