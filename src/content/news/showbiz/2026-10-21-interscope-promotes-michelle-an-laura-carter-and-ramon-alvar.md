Interscope Geffen A&M has announced promotions within its leadership team. Michelle An has been promoted to President and Chief Creative Officer, while Laura Carter and Ramon Alvarez-Smikle have also received promotions to executive positions.

## Key facts
- Michelle An will report to John Janick in her new role.
- An will continue to lead IGA Pop/Rock and expand her responsibilities across IGA.
- The promotions are part of new Creative and Urban leadership roles at Interscope.
