Dermot Mulroney has expressed his emotions about filming the sequel to "The Family Stone," stating it feels like a real-life family reunion. He reprises his role as Everett Stone in the upcoming sequel titled "The Families Stone."

## Key facts
- The sequel to "The Family Stone" has been in development more than 20 years after the original film.
- Dermot Mulroney is returning to his role as Everett Stone.
- Mulroney described his feelings about reuniting with his co-stars as being "so moved."
