Julian Fellowes attended the Season 4 premiere of "The Gilded Age" at the Plaza Hotel in New York City. He expressed gratitude to Casey Bloys for his support of the series from its inception.

## Key facts
- Julian Fellowes is the creator of "The Gilded Age."
- The Season 4 premiere took place at the Plaza Hotel in New York City.
- Fellowes referred to Casey Bloys as "our protector, our gladiator, our defender from very, very early days."
