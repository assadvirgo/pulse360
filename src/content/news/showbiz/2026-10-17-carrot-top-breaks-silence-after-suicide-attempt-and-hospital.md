Carrot Top, the comedian known as Scott Thompson, has expressed gratitude for the support he received following a recent suicide attempt and hospitalization. He announced plans to return to the stage at Luxor Las Vegas on October 19.

- Scott Thompson, known as Carrot Top, has thanked supporters for their love and well wishes.
- He is preparing to return to performing at Luxor Las Vegas.
