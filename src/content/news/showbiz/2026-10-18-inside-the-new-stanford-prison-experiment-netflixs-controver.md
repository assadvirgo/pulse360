A new reality series on Netflix explores the infamous Stanford Prison Experiment, a social study conducted in 1971. The experiment, led by Stanford psychology professor Philip Zimbardo, involved 24 male college students and was funded by the U.S. Office of Naval Research.

## Key facts
- The Stanford Prison Experiment is considered one of the most notorious social experiments.
- It inspired numerous films, documentaries, and psychological studies.
- The study focused on antisocial behavior and the effects of imprisonment.
