Netflix is undergoing significant changes in its executive team and workforce, prompting questions from Wall Street about the company's future direction. The company has made several C-suite changes and is preparing for its latest quarterly earnings report and investor call. 

## Key facts
- Netflix has experienced a series of C-suite changes, including the appointment of Peter Rice and the departure of Bela Bajaria and others.
- The company is implementing its deepest round of layoffs since 2022.
- Wall Street is questioning the new path Netflix is taking as it approaches its quarterly earnings report.
