Betty McGuire, an actress known for her role as grandmother Kate Malone on the ABC sitcom "Growing Pains," has died at the age of 100. She passed away on October 8 at the Motion Picture & Television Fund's Wasserman Campus in Woodland Hills, California, due to natural causes.

## Key facts
- McGuire worked alongside notable figures such as Ethel Merman, Bea Arthur, and Susan Hayward on stage.
- She played a nun in the film "Up in Smoke."
- Her death was confirmed by Keith Gerchak, McGuire’s power of attorney.
