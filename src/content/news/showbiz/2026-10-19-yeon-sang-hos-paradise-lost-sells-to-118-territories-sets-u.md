Yeon Sang-ho's film "Paradise Lost" has been sold to 118 territories globally. The film, a psychological thriller, will also have its U.S. premiere at the Hawaii International Film Festival.

## Key facts
- "Paradise Lost" is directed by Yeon Sang-ho, known for "Train To Busan."
- The film has been sold to 118 countries, including Japan, France, and North America.
- In the U.S., it will premiere in the 'Spotlight on Korea' section of the festival.
