Banijay Entertainment has announced its senior leadership team following its merger with All3Media. The company has appointed Jeff Zucker as Chairman, Marco Bassetti as CEO, and Jane Turton as Deputy CEO.

## Key facts
- Banijay Entertainment confirmed its leadership structure after merging with All3Media.
- Jeff Zucker has been appointed as Chairman.
- Marco Bassetti will serve as CEO.
- Jane Turton has been named Deputy CEO.
