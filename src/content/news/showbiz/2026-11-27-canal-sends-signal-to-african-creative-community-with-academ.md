Canal+ launched a skills academy in South Africa, supported by the country's President Cyril Ramaphosa. The initiative aims to benefit between 800 and 1,000 individuals in its first year.

## Key facts
- The academy was launched in collaboration with Canal+ boss Maxime Saada.
- The initiative follows Canal+'s acquisition of MultiChoice for $2 billion.
- Cyril Ramaphosa expressed strong support for Canal+'s efforts in South Africa and beyond.
