Emmy Rossum has joined RAINN as an ambassador to address sexual violence, coinciding with her return for Season 2 of the show "Furious." She aims to promote open discussions about the topic, particularly in light of her role as FBI agent Alice Black.

## Key facts
- Emmy Rossum is preparing for Season 2 of "Furious."
- She has recently become an ambassador for RAINN, an anti-sexual violence organization.
- Rossum expressed a desire to "take the shame out of the conversation" regarding sexual violence.
