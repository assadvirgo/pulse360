The representation of women composers in Hollywood remains low, with only eight of this year's top 100 grossing films featuring original scores by women. This marks a decline from previous years, where the percentages of films with female composers ranged between 7% and 9%, down from 14% in 2023.

## Key facts
- Only eight of this year's top 100 grossing films have original scores by women, according to Variety.
- The percentage of films featuring female composers has decreased from 14% in 2023 to between 7% and 9% over the past two years.
- Female composers are still recognized in award discussions.
