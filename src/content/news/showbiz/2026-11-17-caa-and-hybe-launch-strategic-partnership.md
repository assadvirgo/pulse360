Creative Artists Agency (CAA) and Hybe have launched a strategic partnership aimed at enhancing Hybe's creative and commercial opportunities. The collaboration will leverage Hybe's artist network and CAA's global reach to work across various sectors, including content, brand partnerships, sports, live experiences, and licensing and merchandising.

## Key facts
- CAA and Hybe aim to grow Hybe's creative and commercial opportunities.
- The partnership will involve collaboration across content, brand partnerships, sports, live experiences, and licensing and merchandising.
- Hybe is known for its K-pop groups, including BTS, Le Sserafim, Cortis, and Seventeen.
