A new screenwriting lab in India aims to shift the focus of the country's horror films away from traditional ghost stories and horror-comedy hybrids. The initiative, led by independent outfit Platoon One Films, will select 15 writers for a three-month program centered on original genre scripts.

## Key facts
- The program is called the Platoon One Horror Lab.
- It will be conducted entirely online.
- The lab will select 15 participants.
