Producers of the film "Renoir," directed by Hayakawa Chie, will participate in a panel on Asia-Europe co-production at the TIFFCOM market in Tokyo on October 30. The session, titled "Building Creative Partnerships," will focus on collaboration between producers and directors. Additionally, the Japanese accelerator Atmovie Global Track has established a partnership with the Europe-based workshop Ties That Bind.

## Key facts
- Producers of "Renoir" will speak at the TIFFCOM market in Tokyo.

- The session will explore collaboration between producers and directors.
- Atmovie Global Track has partnered with Ties That Bind.
