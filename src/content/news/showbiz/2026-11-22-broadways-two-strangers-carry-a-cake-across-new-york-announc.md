The production "Two Strangers (Carry a Cake Across New York)" will transition from Broadway to the Off Broadway venue New World Stages this spring after its Broadway run concludes. The current cast is not expected to continue with the production in its new location.

## Key facts
- "Two Strangers (Carry a Cake Across New York)" will move to New World Stages.
- The move is scheduled for spring.
- The current Broadway cast, Sam Tutty and Christiani Pitts, is not expected to make the transition.
