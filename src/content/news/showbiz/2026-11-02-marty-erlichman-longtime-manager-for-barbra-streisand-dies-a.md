Marty Erlichman, the entertainment manager known for discovering and managing Barbra Streisand for over sixty years, has died at the age of 97 due to age-related causes. His family described him as a devoted father, husband, and grandfather.

## Key facts
- Marty Erlichman managed Barbra Streisand for more than six decades.
- He discovered Streisand when she was 19 years old.
- Erlichman passed away on Saturday.
