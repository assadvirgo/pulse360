"Kuch Kuch Hota Hai," directed by Karan Johar, will be screened at the Edinburgh International Film Festival in 2028 to celebrate its 30th anniversary. Karan Johar is set to attend the event.

## Key facts
- "Kuch Kuch Hota Hai" is Karan Johar's first film as a director.
- The film stars Shah Rukh Khan, Kajol, and Rani Mukerji.
- The screening will take place in 2028.
- Karan Johar received the invitation during a meeting at his Mumbai offices.
