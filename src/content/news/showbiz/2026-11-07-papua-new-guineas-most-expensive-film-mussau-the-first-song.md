Papua New Guinea's most expensive film, "Mussau: The First Song," has completed principal photography after a 40-day shoot. The production took place across two provinces, Mussau Island in New Ireland Province and Goroka in Eastern Highlands Province.

## Key facts
- The film has a budget of $4.5 million.
- Principal photography lasted for 40 days.
- Filming locations included Mussau Island and Goroka.
- The production is by PNG-based NAFA Productions.
