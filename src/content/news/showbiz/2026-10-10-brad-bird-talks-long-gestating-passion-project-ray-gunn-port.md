## Brad Bird Discusses Long-Awaited Project ‘Ray Gunn’ and Future Visions at Contenders London

During a recent appearance at Deadline’s Contenders London event, acclaimed director Brad Bird shared insights into his long-gestating passion project, ‘Ray Gunn’, while also addressing the broader themes of dystopia and utopia in contemporary storytelling. 

Bird, known for his work on animated classics such as ‘The Incredibles’ and ‘Ratatouille’, expressed a nuanced perspective on how the future is often portrayed in film and media. “It seems like the only way to look at the future these days is as dystopia,” he remarked. This observation reflects a prevailing trend in modern cinema, where narratives often lean towards dark, cautionary tales about societal collapse and environmental degradation.

However, Bird emphasized that while he appreciates many dystopian films, he believes there are alternative narratives that can be explored. “That’s fine. I love a lot of movies that take that thing, but there are other ways to look at the future too, and you can,” he stated. This sentiment highlights Bird’s desire to challenge the dominant narrative and explore more optimistic or diverse visions of the future.

### The Vision Behind ‘Ray Gunn’

‘Ray Gunn’ has been a project that Bird has nurtured for years, and it represents a significant departure from the themes commonly associated with his previous works. While specific details about the film remain under wraps, Bird hinted at its unique approach to character development and storytelling. He described the characters in ‘Ray Gunn’ as “desperate,” suggesting that their struggles and aspirations will play a central role in the narrative.

Bird’s focus on portraying characters who are grappling with their circumstances aligns with his broader artistic philosophy. He has often sought to create relatable protagonists who face significant challenges, making their journeys resonate with audiences. By exploring the desperation of his characters, Bird aims to delve into the human experience, showcasing resilience and hope even in the face of adversity.

### A Shift in Narrative Landscape

The conversation at Contenders London also touched upon the shifting landscape of storytelling in the film industry. As audiences become increasingly aware of global issues such as climate change, social inequality, and political unrest, filmmakers are tasked with reflecting these realities in their work. This has led to a surge in dystopian narratives, which often serve as cautionary tales about the potential consequences of current societal trends.

Bird’s call for a more balanced representation of the future suggests a desire for filmmakers to embrace a wider range of themes. By incorporating elements of hope and possibility alongside the darker aspects of human existence, filmmakers can create a more comprehensive narrative landscape that resonates with diverse audiences.

### Conclusion

As Brad Bird continues to develop ‘Ray Gunn’, his insights at Contenders London serve as a reminder of the power of storytelling in shaping perceptions of the future. By advocating for narratives that explore both despair and hope, Bird is positioning himself as a visionary in the film industry, encouraging creators to look beyond dystopia and imagine a future filled with potential. As the film progresses, audiences will undoubtedly be eager to see how Bird’s vision unfolds and what new perspectives he will bring to the cinematic landscape.
