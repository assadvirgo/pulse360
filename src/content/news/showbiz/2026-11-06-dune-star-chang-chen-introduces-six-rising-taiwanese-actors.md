Taiwanese actor Chang Chen introduced six rising actors during a showcase co-organized by the Golden Horse Awards and the Taiwan Creative Content Fest (TCCF). This event, held at the seventh edition of TCCF, marks the first collaboration between TCCF and the Golden Horse Awards.

## Key facts
- The showcase featured six rising Taiwanese actors.
- It was co-organized by the Golden Horse Awards and TCCF.
- The event took place during the seventh edition of TCCF.
- This is the first collaboration between TCCF and the Golden Horse Awards.
