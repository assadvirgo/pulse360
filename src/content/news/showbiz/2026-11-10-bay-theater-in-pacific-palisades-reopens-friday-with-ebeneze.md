The Bay Theater in Pacific Palisades is set to reopen on Friday, following a month after the announcement of its takeover by Paramount Pictures. The theater has been updated with new technology and amenities.

## Key facts
- The Bay Theater will reopen on Friday.
- Paramount Pictures is taking over the cinemas.
- The theaters have been reimagined with cutting-edge cinema technology, luxury seating, elevated dining, and exceptional hospitality.
- The renovations aim to preserve the intimate neighborhood character of the theater. 
- Skydance CEO David Ellison supports the location as a symbol of commitment to the community and theatrical exhibition.
