Shooting is currently taking place in Venice for "Upgraded 2," the sequel to the 2024 rom-com "Upgraded." The film stars Camila Mendes and Leslie Bibb, with Mendes reprising her role as Ana Santos.

- The film is being produced by Amazon MGM Studios.

- Leslie Bibb is also starring in the film.
- The storyline includes elements related to the Biennale Art Show in Venice.
