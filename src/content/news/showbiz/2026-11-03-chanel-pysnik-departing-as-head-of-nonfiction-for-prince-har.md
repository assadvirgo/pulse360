Chanel Pysnik is departing from her role as Head of Nonfiction for Prince Harry and Meghan Markle’s Archewell Productions. She has held this position for the past five and a half years.

## Key facts
- Chanel Pysnik is leaving Archewell Productions.
- She served as Head of Nonfiction, Film & TV for five and a half years.
- Pysnik joined Archewell Productions in May 2021.
- No immediate replacement has been named for her position.
