Jaime Pressly has defended her former "Mom" co-star Anna Faris in response to comments made by co-creator Chuck Lorre in his memoir "Sitcom." Lorre expressed his hesitation to cast Faris as the lead character, Christy Plunkett, stating he "was, in fact, right" about his reluctance.

## Key facts
- Jaime Pressly responded to Chuck Lorre's professional criticism of Anna Faris.
- Lorre's memoir "Sitcom" includes his thoughts on casting Faris.
- Pressly described her co-stars as "an incredible cast of women."
