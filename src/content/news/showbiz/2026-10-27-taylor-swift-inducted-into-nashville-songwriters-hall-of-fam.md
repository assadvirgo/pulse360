Taylor Swift was inducted into the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame, becoming its youngest member at the age of 36. The induction ceremony took place on Monday, where she reflected on her early songwriting days in Nashville.

## Key facts
- Taylor Swift was inducted into the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame.
- She is the youngest member of the Hall of Fame at 36 years old.
- The induction ceremony featured former tourmate Brad Paisley and singer Lainey Wilson.
