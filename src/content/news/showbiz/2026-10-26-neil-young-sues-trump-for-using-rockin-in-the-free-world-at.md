Neil Young has filed a $10 million lawsuit against Donald Trump for using his song "Rockin' in the Free World" at a rally. This marks the second time Young has taken legal action regarding Trump's use of the song, having previously sued another Trump group in 2020.

## Key facts
- Young's lawsuit is for $10 million.
- This is the second lawsuit Young has filed over Trump's use of the song.
- Young previously sued another Trump group in 2020.
