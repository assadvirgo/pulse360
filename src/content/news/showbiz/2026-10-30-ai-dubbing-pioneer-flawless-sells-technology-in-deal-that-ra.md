Flawless, a British-American company known for its AI dubbing technology, has sold its intellectual property, including its TrueSync dubbing technology and DeepEditor. The buyer is TBellScout, a startup owned by Dutch tech billionaire Steven Schuurman. This deal has raised questions about the future of Flawless.

## Key facts
- Flawless spent $100 million developing its AI dubbing technology.
- The technology has been endorsed by SAG-AFTRA.
- The deal includes the acquisition of TrueSync and DeepEditor.
- The acquisition was made by TBellScout, a startup owned by Steven Schuurman.
