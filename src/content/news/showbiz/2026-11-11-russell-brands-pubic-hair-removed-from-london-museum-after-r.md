Russell Brand's pubic hair has been removed from display at a London museum following his conviction and sentencing for rape and sexual assault. The curator of the Viktor Wynd Museum of Curiosities, Fine Art & UnNatural History in Hackney confirmed the removal of the artifact.

## Key facts
- Russell Brand's pubic hair was on display at the Viktor Wynd Museum of Curiosities.
- The removal occurred after Brand's conviction and sentencing for rape and sexual assault.
- Viktor Wynd, the museum's curator, confirmed the removal of the artifact.
