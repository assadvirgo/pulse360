The British Academy of Film and Television Arts (BAFTA) has announced its 2026 Breakthrough U.K. cohort, which includes 21 emerging creatives from film, television, and games. Among the selected individuals are Imran Perretta, known for "Ish," and Ella Bruccoleri, who stars in "The Other Bennet Sister."

## Key facts
- BAFTA Breakthrough recognizes individuals on the cusp of their breakthrough moment as creatives.
- The program is in its 13th year and is supported by Netflix.
- It offers year-round career support, mentorship, networking opportunities, and full BAFTA membership.
