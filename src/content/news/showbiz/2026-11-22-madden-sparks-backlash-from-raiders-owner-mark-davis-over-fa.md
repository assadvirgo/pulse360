Mark Davis, owner of the Las Vegas Raiders, has expressed dissatisfaction with the portrayal of his father, Al Davis, in the biopic about John Madden. He stated that the depiction of Al Davis has upset the family.

## Key facts
- Mark Davis criticized the portrayal of his father, Al Davis, in the Madden biopic.
- Al Davis was instrumental in recruiting John Madden to coach the Raiders in 1967.
- The biopic is directed by David O. Russell.
