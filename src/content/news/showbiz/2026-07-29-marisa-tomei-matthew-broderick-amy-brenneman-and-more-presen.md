## Staged Reading of ‘The Big Chill’ to Benefit The Center at West Park

The Center at West Park, a nonprofit organization based in New York City, is set to host a one-night-only staged reading of the iconic film "The Big Chill." This special event will take place in East Hampton and will feature a distinguished ensemble cast, including Oscar-winner Marisa Tomei, Tony Award-winner Matthew Broderick, and Emmy Award-nominee Amy Brenneman, among others.

### A Celebration of Community and Arts

The Center at West Park has long been a hub for community engagement, arts, and culture, providing a platform for various artistic expressions and initiatives. This staged reading is not only a tribute to the beloved film but also serves as a fundraising effort to support the center's ongoing programs and activities.

"The Big Chill," released in 1983, is a film that resonates with many for its exploration of friendship, nostalgia, and the complexities of adult life. The reading will bring together a cast of talented actors who have made significant contributions to the arts, promising an engaging and heartfelt performance.

### Notable Cast Members

Marisa Tomei, known for her roles in "My Cousin Vinny" and "The Wrestler," brings her acclaimed talent to the reading. Tomei's performances have garnered critical acclaim and numerous awards, making her a standout choice for this event. Matthew Broderick, celebrated for his work in "The Producers" and "Ferris Bueller's Day Off," adds his unique charm and wit to the ensemble. Amy Brenneman, recognized for her role in "Judging Amy," rounds out the cast with her compelling presence and experience in both television and theater.

The event promises to be a memorable evening, not just for attendees but also for the actors involved, as they revisit the themes and characters that have left a lasting impact on audiences over the decades.

### Supporting a Worthy Cause

Proceeds from the staged reading will directly benefit The Center at West Park, enabling it to continue its mission of fostering community connections through the arts. The center has been pivotal in providing a space for creativity and collaboration, hosting various performances, workshops, and cultural events throughout the year.

As the arts sector continues to recover from the challenges posed by the pandemic, events like this staged reading play a crucial role in revitalizing the community and supporting artists. Attendees will not only enjoy a night of entertainment but also contribute to the sustainability of a vital cultural institution.

### Conclusion

The one-night-only staged reading of "The Big Chill" at The Center at West Park is set to be a significant event, showcasing the talents of renowned actors while supporting a nonprofit dedicated to the arts. As the date approaches, anticipation builds for what promises to be an evening filled with nostalgia, laughter, and community spirit.
