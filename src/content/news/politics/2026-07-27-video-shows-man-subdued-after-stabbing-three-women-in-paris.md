## Knife Attack in Paris Leaves Three Women Injured

In a disturbing incident that unfolded near Porte de Clichy in Paris, three women were injured in a knife attack, with two of them suffering serious wounds. The attack occurred in the early hours of the day, prompting a swift response from local law enforcement and emergency services.

According to eyewitness accounts, the assailant approached the victims in a public area and launched an unprovoked attack. The police were alerted shortly after the incident began, and officers arrived on the scene to subdue the attacker. Video footage circulating on social media shows the chaotic moments following the attack, where law enforcement personnel can be seen apprehending the suspect.

The victims, all women, were immediately transported to nearby hospitals for treatment. Authorities have confirmed that two of the women sustained serious injuries, while the third is reported to have less severe wounds. The identities of the victims have not been disclosed, and their current conditions remain under medical evaluation.

The Paris police have launched a thorough investigation into the circumstances surrounding the attack.
