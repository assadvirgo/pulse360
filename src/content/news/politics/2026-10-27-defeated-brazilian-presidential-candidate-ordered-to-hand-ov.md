Flavio Bolsonaro, Brazil's defeated presidential election candidate, has reported that he is being prevented from leaving the country. He claims that police have ordered him to surrender his passports on the instructions of a top judge.

## Key facts
- Flavio Bolsonaro is a defeated presidential election candidate in Brazil.
- He claims police have ordered him to surrender his passports.
- The order to surrender the passports reportedly comes from a top judge.
