Two Italian technicians were killed in Kuwait, reportedly due to debris from a drone strike. They were employed by the German company Rheinmetall and were working at an air-defense surveillance station.

## Key facts
- The victims were technicians for Rheinmetall, according to Italy's foreign ministry.
- They were working at an air-defense surveillance station in Kuwait.
- Their deaths are the first reported fatalities from Iranian attacks since renewed exchanges of fire between Washington and Tehran this month, according to the Financial Times.
- Iran has denied responsibility for the incident, as reported by Al Jazeera.
