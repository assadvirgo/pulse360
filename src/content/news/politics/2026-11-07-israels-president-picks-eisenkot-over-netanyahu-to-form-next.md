Israel's president, Isaac Herzog, has tasked Gadi Eisenkot with forming a new government, giving him 28 days to secure the necessary support in the Knesset. This decision comes after a deadlocked election and represents a significant challenge to Benjamin Netanyahu's leadership.

## Key facts
- Isaac Herzog has formally given Gadi Eisenkot 28 days to secure 61 Knesset seats.
- Eisenkot is considered Netanyahu's strongest rival.
- The request follows a deadlocked election in Israel.
