A Ukrainian drone attack on St. Petersburg resulted in one death and targeted energy sites, according to reports. This incident follows Russian attacks on Ukraine that reportedly killed two people.

## Key facts
- The drone attack on St. Petersburg is described as the biggest of its kind.
- The strikes affected tech infrastructure in the city.
- The attack occurred after Russia targeted electricity substations in Ukraine.
- Ukrainian President Zelenskyy stated that Russian attacks on Ukraine continue to violate an energy ceasefire.
