The Starlink satellite system has resumed its necessary operational speed in Ukraine, according to President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. The system had experienced a slowdown for over a week, which impacted Kyiv's defense capabilities against Russian attacks.

## Key facts
- The Starlink satellite system is now operating at the speed Ukraine requires.
- The slowdown lasted for more than a week.
- The reduced speed affected Ukraine's ability to defend itself from Russian attacks, as stated by President Zelenskyy.
