The US government plans to impose further restrictions on China's access to advanced AI chips, specifically those supplied by Nvidia. This move is part of ongoing efforts to limit China's technological capabilities.

## Key facts
- The US government is set to restrict China's access to powerful AI chips.
- The chips in question are provided by Nvidia.
- The restrictions include measures to block remote access to these AI chips.
