Seven people were shot dead and thirty-four others were injured during a shooting at a church funeral in Jordan. The incident occurred in the southern Karak governorate, and authorities have initiated a manhunt.

## Key facts
- Seven people have died as a result of the shooting.
- Thirty-four individuals were injured in the attack.
- The shooting took place during a funeral at a church in Jordan.
- A manhunt is currently underway for the assailants.
