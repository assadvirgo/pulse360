Poland's president returned from a trip to Senegal amid a crisis triggered by the ousting of the president of the Constitutional Tribunal. Riot police were deployed as hundreds of demonstrators protested against the suspension of the court's president by the pro-European government.

## Key facts
- Riot police were deployed to Poland's Constitutional Tribunal.
- Hundreds of demonstrators gathered to protest the suspension of the court's president.
- The suspension was enacted by the current pro-European government.
