Voter turnout in Israel's elections is reported to be the highest on record. These elections are the first since the Hamas-led attacks on Israel and the subsequent wars, and they are viewed as a referendum on Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

## Key facts
- The elections are the first since the October 7, 2023, attacks by Hamas.
- The contest features Netanyahu's far-right coalition against an opposition bloc.
- The elections are described as a knife-edge contest.
