India defeated New Zealand by six wickets in the third T20 match, successfully chasing down a target of 199 runs. Abhishek Sharma played a key role, scoring a rapid century.

## Key facts
- Abhishek Sharma scored 113 runs off 38 balls.
- India chased down the target in just 13.2 overs.
- The opening partnership contributed 133 runs to the innings.
- This victory concluded the five-match T20 series.
