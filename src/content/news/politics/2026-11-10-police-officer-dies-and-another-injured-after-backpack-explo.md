A police officer has died and another has been seriously injured following an explosion in northern Spain. The incident occurred while officers were responding to a separate situation at a residential building.

## Key facts
- One police officer died as a result of the explosion.
- Another officer lost a leg due to the detonation of an explosive device.
- The explosion took place at the scene of a shooting.
- Officers were attending the scene in connection with a different incident.
