A luxury cruise ship has suspended its three-year round-the-world voyage in Cambodia due to rising fuel costs. The operators have stated that they are working with passengers on temporary living arrangements.

## Key facts
- The cruise ship is named Villa Vie Odyssey.
- The trip was suspended while the ship was in Cambodia.
- Operators are making arrangements for passengers' temporary living situations.
