More than 5,000 people have died from an Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC), according to government data. The outbreak is reported to be the deadliest in the country's history, with over 10,000 people having contracted the virus.

## Key facts
- More than 5,000 people have died from the Ebola outbreak in DRC.
- Government data indicates that over 10,000 people have contracted the virus.
