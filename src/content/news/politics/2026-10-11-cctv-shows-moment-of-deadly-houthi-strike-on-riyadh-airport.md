## Houthi Strike on Riyadh Airport Captured on CCTV

In a significant escalation of regional tensions, CCTV footage released by Al Arabiya has revealed the moment of a Houthi missile strike on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport on Saturday. This incident marks a notable development in the ongoing conflict involving the Iran-aligned Houthi movement based in Yemen and the Saudi-led coalition engaged in military operations in the region.

### Details of the Incident

The footage, which has garnered widespread attention, shows a missile striking the vicinity of the airport. While the exact details of the damage and casualties are still being assessed, initial reports indicate that the strike resulted in significant disruption at one of the Kingdom's major transport hubs. The Saudi authorities have not yet provided a comprehensive assessment of the incident, but they have confirmed that security measures are being reinforced in light of the attack.

### Context of the Conflict

The Houthis have been engaged in a protracted conflict with the Saudi-led coalition since 2015, following the coalition's military intervention in Yemen to restore the internationally recognized government. The group has increasingly targeted Saudi Arabia with missile and drone attacks, claiming these actions are in response to the coalition's military operations and blockade against Yemen.

The recent strike on Riyadh airport is part of a broader pattern of Houthi offensives that have escalated in frequency and sophistication. These attacks have raised concerns about the security of critical infrastructure in Saudi Arabia and have prompted international calls for renewed dialogue to address the ongoing humanitarian crisis in Yemen.

### International Reactions

The missile strike has drawn condemnation from various international actors, who have called for restraint and an immediate cessation of hostilities. The United Nations has reiterated its calls for a peaceful resolution to the conflict, emphasizing the need for dialogue between the conflicting parties to address the dire humanitarian situation in Yemen, which has been described as one of the world's worst humanitarian crises.

### Implications for Regional Stability

As tensions continue to rise, the implications of the Houthi strike extend beyond immediate security concerns. The incident underscores the fragile state of regional stability and the potential for further escalation. Analysts suggest that such attacks could provoke retaliatory measures from Saudi Arabia, potentially leading to a cycle of violence that could destabilize the already volatile region.

The international community remains watchful, as the situation develops, and stakeholders are urged to seek diplomatic avenues to de-escalate the conflict. The ongoing violence not only affects the immediate parties involved but also poses risks to broader regional security and stability.

### Conclusion

The Houthi strike on Riyadh airport serves as a stark reminder of the complexities and challenges that define the conflict in Yemen and its repercussions for the wider Middle East. As the situation unfolds, the need for a comprehensive and lasting solution becomes increasingly urgent, highlighting the importance of sustained international engagement and dialogue.
