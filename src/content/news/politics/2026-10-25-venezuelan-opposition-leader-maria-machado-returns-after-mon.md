María Corina Machado, a Venezuelan opposition leader, has returned to Venezuela after months in exile. Her return comes amid national protests calling for free elections.

## Key facts
- Machado had left Venezuela a month before the US government seized control from President Nicolás Maduro, according to the BBC.
- Her homecoming follows widespread protests in the country demanding free elections, as reported by Al Jazeera.
- Machado's arrival presents a challenge to the interim government backed by former President Trump, according to the Financial Times.
