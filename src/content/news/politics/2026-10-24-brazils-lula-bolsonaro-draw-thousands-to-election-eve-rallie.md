Brazil's presidential candidates, Lula and Bolsonaro, held significant election-eve rallies as they prepare for a pivotal run-off election. The outcome will decide whether Brazil continues with its left-wing leadership or shifts towards a right-wing administration.

## Key facts
- The run-off election will determine Brazil's political direction.
- Lula is the left-wing incumbent president.
- Bolsonaro is the right-wing challenger aiming to unseat Lula.
- The election is seen as a critical moment for Brazil's alignment in Latin America.
