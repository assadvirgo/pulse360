Two brothers, part of a trio that included high-profile US real estate moguls, were each sentenced to 30 years in prison for their involvement in a sex trafficking case. They were found guilty of using their social status to lure women to parties and subsequently raping them.

## Key facts
- The Alexander brothers were sentenced to 30 years in prison.
- They were part of a trio found guilty of sex trafficking.
- The case involved using social status to lure women to parties.
- Judge Valerie Caproni rejected a request for life sentences, calling it “draconian.”
- Eleven victims addressed the court during the proceedings.
