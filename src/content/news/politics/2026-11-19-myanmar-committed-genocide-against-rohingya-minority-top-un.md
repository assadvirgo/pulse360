The International Court of Justice has ruled that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya minority during a military crackdown in 2017. This unprecedented verdict addresses the killings of thousands during that period.

## Key facts
- The ruling is from the International Court of Justice.
- It concerns a military crackdown in Myanmar that occurred in 2017.
- The crackdown resulted in the deaths of thousands of Rohingya individuals.
