A train crash near Cairo has resulted in at least seven fatalities and over 100 injuries. The passenger train was traveling from Cairo to Aswan when it derailed and overturned.

## Key facts
- The train was traveling from Egypt's capital to Aswan when it derailed.
- At least seven people have died in the incident.
- More than 100 individuals have been reported injured, according to the Egyptian Chief Prosecutor’s office.
