The US Federal Communications Commission has authorized SpaceX to deploy one million satellites for data centers in space. This approval is part of SpaceX's Starmind project.

## Key facts
- The Federal Communications Commission is granting authorization for SpaceX to send one million satellites into space.
- The satellites are intended for use as data centers.
- Elon Musk has praised the FCC's approval, calling it a significant asset to America and the world, according to Al Jazeera.
