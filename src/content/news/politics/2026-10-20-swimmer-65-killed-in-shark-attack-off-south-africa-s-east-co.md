A 65-year-old woman was killed in a shark attack while swimming off South Africa's east coast. The incident occurred during her daily morning swim with her club, according to her family.

## Key facts
- The victim was identified as Janine Lochrenberg.
- The attack took place at a popular beach on South Africa's southeastern coast.
- Local authorities confirmed the fatality.
