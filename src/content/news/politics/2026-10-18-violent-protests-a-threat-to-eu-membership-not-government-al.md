Albania's prime minister stated he will not resign amid ongoing protests and unrest. He warned that the escalating violence poses a threat to the country's prospects for joining the European Union.

## Key facts
- Albania's prime minister has refused to resign despite days of protests.
- The prime minister indicated that the violence is threatening Albania's EU membership chances.
