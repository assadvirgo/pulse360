An Israeli air strike has killed a senior Hamas commander in Gaza. Hamas confirmed the death of Rafah brigade commander Samir Abu Younis in an attack on Khan Younis.

## Key facts
- The air strike occurred on Saturday.
- Samir Abu Younis was the commander of the Rafah brigade.
- The attack took place in Khan Younis, Gaza.
