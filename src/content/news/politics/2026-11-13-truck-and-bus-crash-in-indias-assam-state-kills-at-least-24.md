A truck and bus collision in Assam, India, has resulted in at least 24 fatalities. Among the deceased are several children and college students, as reported by officials.

## Key facts
- The crash involved a bus and a truck in Assam, India.
- At least 24 people have been confirmed dead.
- Several of the deceased are college students, according to local officials.
- The bus driver was also among those killed.
- The death toll is expected to rise, according to Al Jazeera.
