The UK Ministry of Defence announced the deployment of Typhoon jets to assist in defending Saudi Arabia from Houthi attacks. The warplanes will operate in a defensive role and will not be used to attack the Yemeni group.

## Key facts
- The UK will deploy Typhoon jets to Saudi Arabia.
- The Ministry of Defence stated the jets will operate in a defensive role.
- The deployment comes in response to ongoing strikes from the Houthi militant group against Saudi Arabia.
