The US has lost its measles elimination status after 26 years due to ongoing outbreaks. This marks a significant public health setback as the country faces its largest outbreak in over three decades.

## Key facts
- The US has lost its measles elimination status after 26 years.
- The loss of status is attributed to the failure to prevent the virus from spreading for more than a year.
- The current outbreak is the largest in over 30 years.
