Elena Rybakina, the world number one in tennis, has withdrawn from the WTA Finals due to a viral illness. Marta Kostyuk from Ukraine will replace her as the top seed.

## Key facts
- Rybakina withdrew on the eve of the Indian Wells tournament.
- She is the current world number one in tennis.
- Marta Kostyuk will take her place in the tournament.
