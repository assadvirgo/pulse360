Three Mexican men have been sentenced to 158 years each in prison for the murders of Australian and US surfers. The sentencing follows their conviction for the 2024 killings.

## Key facts
- A Mexican judge sentenced the three men to 158 years each for the murders.
- The sentencing occurred a week after the men were found guilty.
- The victims were brothers Jake and Callum Robinson and their friend Carter Rhoad.
- The surfers were killed during a surfing trip in an apparent botched robbery.
