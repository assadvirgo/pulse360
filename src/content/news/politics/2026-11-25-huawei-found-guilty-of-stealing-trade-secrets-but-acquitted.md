Huawei has been found guilty of stealing trade secrets in a federal court in New York. The jury acquitted the company of the more serious charge of engaging in a criminal racketeering conspiracy.

## Key facts
- A jury found Huawei guilty of stealing US trade secrets.
- The verdict was reached after a months-long trial.
- The case involved accusations of stealing source code and proprietary technology from companies such as T-Mobile and Cisco.
- Huawei was acquitted of the charge of engaging in a criminal racketeering conspiracy.
