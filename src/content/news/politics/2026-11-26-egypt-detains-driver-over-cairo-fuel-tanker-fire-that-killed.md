Egyptian authorities have detained a driver suspected of causing a fuel tanker crash that resulted in the deaths of 31 people. The driver, who is 21 years old, claims he was overtaking when the incident occurred.

## Key facts
- The driver has been detained by prosecutors.
- Authorities have ordered drug tests for the suspect.
- The crash involved a fuel tanker that overturned.
