Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) launched six drones at a US radar installation located at an air base in Bahrain. This incident marks the second attack on the country within three days amid ongoing tensions between the US and Iran over the Strait of Hormuz.

## Key facts
- Six drones were launched by Iran's IRGC at a US radar installation in Bahrain.
- This attack is the second on Bahrain in three days.
- The incident occurs during a stand-off between the US and Iran over the Strait of Hormuz.
