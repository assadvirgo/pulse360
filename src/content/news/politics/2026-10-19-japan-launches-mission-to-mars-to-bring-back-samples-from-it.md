Japan has launched a spacecraft towards Mars as part of a mission to collect samples from Phobos, the largest of the planet's two moons. This initiative is led by JAXA and aims to return soil samples to Earth.

## Key facts
- Japan's spacecraft is part of a mission to bring back samples from Mars' moon Phobos.
- The mission is known as MMX (Martian Moons Exploration).
- The spacecraft will attempt up to two landings on Phobos in 2029.
- The mission is expected to provide insights into the history of the solar system.
