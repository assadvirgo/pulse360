A shooting suspect in Krakow, Poland, has died in the hospital, according to police reports. The suspect was linked to a shooting that resulted in the deaths of six individuals.

## Key facts
- Six people were killed in the shooting.
- Three bodies of the suspect's family members were found at their home, according to BBC.
- The suspect was believed to have killed six people, including a British national, according to Sky News.
