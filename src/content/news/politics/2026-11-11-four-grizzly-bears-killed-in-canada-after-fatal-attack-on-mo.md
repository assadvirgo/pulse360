Four grizzly bears were killed in Canada after being identified as responsible for the deaths of a mother and daughter. The incident involved a sow and her three cubs, who attacked Jennifer Balcarras and her daughter Selah Reed.

## Key facts
- The bears were captured and killed by officials.
- The attack occurred earlier this month.
- The victims were identified as Jennifer Balcarras, 48, and her daughter Selah Reed, 20.
