Paul Mackenzie, the leader of a doomsday cult, has been sentenced to 200 years in prison for the murder of 191 individuals, including 190 children and one adult. The murders took place in the Shakahola Forest on the Kenyan coast between January 2021 and September 2023.

## Key facts
- Paul Mackenzie was sentenced to 200 years in prison.
- He was convicted of murdering 190 children and one adult.
- The murders occurred in the Shakahola Forest in Kenya.
- The timeframe for the murders was between January 2021 and September 2023.
