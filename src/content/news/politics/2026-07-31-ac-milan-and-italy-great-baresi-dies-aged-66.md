## AC Milan Legend Franco Baresi Passes Away at 66

Franco Baresi, the legendary defender and captain of AC Milan, has passed away at the age of 66, as confirmed by the Italian football club. His death marks the end of an era for both the club and Italian football, where Baresi's contributions have left an indelible mark.

### A Storied Career

Baresi was born on May 8, 1960, in Travagliato, Italy. He joined AC Milan's youth academy in 1977 and made his first-team debut in 1978. Over the course of his 20-year professional career with the club, he became synonymous with defensive excellence and leadership. Baresi's tenure at AC Milan saw him win numerous titles, including six Serie A championships and three UEFA Champions League titles. His tactical intelligence, positioning, and ability to read the game set him apart as one of the greatest defenders in football history.

### International Recognition

Baresi also enjoyed a distinguished international career with the Italian national team. He earned 81 caps and was a key player in Italy's squad during the 1986 and 1990 FIFA World Cups. Although Italy did not win the World Cup during his playing days, Baresi's performances were highly regarded, and he was known for his resilience and sportsmanship on the pitch.

### Legacy and Impact

Baresi's influence extended beyond his playing career. After retiring in 1993, he continued to be involved in football, taking on various roles within AC Milan's management and contributing to the development of young players. His legacy is not only defined by his trophies and accolades but also by the respect he garnered from teammates, opponents, and fans alike.

The news of Baresi's passing has prompted an outpouring of tributes from across the football community. Current players, former teammates, and football analysts have expressed their condolences and shared memories of the impact he had on the sport.
