Pope Francis has been discharged from the hospital following lung cancer surgery. He is set to give the traditional Angelus prayer and blessing in St Peter's Square on Sunday.

## Key facts
- The Pope will deliver the Angelus prayer and blessing in St Peter's Square on Sunday, according to BBC.
- He spoke with Russian President Vladimir Putin on Friday, urging a truce with Ukraine, as reported by Sky News.
- The Vatican confirmed the Pope's continued recovery from lung cancer surgery.
