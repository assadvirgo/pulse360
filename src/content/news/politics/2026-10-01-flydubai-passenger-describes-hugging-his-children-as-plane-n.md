## Passenger Describes Harrowing Experience During Flydubai Flight Incident

In a troubling account of a recent Flydubai flight, passenger Adam Polovincik recounted the terrifying moments when the aircraft nosedived during what has been described as an attack. The incident, which saw the plane drop more than 17,000 feet (approximately 5,200 meters), left passengers in a state of panic and fear.

Polovincik, who was traveling with his young children, shared his experience with the BBC, emphasizing the emotional turmoil he faced during the descent. As the plane rapidly lost altitude, he attempted to reassure his children, telling them, "everything is going to be all right." This instinctive response highlights the profound parental instinct to protect and comfort loved ones in times of crisis.
