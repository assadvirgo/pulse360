Flavio Bolsonaro, the son of former Brazilian President Jair Bolsonaro, has traveled from Argentina to Ecuador following his defeat in Brazil's presidential election. He reportedly entered Argentina before boarding a private flight.

## Key facts
- Flavio Bolsonaro entered Argentina before taking a private flight to Ecuador.
- He lost Brazil's presidential election.
- He arrived in Florida on Tuesday evening after leaving Brazil.
