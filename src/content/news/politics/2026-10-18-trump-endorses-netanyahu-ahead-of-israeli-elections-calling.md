Donald Trump has endorsed Benjamin Netanyahu ahead of the upcoming Israeli elections. He referred to Netanyahu as a "great warrior prime minister" in his endorsement.

- Trump endorsed Netanyahu ahead of the Israeli elections scheduled for later this month.
- He described Netanyahu as a "great warrior prime minister" according to Sky News.
- In a social media post, Trump called Netanyahu a "Strong, Brave, and Brilliant" leader according to Al Jazeera.
