Former President Trump has requested the U.S. Supreme Court to block 20,000 mail-in ballots in California before the midterm elections. The request is based on claims that these ballots belong to non-citizens.

## Key facts
- Trump is asking the Supreme Court to intervene regarding ballots in California.
- The White House claims that some mail-in votes should be set aside due to concerns about non-citizen voters.
- California officials warn that blocking ballots could disenfranchise eligible U.S. voters.
