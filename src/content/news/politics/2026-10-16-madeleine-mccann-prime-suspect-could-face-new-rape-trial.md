The prime suspect in the Madeleine McCann case may face a new rape trial. German prosecutors have initiated a legal effort to overturn his acquittal on multiple sex charges that are not related to the case of the missing British girl.

## Key facts
- The prime suspect is involved in the Madeleine McCann case.
- German prosecutors have launched a legal bid to overturn his acquittal.
- The charges are a series of sex charges unrelated to Madeleine McCann.
