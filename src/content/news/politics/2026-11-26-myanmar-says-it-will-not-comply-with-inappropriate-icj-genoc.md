Myanmar's leader announced that the government will not comply with all measures ordered by the International Court of Justice (ICJ) following a ruling that found Myanmar guilty of committing genocide against the Rohingya minority.

## Key facts
- Myanmar's government has stated it will not comply with measures ordered by the ICJ.
- The ICJ convicted Myanmar of genocide against the Rohingya minority.
