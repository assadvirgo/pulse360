Tens of thousands of people participated in a general strike in Spain, which was called by the country's main unions to address issues related to housing and the high cost of living. Clashes erupted in Barcelona and other cities as unrest spread across the country.

## Key facts
- The general strike is the first in Spain in 14 years.
- Millions of workers joined the strike, according to reports.
- The strike was organized by the main unions in Spain.
