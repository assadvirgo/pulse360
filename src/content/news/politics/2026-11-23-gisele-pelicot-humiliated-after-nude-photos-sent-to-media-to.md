Gisèle Pelicot expressed feelings of shock and humiliation after nude photos of her were sent to the media without her consent to promote her new book on coercive control. The publisher of the book has since apologized for what they described as "an unfortunate and inappropriate communication."

## Key facts
- Nude images of Gisèle Pelicot were sent to the media without her consent.
- The images were intended to promote her new book about coercive control.
- Pelicot described herself as "deeply shocked" and "humiliated."
- The publisher issued an apology regarding the incident.
