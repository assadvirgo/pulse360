Trump has advised Republican gubernatorial candidate Vivek Ramaswamy to continue airing a controversial advertisement that features the estranged mother of rival candidate Amy Acton. 

## Key facts
- Trump supports Ramaswamy's use of the advertisement.
- The ad includes Acton's mother, whose husband faced charges of sexual abuse during her childhood.
- Acton has publicly criticized the advertisement, calling it "despicable."
