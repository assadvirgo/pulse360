The Ukrainian president stated that U.S. envoys have proposed the idea of Ukraine ceding the Donbas region in exchange for NATO membership as part of efforts to end the war with Russia. He plans to present this proposal to the Ukrainian people.

## Key facts
- The U.S. suggested that Ukraine might cede land to Moscow in exchange for NATO membership.
- The proposal is part of Washington's exploration of terms to end the war.
- President Zelenskyy indicated that he would take the proposal to the Ukrainian people.
