A plane crash off the coast of Mississippi has resulted in the deaths of six individuals, including two children. US authorities are currently investigating the cause of the crash.

## Key facts
- Six people were killed in the plane crash, including two children.
- The bodies were found by divers after the crash.
- The incident involved members of the Stokes and Knox families.
- The US Coast Guard is involved in the recovery efforts.
