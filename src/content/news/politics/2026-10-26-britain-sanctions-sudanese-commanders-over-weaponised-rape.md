The UK government has imposed sanctions on senior commanders from the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) and the Sudanese Armed Forces due to their alleged involvement in a campaign of weaponised rape during the conflict in Sudan.

## Key facts
- The sanctions include travel bans and asset freezes.
- The commanders are from the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) and the Sudanese Armed Forces.
- The UK government cites their implication in a campaign of rape as the reason for the sanctions.
