The United States has approved the sale of 500 Patriot missiles to Ukraine. This decision comes as the American-made interceptors are recognized as the only effective weapon for downing Russia's ballistic missiles.

## Key facts
- The sale includes 500 Patriot missiles.
- The total value of the sale is $2.1 billion.
- The approval follows accusations from Volodymyr Zelenskyy regarding delays in weapon deliveries during the Trump administration.
