## Police Arrest Teen in Alleged Plot to Attack Illinois Hindu Temple

Authorities in Illinois have arrested a 19-year-old male in connection with an alleged mass shooting plot targeting a Hindu temple. The arrest followed a report from a coworker who received concerning messages from the suspect, prompting immediate police action.

The suspect, whose identity has not been disclosed, was taken into custody after the coworker alerted law enforcement about the disturbing communications. According to police reports, the messages indicated a potential plan to carry out a violent act at the temple, raising alarms about the safety of the community.

In a statement, the police department emphasized the importance of community vigilance and the role that the coworker played in preventing a potential tragedy. "We encourage anyone who sees or hears something suspicious to report it to the authorities," the statement read.
