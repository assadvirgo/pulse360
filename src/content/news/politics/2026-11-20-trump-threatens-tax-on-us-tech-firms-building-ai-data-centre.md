US President Donald Trump warned that tech companies will face a "tremendous tax" if they build AI data centers outside of the United States. This statement comes amid ongoing discussions about restrictions on new applications for data center construction in various states and cities.

## Key facts
- Trump stated that companies building AI data centers abroad will incur a significant tax.
- He emphasized that the U.S. will not allow its companies to establish data centers outside the country.
- The president's comments are part of a broader concern about preventing Beijing from leading in AI technology development.
