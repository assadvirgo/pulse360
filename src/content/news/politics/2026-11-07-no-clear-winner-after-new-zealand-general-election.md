The New Zealand general election has concluded without a clear winner, as the two main parties are nearly tied in the popular vote. Preliminary results indicate a divided national parliament, suggesting that coalition talks may be necessary in the coming weeks.

## Key facts
- The election count has ended without a definitive result.
- The two main parties remain almost deadlocked in the popular vote.
- Preliminary results point to a highly divided national parliament.
- Weeks of coalition talks could follow.
