US President Trump met with Russian President Putin and Chinese President Xi for trilateral talks at the APEC summit in Shenzhen, China. The meetings are part of a week of high-stakes diplomacy.

## Key facts
- Trump referred to both Putin and Xi as a "friend" in separate meetings.
- The trilateral talks took place in the southern Chinese city of Shenzhen.
- The summit is characterized as a historic meeting among the three leaders.
