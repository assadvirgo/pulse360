Researchers conducted an expedition to a remote Pacific island to investigate an object identified in satellite imagery in 2020, which was thought to be the wreckage of Amelia Earhart's plane. The investigation concluded that the object is likely just large trees.

## Key facts
- Researchers aimed to confirm the identity of an object seen in satellite imagery from 2020.
- The object was located on a remote island in the South Pacific.
- The expedition did not recover any wreckage from the site.
