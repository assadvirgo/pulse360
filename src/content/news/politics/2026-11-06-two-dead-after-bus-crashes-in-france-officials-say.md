A bus crash in eastern France has resulted in the deaths of two individuals, a child and a young adult, according to local authorities. Several others were reported injured in the incident.

## Key facts
- One child and one young adult were killed in the crash, according to BBC.
- Sky News reported that the victims were a seven-year-old girl and an 18-year-old boy.
- French authorities confirmed the fatalities and reported additional injuries.
