House Republicans have blocked an impeachment effort against Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth. The resolution was referred to committee, preventing it from being voted on by the full House.

## Key facts
- Hegseth has been accused of leading a war on Iran without the necessary congressional approval, according to Al Jazeera.
- The House voted 211 to 204 to refer the impeachment resolution to committee, as reported by Deadline.
- The outcome was influenced by absences during the vote.
- Democrats largely support Hegseth's impeachment, but the resolution was initiated by Republicans.
