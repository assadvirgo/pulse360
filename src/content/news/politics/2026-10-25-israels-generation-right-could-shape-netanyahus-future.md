In Israel, a political trend is emerging among young people who are leaning to the right, contrasting with global tendencies of youth leaning left, according to Al Jazeera. Additionally, the ultra-Orthodox community's resistance to military service raises questions about the stability of the Netanyahu government, as reported by BBC World.

## Key facts
- Young people in Israel are leaning politically to the right, according to Al Jazeera.
- This trend is in contrast to a global tendency of youth leaning to the left.
- The ultra-Orthodox community is resisting being drafted into the Israeli army, as noted by BBC World.
- There are concerns that this resistance could potentially bring down the Netanyahu government.
