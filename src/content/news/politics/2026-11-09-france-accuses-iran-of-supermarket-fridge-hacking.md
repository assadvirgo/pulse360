France has accused Iran of being behind a cyberattack that affected supermarkets, resulting in significant spoilage of chilled and frozen food. The French Foreign Minister has specifically pointed to the Iranian Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps as responsible for the incident.

## Key facts
- Tons of chilled and frozen food was spoiled due to fridges and freezers heating up in hundreds of supermarkets.
- The cyberattack occurred last month.
- French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot has attributed the attack to the Iranian Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, according to Al Jazeera.
