Former FBI director James Comey has been charged for a third time under the Trump administration. He is accused of unlawfully disclosing national security information.

## Key facts
- Comey was fired during Trump's first term.
- The latest charges involve allegations of disclosing national defense information.
- The case is part of actions taken by the Trump administration against Comey.
