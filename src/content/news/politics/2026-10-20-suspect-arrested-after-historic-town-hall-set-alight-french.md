A suspect has been arrested following a fire at the town hall of Limoges, according to reports from French media. The fire caused significant damage, including the dramatic collapse of the spire of the clock tower.

## Key facts
- A man has been arrested after a fire at the town hall of Limoges.
- The spire of the clock tower at Limoges city hall collapsed as it was engulfed in flames.
