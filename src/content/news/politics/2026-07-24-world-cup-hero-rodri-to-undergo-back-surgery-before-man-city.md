In a significant development for Manchester City, midfielder Rodri is set to undergo back surgery that will require a period of rest before he can rejoin the team. This announcement comes from the club's new head coach, Enzo Maresca, who confirmed the news during a recent press briefing.

Rodri, who played a pivotal role in Spain's triumph at the recent World Cup, has been a key player for Manchester City since joining the club in 2019. His contributions in midfield have been instrumental in securing multiple titles, including the Premier League and the UEFA Champions League. With the upcoming season on the horizon, his absence could pose challenges for the team as they look to defend their titles and maintain their competitive edge.

Maresca, who has recently taken over the managerial role at City, expressed his concern regarding Rodri's health but emphasized the importance of addressing the issue promptly. "Rodri is a vital part of our squad, but we must prioritize his long-term health. The surgery is necessary, and we will support him throughout his recovery process," Maresca stated.
