Nvidia's CEO Jensen Huang has returned to the company, prompting various reactions regarding the implications of his reinstatement. Concerns have been raised about potential federal government interference in corporate decisions, particularly following claims of credit by former President Trump.

## Key facts
- Trump claims credit for Huang's return, raising concerns over federal government meddling in corporate board decisions, according to Al Jazeera.
- Huang's comeback has provided relief to the AI trade and has driven significant boosts across the tech sector, as reported by MarketWatch.
- Financial Times notes that Trump's intervention to reinstate Huang sets a dangerous precedent.
