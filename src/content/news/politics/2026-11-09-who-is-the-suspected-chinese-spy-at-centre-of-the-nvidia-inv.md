Nvidia is conducting an investigation into its CEO, Jensen Huang, following reports of an affair with Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman. The investigation is linked to claims that Jensen Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort.

## Key facts
- Alice Huang is reported to have had a close relationship with Nvidia's CEO, Jensen Huang, according to the BBC.
- Jensen Huang has admitted to having an affair, as reported by Sky News.
- Nvidia is looking into the implications of the affair in relation to potential foreign intelligence activities.
