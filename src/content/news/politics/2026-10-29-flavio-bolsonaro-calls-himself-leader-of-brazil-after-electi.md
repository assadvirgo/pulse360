Flávio Bolsonaro, the defeated presidential candidate, has stated that he will lead the opposition from the United States. He made this declaration following his election loss. 

## Key facts
- Flávio Bolsonaro referred to himself as the "leader of Brazil" after his defeat.
- He plans to lead the opposition from the US.
- He fled Brazil amid a money laundering investigation.
- Bolsonaro claimed he had never considered leaving Brazil before his departure.
