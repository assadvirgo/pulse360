## Somalia President Criticizes Houthis' Role in Regional Stability

In a recent statement, Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud expressed strong concerns regarding the Houthi movement's influence in the region, particularly in relation to the ongoing conflict in Yemen. The president emphasized that the Houthis are "not in a position" to facilitate peace in Yemen, citing their actions as destabilizing forces that threaten not only Yemen but also the broader Horn of Africa.

## Regional Implications of Houthi Actions

President Mohamud's remarks come amid heightened tensions in the region, where the Bab al-Mandeb Strait serves as a critical maritime route for international shipping. The strait connects the Red Sea to the Gulf of Aden and is vital for global trade. The president highlighted that the Houthis' activities have disrupted navigation through this key passage, raising concerns about maritime security and the potential for increased military confrontations.

The Houthis, who have been engaged in a protracted conflict with the Yemeni government since 2014, have received support from Iran, further complicating the geopolitical landscape. President Mohamud's comments reflect a broader apprehension among regional leaders regarding Iranian influence and its implications for stability in the Horn of Africa.

## Somalia's Position on Yemen Conflict

Somalia has been grappling with its own security challenges, including the threat posed by the militant group Al-Shabaab. The Somali government has been keen to foster regional cooperation to address these challenges, and President Mohamud's statements underscore the importance of a unified approach to counteract destabilizing forces like the Houthis.

The president's assertion that the Houthis cannot bring peace to Yemen is a call for a reevaluation of the peace process, emphasizing the need for inclusive dialogue that involves all relevant stakeholders. He urged the international community to recognize the complexities of the conflict and to support efforts that promote genuine reconciliation in Yemen.

## International Response and Future Outlook

The international community has been actively engaged in attempts to mediate the conflict in Yemen, with various peace initiatives launched over the years. However, the ongoing hostilities and the Houthis' refusal to engage constructively in negotiations have hindered progress. President Mohamud's comments may resonate with other regional leaders who share similar concerns about the Houthis' role in perpetuating conflict rather than resolving it.

As Somalia continues to navigate its own path towards stability, the president's remarks serve as a reminder of the interconnectedness of conflicts in the region. The situation in Yemen remains a critical issue that requires sustained attention and collaboration among neighboring countries, as well as support from global powers.

In conclusion, President Mohamud's critique of the Houthis highlights the urgent need for a comprehensive approach to peace in Yemen, one that acknowledges the intricate dynamics at play and seeks to foster a stable and secure environment for all nations in the Horn of Africa.
