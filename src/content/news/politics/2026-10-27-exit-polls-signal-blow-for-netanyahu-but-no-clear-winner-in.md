Israel's recent election appears to be deadlocked, with exit polls indicating no clear winner. Neither Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu nor Gadi Eisenkot is projected to secure a majority.

## Key facts
- The election is Israel's first since October 7.
- Exit polls show Gadi Eisenkot's bloc slightly ahead of Netanyahu's coalition.
- Neither bloc is forecasted to secure a majority, according to TV surveys.
