Thomas Markle, the father of Meghan Markle, has been placed under house arrest following charges related to child sexual abuse material. He appeared in court in Florida after the FBI conducted searches of two storage units he rented in the Los Angeles area.

## Key facts
- Thomas Markle is 82 years old.
- He was arrested and charged with allegedly possessing child sexual abuse images.
- The FBI searched two storage units rented by him in the Los Angeles area.
