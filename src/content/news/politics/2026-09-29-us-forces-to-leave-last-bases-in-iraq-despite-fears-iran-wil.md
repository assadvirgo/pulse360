## US Forces to Withdraw from Last Bases in Iraq

In a significant shift in military strategy, the United States is preparing to withdraw its remaining forces from Iraq, marking the end of a prolonged military presence that began over two decades ago. This decision comes amid growing concerns about the potential for Iran to exploit the vacuum left by the departing American troops.

## Background of the US Military Presence in Iraq

The US military intervention in Iraq commenced in 2003, following the invasion that aimed to dismantle the regime of Saddam Hussein. Over the years, the conflict has evolved, with US forces initially focused on combat operations against insurgents and later transitioning to training and advising Iraqi security forces. Throughout this period, approximately 4,500 American service members lost their lives, underscoring the human cost of the prolonged engagement.

## The Withdrawal Decision

The decision to withdraw the last bases is part of a broader strategy by the Biden administration to recalibrate US military commitments abroad. Officials have indicated that the remaining troops will be pulled out in a phased manner, with an emphasis on ensuring that Iraqi forces are capable of maintaining security and stability in the region. The timeline for the withdrawal has not been explicitly detailed, but it is expected to occur in the coming months.

## Concerns Over Regional Stability

The impending withdrawal has raised alarms among various stakeholders, particularly regarding the influence of Iran in Iraq. Iran has long been a significant player in Iraqi politics, supporting various militia groups that could potentially fill the power vacuum left by US forces. Analysts warn that a rapid withdrawal could embolden these groups, leading to increased instability and violence in the region.

Moreover, the potential resurgence of extremist groups, such as ISIS, remains a concern. While Iraqi forces have made significant strides in combating these threats, the absence of US support could hinder their efforts and allow for a resurgence of militant activity.

## Reactions from Iraqi Officials

Iraqi officials have expressed mixed feelings about the US withdrawal. Some view it as a necessary step towards full sovereignty and independence, while others fear the implications for national security. The Iraqi government has reiterated its commitment to maintaining security and stability, emphasizing the need for continued cooperation with international partners, including the United States.

## Conclusion

As the US prepares to withdraw its last bases in Iraq, the implications of this decision will be closely monitored by both regional and global observers. The potential for Iranian influence to grow, coupled with the risks of instability and the resurgence of extremist groups, presents a complex challenge for Iraq and its allies. The coming months will be crucial in determining the future trajectory of Iraq's security landscape and the broader implications for US foreign policy in the Middle East.
