## West Indies Triumph Over India in Fourth T20 Match

In a thrilling encounter at the cricket grounds, the West Indies secured a 24-run victory over India in the fourth T20 match, effectively leveling the series. The standout performer of the match was Shai Hope, who scored an impressive 102 runs, marking another significant milestone in his burgeoning cricket career.

Shai Hope's century not only contributed to the West Indies' total but also underscored his remarkable form in the series. His innings was characterized by a blend of aggressive shots and calculated risks, allowing him to anchor the West Indies' batting lineup. This performance has further solidified his position as one of the leading batsmen in T20 cricket, as he continues to break records and set new benchmarks for consistency and excellence.

Hope's century came off just 56 balls, showcasing his ability to score quickly while maintaining a high level of precision. His innings included several boundaries, which energized the West Indies' innings and set a challenging target for the Indian team.

## Match Overview

Batting first, the West Indies posted a total of 180 runs, with Hope's century being the highlight of their innings. The team demonstrated a balanced approach, with contributions from other players, but it was Hope's explosive performance that ultimately defined the match.
