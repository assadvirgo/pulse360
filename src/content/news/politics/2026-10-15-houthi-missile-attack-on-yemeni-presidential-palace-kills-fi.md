The incident has raised concerns about the ongoing conflict and its impact on the fragile political landscape in the region.

### Context of the Conflict

The ongoing conflict in Yemen has its roots in a complex interplay of political, social, and economic factors. The Houthis, who are aligned with Iran, have been fighting against the Yemeni government, which is supported by a Saudi-led coalition. This coalition intervened in the conflict in 2015, aiming to restore the government to power and counter Houthi influence in the region.

The war has led to one of the world's worst humanitarian crises, with millions of Yemenis facing food insecurity and lack of access to essential services. The recent missile attack underscores the volatile situation in the country and the persistent threat posed by armed groups.
