Artificial intelligence-powered "doorbell cameras" are being tested in Canada to alert communities about approaching polar bears. This initiative is part of efforts by international experts to address the increased risk of dangerous encounters due to climate change.

- AI-powered doorbell cameras are being tested in Canada.
