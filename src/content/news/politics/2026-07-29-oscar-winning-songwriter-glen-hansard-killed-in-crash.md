## Oscar-Winning Songwriter Glen Hansard Killed in Crash

Glen Hansard, the acclaimed Irish songwriter and musician, has tragically passed away at the age of 56 following a car accident. Hansard, who gained international recognition for his work on the 2007 film "Once," was celebrated for his heartfelt songwriting and powerful performances.

### A Life in Music

Hansard first rose to prominence as the lead vocalist and guitarist of the band The Frames, which he co-founded in 1990. The group became a staple of the Irish music scene, known for their emotive sound and passionate live performances. However, it was his role in "Once" that brought him global acclaim. The film, which tells the story of a street musician in Dublin, featured the original song "Falling Slowly," co-written by Hansard and Markéta Irglová. The song won the Academy Award for Best Original Song, solidifying Hansard's reputation as a talented songwriter.

### Impact on the Music Community

Hansard's contributions to music extended beyond his own performances. He was known for his collaborations with various artists across genres and for his commitment to nurturing emerging talent. His work often reflected themes of love, loss, and the human experience, resonating deeply with audiences worldwide. Many in the music community have expressed their shock and sorrow at the news of his untimely death, highlighting the profound impact he had on their lives and careers.
