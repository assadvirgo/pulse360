A zookeeper has died following an attack by a leopard at a safari park where she had worked for nearly 40 years. The incident occurred while she was performing her duties at the park.

## Key facts
- A woman died after being attacked by a leopard at a safari park.
- She had spent almost her entire 40-year career at the park.
- The zookeeper began her career at the park when she was 17 years old.
