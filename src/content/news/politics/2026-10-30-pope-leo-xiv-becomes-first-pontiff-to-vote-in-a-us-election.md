Pope Leo XIV has become the first pontiff to vote in a U.S. election. The Chicago-born pope mailed in a ballot for the upcoming midterm elections, which will determine control of the U.S. Congress.

## Key facts
- The Vatican confirmed that Pope Leo XIV submitted a mail-in ballot.
- The ballot was sent from his native Chicago.
- The midterm elections are scheduled for November 3.
