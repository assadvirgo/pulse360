Self-proclaimed pastor Paul Mackenzie and his co-accused have been found guilty of multiple murder charges in Kenya. The charges include the murders of 190 children and one adult.

## Key facts
- Paul Mackenzie was found guilty of 191 murder charges, with all but one victim being a child, according to the BBC.
- Sky News reports that Mackenzie, his wife Rhoda Maweu, and 29 other co-accused were involved in the murders.
- The victims were part of a doomsday cult led by Mackenzie.
