A private Learjet crashed in Greenacres, Florida, resulting in the deaths of nine people. The jet was traveling from Austin, Texas, to the Bahamas.

## Key facts
- The crash occurred near West Palm Beach, Florida.
- The Learjet plunged into a street.
- The incident is under investigation by aviation authorities.
- The crash site is eight miles from Donald Trump’s Mar-a-Lago resort.
