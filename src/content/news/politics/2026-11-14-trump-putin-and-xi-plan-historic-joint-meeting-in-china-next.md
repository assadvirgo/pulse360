The leaders of the United States, China, and Russia are set to hold an unprecedented meeting in China next week, according to the White House. The discussions will include topics such as the reduction of nuclear weapons.

## Key facts
- Trump, Putin, and Xi will meet on the sidelines of a major economic summit.
- The meeting is described as unprecedented by the White House.
- Trump will discuss the reduction of nuclear weapons during the talks.
