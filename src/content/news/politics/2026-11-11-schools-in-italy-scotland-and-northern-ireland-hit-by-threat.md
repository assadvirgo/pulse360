More than 6,000 schools in Italy, along with schools in Scotland and Northern Ireland, received threats in what is suspected to be a coordinated bomb hoax. Authorities in all three countries have stated that there appears to be no risk to the public.

## Key facts
- Over 6,000 Italian schools received a bomb threat.
- Schools in Scotland and Northern Ireland were also targeted.
- Authorities have indicated there is no public risk associated with the threats.
- The incidents may be part of a coordinated attack aimed at disrupting life in Europe.
