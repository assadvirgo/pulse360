A man has been arrested in Pakistan for allegedly operating a counterfeit postage scheme that cost the US Postal Service $126 million. The suspect is accused of selling over 5 million fake postage labels.

## Key facts
- The arrested man is accused of running a counterfeit postage operation.
- The scheme reportedly cost the US Postal Service $126 million.
- The suspect allegedly sold more than 5 million fake postage labels.
- Each fake postage label was sold for a fixed price of $2.
