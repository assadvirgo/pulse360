European football chiefs have sent a list of questions to FIFA regarding Gianni Infantino's alleged conflicts of interest related to business dealings with the Trump family and the World Cup sell-off attempt.

## Key facts
- UEFA has sent a list of 42 questions to FIFA.
- The questions concern Gianni Infantino's alleged conflicts of interest.
- The inquiries relate to business dealings with the Trump family.
- The questions also address the World Cup sell-off attempt.
