## Flydubai Pilot Recounts Midair Attack During Call with Indian PM Modi

In a harrowing account of a midair incident, Smit Machchhar, a pilot for Flydubai, shared his experience of a violent attack by his co-pilot during a flight to Tel Aviv. The incident, which has raised significant concerns regarding airline safety and mental health among aviation professionals, was discussed in a recent call with Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

### The Incident

The attack occurred while Flydubai Flight 123 was en route to Tel Aviv. According to Machchhar, the co-pilot suddenly turned violent, leading to a chaotic situation in the cockpit. Fortunately, Machchhar was able to regain control of the aircraft and ensure the safety of all passengers and crew aboard. The quick response and decisive actions taken by Machchhar were pivotal in averting what could have been a catastrophic outcome.

### Sharing the Experience

During his conversation with PM Modi, Machchhar detailed the emotional and psychological toll that such an experience can have on pilots and crew members. He emphasized the importance of mental health support within the aviation industry, particularly in light of the increasing pressures faced by airline professionals. Machchhar's recounting of the incident serves as a reminder of the vulnerabilities that exist even in highly trained individuals operating complex machinery in high-stress environments.

### Industry Response

The aviation industry has been grappling with various challenges, including mental health issues among pilots, especially following the COVID-19 pandemic. Airlines and regulatory bodies are now being urged to implement more robust mental health programs and support systems. The incident involving Flydubai has reignited discussions about the need for comprehensive psychological evaluations and ongoing support for pilots, ensuring that they are fit to fly.

### Government Involvement

Prime Minister Modi's engagement in this matter highlights the broader implications of aviation safety and the welfare of airline personnel. His administration has expressed a commitment to addressing mental health issues across various sectors, including aviation. The Indian government is expected to collaborate with international aviation authorities to enhance safety protocols and mental health resources for airline employees.

### Conclusion

The incident on Flydubai Flight 123 serves as a stark reminder of the potential dangers that can arise in the aviation industry, not only from external threats but also from within. As the industry moves forward, it is crucial to prioritize the mental well-being of pilots and crew members to ensure the safety of all passengers. The dialogue initiated by Machchhar and PM Modi may pave the way for significant changes in how the aviation sector addresses mental health, ultimately leading to safer skies for everyone.
