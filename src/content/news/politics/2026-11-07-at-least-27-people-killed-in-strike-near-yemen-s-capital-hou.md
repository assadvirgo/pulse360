At least 27 people have been killed and 36 others wounded in airstrikes that hit homes in a village near Yemen's capital, Sanaa, according to a spokesman for Yemen's Houthi-run health ministry. The casualties reportedly include mostly women and children.

## Key facts
- At least 27 people were killed in the airstrikes.
- 36 others were reported wounded.
- The strikes targeted homes in a village near Sanaa.
- The information was provided by a spokesman for Yemen's Houthi-run health ministry.
