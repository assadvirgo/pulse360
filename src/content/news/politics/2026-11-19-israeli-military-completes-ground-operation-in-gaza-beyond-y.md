The Israeli military has completed a ground operation in Gaza beyond the Yellow Line, which Hamas has condemned as a violation of the ceasefire. The operation aimed to dismantle a "terror tunnel," according to the Israeli military.

## Key facts
- The Israeli military completed a ground operation in Gaza beyond the Yellow Line.
- Hamas condemned the operation as a "flagrant" ceasefire violation.
- The operation was conducted in an area that had been under a ceasefire last year.
- Dozens of displaced Palestinians have fled their tents and shelters in central Gaza due to the operation.
