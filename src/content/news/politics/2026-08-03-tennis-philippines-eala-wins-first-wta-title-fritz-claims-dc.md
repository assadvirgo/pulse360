The match, which extended over two days due to inclement weather, showcased Eala's determination and ability to perform under pressure.

The final, held at a prestigious venue, was initially scheduled for a single day but was interrupted by rain, forcing both players to adapt to the changing conditions. Eala's triumph is particularly noteworthy as she defeated Pegula, who is currently ranked among the top players in the world. This victory not only adds a WTA title to Eala's resume but also highlights her potential to become a formidable competitor on the international stage.
