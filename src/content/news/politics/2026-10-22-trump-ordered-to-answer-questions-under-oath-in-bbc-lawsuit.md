Former President Donald Trump has been ordered to answer questions under oath in a defamation lawsuit against the BBC. The lawsuit stems from an episode of Panorama that edited sections of a speech he delivered in 2021.

## Key facts
- Trump is suing the BBC over an edited episode of Panorama.
- A judge has ordered Trump to sit for questioning by a BBC lawyer by December 11.
- If Trump fails to comply, he may face possible dismissal of his lawsuit.
- The deposition was initially scheduled for a date that coincided with a campaign rally.
