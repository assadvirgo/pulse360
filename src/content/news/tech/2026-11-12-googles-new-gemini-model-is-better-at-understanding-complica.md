Google has launched its new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model, which is designed to provide more helpful answers to complex questions. The model can handle inquiries that include multiple parts, difficult typos, or specific requests.

## Key facts
- The Gemini 4 Hydrogen model can break down questions and address each part in its responses.
- It is available starting today in search, the Gemini app, and all tiers of Google services.
