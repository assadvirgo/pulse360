Global PC shipments experienced a significant decline in the third quarter of 2026, dropping by 21 percent year-over-year, according to Counterpoint Research. This decrease is attributed to rising prices, particularly for RAM, which has impacted demand for new PCs.

## Key facts
- Global PC shipments fell 21 percent year-over-year in Q3 2026, according to Counterpoint Research.
- The decline in PC shipments aligns with earlier reports from IDC and Omdia.
- Smartphone shipments also reached a 14-year low in the same quarter.
