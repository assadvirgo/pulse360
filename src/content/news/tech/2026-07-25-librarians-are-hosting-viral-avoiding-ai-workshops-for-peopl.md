## Rising Demand for "Avoiding AI" Workshops at Libraries

In an era where artificial intelligence (AI) is becoming increasingly integrated into daily life, public libraries across the United States are responding to a growing concern among citizens regarding the influence of Big Tech. The "Avoiding AI" workshops have emerged as a popular initiative, attracting significant interest from individuals seeking to navigate a world increasingly dominated by technology.

## The Workshop Phenomenon

The "Avoiding AI" workshops are designed to educate participants about the implications of AI technologies and provide practical strategies for minimizing their reliance on these systems. Librarians, traditionally seen as custodians of information, are now stepping into the role of educators on the potential risks associated with AI. These workshops are not only about teaching participants how to limit their use of AI tools but also about fostering critical thinking regarding technology's role in society.

Participants in these workshops express a range of concerns, from privacy issues to the ethical implications of AI decision-making. Many attendees are motivated by a desire to reclaim control over their personal data and to understand how AI technologies can impact their lives. The workshops offer a space for discussion and exploration of these themes, allowing individuals to voice their apprehensions in a supportive environment.

## Libraries as Community Hubs

Public libraries have long served as vital community resources, providing access to information and technology. In recent years, they have adapted to the changing landscape of information consumption, and the rise of AI has prompted a new wave of programming. The "Avoiding AI" workshops exemplify this adaptability, showcasing libraries as proactive institutions that respond to community needs.

The workshops typically cover a variety of topics, including the basics of AI, how to identify AI-generated content, and strategies for reducing dependency on AI tools in everyday tasks. Participants are encouraged to engage in discussions about their experiences with technology, fostering a sense of community among those who share similar concerns.

## Addressing Public Concerns

The unprecedented demand for these workshops highlights a significant shift in public sentiment regarding technology. Many individuals feel overwhelmed by the rapid advancement of AI and its pervasive presence in daily life. The workshops serve as a counterbalance to the often-unchecked growth of technology companies, providing a platform for individuals to learn about their rights and options in a digital age.

Librarians facilitating these sessions are equipped not only with knowledge about AI but also with an understanding of the ethical considerations surrounding technology. This dual expertise allows them to guide discussions that are both informative and reflective, encouraging participants to think critically about their relationship with technology.

## Conclusion

As the conversation around AI continues to evolve, the "Avoiding AI" workshops represent a significant response to public concern. By leveraging their role as community educators, libraries are fostering informed dialogue about technology and its implications. This initiative underscores the importance of critical engagement with AI and highlights the evolving role of libraries as essential resources in the digital age. As more individuals seek to navigate the complexities of technology, these workshops may play a crucial role in empowering communities to make informed choices about their digital lives.
