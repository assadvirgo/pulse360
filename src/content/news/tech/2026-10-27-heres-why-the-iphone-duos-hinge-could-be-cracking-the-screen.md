Reports have emerged following the launch of the iPhone Duo, indicating that some users experienced issues with the inner screen cracking. This problem appears to be related to the hinge of the foldable phone.

## Key facts
- Users reported broken inner screens on the iPhone Duo shortly after its launch.
- The damage typically manifests as cracks above the hinge in the middle of the screen.
- Many users shared photos of the issue on social media.
