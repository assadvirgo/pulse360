OpenAI has reached a valuation of $1.25 trillion following a new funding round that raised $11 billion. This valuation positions OpenAI among the world's most valuable companies.

## Key facts
- OpenAI's valuation increased from $852 billion to $1.25 trillion.
- The recent funding round raised an additional $11 billion.
- OpenAI previously raised $122 billion from investors in March.
