Instagram is introducing a "content protection" feature aimed at helping creators identify and manage copycat videos on the platform. This tool will allow eligible creators to see and review copies of their original videos.

## Key facts
- Instagram is rolling out a "content protection" feature.
- The feature alerts creators when copies of their videos are found on the platform.
- It automatically searches for visual matches of original videos for enrolled creators.
