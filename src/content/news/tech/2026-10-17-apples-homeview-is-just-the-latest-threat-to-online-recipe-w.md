Apple has introduced HomeView, a new smart display, showcasing it through an unconventional advertisement that highlights a family's chaotic morning routine. This approach contrasts with Apple's typical polished marketing style.

## Key facts
- HomeView was revealed by Apple in a unique advertisement.
- The ad features a messy morning routine of a family of five.
- The smart display is positioned at the center of the family's kitchen chaos.
