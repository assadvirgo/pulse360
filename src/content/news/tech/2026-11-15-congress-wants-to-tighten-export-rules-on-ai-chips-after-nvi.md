Congress is seeking to tighten export rules on AI chips following a scandal involving former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and a suspected Chinese spy. Both Democrats and Republicans are in favor of various measures to restrict exports and have called for Huang and other Nvidia leaders to testify before Congress.

## Key facts
- Congress is looking to further restrict exports of AI chips after the Nvidia scandal.
- There is bipartisan support for measures and bills aimed at tightening export rules.
- Lawmakers have demanded testimony from Jensen Huang and other Nvidia leaders.
- The scandal is said to have increased momentum for cracking down on US hardware exports to China, according to the Financial Times.
