OpenAI is launching a wallet for its Dots AI agents, called ChatGPT Wallet. This feature is rolling out to ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US.

## Key facts
- ChatGPT Wallet is available for ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and older.
- Users must have a US billing address to access the wallet.
- Eligible payment methods include US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard, and Visa cards.
