Massive new data centers are being developed to support the growing demands of AI technology. However, this rapid expansion has led to conflicts regarding their effects on power grids, utility costs, local communities, and the environment.

## Key facts
- The expansion of data centers is crucial for tech companies' ambitions in AI.
- There are concerns about the impact of these energy-intensive facilities on power grids.
- The development of data centers has sparked debates over utility bills and environmental issues.
- Data centers have become a significant bipartisan issue.
