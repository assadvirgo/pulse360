The TechCrunch Founder Summit 2026 will take place on November 4 at SoWa Power Station in Boston. The event will feature networking powered by Braindate, aimed at facilitating easier connections through 1:1 or small-group meetings.

## Key facts
- The TechCrunch Founder Summit is scheduled for November 4, 2026.
- The event will be held at SoWa Power Station in Boston.
- Networking will be facilitated by Braindate for easier connections.
- Attendees can register before the event to save up to $100 on their pass and receive a second pass for free.
