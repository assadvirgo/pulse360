The FCC has authorized SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million AI chip-equipped satellites as part of its "Starmind" initiative. This marks the first approval from the agency for such a project, amidst competition from other companies like Google and Blue Origin.

## Key facts
- The FCC announced the authorization on Wednesday.
- SpaceX's initiative is named "Starmind."
- The satellites are equipped with AI chips.
- Other companies, including Google and Blue Origin, are also pursuing similar projects.
