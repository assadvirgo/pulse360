Google's Familiar Faces AI feature is now compatible with select Walmart smart home cameras. The update allows the $35 Onn Outdoor Camera Plug-In and the $77 Onn Floodlight Camera Hardwired to recognize individuals in the home.

## Key facts
- Walmart's smart home cameras can now use Google's Familiar Faces detection feature.
- The compatible models include the $35 Onn Outdoor Camera Plug-In and the $77 Onn Floodlight Camera Hardwired.
- A Google Home Premium subscription is required to access the Familiar Faces feature.
