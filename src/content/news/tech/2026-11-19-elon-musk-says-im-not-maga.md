Elon Musk stated during an appearance at The New York Times' DealBook Summit that he is not associated with the term "MAGA." He expressed uncertainty about the meaning of the term and mentioned feeling "pretty foolish" for his spending during the U.S. midterms, according to TechCrunch.

## Key facts
- Elon Musk spoke at The New York Times' DealBook Summit.
- He stated he wasn't sure what the term "MAGA" meant.
