Xbox's first chief operating officer, Helen Chiang, is leaving the company after a brief tenure in the newly created role. According to an internal memo, her departure is effective today. Chiang has been with Microsoft for 21 years and previously served as the head of Minecraft.

## Key facts
- Helen Chiang is leaving her position as Xbox's first chief operating officer.
- The role was created just a few months ago.
- Chiang has been with Microsoft for 21 years.
- Before her promotion to Xbox COO, she was the head of Minecraft.
