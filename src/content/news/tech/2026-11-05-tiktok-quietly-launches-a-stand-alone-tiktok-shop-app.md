TikTok has launched a standalone app for TikTok Shop in several countries. The app provides features aimed at enhancing the shopping experience for users.

## Key facts
- The TikTok Shop app offers personalized product recommendations.
- Users can shop through livestreams and short-form videos.
- The app includes exclusive deals, in-app checkout, order tracking, and customer support.
- The launch was noted by social media consultant Matt Navarra.
