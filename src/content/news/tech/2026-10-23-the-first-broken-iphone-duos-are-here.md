The iPhone Duo officially went on sale today, but reports indicate that some units already have broken screens. The issue appears to be occurring at a similar location on the inner screen along the hinge, according to posts on Reddit.

## Key facts
- The iPhone Duo went on sale today.
- Some units have been reported with broken screens.
- The issue is occurring along the hinge on the inner screen.
- Reports of the problem have been shared on Reddit.
