title: Blue Planet III is keeping the rough edges in the age of AI

BBC's Blue Planet III aims to showcase authenticity by including less-than-perfect shots in its footage. This approach contrasts with previous versions of the nature series, which featured pristine visuals that appeared almost unreal. The decision to retain rough edges is a response to the polished look often associated with AI-generated content, according to The Verge. 

## Key facts
- Blue Planet III is produced by the BBC.
- The series is focusing on authenticity by including less-than-perfect shots.
- Previous versions of the series were known for their crystal-clear footage.
- The inclusion of rough edges is a counter to the overly polished appearance of AI content.
