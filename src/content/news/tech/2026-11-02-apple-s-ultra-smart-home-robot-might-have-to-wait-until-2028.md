Apple has reportedly delayed the launch of its rumored smart home robot until next year at the earliest or possibly even 2028, according to The Information. The hardware for the robot has been finished for around two years, but the software is not ready.

## Key facts
- Apple has delayed the launch of its smart home robot.
- The launch may not occur until next year at the earliest or as late as 2028.
- The robot's hardware has been completed for approximately two years.
- The delay is attributed to the software not being ready.
