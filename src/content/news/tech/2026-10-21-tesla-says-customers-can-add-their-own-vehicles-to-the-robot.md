Tesla has announced that customers will be able to add their own vehicles to the company's robotaxi fleet next year. This information was shared during the third-quarter earnings call by Tesla's VP for Autonomous Driving, Ashok Elluswamy.

- The announcement was made during the company's third-quarter earnings call.
- The information was provided by Tesla VP for Autonomous Driving Ashok Elluswamy.
