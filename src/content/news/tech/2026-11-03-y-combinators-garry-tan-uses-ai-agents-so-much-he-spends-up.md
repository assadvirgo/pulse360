Y Combinator's Garry Tan has revealed that he spends up to $200,000 a year on tokens for AI agents. He humorously described his enthusiasm for working with these agents as leading to "cyber psychosis," affecting his sleep patterns. This information was shared during an episode of Walter Isaacson's podcast, The Builders, where Tan discussed his experiences.

## Key facts
- Garry Tan is associated with Y Combinator.
- He spends up to $200,000 annually on tokens for AI agents.
- Tan mentioned experiencing "cyber psychosis" due to his excitement about AI.
- His comments were made on Walter Isaacson's podcast, The Builders.
