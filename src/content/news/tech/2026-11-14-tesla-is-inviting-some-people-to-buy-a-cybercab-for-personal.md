Tesla is offering select customers the opportunity to purchase a Cybercab for personal use. However, the vehicle will only be available in certain service areas.

## Key facts
- The Cybercab is a two-seater vehicle designed without a steering wheel.
- The offer is limited to "select customers."
- Availability is restricted to specific service areas.
