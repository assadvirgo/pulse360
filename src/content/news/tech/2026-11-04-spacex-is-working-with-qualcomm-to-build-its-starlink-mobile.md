SpaceX is collaborating with Qualcomm to develop a nationwide mobile network. The partnership involves deploying Qualcomm's Dragonwing technology at numerous 5G cell sites across the United States.

## Key facts
- SpaceX and Qualcomm are entering a multi-year deal.
- The collaboration aims to implement Dragonwing technology at "tens of thousands" of 5G cell sites.
- Initial deployments are scheduled to begin in 2027.
