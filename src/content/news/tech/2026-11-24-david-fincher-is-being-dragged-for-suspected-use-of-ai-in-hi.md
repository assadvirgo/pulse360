David Fincher's latest film, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, has come under scrutiny following early screenings. Viewers have raised concerns about the potential use of generative AI in the film, citing issues with visuals.

## Key facts
- Early screenings of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth have prompted questions about the use of generative AI.
- Critics have noted "uncanny, inconsistent visuals" in the film.
- The film is a sequel to Once Upon a Time in Hollywood and stars Brad Pitt.
