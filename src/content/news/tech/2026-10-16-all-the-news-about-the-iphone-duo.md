Apple has announced its new foldable phone, the iPhone Duo. The device features a 5.4-inch outer display and a 7.6-inch inner display, along with a “nano-texture” finish on the inner screen.

## Key facts
- The iPhone Duo has a 5.4-inch outer display and a 7.6-inch inner display.
- It features a “nano-texture” finish on the inner screen and has minimal visible crease.
- The device uses Touch ID on the power button instead of Face ID.
- It includes two cameras, a main lens, and additional unspecified features.
