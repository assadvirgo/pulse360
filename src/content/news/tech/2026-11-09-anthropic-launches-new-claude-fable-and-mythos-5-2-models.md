Anthropic has launched two new models, Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, aimed at addressing significant technical challenges. The company claims these models exhibit enhancements in various areas, including coding, reasoning, and vision.

## Key facts
- Anthropic announced the release of Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2.
- The new models are designed to tackle "daunting" technical challenges.
- Improvements include faster output generation compared to the previous 5.1 versions.
- The models show enhancements in coding, reasoning, vision, and user communication, according to the company.
