Mercedes-AMG has introduced its first ground-up SUV, the 2028 Mercedes-AMG GT SUV, which features over 1,150 horsepower and ultra-fast charging capabilities. This new model follows the earlier release of the GT four-door coupe, which also showcased the brand's performance potential.

## Key facts
- The 2028 Mercedes-AMG GT SUV is an electric vehicle.
- It delivers over 1,150 horsepower.
- The SUV is designed for ultra-fast charging.
- This model is part of Mercedes' AMG performance brand lineup.
