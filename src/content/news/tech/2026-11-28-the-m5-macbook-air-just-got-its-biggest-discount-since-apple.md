The 13-inch M5 MacBook Air is currently on sale for Black Friday, offering a significant discount since Apple raised its prices. The device, featuring 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage, is available at Amazon and Best Buy starting at $999, which is $300 off the original price.

## Key facts
- The M5 MacBook Air was priced $200 higher in June.
- The current sale price for the MacBook Air is $999.
- The discount of $300 is noted as the largest since the price increase.
- The sale is available at Amazon and Best Buy.
