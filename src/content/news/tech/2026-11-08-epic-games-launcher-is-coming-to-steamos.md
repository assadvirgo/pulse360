Epic Games has confirmed that the Epic Games Launcher will be compatible with SteamOS. This announcement follows an earlier statement from October, where Epic Games indicated it was exploring the possibility of making Fortnite compatible with Valve's Linux-based operating system.

## Key facts
- Epic Games has confirmed compatibility of the Epic Games Launcher with SteamOS.
- The announcement was made by Communications Director Jake Jones.
- The confirmation was reported to GamingOnLinux.
- Previously, SteamOS was not specifically listed as a supported distribution.
