Rep. Ritchie Torres of New York introduced the Power To The Players Act, which aims to ensure consumers have ownership of video games upon purchase rather than a licensing agreement. The bill would require the Federal Trade Commission to establish regulations to support this ownership model.

## Key facts
- The Power To The Players Act was introduced this week.
- The bill seeks to guarantee consumers ownership of video games when they pay for them.
- The legislation would direct the Federal Trade Commission to create relevant regulations.
- There are significant hurdles ahead for the bill, both technical and legislative, according to Ars Technica.
