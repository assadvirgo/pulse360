The Trump administration is expected to announce plans to weaken rules that protect against methane leaks from oil and gas companies. Methane is identified as a potent greenhouse gas and a significant contributor to smog.

## Key facts
- The Trump administration plans to weaken regulations on methane leaks.
- Methane is a powerful greenhouse gas, up to 80 times more potent than carbon dioxide over a 20-year period.
- Methane is the primary component of smog.
