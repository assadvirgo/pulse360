The Crkd Nitro Deck 2, designed for both the original Nintendo Switch and the larger Switch 2, is a new accessory that follows the success of its predecessor. The original Nitro Deck was well-received for providing a solution to Joy-Con drift issues without requiring complicated repairs.

## Key facts
- The Crkd Nitro Deck was a popular accessory for the original Nintendo Switch.
- It features Hall effect sticks to address drift issues in Joy-Con controllers.
- The Nitro Deck 2 is compatible with both the original Switch and the Switch 2.
- The price of the Nitro Deck 2 is $59.99.
