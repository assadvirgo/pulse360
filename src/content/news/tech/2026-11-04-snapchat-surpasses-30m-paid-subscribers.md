Snapchat has surpassed 30 million paid subscribers. This milestone follows a year after the platform announced a limit on free storage for its Memories feature.

## Key facts
- Snapchat reached over 30 million paid subscribers.
- The milestone was achieved a year after a limit on free storage for the Memories feature was announced.
- Users were informed they would need to pay $1.99 per month for additional storage or subscribe to Snapchat+ to retain their Memories.
