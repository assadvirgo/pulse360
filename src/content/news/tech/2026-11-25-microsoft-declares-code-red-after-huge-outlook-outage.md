Microsoft has declared a "Code Red" internally following a significant outage affecting Exchange Online and Microsoft Outlook. The outage lasted nearly 24 hours and impacted millions of customers.

## Key facts
- The outage was caused by a bug on Microsoft 365 networking gateway servers that had been present since July.
- The declaration of "Code Red" is unusual for the company.
- The outage affected email services for millions of users.
