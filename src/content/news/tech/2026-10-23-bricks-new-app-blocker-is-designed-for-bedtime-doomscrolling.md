Brick has launched a new device called the Brick Night, aimed at helping users reduce doomscrolling before bedtime. This device is priced at $129 and introduces a location-based feature, differing from its predecessor that required users to tap their phones against it.

## Key facts
- Brick is the company behind a $59 gadget that blocks app usage on phones.
- The new Brick Night device is designed to help users cut back on doomscrolling in bed.
- The Brick Night costs $129 and includes a location-based element.
