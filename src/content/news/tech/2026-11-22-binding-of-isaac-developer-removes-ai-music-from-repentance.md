The developer of The Binding of Isaac: Repentance Plus has removed AI-generated music from the game following backlash from players. The removal comes after a segment of the game was review-bombed on Steam due to the discovery of AI music used in a specific section.

## Key facts
- The game faced review-bombing on Steam after the use of AI music was revealed.
- A Reddit user identified the AI songs during a Grand Theft Auto-themed section of the game.
- Audio files with Suno metadata were found, linking them to the AI music.
