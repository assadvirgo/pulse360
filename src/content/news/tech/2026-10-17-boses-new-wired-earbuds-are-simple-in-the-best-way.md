Bose has introduced new wired earbuds, which come at a time when sales of wired earbuds have increased after five years of decline. This resurgence in wired earbud sales is attributed to their renewed popularity.

## Key facts
- Wired earbud sales have increased this year after five years of decline.
- The rise in sales is partly attributed to their perceived coolness.
- The trend follows Apple's removal of the headphone jack from the iPhone in 2016, which led many users to switch to Bluetooth earbuds.
