SpaceX plans to attempt catching its Starship and booster next month, according to CEO Elon Musk. This effort aims to recover the entire orbital launch system for the first time.

## Key facts
- SpaceX will try to catch Starship and its booster next month.
- The goal is to recover the entire orbital launch system.
- If successful, it would mark the first time in history that an orbital launch vehicle has been fully recovered.
