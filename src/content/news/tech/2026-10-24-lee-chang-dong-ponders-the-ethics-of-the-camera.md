Lee Chang Dong's seventh feature film, Possible Love, explores the ethics of the camera. The film is inspired by true events from 2009, when a Korean conglomerate fired a significant portion of its staff, leading to numerous suicides.

## Key facts
- Possible Love is Lee Chang Dong's seventh feature film.
- The film is inspired by true events from 2009.
- In 2009, a Korean conglomerate fired 2,600 employees, which resulted in many deaths by suicide.
- The film continues Lee's exploration of themes related to class and labor.
