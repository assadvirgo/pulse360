The US government is implementing de facto import bans on certain Chinese consumer gadgets, including drones, routers, and Roombas. This action reflects the government's stance on foreign technology.

## Key facts
- The US government aims to prevent Chinese drones, routers, and Roombas from entering the country.
- These gadgets are the first to face import bans due to the government's concerns.
- The bans may extend to additional consumer gadgets in the future.
