Apple has acknowledged an issue with the inner display of a small number of iPhone Duo units, following reports of cracked and broken screens. The company stated that the problem often manifests as a bright line running from the center to the right side of the display.

## Key facts
- Apple is aware of an issue impacting the inner display on a small number of iPhone Duo units.
- Reports indicate that the screens are often cracked or broken.
- The issue is characterized by a bright line running from the center to the right side of the display.
