The data center boom is facing significant challenges that may prevent many announced projects from being built. Local opposition, permitting delays, and a scramble for electricity are among the obstacles impacting the development of these facilities.

## Key facts
- The data center boom is encountering serious real-world obstacles.
- Local opposition and permitting delays are significant challenges.
- There is a scramble for electricity affecting project development.
- Many announced data center projects may never be built.
