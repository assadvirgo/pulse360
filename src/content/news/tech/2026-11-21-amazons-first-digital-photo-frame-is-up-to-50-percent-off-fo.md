Amazon has announced a significant discount on its first digital photo frame, the Alexa Photo Frame, for Black Friday. The frame, which was introduced less than a month ago, is now available for $114, reflecting a $115 discount.

## Key facts
- The Alexa Photo Frame is Amazon's first digital photo frame.
- It was announced less than a month ago.
- The current price is $114, which is $115 off the original price.
- The frame is available at Amazon and Best Buy.
- It is marketed as a holiday gift for displaying family photos.
