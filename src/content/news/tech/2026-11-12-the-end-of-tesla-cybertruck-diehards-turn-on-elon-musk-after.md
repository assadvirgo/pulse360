The Cybertruck is reportedly being discontinued, leading to backlash from fans who had built online identities around it. This reaction is unusual, as Elon Musk's fanbase typically avoids criticizing his decisions. 

## Key facts
- The Cybertruck is described as heading to a "cyber-dumpster."
- Fans of the Cybertruck are feeling bereft due to its discontinuation.
- The backlash from the Tesla community is directed at Elon Musk.
