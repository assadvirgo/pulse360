Sheherazaad has released her debut LP titled Khudgarzi, which features a blend of traditional South Asian music styles. The album is primarily sung in Urdu and showcases a reverb-drenched folk-noir sound.

## Key facts
- Sheherazaad gained attention in the music industry with her EP Qasr.
- Qasr was produced by Grammy Award winner Arooj Aftab.
- Khudgarzi incorporates instrumentation from Carnatic and Hindustani music styles.
