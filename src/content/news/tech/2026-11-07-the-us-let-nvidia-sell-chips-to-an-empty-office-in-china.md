Concerns have emerged regarding the sale of Nvidia's H200 AI chips to Chinese firms, particularly New Matrix, which is reportedly on the list of approved companies. Investigations indicate that New Matrix's offices are empty, raising questions about the vetting process by both the Commerce Department and Nvidia.

## Key facts
- Nvidia is selling H200 AI chips to Chinese firms.
- New Matrix is listed as an approved firm for these sales.
- Reports indicate that New Matrix's offices are empty. 
- Concerns have been raised about the due diligence of the Commerce Department and Nvidia.
