Apple has announced the eighth-generation iPad Mini, which will feature OLED technology for the first time. Preorders for the device are currently available through various retailers, although it will not be released until November 6th.

## Key facts
- The eighth-generation iPad Mini was announced earlier this week.
- This model will be the first to include OLED technology.
- Preorders are available from Amazon, Walmart, and Apple.
- The iPad Mini is set to be released on November 6th.
