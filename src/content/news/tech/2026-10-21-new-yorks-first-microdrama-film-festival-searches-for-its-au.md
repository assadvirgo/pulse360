New York City hosted its first microdrama film festival, Alza, founded by former Tribeca Festival chief operating officer Pete Torres. Filmmakers and creators expressed confidence that their projects would resonate with audiences.

## Key facts
- Alza is New York City's first microdrama film festival.
- The festival was founded by Pete Torres.
- Filmmakers are optimistic about the audience's reception of their projects.
