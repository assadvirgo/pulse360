Meta reported that it would have earned over $22 billion if it had not been required to pay a child safety settlement. The company disclosed that it paid $9.89 billion as part of this settlement in its third-quarter earnings report.

## Key facts
- Meta paid $9.89 billion for a portion of a child safety settlement with US attorneys general.
- The company's income from operations would have been $22.89 billion without the settlement.
- The information was released in Meta's third-quarter earnings report.
