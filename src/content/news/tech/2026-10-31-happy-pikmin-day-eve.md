Nintendo announced that November 1st would be the first annual Pikmin Day. The announcement was made during a Pikmin Day Direct, where Shigeru Miyamoto shared new information about Pikmin 4 for the Switch 2, an update for the mobile app Pikmin Bloom, and the Hello! Pikmin series of shorts.

## Key facts
- November 1st is designated as the first annual Pikmin Day.
- Shigeru Miyamoto shared new information about Pikmin 4 for the Switch 2.
- An update for the mobile app Pikmin Bloom was announced.
- The Hello! Pikmin series of shorts was introduced.
