Google's Chrome browser has expanded its enhanced autofill feature to include information sourced from Gmail. This update aims to streamline the process of filling out forms by utilizing data from users' Google Accounts.

## Key facts
- Chrome's enhanced autofill previously included name, passport number, email address, and physical addresses.
- The feature now incorporates suggestions from Gmail using Gemini with Personal Intelligence.
- The update is intended to save users time when completing online forms.
