Palmer Luckey has launched a startup named Warhound, which aims to develop genetically modified military dogs. The company has secured $200 million in funding and focuses on creating "elite working dogs" with advanced body armor.

## Key facts
- Warhound emerged from stealth on Wednesday.
- The startup has received $200 million in funding.
- The mission is to create "elite working dogs" equipped with advanced body armor.
- The company was conceived by Anduril's co-founder Ben Lamm, who aims to clone and genetically edit working canines.
