Microsoft has released a firmware update for its Surface Laptop Ultra to address several bugs that were present at launch. The update, released on October 22, aims to fix issues related to screen flickering, unexpected restarts, and lengthy reboot times.

## Key facts
- The update addresses screen flickering, unexpected restarts, and lengthy reboot times.
- Additional unspecified bug fixes and general performance improvements are included.
- The announcement was made by Microsoft.
