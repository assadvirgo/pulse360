JustWatch is launching its own streaming service, expanding from being a streaming search guide to offering content directly to users. The service will include free, ad-supported movies and shows, as well as a $3.99-per-month ad-free tier and movie rentals.

## Key facts
- JustWatch is a streaming search guide used by 50 million people.
- The new service will offer free, ad-supported movies and shows.
- There will be a $3.99-per-month ad-free tier available.
- Movie rentals will also be part of the service.
