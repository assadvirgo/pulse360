In the 1990s, Sony believed society was nearing a robot takeover, envisioning a future with humanoid robots as coworkers, friends, and pets. The company focused on creating a robotic pet, which led to the development of the Aibo.

## Key facts
- Sony was convinced of an impending robot takeover in the 1990s.
- The company envisioned humanoid robots as coworkers and friends.
- Sony aimed to create a robotic pet, resulting in the Aibo.
