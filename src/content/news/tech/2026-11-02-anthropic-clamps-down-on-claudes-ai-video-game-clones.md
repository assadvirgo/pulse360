Anthropic has updated Claude Code's system prompt to prevent the AI tool from recreating existing video games. This decision comes amid concerns over AI-made decompilations, recompilations, and ports in the retro gaming space.

## Key facts
- Anthropic has made changes to Claude Code's system prompt.
- The update aims to stop the recreation of existing video games.
- The retro gaming community is experiencing an influx of AI-generated content.
