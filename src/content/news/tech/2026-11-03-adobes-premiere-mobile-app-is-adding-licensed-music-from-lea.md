Adobe is collaborating with Universal Music Group and Warner Music Group to enhance its Premiere mobile video editing app with a library of licensed music clips. The partnership aims to include popular songs from leading artists, although specific musicians have not been disclosed.

## Key facts
- Adobe is partnering with Universal Music Group and Warner Music Group.
- The collaboration will add licensed music clips to the Premiere mobile app.
- The music library will feature popular songs from leading artists.
- Specific musicians involved in the partnership have not been announced.
