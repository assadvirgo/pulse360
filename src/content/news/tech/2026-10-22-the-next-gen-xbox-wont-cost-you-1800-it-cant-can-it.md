A report from Fortune suggests that the next-generation Xbox, expected to launch in 2028, may cost between $1,200 and $1,800. This projection has raised concerns about the viability of the console, with some speculating that such a high price could lead to its failure in the market.

## Key facts
- Fortune reports that Microsoft insiders have indicated a projected price range for the next Xbox.
- The anticipated launch year for the next Xbox is 2028.
- There are concerns that the high price could make the console unappealing to consumers.
