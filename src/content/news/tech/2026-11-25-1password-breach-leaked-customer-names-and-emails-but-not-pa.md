1Password reported a breach in which a hacker accessed its backend systems, leading to the theft of customer names and email addresses. The company stated that passwords and other information stored in customer vaults were not compromised, but the incident may increase the risk of phishing attempts.

## Key facts
- A hacker stole customer names and email addresses from 1Password.
- The breach did not expose passwords or other information in customer vaults.
- The company indicated that users could be at risk of phishing attempts.
- It is unclear how many users are affected by the breach.
