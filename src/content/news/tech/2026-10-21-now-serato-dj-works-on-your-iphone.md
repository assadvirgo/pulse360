Serato DJ has launched its DJ suite for iOS, making it available on iPhone and iPad. This marks the first time the company has introduced most of its features to mobile devices.

## Key facts
- Serato DJ for iOS is now available for iPhone and iPad.
- Previously, Serato had limited mobile offerings, such as the Pyro app.
- The new app brings pro-level DJ features to mobile users.
