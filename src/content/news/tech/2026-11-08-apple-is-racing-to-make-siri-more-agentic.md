Apple is working to enhance Siri's capabilities to make it more competitive with AI agents from OpenAI and Meta. CEO John Ternus is reportedly urging AI chief Craig Federighi to expedite the development of a more powerful Siri.

## Key facts
- CEO John Ternus is pressuring Craig Federighi to accelerate the Siri roadmap.
- The goal is to create a powerful AI agent that can compete with OpenAI's Dots and Meta's Muse.
- Federighi has delivered a functional version of Siri AI in a short timeframe.
