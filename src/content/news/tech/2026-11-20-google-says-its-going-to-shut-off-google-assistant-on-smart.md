Google has announced plans to make significant changes to its smart home software. The company will retire Google Assistant on its smart home devices and shut down the Nest app in favor of the Google Home app.

## Key facts
- Google will shut down the Nest app in favor of the Google Home app.
- The Nest app shutdown is scheduled for January 25, 2027.
- Google Assistant will be retired on its smart home devices.
