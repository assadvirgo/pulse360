Sony has released the WH-1000XM4C headphones, an updated version of its popular WH-1000XM4 design. The new model is currently available for $198 during Black Friday sales at retailers including Amazon and Best Buy.

## Key facts
- The WH-1000XM4C headphones are an improvement on the WH-1000XM4 design.
- The WH-1000XM4C model is priced at $198 for Black Friday.
- The WH-1000XM4C sounds nearly as good as the pricier XM6 model, which is currently priced at $348.
