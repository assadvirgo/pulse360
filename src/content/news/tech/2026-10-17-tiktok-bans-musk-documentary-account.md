TikTok has banned the social media account associated with Alex Gibney's documentary about Elon Musk. The ban occurred just before the film's wider release, and the distributor, Bleecker Street, was informed of the impending "permanent suspension."

## Key facts
- TikTok banned the account of the Musk documentary ahead of its wider release.
- The film's distributor, Bleecker Street, was notified of the imminent suspension.
- TikTok did not respond to a request for comment regarding the ban.
