Apple's Magic Keyboards for iPad are currently available at a significant discount through Woot. However, these keyboards come with a catch regarding warranty and support.

## Key facts
- The Magic Keyboards for iPad Air or iPad Pro are being offered at about half off their usual price at Woot.
- These keyboards have a 90-day warranty through Woot.
- The keyboards are not eligible for AppleCare.
