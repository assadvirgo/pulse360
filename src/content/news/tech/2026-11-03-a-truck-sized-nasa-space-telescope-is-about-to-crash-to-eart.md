NASA's Swift Observatory, launched nearly 22 years ago, is expected to re-enter Earth's atmosphere on Wednesday. Attempts to push the aging space telescope into a higher orbit were unsuccessful.

## Key facts
- NASA's Swift Observatory is set to fall back to Earth after nearly 22 years in operation.
- The anticipated re-entry time is around 3:30 AM ET, with a possible variance of 11 hours.
- Efforts to move the telescope into a higher orbit failed.
