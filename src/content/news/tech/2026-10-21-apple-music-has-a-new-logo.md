Apple has officially launched a rebrand of Apple Music, introducing a new logo and updated wordmark. This announcement follows the appearance of the redesigned elements in a video and an updated app icon in an iOS 27.2 beta.

## Key facts
- Apple Music has a new logo as part of its rebranding.
- The rebrand includes an updated wordmark.
- A new sound composed by British producer Fred Again has been introduced.
- The redesigned elements were previewed in a video and an iOS 27.2 beta.
