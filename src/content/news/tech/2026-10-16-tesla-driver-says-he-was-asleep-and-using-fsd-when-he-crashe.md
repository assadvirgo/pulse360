A Tesla driver reportedly stated he was asleep and using the Full Self-Driving (FSD) mode when he crashed into highway patrol cars. The California Highway Patrol has issued a warning regarding the use of FSD, emphasizing that it does not make vehicles fully autonomous.

## Key facts
- The driver claimed to have been asleep while using FSD during the crash.
- The California Highway Patrol released video showing the Tesla speeding.
- CHP cautioned that FSD does not relieve drivers of their responsibility to operate their vehicles safely.
