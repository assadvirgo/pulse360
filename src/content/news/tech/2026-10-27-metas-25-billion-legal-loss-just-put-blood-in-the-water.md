Meta has been ordered by a New Mexico judge to pay $25 billion to resolve claims that it misled users regarding privacy issues from 2018. This ruling significantly exceeds a previous $17 billion settlement announced by attorneys general from across the US.

## Key facts
- A New Mexico judge ruled that Meta must pay $25 billion.
- The ruling addresses claims that Meta misled users about privacy.
- This amount surpasses a previously announced $17 billion settlement.
