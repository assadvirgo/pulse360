Interim Nvidia CEO Colette Kress announced the company's Q3 earnings without mentioning former CEO Jensen Huang or the details of his departure. Board chair Harvey Jones stated that the board does not intend to reinstate Huang.

## Key facts
- Nvidia's Q3 earnings were announced on Tuesday.
- Colette Kress is the interim CEO of Nvidia.
- The earnings call did not initially address Jensen Huang's departure.
- Board chair Harvey Jones confirmed that the board does not intend to reinstate Huang.
