Veritas is expanding its anti-counterfeit chip technology to include jewelry, trophies, and fragrance bottles. The startup aims to combat the increasing sophistication of counterfeiters who create "superfakes" that mimic genuine products.

## Key facts
- Veritas has developed a chip designed to verify the authenticity of goods.
- The company is targeting jewelry, trophies, and fragrance bottles with its technology.
- Counterfeiters are improving their methods, making it harder to distinguish between real and fake products.
