Last week, SpaceX's Starshield, the version of Starlink used by the Ukrainian military, was throttled to restrict access for terminals traveling above certain speeds. This change affected the Ukrainian army's interceptors, which are used to counter Russian drones, causing them to stop working at high speeds. As a result, it became easier for Russia to attack Ukraine's energy infrastructure, according to The Verge.
