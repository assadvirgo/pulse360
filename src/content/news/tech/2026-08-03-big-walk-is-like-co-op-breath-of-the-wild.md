## House House Announces New Game "Big Walk" Inspired by Co-op Adventure

House House, the Australian game development studio renowned for its quirky title "Untitled Goose Game," has unveiled its latest project, "Big Walk." This new game is anticipated to capture the whimsical essence of its predecessor while introducing cooperative gameplay elements reminiscent of the popular title "The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild."

## A New Direction for House House

"Untitled Goose Game" achieved significant acclaim for its unique premise and playful mechanics, allowing players to embody a mischievous goose causing chaos in a serene village. Following the success of this title, House House aims to explore new horizons with "Big Walk." The developers have hinted that this game will diverge from the solitary antics of the goose to foster a more collaborative experience among players.

## Gameplay Mechanics and Features

While specific gameplay details remain sparse, early descriptions suggest that "Big Walk" will incorporate elements of exploration and adventure akin to "Breath of the Wild." Players will likely navigate expansive environments, solve puzzles, and engage in various activities that promote teamwork. The cooperative aspect is expected to enhance the experience, allowing friends to join forces and tackle challenges together.

The studio's commitment to creativity and humor is expected to be a hallmark of "Big Walk." Players can anticipate a blend of lighthearted interactions and engaging gameplay that encourages exploration and collaboration. As with previous titles, House House is likely to focus on intuitive controls and charming visuals, creating an inviting atmosphere for both casual and dedicated gamers.
