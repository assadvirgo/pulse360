Kodak has introduced a new product called the Kodak SlideTag, a tiny digital photo frame that also functions as a tracker. This follows the expansion of its Charmera collection, which included a Millennium Edition with Y2K-inspired designs earlier this year.

## Key facts
- The Kodak SlideTag is designed to display photos.
- It is part of Reto's follow-up to its tiny Kodak camera.
- The previous Charmera product was limited to taking photos and videos.
