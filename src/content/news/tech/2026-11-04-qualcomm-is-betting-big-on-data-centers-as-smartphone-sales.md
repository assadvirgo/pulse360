Qualcomm is shifting its focus to data centers as smartphone sales decline significantly. The company announced its plans to enter the AI data center market during its Q4 2026 earnings call.

## Key facts
- Smartphone sales have decreased by around 20 percent compared to 2025.
- Qualcomm is looking to data centers to improve its financial performance.
- The company has formed partnerships with Amazon and Meta to expand in the AI data center business.
