Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella expressed his belief in the transformative potential of artificial general intelligence (AGI) during a recent discussion. He highlighted the significant changes AGI could bring to the economy and political structures.

## Key facts
- Satya Nadella stated he is "AGI-pilled" regarding the impact of AI on the economy and politics.
- He questioned the organization of the political economy around AGI.
