Pennsylvania has enacted the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act, which mandates labeling for ads that are significantly modified or generated by artificial intelligence. This requirement applies to various forms of advertising, including still images, videos, audio, and text.

## Key facts
- The law was signed by Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro.
- It requires disclosures on AI-generated ads.
- The labeling must be in the same format as the original ad.
