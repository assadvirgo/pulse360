Apple has announced that it will have "limited" iPhone Duo models available for in-store sales. This follows the quick sell-out of online preorders, with some delivery estimates extending into next year.

## Key facts
- Apple will have limited iPhone Duo models for sale in stores.
- Online preorders for the iPhone Duo quickly sold out.
- Some delivery estimates for the iPhone Duo extend into next year.
- The iPhone Duo is set to launch on Friday, October 23rd.
