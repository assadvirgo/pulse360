The moderators of the subreddit r/opensource have implemented new submission rules that prohibit projects involving artificial intelligence. The announcement specifies that any project that uses, is made by, or is intended for AI will not be accepted on the platform.

## Key facts
- r/opensource has banned projects that use AI.
- The new rules state that AI-related projects are not welcome.
- The announcement was made by the moderators on Saturday evening.
