Oura is introducing two new cardiovascular health tracking features to its smart rings. These features are designed to help users understand the impact of regular workouts and exercise on their long-term health.

## Key facts
- The new features are called Cardio Effort and automatic VO2 Max.
- The automatic VO2 Max feature was previously known as Cardio Capacity.
- The features aim to assist users in determining whether to increase or decrease their exercise intensity.
