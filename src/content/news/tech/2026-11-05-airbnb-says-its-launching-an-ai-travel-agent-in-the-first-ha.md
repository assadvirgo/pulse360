Airbnb announced plans to launch an AI travel agent in the first half of 2027. The AI agent is designed to assist travelers with trip planning, comparing options, managing bookings, and providing support during their stay.

## Key facts
- The AI agent will help users plan and book travel.
- Airbnb stated that the AI agent will assist with various tasks related to travel.
- The announcement was made in Airbnb's Q3 2026 earnings shareholder letter.
