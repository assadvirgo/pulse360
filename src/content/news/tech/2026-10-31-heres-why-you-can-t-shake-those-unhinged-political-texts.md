The Verge reports on the prevalence of political texts that often appear unhinged or overly aggressive in their messaging. Examples include requests for small donations and sensational announcements related to elections.

## Key facts
- Political texts often include urgent requests for donations, such as asking for "even just $1."
- Some messages contain sensational claims about political figures, such as "Mark Kelly just made a HUGE announcement."
- The texts may also include references to political affiliations, such as labeling supporters as "Marxists."
