Nvidia's board is reportedly preparing to dismiss CEO Jensen Huang following allegations of a second extramarital affair. This comes shortly after Huang admitted to a separate affair with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy.

## Key facts
- Nvidia's board is preparing to dismiss CEO Jensen Huang, according to The Verge.
- The decision follows a report from the New York Times regarding a second alleged extramarital affair.
- Huang had previously admitted to an affair with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy.
- MarketWatch reports that Nvidia's board of directors could meet as soon as today to determine Huang's fate.
