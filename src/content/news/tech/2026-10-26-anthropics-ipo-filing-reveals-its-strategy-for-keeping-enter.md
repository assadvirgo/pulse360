Anthropic's S-1 filing reveals its strategy for retaining enterprise customers. The company reports that more than half of its customers who spend over $100,000 are using both the Claude Platform and a first-party app.

## Key facts
- Anthropic's S-1 filing shows its strategy for keeping enterprise customers engaged.
- More than half of its $100,000-plus customers use both the Claude Platform and a first-party app.
- The strategy is compared to a cross-selling approach similar to that of Amazon.
