Baseus has introduced a new AI-assisted security camera designed to actively deter unwanted visitors. The Baseus Security PrimeSentinel U2 Pro Outdoor 3K Dual Camera features a motorized spotlight that tracks and follows moving targets.

## Key facts
- The camera is part of Baseus' lineup of AI-assisted security devices.
- It is designed to be proactive in discouraging unwanted visitors.
- The spotlight can shine directly on a moving target, even if they are running.
