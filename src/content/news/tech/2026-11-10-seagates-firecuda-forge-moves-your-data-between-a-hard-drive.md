Seagate has announced a new external storage solution named FireCuda Forge. This product combines the speed advantages of a solid-state drive (SSD) with the cost-effectiveness of a higher-capacity hard drive.

## Key facts
- FireCuda Forge is designed to enhance data performance by moving data between a hard drive and SSD.
- It is the first drive powered by Seagate's new Alkemi intelligent storage platform.
- The Alkemi platform learns how data is used and relocates files accordingly.
