Weeks before the end of the legislative session, some members of Congress are working to reauthorize a controversial warrantless wiretapping authority. This effort may involve attaching Section 702 of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act (FISA) to a must-pass military spending bill.

## Key facts
- Some members of Congress are scrambling to reauthorize a controversial warrantless wiretapping authority.
- The reauthorization effort may involve attaching it to the National Defense Authorization Act.
- Sen. Mike Rounds (R-SD) discussed the potential attachment with Politico.
