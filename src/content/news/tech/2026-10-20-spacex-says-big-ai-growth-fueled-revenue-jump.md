SpaceX reported revenues of $14.6 billion in its Q3 earnings, marking a growth of 161 percent year-over-year. The company attributed this increase to its Super Intelligence segment and accelerated growth in Connectivity.

## Key facts
- SpaceX's Q3 revenues reached $14.6 billion.
- The revenue growth was 161 percent year-over-year.
- The increase was attributed to the Super Intelligence segment and growth in Connectivity.
- The Space division reported a $7 million loss in income from operations.
- The Connectivity group posted income from operations of $2.4 million.
