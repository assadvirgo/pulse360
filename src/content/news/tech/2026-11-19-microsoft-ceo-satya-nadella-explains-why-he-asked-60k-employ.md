Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella explained the rationale behind offering buyouts to 60,000 employees, encouraging them to voluntarily quit instead of implementing another large-scale layoff. He attributed this decision to the impact of artificial intelligence.

## Key facts
- Satya Nadella offered buyouts to 60,000 employees in October.
- The approach was intended to avoid another massive layoff.
- Nadella's reasoning is linked to the influence of artificial intelligence.
