Magic: The Gathering is set to receive an animated series titled "Planeswalker," which will be available on Netflix in 2027. This announcement was made by Roy Graham, Head of Story at Magic: The Gathering, in a video released recently. The series was initially teased in 2019.

## Key facts
- The animated series is titled "Planeswalker."
- It will be available on Netflix in 2027.
- The announcement was made by Roy Graham in a video.
- The series was first teased back in 2019.
