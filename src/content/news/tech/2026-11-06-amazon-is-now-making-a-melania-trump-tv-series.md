Amazon is developing a two-part series titled "Melania: The Series," which will focus on Melania Trump. The series is set to debut on November 20th.

## Key facts
- The series follows a documentary movie about Melania Trump that premiered earlier this year.
- "Melania: The Series" will offer insights into Melania Trump's life.
- The show is being produced for Prime Video.
