The Sigma FP2 has been announced as a compact follow-up to the original fp, which was claimed to be the world's smallest and lightest full-frame mirrorless camera. The new model features several upgrades, including an improved sensor, an articulating LCD display, and longer battery life.

## Key facts
- The Sigma FP2 is a follow-up to the original fp, released seven years ago.
- The new model includes an improved sensor and an articulating LCD display.
- The battery life of the FP2 is longer compared to its predecessor.
- Sigma is only claiming the FP2 is the "world's smallest" full-frame camera, without the "lightest" designation.
