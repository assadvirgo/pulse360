Amazon is opposing the New York City Council's Delivery Protection Act by placing labels on shipments. The labels assert that the Council's actions could increase the cost of Amazon orders by up to $5.

## Key facts
- Amazon is actively fighting against the New York City Council regarding the Delivery Protection Act.
- The company is using shipment labels to communicate its stance on the issue.
- The labels claim that the Council's actions could add up to $5 to any Amazon order.
