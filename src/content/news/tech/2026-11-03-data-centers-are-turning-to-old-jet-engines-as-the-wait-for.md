Data center builders are increasingly using old jet engines as a power source due to prolonged delays in accessing the grid and obtaining new turbines. This solution is described as unusual, less efficient, and less environmentally friendly.

## Key facts
- Data center builders are facing years-long waits for access to the grid and new turbines.
- As a result, they are turning to once-discarded jet engines for power.
- The use of jet engines is characterized as less efficient and less environmentally friendly.
