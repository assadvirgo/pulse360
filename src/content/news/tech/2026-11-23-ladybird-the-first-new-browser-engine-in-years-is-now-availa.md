Ladybird, an independent web browser featuring a newly developed engine, is now available for testing. The browser was announced by developer Andreas Kling and is launching in alpha for macOS and Linux after over four years of development.

## Key facts
- Ladybird is an independent web browser with a built-from-scratch engine.
- The browser is now available for testing in alpha on macOS and Linux.
- The release marks the first new browser engine in years. 
- Development of Ladybird took more than four years.
