The US power sector may see a significant increase in carbon dioxide pollution due to the growing demand for electricity from data centers. A new analysis by the Sierra Club suggests that emissions could rise by 42 percent by 2030 compared to 2025 levels.

## Key facts
- The increase in emissions is attributed to the construction of more fossil fuel infrastructure to meet electricity demand from data centers.
- The projected rise in pollution could negate 15 years of greenhouse gas reductions.
