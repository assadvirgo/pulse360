United Launch Alliance (ULA) experienced its first mission failure in 20 years, resulting in the destruction of 40 Amazon Leo satellites intended for orbit. The Leo project is Amazon's competitor to SpaceX's Starlink, which has faced challenges in launching the necessary satellites.

## Key facts
- ULA's mission failure marks the first in its 20-year history.
- The failure involved the destruction of 40 Amazon Leo satellites.
- Amazon Leo is positioned as a competitor to SpaceX's Starlink.
- The Leo project has struggled to launch a sufficient number of satellites.
