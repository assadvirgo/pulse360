ChatGPT is introducing verified "Business Agents" that will represent brands and assist users with tasks such as comparison shopping. This feature is set to launch in beta for eligible e-commerce and online service businesses in the United States.

## Key facts
- ChatGPT will allow users to interact with verified "Business Agents."
- The feature is designed to help with comparison shopping.
- It is part of a suite of business-focused features being announced today.
- The launch will initially be available to eligible businesses in the US.
