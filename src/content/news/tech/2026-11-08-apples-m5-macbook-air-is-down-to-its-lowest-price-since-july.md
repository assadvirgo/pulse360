Apple's M5 MacBook Air is currently available for $1,099.99, reflecting a $200 discount. This price drop marks the second occurrence of this discount since the company's price increases over the summer.

## Key facts
- The 13-inch MacBook Air features Apple's M5 processor, 16GB of RAM, and 512GB of storage.
- The base model previously sold for around $950 to $1,000 shortly after its release.
- The current price is the lowest since July.
