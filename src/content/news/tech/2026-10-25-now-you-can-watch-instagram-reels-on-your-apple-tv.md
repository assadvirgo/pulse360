Instagram has launched its platform for Apple TV, allowing users to watch Instagram Reels on their television. This expansion into living rooms follows a testing phase that began in December of last year.

## Key facts
- Instagram has introduced its platform for Apple TV.
- Users can now watch Instagram Reels on their television.
- The testing phase for this feature started in December of the previous year.
