After two years of close relations with the White House, Big Tech is now attempting to build relationships with Democrats. There is uncertainty about whether this effort to improve their image will withstand pressures from states, local communities, and the markets, according to TechCrunch.

## Key facts
- Big Tech has been fostering relationships with the White House for two years.
- The sector is now trying to make friends with Democrats.
- There is an open question about the sustainability of this image makeover against external pressures.
