Major League Baseball is actively seeking to engage a new audience as the Division Series begins. The league's main Instagram and TikTok accounts recently shared a video promoting the teams competing for the World Series.

## Key facts
- MLB posted a video on its main Instagram and TikTok accounts on October 2nd.
- The video features clips of players from teams such as the Milwaukee Brewers, Los Angeles Dodgers, and Chicago White Sox.
- The content aims to hype up the teams still in contention for the World Series.
