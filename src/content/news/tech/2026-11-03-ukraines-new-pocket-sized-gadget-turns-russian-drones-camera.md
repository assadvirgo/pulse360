A Kyiv startup has developed a pocket-sized receiver that allows Ukrainian troops to access the video feeds of Russian drones. The device weighs 10 ounces, but advancements in Russian technology, including digital video and AI-guided drones that emit no signals, could limit its effectiveness.

## Key facts
- A startup in Kyiv created a 10-ounce receiver for Ukrainian troops.
- The receiver enables access to the video feeds of Russian drones.
- Russian advancements in digital video and AI-guided drones may reduce the device's effectiveness.
