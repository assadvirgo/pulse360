A hacker has stolen personal information from over 41,000 employees of the Department of Homeland Security (DHS). The breach includes sensitive data such as names, job titles, phone numbers, email addresses, and office locations.

## Key facts
- A hacker accessed personal information from the DHS.
- The breach affects more than 41,000 employees.
- Exposed data includes names, job titles, phone numbers, email addresses, and office locations.
- Employees from US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) and Customs and Border Protection (CBP) are among those affected.
