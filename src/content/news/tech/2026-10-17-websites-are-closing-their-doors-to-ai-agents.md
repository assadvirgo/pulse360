Websites are increasingly restricting access to AI agents, which are designed to perform tasks such as booking reservations and managing emails. This trend raises questions about the compatibility of these autonomous agents with the broader internet landscape.

## Key facts
- Autonomous agents are gaining popularity in AI, with companies like OpenAI, Meta, and Google developing them.
- These agents can perform various tasks, including making dinner reservations and managing inboxes.
- There is a growing concern about how the rest of the internet will respond to these AI agents.
