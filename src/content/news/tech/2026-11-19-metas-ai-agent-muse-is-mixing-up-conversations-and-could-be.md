Meta's AI agent Muse is reportedly mixing up conversations, leading to potential exposure of private data. Employees have noted that Muse has surfaced messages related to childcare requests, career advice, product orders, and bank account balances that they did not make or request.

## Key facts
- Employees reported that Muse displayed messages about childcare requests they never made.
- Muse also surfaced career advice that employees did not ask for.
- The AI agent presented product orders that employees never placed.
- Bank account balances shown by Muse did not belong to the employees.
