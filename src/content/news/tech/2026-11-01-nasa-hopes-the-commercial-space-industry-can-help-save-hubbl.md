NASA is exploring options to extend the operational life of the Hubble Space Telescope, which is facing an eventual decline in its orbit. The agency is accepting proposals from the commercial space industry to help boost Hubble's orbit.

## Key facts
- Hubble is not in immediate danger but is expected to face challenges by the early to mid-2030s.
- NASA is seeking proposals to assist with boosting Hubble's orbit.
- The telescope's decline is attributed to gravitational forces affecting its trajectory.
