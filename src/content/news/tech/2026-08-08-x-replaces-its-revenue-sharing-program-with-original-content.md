## X Introduces ‘Original Content Rewards’ to Replace Revenue-Sharing Program

In a significant shift for content creators on its platform, X, formerly known as Twitter, has announced the termination of its revenue-sharing program. This decision comes after a series of revisions and controversies that have characterized the program under the leadership of Elon Musk. The new initiative, named the Original Content Rewards program, is set to launch on September 8th, aiming to redefine how creators engage with their audience and monetize their content.

### Background of the Revenue-Sharing Program

The original revenue-sharing program was introduced as a way to incentivize creators to produce high-quality content and engage more actively with their followers. However, the initiative faced criticism for its complexity and the perceived inequity in how funds were distributed among creators. The program underwent multiple revisions, but dissatisfaction persisted, prompting the need for a more streamlined approach.

### Details of the Original Content Rewards Program

The Original Content Rewards program introduces a new eligibility framework for creators. To qualify, creators must have a minimum of 500 verified followers and achieve at least 500,000 impressions on their Home Timeline from verified users. This marks a significant shift from the previous model, which did not impose such stringent requirements.

The new program aims to focus on rewarding creators who can generate substantial engagement and visibility on the platform. By setting these thresholds, X appears to be targeting a more active and influential segment of its user base, potentially enhancing the overall quality of content shared on the platform.

### Implications for Content Creators

The introduction of the Original Content Rewards program could have several implications for content creators. On one hand, the new criteria may encourage creators to foster a more engaged following and produce content that resonates with a broader audience. On the other hand, the stringent requirements may exclude smaller creators who are still building their presence on the platform.

This change also reflects a broader trend in social media platforms, where companies are increasingly seeking to balance creator compensation with the need for sustainable business models. As platforms like X navigate the complexities of monetization, the emphasis on verified engagement may become a standard practice.

### Community Reactions

The announcement has elicited mixed reactions from the creator community. Some creators welcome the clarity and potential rewards associated with the new program, while others express concern over the barriers to entry that may limit opportunities for emerging talents. The effectiveness of this program in fostering a vibrant creator ecosystem remains to be seen, and its impact will likely depend on how well it is implemented and communicated to users.

### Conclusion

As X moves forward with the Original Content Rewards program, it is poised to reshape the landscape for content creators on its platform. By establishing new eligibility criteria and focusing on verified engagement, X aims to create a more sustainable and rewarding environment for creators. The success of this initiative will depend on its acceptance within the creator community and its ability to adapt to the evolving needs of users in the digital content space.
