Tidal will begin banning AI-generated music on December 9th. The platform plans to delete approximately 29 million AI-generated songs and will reject an estimated 80,000 to 85,000 tracks daily.

## Key facts
- Tidal is officially banning AI-generated music starting December 9th.
- The company will delete around 29 million AI-generated songs.
- Tidal will reject an estimated 80,000 to 85,000 AI-generated tracks per day.
- This decision follows Tidal's previous actions of labeling and demonetizing certain tracks.
