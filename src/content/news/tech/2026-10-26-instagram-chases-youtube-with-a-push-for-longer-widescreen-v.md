Instagram is making a significant move into longform horizontal video, a feature it is branding as "Features." This shift comes despite Instagram boss Adam Mosseri's previous reluctance to embrace longform video on the platform.

## Key facts
- Instagram is launching a new feature called "Features" for longform horizontal video.
- Adam Mosseri, Instagram's boss, has previously been against longform video on the platform.
- The announcement was made in a six-and-a-half-minute video featuring Mosseri and VP of product Tessa Lyons.
