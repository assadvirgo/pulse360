Whoop has launched a new social network called Whoop Humans, allowing members to share data from their wearables. The platform enables users to display their metrics, activities, and achievements, as well as post "Moments" with photos and captions.

## Key facts
- Whoop members can share data from their wearables on the Whoop Humans social network.
- Users can post Story-like "Moments" and add comments and reactions to other members' posts.
- The platform aims to enhance Whoop's identity as a personal health brand, with potential acquisitions hinted by the CEO.
