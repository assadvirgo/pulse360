Wyze has announced its new Window Bird Cam, which features a window-mounted camera designed to record birds from inside the home. This product builds on a previous security camera introduced by the company a year ago.

## Key facts
- The Window Bird Cam is designed to be mounted on the inside of a window.
- It allows users to monitor birds outside their home without complicated installation.
- The price of the new Window Bird Cam has not been specified in the excerpt.
