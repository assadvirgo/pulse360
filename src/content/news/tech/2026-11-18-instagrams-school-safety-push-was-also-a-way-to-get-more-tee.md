Meta's initiative to enhance Instagram's safety for schools was also aimed at increasing user engagement among teens. Internal documents revealed that the program, referred to as "Project Gryffindor," was designed to boost user acquisition, friending, and invites.

## Key facts
- Meta's initiative was part of efforts to increase user "acquisition," "friending," and "invites," according to a report from NPR.
- Internal documents related to child safety lawsuits against Meta reference a program called "Project Gryffindor."
- "Project Gryffindor" aims to "fuel organic and unattributed acquisition" of users.
