Instagram is testing long-form videos to attract creators from YouTube, but it has not established a plan for monetizing this content. Following an announcement by Instagram head Adam Mosseri, creators expressed their concerns about the absence of monetization opportunities compared to ad revenue sharing on other platforms.

## Key facts
- Instagram is testing long-form videos to attract creatives from YouTube.
- The platform has not determined how or if it will pay creators for long-form content.
- Creators have voiced complaints about the lack of monetization opportunities on Threads and Reddit.
