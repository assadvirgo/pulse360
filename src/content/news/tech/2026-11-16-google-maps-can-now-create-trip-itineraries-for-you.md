Google is launching a new AI trip-planning feature for Google Maps that enables the app to create custom itineraries. This update is part of the Gemini-powered Ask Maps chatbot and provides real-time details about each stop.

## Key facts
- The new feature allows users to plan both one-day outings and trips spanning several days.
- The update is rolling out to the Ask Maps chatbot within the Google Maps app.
