Apple is reportedly set to announce its first model of smart glasses at next year's WWDC in June. According to Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, these glasses will not feature augmented reality capabilities and are expected to begin shipping before the end of the year.

## Key facts
- Apple's smart glasses are described as among the worst-kept secrets in the tech world.
- The first model will be announced at WWDC in June 2024.
- The glasses will lack augmented reality features.
- Shipments of the glasses are expected to begin before the end of 2024.
