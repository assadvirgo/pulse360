Real Motors has introduced Project Ares, showcasing a new approach to motorcycle design enabled by electrification. The removal of traditional components like the engine and fuel tank allows designers greater creative freedom, resulting in innovative designs.

## Key facts
- Project Ares is a new motorcycle design by Real Motors.
- Electrification has allowed for significant changes in motorcycle design.
- The absence of an engine and fuel tank provides designers with more creative freedom.
