Microsoft Outlook is currently experiencing outages, affecting users globally. The issues began just before 1 PM ET on Thursday, with users unable to access their email through both web and app platforms.

## Key facts
- Users worldwide are reporting issues accessing Outlook email.
- The outage began just before 1 PM ET on Thursday.
- Microsoft acknowledged the issue via its Microsoft 365 Status account.
- The company stated that a portion of infrastructure is experiencing elevated load across multiple regions.
