The Wikimedia Foundation is advocating for legal protections for large language models (LLMs) that train on copyrighted work in ongoing AI copyright cases. The organization warns that a rigid ruling could either limit the tools available to Wikipedia contributors or pose risks to the open web.

## Key facts
- The Wikimedia Foundation is involved in landmark AI copyright cases.
- It supports legal protections for LLMs that train on copyrighted material.
- The Foundation opposes protections for chatbots that simply reproduce copyrighted work.
- The organization emphasizes the potential negative impacts of an overly simplistic ruling.
