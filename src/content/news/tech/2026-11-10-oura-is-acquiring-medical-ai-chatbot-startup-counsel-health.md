Oura has announced its agreement to acquire Counsel Health for $625 million. This acquisition follows the integration of Counsel Health's AI chatbot into Oura's app earlier this year.

## Key facts
- Oura announced its quarterly earnings and the acquisition of Counsel Health.
- The acquisition is valued at $625 million.
- Counsel Health describes itself as an "AI-native primary care company."
- The AI chatbot provides AI-generated responses and insights based on users' health data.
