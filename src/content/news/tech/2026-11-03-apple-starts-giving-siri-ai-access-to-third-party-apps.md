Apple has announced that Siri AI will now have the ability to perform actions across third-party apps. This feature is part of the iOS 27.3 update that is rolling out.

## Key facts
- Apple is allowing developers to integrate Siri AI's new features in all apps.
- Siri AI will be able to draw on a user's personal context.
- The voice assistant will process natural language prompts.
- Siri AI will utilize on-screen awareness.
