Signal is implementing restrictions on screenshot capabilities for its app on Android and iOS devices. This move aims to prevent AI agents, such as Gemini and Siri, from accessing and analyzing users' messages and other data.

## Key facts
- Signal is blocking the ability to take screenshots on Android and iOS devices.
- The restriction is intended to prevent AI agents from reading users' messages and data.
- Signal's president stated that AI capabilities built into operating systems undermine the app's ability to guarantee privacy.
