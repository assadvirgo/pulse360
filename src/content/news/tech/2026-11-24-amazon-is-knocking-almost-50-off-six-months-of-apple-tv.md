Amazon is offering a discount on Apple TV subscriptions for its US customers. The streaming service, which typically costs $14.99 per month, is available at a reduced rate for those with an Amazon account.

## Key facts
- Amazon is providing a discount of nearly $50 on six months of Apple TV.
- The standard monthly price for Apple TV is $14.99.
- The offer is available to customers with an Amazon account in the US.
