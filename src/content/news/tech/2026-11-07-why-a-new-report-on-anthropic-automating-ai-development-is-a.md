Anthropic is reportedly using AI to develop more advanced AI systems, with significant success. A new report from the AI safety research organization METR indicates that the company's models are improving at a rate approximately double that of the previous year.

## Key facts
- Anthropic is automating the development of AI.
- The company's AI models are improving roughly twice as quickly as they did a year ago.
- The findings are based on an 11-week investigation by METR.
