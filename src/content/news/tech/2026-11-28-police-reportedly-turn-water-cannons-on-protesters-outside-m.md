Police in Haryana, India, used water cannons on protesters from the opposition Congress party outside Meta's offices. The protests are part of ongoing demonstrations by the party's youth wing, which has accused Meta of yielding to pressure from the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) and censoring the Congress party, according to Reuters.

## Key facts
- Police in Haryana turned water cannons on Congress party protesters outside Meta's offices.
- The protests are organized by the youth wing of the Congress party.
- The Congress party has accused Meta of censoring its content under pressure from the BJP.
