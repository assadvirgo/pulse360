title: Protesters storm Meta offices in India

Members of the opposition Congress party protested outside and reportedly entered Meta's offices in Delhi. The protesters are demanding the restoration of accounts belonging to party leaders and have warned of additional protests in the future.

## Key facts
- Members of the Congress party protested outside Meta's offices in Delhi.
- Protesters appear to have entered the building, as shown in a video shared by CNN.
- The Congress party's youth wing is leading the protest.
- The party is demanding the restoration of accounts for its leaders.
- The Congress party has indicated that more protests may occur.
