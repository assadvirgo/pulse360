During a recent earnings call, Paramount Skydance CEO David Ellison expressed optimism about the potential impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on the film industry. He stated that AI could surpass the influence of CGI in filmmaking.

## Key facts
- David Ellison described AI as having the potential to be the "biggest creative unlock in modern history."
- He emphasized that it is "hard to imagine" AI not having a significant impact on the film industry.
- Ellison's comments were made during the company's latest earnings call.
