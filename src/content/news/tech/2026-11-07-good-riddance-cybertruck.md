Tesla will be "winding down" production of the Cybertruck next year, according to Elon Musk. This decision is made to make room for the Cybercab.

## Key facts
- Elon Musk announced the winding down of Cybertruck production via tweet.
- The decision is set to take place next year.
- The Cybercab will replace the Cybertruck in production.
