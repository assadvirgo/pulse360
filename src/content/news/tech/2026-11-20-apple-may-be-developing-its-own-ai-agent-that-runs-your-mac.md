Apple is reportedly developing its own AI agent designed to perform various tasks on Mac computers. The company is seeking engineers to lead the creation of this AI assistant, which aims to compete with existing products from other tech companies.

## Key facts
- Apple is building AI agents that could compete with products like Meta Muse, Instinct, Claude, and OpenAI Dots, according to TechCrunch.
- The AI agent is intended to perform tasks such as browsing the web, working across applications, writing code, and calling services, as reported by The Verge.
- A job listing indicates that Apple is looking for an engineer to lead the development of this AI assistant.
