A communications issue with the Federal Aviation Administration affected air traffic control across the country, causing disruptions. The problem was linked to a single bad router and was announced by a pilot thirty minutes outside Atlanta.

## Key facts
- A communications issue with the FAA was reported to be cascading across the country.
- The pilot indicated that the issue might affect gate access and connecting flights.
