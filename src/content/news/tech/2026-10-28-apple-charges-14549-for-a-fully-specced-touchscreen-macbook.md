Apple has announced a new MacBook Pro featuring an OLED display and touchscreen capabilities, which is priced at $14,549 when fully upgraded. This model is more expensive than the previously announced MacBook Pro models.

## Key facts
- The new MacBook Pro has an OLED display and touchscreen capabilities.
- The base model starts at $2,899 for the 14-inch version and $3,399 for the 16-inch version.
- Upgrading to every premium hardware option results in a price of $14,549.
