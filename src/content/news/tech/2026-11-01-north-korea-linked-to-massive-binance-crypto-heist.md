The FBI has linked North Korea to a significant crypto heist that affected Binance, resulting in a loss of $6.3 billion. The agency described the operation as "TraderTraitor" and noted that it has observed individuals associated with the hack converting stolen tokens to Ethereum and preparing to launder the funds.

## Key facts
- The FBI claims North Korea was behind the $6.3 billion crypto heist targeting Binance.
- The operation has been labeled "TraderTraitor" by the FBI.
- The FBI observed actors connected to the hack converting tokens to Ethereum.
