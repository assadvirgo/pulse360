Sony has canceled the live-service shooter Fairgames, which was announced in 2023. The decision is part of a broader cancellation of several planned live-service titles by the company.

## Key facts
- Fairgames was a heist game that was part of Sony's planned push for PlayStation live service games.
- The cancellation follows the termination of other planned live service titles, including those in the God of War and The Last of Us franchises.
- The information was reported by Bloomberg, according to The Verge.
