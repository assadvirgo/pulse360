Meta is updating its terms to remove language that suggested it could scrape content from news sites to train its AI. This change follows backlash regarding the company's practices.

## Key facts
- Meta is removing language from its commercial terms page that implied it could scrape content from news sites.
- The update comes after backlash against Meta's practices.
- The specific language removed indicated that Meta may use content from various domains to provide, develop, and improve its services.
