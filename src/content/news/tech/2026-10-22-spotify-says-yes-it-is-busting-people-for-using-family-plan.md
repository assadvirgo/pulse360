Spotify is implementing a new QR code verification method to identify Family plan users who do not reside at the same address. This was confirmed by co-CEO Alex Norström during an earnings call.

## Key facts
- Spotify is targeting Family plan users who share the plan but do not live together.
- A new QR code verification method requires users to scan the plan manager's phone.
- The information was shared by co-CEO Alex Norström during an earnings call, according to The Verge.
