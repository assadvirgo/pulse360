The White House has stated that President Donald Trump was significantly involved in the reinstatement of Jensen Huang as CEO of Nvidia. White House communications director Steven Cheung emphasized this involvement in a statement.

## Key facts
- The White House claims President Trump played a role in Jensen Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia CEO.
- White House communications director Steven Cheung described the decision as a "great decision by the NVIDIA board."
