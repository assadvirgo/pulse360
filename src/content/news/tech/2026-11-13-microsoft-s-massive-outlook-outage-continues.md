Microsoft Outlook and 365 are experiencing a gradual recovery following an almost two-day outage that affected millions of users globally. Some users are reporting restored access to their emails, although certain apps and features remain in a degraded state.

## Key facts
- Microsoft Outlook and 365 faced an almost two-day outage.
- The outage impacted millions of users worldwide.
- Some users have reported that access to their emails has been restored.
- Certain apps and features are still operating in a degraded state, according to reports from users.
