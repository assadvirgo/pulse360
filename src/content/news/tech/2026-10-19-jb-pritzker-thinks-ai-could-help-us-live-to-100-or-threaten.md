Illinois Governor JB Pritzker has shared his views on artificial intelligence, suggesting it has the potential to significantly extend human life or pose serious threats comparable to nuclear weapons. He discussed these ideas in a recent conversation, linking them to broader themes of technology and privacy. 

## Key facts
- JB Pritzker is the Governor of Illinois.
- He believes AI could help humans live to 100 years old.
- He also warns that AI could pose threats similar to those of nuclear weapons.
- His comments were made during a discussion about technology and privacy.
