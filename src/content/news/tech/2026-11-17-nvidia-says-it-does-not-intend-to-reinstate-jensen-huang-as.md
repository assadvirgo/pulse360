Nvidia's new board chair, Harvey Jones, announced during the company's quarterly earnings call that the board does not intend to reinstate former CEO Jensen Huang following his resignation. The board unanimously accepted Huang's resignation and will not address questions regarding the circumstances surrounding it.

## Key facts
- Harvey Jones stated that the Nvidia board unanimously accepted Jensen Huang's resignation.
- The board does not intend to reinstate Huang as CEO.
- The earnings call did not address the circumstances of Huang's resignation.
