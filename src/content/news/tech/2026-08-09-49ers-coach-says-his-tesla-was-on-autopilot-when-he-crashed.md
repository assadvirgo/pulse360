## 49ers Coach Kyle Shanahan Involved in Tesla Autopilot Crash

In a recent press conference, Kyle Shanahan, the head coach of the San Francisco 49ers, disclosed new details regarding a car accident he was involved in four weeks ago near downtown Palo Alto. Initially, Shanahan had acknowledged that the incident was his fault but refrained from elaborating on the circumstances surrounding the crash. 

### Autopilot Engagement During the Incident

During the press conference, Shanahan revealed that he had engaged his Tesla's Autopilot feature at the time of the accident. This admission has raised questions about the safety and reliability of autonomous driving technology, particularly as more drivers incorporate such features into their daily routines. Autopilot, which is designed to assist drivers with steering, acceleration, and braking, has been a topic of ongoing debate regarding its limitations and the responsibilities of drivers when using the system.
