Tesla unveiled its next luxury sports car, the Roadster 2, at an event in Texas. The vehicle features thrusters that enable it to become airborne.

## Key facts
- The Roadster 2 was unveiled at a Texas event.
- It is equipped with thrusters that allow it to fly.
- During a demonstration, the car leaped over a Cybercab, Cybertruck, Tesla Semi, Model Y, and Model 3.
