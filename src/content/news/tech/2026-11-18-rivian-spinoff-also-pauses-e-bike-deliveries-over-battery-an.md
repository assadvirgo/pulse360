Also, the mobility startup that spun out of Rivian last year has paused e-bike deliveries due to battery and software bugs. The company has faced challenges in delivering its first e-bikes to customers, which were already complicated by supply chain issues.

## Key facts
- The startup is a spinoff from Rivian.
- E-bike deliveries have been paused due to battery and software bugs.
- The company has been struggling with supply chain issues.
