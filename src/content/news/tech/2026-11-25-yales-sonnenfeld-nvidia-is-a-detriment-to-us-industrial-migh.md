Yale's Jeffrey Sonnenfeld has expressed concerns regarding Nvidia, suggesting that the company's recent developments could be detrimental to U.S. industrial strength. He cautioned that the return of CEO Jensen Huang, supported by the White House, might lead to significant personal liability for the company's directors due to increasing lawsuits.

## Key facts
- Jeffrey Sonnenfeld warns that Nvidia may be a detriment to U.S. industrial might.
- He raised concerns about the implications of Jensen Huang's return as CEO and board member.
- Sonnenfeld noted that Nvidia's actions could expose its directors to "historic" personal liability from lawsuits.
- He questioned whether the U.S. is celebrating a company that has supplied advanced chips to adversaries.
