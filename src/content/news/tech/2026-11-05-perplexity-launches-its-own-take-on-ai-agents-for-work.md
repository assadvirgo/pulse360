Perplexity has launched its own work-focused AI agents called "Teammates," aiming to compete with OpenAI and Anthropic. These agents are designed to perform multiple roles within a workspace and can utilize existing projects, skills, and memory from Perplexity tools.

## Key facts
- Perplexity's "Teammates" are always-on AI agents.
- The agents can take on multiple roles within a workspace.
- "Teammates" pull from existing projects, skills, and memory from Perplexity tools.
- They integrate with Perplexity Computer, an AI system that can deploy agents on devices.
