Utah has filed criminal charges against Kalshi and its executives for allegedly violating anti-gambling laws. The state has brought 14 charges against the prediction market, including counts against CEO Tarek Mansour and COO Luana Lopes Lara.

## Key facts
- Utah has filed criminal charges against Kalshi and its executives.
- The charges include 12 counts of illegally promoting online gambling.
- The information was reported by The Salt Lake Tribune and the Associated Press.
