Anthropic is expected to receive a high 'junk' rating from credit agencies following its IPO, according to a report by the Financial Times. This rating could complicate and increase the costs associated with raising funds for the company's significant expenditures.

## Key facts
- Anthropic is predicted to receive a high 'junk' rating from credit agencies.
- A high junk rating would make it more difficult for Anthropic to raise money.
- The company's funding needs are driven by its massive spending.
