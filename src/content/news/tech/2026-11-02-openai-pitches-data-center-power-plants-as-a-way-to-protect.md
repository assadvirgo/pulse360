OpenAI announced its "Grid Protectors Program," which will be piloted at a data center in Effingham County, Georgia. The program aims to provide up to 1,000 megawatts of power during extreme weather or emergencies.

## Key facts
- OpenAI's program is designed to free up power during emergencies.
- The pilot will take place at a data center in Effingham County, Georgia.
- The program could supply enough power for air conditioners in approximately 700,000 homes.
