Google is introducing Personal Intelligence to its Gemini AI for use in cars. This update will allow Gemini in Android Auto and vehicles with Google built-in to access user information from various Google apps.

## Key facts
- Gemini AI will utilize Personal Intelligence in cars.
- The update will be available in Android Auto and cars with Google built-in.
- Gemini can access information from Gmail, Calendar, Maps, Photos, YouTube, and Search.
- Users can inquire about their flight status while driving.
