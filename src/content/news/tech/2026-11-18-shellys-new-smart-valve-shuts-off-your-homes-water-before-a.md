Shelly has announced a new smart valve designed to prevent flooding by shutting off water flow when a leak is detected. This valve is intended to work in conjunction with a previously released leak sensor.

## Key facts
- The smart valve can be installed on a home's main water line.
- It automatically turns off the water flow when a leak is detected by the sensor.
- The leak sensor, released earlier this year, runs on battery for two years.
- The price of the leak sensor is $28.
