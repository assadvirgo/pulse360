Anti-oligarchy activists known as the Capytistas plan to dox Elon Musk and homes linked to hundreds of billionaires. The group claims to have over $1.2 million in backing and has begun its efforts with Musk and Tesla's board.

## Key facts
- The Capytistas are a secretive group.
- They claim to have more than $1.2 million in financial backing.
- The group's members argue that individuals who buy political power should face scrutiny.
