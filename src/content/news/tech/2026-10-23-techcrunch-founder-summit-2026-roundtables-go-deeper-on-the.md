The TechCrunch Founder Summit 2026 will take place at SoWa Power Station in Boston on November 4. The event will feature founders, investors, and operators engaging in a full day of discussions, practical sessions, live pitching, networking, and smaller roundtables for in-depth conversations.

## Key facts
- The summit is scheduled for November 4, 2026.
- It will be held at SoWa Power Station in Boston.
- The event will include candid conversations, practical sessions, and live pitching.
- Networking opportunities will be available for attendees.
- Smaller roundtables will focus on deeper discussions among participants.
