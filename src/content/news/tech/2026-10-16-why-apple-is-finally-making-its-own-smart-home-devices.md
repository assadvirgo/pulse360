Apple has launched its own smart home devices after over a decade of encouraging other companies to create products for its platform. The new Apple Home Collection includes cameras, thermostats, sensors, a smart door lock, and a video doorbell, developed in collaboration with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric.

## Key facts
- Apple has launched its own smart home devices.
- The Apple Home Collection includes various products such as cameras, thermostats, sensors, a smart door lock, and a video doorbell.
- The devices were co-developed with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric.
