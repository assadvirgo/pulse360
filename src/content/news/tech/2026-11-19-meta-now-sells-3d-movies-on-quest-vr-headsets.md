Meta has announced that it now offers over 150 3D movies for purchase or rental on its Quest 3 and 3S VR headsets. The movies can be accessed through Meta's TV app.

## Key facts
- More than 150 3D movies are available for purchase or rental.
- The movies can be watched in VR on Quest 3 and 3S headsets.
- Titles include Avatar, Spider-Man: Far From Home, Jurassic World Rebirth, and Wicked: For Good.
