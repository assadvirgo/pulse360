Laika has been developing its latest project, Wildwood, over the course of 20 years. The film involves notable contributions from cinematographer Caleb Deschanel, who has a long history in Hollywood.

## Key facts
- Laika has spent two decades building up to the film Wildwood.
- Caleb Deschanel has worked on various high-profile films, including The Passion of the Christ and The Patriot.
- Deschanel has also directed episodes of Twin Peaks.
- Wildwood marks Deschanel's first stop-motion project.
