SpaceX successfully executed the first 'chopstick' catch of its Starship vehicle using its "Mechazilla" launch and catch tower. This marks a significant achievement for the company, as it had previously aborted a planned catch of Starship Flight 15's booster.

## Key facts
- SpaceX captured Starship with its "Mechazilla" launch and catch tower for the first time.
- The company has previously performed 'chopstick' catches of its boosters.
- This is the first successful execution of the maneuver on the Starship vehicle itself.
