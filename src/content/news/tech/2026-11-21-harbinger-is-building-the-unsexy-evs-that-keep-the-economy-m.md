Harbinger, an electric truck maker, has raised $240 million in a Series D funding round. This follows a significant $300 million order from FedEx and a new defense contract with the US military.

## Key facts
- Harbinger is focused on building electric vehicles for commercial use.
- The company recently completed a $240 million Series D funding round.
- Harbinger secured a $300 million order from FedEx.
- The startup has also obtained a new defense contract with the US military.
