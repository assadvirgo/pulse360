Leakers have created a playable version of the canceled Portal prequel F-Stop. This version was pieced together using assets from a significant leak that occurred in August.

## Key facts
- The playable version of F-Stop was assembled by leakers.
- Most assets used in the version came from a 12-terabyte leak.
- The August leak did not include the game's source code or map data.
- The final pieces needed to complete the version were shared on the Valve Cut Content Discord this week.
