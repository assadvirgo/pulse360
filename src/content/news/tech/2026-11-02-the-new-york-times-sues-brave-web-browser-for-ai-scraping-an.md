The New York Times has filed a lawsuit against the company behind the Brave web browser, alleging unlawful scraping of content to train AI and enabling users to bypass paywalls. This lawsuit is part of a broader legal action involving several other publications.

## Key facts
- The New York Times and other publications are suing Brave for alleged unlawful scraping.
- The lawsuit claims that Brave allows users to bypass paywalls.
- The information was reported by Reuters and mentioned in a complaint filed by the New York Daily News and the Chicago Tribune.
