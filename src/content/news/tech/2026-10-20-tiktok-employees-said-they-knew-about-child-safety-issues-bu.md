TikTok employees reported that they had raised concerns about child safety issues on the platform, but executives did not take appropriate action. Court records obtained by NPR indicate that staffers were frustrated with the lack of response to warnings about risks, including minors bypassing age restrictions and appearing in inappropriate content.

## Key facts
- TikTok employees warned about risks to child safety on the platform.
- Concerns included minors bypassing age-restricted content.
- Employees expressed frustration over executives' inaction regarding these issues.
- The information comes from court records obtained by NPR.
