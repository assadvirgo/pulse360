Chess.com is expanding its offerings beyond chess with the launch of a new Classic Games platform. The platform will include ten additional games, enhancing the company's gaming portfolio.

## Key facts
- Chess.com is launching a new Classic Games platform.
- The platform will feature ten games: backgammon, checkers, go, hearts, mahjong, shogi, sudoku, solitaire, xiangqi, and a Scrabble alternative called word.
- This expansion follows the earlier launch of Gambit, a gambling-free poker site.
