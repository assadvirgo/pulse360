The indie game community is experiencing turmoil regarding the use of generative AI in game development. Some jurors from the Independent Games Festival (IGF) announced they would not vote for games that incorporate generative AI, while the developer of the indie game Big Walk revealed that AI was used "sparingly" in the final stages of its development.

## Key facts
- Some IGF jurors stated they would not vote for games using generative AI.
- The developer of Big Walk acknowledged using AI in the final stretch of the game's development.
- The use of AI was described as a means to help work through difficult aspects of development.
