title: Binance CEO says 90 percent of stolen assets have been ‘replenished’

After a significant theft of $6.3 billion from Binance customers, the company's CEO announced that 90 percent of the stolen assets have been replenished. Binance is directly covering the stolen funds and intends to address the remaining 10 percent as well.

## Key facts
- Hackers stole $6.3 billion from Binance customers.
- The CEO stated that 90 percent of the stolen funds have been replaced.
- Binance plans to cover the remaining 10 percent of the stolen assets directly.
