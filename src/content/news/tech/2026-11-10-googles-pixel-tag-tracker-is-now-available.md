Google's Pixel Tag tracker is now available for purchase. This product is designed to work with Google's tracking network and app, which was renamed the Find Hub last year.

## Key facts
- Google's Pixel Tag was first announced in August.
- The Pixel Tag is priced similarly to Apple's AirTag.
- It allows users to track lost items using their Android devices.
