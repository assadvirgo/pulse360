Andrey Zvyagintsev's film "Minotaur," set in the fictional town of Krasoborsk, explores the lives of a couple, Gleb and Galina, against the backdrop of geopolitical tensions following Vladimir Putin's announcement of a military draft in 2022. The narrative focuses on the couple's struggles to maintain their lives amid these circumstances, with Galina engaging in an affair.

## Key facts
- "Minotaur" is set in the fictional town of Krasoborsk.
- The film features a couple named Gleb and Galina.
- The story takes place in 2022, coinciding with the announcement of a military draft by Vladimir Putin.
- Galina's attempt to carry on with her life includes having an affair.
