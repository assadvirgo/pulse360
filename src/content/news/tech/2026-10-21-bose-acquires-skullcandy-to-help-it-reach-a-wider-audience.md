Bose has announced its acquisition of Skullcandy, a move aimed at expanding its audience reach. Despite being competitors in the consumer audio market, the two companies have collaborated on integrating Bose's audio technologies into Skullcandy products.

## Key facts
- Bose has acquired Skullcandy.
- The acquisition aims to help Bose reach a wider audience.
- Skullcandy has partnered with Bose to incorporate Bose's audio technologies.
- Technologies being integrated include head-tracking spatial audio and active noise cancellation (ANC).
- Skullcandy's Crusher 1080 ANC headphones will feature these technologies.
