A California ballot measure, Proposition 45, is raising concerns among environmental advocates who warn it may provide a loophole for data center developers to bypass public scrutiny. The measure aims to expedite environmental assessments for certain projects.

## Key facts
- Proposition 45 is designed to fast track "essential" infrastructure projects in California.
- The measure would shorten timelines for state-mandated environmental assessments.
- It would make it more difficult for residents to challenge projects in court.
- Environmental advocates are concerned about the implications for data center development.
