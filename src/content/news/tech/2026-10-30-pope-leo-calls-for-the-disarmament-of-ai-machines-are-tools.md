Pope Leo XIV has called for the disarmament of artificial intelligence (AI) systems, emphasizing that they are tools and not persons. In a recent Papal Address, he urged United Nations member states to take concrete steps toward banning lethal autonomous weapons.

## Key facts
- Pope Leo XIV stated that AI systems should be "disarmed."
- He emphasized that humans have the responsibility to guide, change, and reject AI applications when necessary.
- The Pope encouraged United Nations member states to act on banning lethal autonomous weapons during their upcoming meeting in Geneva.
