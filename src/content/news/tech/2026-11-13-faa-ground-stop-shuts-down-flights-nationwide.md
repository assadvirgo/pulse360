The FAA issued a nationwide ground stop affecting all U.S. airports due to a communication issue impacting Air Route Traffic Control Centers. This pause in takeoffs was described as necessary for safety reasons.

## Key facts
- The FAA announced a national ground stop on Friday.
- The ground stop was implemented for all U.S. airports.
- The reason cited was a communication issue affecting Air Route Traffic Control Centers.
