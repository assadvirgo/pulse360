Anduril and Qualcomm announced a partnership to produce laptops entirely in the United States, beginning with the BlueChip model in 2027. The companies aim to establish a fully domestic supply chain for the computer industry.

## Key facts
- Anduril and Qualcomm are collaborating to build laptops in the U.S.
- The first model, BlueChip, is set to launch in 2027.
- The partnership includes a long-term goal of creating a fully domestic supply chain for the computer industry.
