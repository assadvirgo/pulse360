Anthropic has announced the launch of Claude Duet, a new AI agent designed to compete with Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots. The agent is aimed at professionals and small business owners and is currently available as a "research preview."

## Key facts
- Claude Duet is a competitor to Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots.
- The AI agent is designed for professionals and small business owners.
- It is available as a standalone app on iOS and Android, as well as through Claude on the web.
