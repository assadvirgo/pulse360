Netflix has announced a live-action spinoff of Stranger Things titled Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure, which is expected to debut in 2028. This series will explore events set in the past.

## Key facts
- The spinoff is officially ordered to series at Netflix.
- The title of the spinoff is Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure.
- The show is set to debut in 2028.
- The announcement coincides with "Stranger Things" Day, which commemorates the day Will Byers disappeared in the original series.
