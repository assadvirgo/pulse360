Senate commerce committee Chair Ted Cruz held a meeting to confirm President Donald Trump's nominee to the Federal Communications Commission. The meeting featured a significant absence of Democratic members, who did not participate in the vote.

## Key facts
- The meeting was attended by all 14 Republican members of the Senate commerce committee.
- About a dozen chairs were empty on the Democratic side of the panel.
- The meeting was held to confirm a nominee for the Federal Communications Commission.
