The FAA's nationwide ground stop on Friday was linked to routine maintenance work involving the replacement of outdated copper wire with fiber optic lines at a remote facility, according to Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy.

## Key facts
- The FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop on Friday.
- The ground stop was related to replacing old copper wire with fiber optic lines.
- Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy described the work as routine maintenance.
- The maintenance was not part of a major modernization effort.
