The upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI is set to feature a different kind of ending compared to previous titles in the series. Traditionally, Grand Theft Auto games leave the world open for exploration after the main story concludes, but this installment is expected to diverge from that norm.

## Key facts
- Grand Theft Auto VI will have a different kind of ending than previous games in the series.
- Most Grand Theft Auto games allow players to explore the world after the credits roll.
- The main story has typically been just one part of the overall experience in the series.
