Suno is facing lawsuits from rights holders of Elvis Presley and the publisher of some of Eminem's music. The lawsuits allege that Suno illegally generates music and lyrics that imitate these artists.

## Key facts
- Rights holders of Elvis Presley's music and Eminem's publisher are suing Suno.
- The lawsuits claim that Suno's AI technology illegally generates music and lyrics that mimic the artists.
- The lawyers in the Presley lawsuit assert that Suno has violated Tennessee law.
