All seven co-founders of Anthropic plan to sell the same number of shares in the company's IPO, which would result in each of them receiving approximately $250 million in cash at the midpoint of the price range. Despite this cash-out, the co-founders will maintain control of the company.

## Key facts
- Each of the seven co-founders of Anthropic will sell the same number of shares in the IPO.
- Each co-founder is expected to receive about $250 million in cash.
- The cash-out will occur at the midpoint of the IPO price range.
- The group of co-founders will remain in control of the company after the IPO.
