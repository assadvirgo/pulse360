Peak Design has announced an expansion of its collection of wireless charging accessories. The new devices will focus on combining premium materials with innovative functionality.

## Key facts
- Peak Design is known for its backpacks and travel bags.
- The company is introducing new chargers and charging accessories.
- The expansion aims to challenge the notion that everyday electronics should be cheap and disposable, according to The Verge.
