Allstate has been experiencing an outage since Monday, affecting its website, mobile apps, and roadside assistance service. This incident follows a series of outages that have impacted other companies, including Microsoft and JPMorgan Chase.

## Key facts
- Allstate's outage began on Monday, November 23rd.
- The outage has affected the company's website, mobile apps, and roadside assistance service.
- Access to accounts through the app and site appeared to still be impacted as of Friday afternoon.
- Other companies, including Microsoft and JPMorgan Chase, have also experienced outages recently, according to The Verge.
