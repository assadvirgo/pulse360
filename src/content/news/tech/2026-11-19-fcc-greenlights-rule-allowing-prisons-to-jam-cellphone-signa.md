title: FCC greenlights rule allowing prisons to jam cellphone signals

The Federal Communications Commission (FCC) has voted unanimously to permit state and local prisons to jam cellphone signals. This decision aims to address the issue of contraband cellphones being used to facilitate criminal activities from within prison facilities.

## Key facts
- The FCC's announcement was made on Thursday.
- The rule is intended to combat the "influx of contraband cellphones."
- Jammers are typically prohibited under Section 333 of the Communications Act of 1934.
