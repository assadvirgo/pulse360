Mister Organ is a documentary directed by David Farrier, an Auckland journalist. The film explores the unsettling life of Michael Organ and his campaign of terror against those who come into contact with him.

## Key facts
- Mister Organ is directed by David Farrier.
- The documentary focuses on Michael Organ and his campaign of terror.
- It is described as unsettling, though not reaching the societal horror of other documentaries like The Act of Killing.
