Meta has indicated that it may delay the implementation of its new business terms beyond November 20th due to backlash from publishers regarding concerns about AI training. The company's communications head stated that Meta does not intend to make policy changes without consulting industry groups and leaders.

## Key facts
- Meta may push the implementation of its new business terms past November 20th.
- The delay is in response to backlash from publishers over AI training concerns.
- Meta's communications head emphasized the importance of consulting with industry groups and leaders before making policy changes.
