The Joshua Kushner-led Thrive Holdings is acquiring ADT in an all-cash deal valued at $7.6 billion. The deal is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027 and will take ADT private.

## Key facts
- Thrive Holdings is acquiring ADT for $7.6 billion.
- The deal is an all-cash transaction.
- The acquisition is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027.
- The deal will take ADT private.
