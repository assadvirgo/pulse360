Google is offering discounts on its Pixel 11 phones, with savings of up to 27 percent available until November 8th. This includes the standard Pixel 11 and the more expensive Pixel 11 Pro, which is noted for its superior photography capabilities. The Pixel 11 Pro XL, featuring a longer-lasting battery, is also significantly reduced in price.

## Key facts
- Discounts on Google’s Pixel 11 phones are available until November 8th.
- The Pixel 11 and Pixel 11 Pro are included in the sale.
- The Pixel 11 Pro is recognized for its better photography features.
- The Pixel 11 Pro XL has a longer-lasting battery and is also discounted.
