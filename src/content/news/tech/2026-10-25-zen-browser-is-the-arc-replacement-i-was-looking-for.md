The Arc browser has been replaced by Zen Browser, which is being recognized as a suitable alternative. The Arc browser was noted for its innovative design approach before its acquisition by Atlassian, which has since shifted focus to a new AI-focused browser called Dia.

## Key facts
- The Arc browser was known for its unique approach to browser design.
- Atlassian acquired the company behind Arc last year.
- Following the acquisition, Atlassian shifted its focus to developing Dia, an AI-focused browser.
