title: Big Nuclear isn’t worried about the Democrats stopping data centers

Constellation Energy, the largest operator of nuclear power plants in the U.S., is not concerned about potential Democratic efforts to slow down data center development. During the company's third-quarter earnings call, CEO Joe Dominguez expressed confidence regarding the situation. 

## Key facts
- Constellation Energy is America's largest operator of nuclear power plants.
- The company does not share Wall Street's concerns about Democrats impacting data center development.
- CEO Joe Dominguez addressed these concerns during the third-quarter earnings call.
