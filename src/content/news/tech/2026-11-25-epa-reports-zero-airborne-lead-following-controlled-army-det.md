The US Army conducted a controlled detonation of over 9,700 pounds of lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown, Iowa. The detonation was deemed necessary to prevent a possible spontaneous explosion of a stockpile of material used in igniters and blasting caps.

## Key facts
- The controlled detonation took place at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant.
- The amount of lead azide detonated was over 9,700 pounds.
- The US Army stated the detonation was necessary to prevent a possible spontaneous explosion.
- Videos of the detonation showed plumes of smoke.
