Apple has announced that the first smart home devices from its new Apple Home Collection are now available for preorder. These devices, co-developed with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric, are expected to be delivered in mid-December.

## Key facts
- The smart home devices are available for preorder through Apple's online store.
- The Apple Home Collection was announced a month ago.
- The collection represents Apple's push into the smart home market.
