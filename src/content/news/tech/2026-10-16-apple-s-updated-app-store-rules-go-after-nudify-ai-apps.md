Apple has updated its App Store rules to more explicitly ban "nudify" apps that create intimate images of individuals without their consent using AI tools. This change follows criticism aimed at Apple regarding the use of such apps to produce nonconsensual, sexualized deepfakes.

## Key facts
- Apple's updated rules specifically target "nudify" apps that generate intimate images without consent.
- The changes come after criticism related to the use of Grok for creating nonconsensual deepfakes, including those involving minors.
- Apple did not remove X or Grok from the App Store despite the backlash.
