Etsy is implementing stricter rules regarding the use of AI in product listings to address concerns about low-quality AI-generated content on its platform. The company noted that many buyers are unable to distinguish between items that involve AI-generated content and those that do not.

## Key facts
- Etsy is introducing stricter rules for AI usage in listings.
- The move aims to reduce the volume of low-quality AI-generated content on the platform.
- Many active buyers report difficulty in identifying AI-generated items.
- Sellers have expressed concerns about their work being displayed alongside AI-generated content.
