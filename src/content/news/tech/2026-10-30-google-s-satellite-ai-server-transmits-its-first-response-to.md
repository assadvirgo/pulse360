Google's AI chip-equipped satellite has successfully transmitted its first response from space. The satellite's Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) were booted up, enabling the on-board Gemma 4 model to generate a haiku about its experience in space.

## Key facts
- Google's satellite has generated its first response from space.
- The satellite's Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) were successfully booted up.
- The on-board Gemma 4 model created a haiku about being in space.
