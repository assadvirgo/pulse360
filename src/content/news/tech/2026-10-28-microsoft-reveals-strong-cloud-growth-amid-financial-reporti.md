Microsoft reported its financial results for the first quarter of its 2027 fiscal year, showing significant growth in revenue and net income.

## Key facts
- Microsoft generated $92.3 billion in revenue during Q1.
- The company's net income for the quarter was $33.6 billion.
- Revenue increased by 19 percent compared to the previous year.
- Net income rose by 32 percent year-over-year.
