The US government has grounded all flights across the United States due to extraordinary circumstances. This decision follows communication issues affecting multiple Air Route Traffic Control Centers.

## Key facts
- The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) issued a ground stop for all flights.
- The ground stop was prompted by communication issues impacting air traffic control.
- Multiple Air Route Traffic Control Centers are involved in the situation.
