Disney has agreed to hire the majority of Character.AI's workforce, with most employees accepting job offers. However, the agreement is a stalking horse bid, meaning another company could potentially make a higher offer.

## Key facts
- Disney will hire the majority of Character.AI's workforce.
- The "vast majority" of Character.AI employees who were offered jobs at Disney accepted them.
- The agreement is classified as a stalking horse bid.
