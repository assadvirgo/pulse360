Rivian is preparing to test its point-to-point hands-free driving feature later this year. During an earnings call, CEO RJ Scaringe focused more on the company's autonomous vehicle ambitions than on the production of the R2 SUV.

## Key facts
- Rivian will roll out its point-to-point hands-free driving feature later this year.
- CEO RJ Scaringe emphasized the company's autonomous vehicle ambitions during an earnings call.
- The production ramp of the R2 SUV received less attention during the call.
