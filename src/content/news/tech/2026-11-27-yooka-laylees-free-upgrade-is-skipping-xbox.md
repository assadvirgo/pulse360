Playtronic Games will release a free upgrade for Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition on December 15th for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and PC. However, the developer has stated that it cannot commit to releasing the upgrade for Xbox platforms in the foreseeable future, according to The Verge. 

## Key facts
- The free upgrade will be available on PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and PC.
- The release date for the upgrade is December 15th.
- Playtronic Games has not committed to Xbox platforms for the upgrade.
