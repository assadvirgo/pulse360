Binance reported that the $6.3 billion theft was executed using a combination of hacking techniques, including fake job interviews and an internal AI tool. The company conducted a postmortem investigation revealing that the process began in June when a developer downloaded a tampered installer linked to a malicious Google ad.

## Key facts
- The theft is described as the largest crypto theft to date.
- Binance's investigation highlighted the role of human error in the heist.
- The hacking techniques included fake job interviews and social media profiles.
