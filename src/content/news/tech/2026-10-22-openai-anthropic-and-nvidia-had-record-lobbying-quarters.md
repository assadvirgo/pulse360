OpenAI, Anthropic, and Nvidia reported their highest lobbying expenditures in the third quarter of the year, according to federal disclosures. Nvidia led the group with $2.2 million in spending, followed closely by Anthropic at $2.1 million and OpenAI at $1.5 million.

## Key facts
- Anthropic, OpenAI, and Nvidia had their highest-spending lobbying quarter between July and September.
- Nvidia spent $2.2 million on lobbying.
- Anthropic spent $2.1 million on lobbying.
- OpenAI spent $1.5 million on lobbying.
