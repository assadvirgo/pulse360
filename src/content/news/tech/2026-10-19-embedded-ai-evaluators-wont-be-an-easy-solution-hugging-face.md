AI labs are focusing on model benchmarking to evaluate AI products, but the methods used for these evaluations are complex and still developing, according to Irene Solaiman, Hugging Face's chief policy officer, during a discussion at TechCrunch. 

## Key facts
- AI labs have built their reputations on model benchmarking.
- Model benchmarking is intended to provide neutral and accurate evaluations of AI products.
- The methods behind these evaluations are complicated and still maturing, according to Hugging Face's policy chief.
