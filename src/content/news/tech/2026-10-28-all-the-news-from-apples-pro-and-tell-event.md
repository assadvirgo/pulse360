Apple's "Pro and tell" event is scheduled to begin at 1 PM ET / 10 AM PT on October 28th. This event follows the recent debut of the HomeView, Apple's first smart display, and the announcement of the iPhone Duo, its first foldable device.

## Key facts
- The "Pro and tell" event will take place on October 28th.
- The event starts at 1 PM ET / 10 AM PT.
- Apple recently launched the HomeView, its first smart display.
- Apple announced the iPhone Duo, its first foldable device, a little over a month ago.
