People who received the new Apple TV 4K experienced issues with the YouTube app upon opening it. The app displayed a black screen along with a QR code and an error message indicating that "YouTube is not yet available on this device."

## Key facts
- Users reported strange error messages when opening the YouTube app on the new Apple TV 4K.
- The app initially showed a black screen with a QR code.
- An error message stated that "YouTube is not yet available on this device."
