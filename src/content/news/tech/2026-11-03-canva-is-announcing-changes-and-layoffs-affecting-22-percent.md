Canva is implementing significant changes that will affect 22 percent of its workforce, resulting in layoffs of 1,271 employees. Co-founder and CEO Melanie Perkins announced these changes, which may also involve role adjustments and relocations to centralized hubs for some staff.

## Key facts
- Canva is laying off 1,271 employees, which is 22 percent of its staff.
- The changes were announced by co-founder and CEO Melanie Perkins.
- Some employees may experience changes to their roles or be asked to relocate.
