SpaceX has announced plans to become a major wireless carrier by acquiring a significant portion of low-band spectrum to expand its Starlink network. This move is seen as an ambitious and expensive gamble to compete with established telecom companies like AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon.

## Key facts
- SpaceX has agreed to purchase a large amount of low-band spectrum.
- The spectrum acquisition is intended to expand the Starlink network.
- The plan aims to position SpaceX as a major player in the wireless carrier market.
