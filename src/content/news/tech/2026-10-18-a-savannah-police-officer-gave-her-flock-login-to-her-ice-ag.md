Former Savannah police officer Amber Harpe was fired after an investigation revealed she shared her Flock login information with her husband, who is an ICE agent. The Savannah Police Department stated that it does not share its Flock data with ICE and that this action violated the department's security protocols.

## Key facts
- Amber Harpe was fired from the Savannah Police Department in August.
- She shared her Flock login information with her husband, Tyler Harpe, an ICE agent.
- The Savannah Police Department does not share Flock data with ICE.
- Sharing access to the system was found to violate the department's security protocols.
