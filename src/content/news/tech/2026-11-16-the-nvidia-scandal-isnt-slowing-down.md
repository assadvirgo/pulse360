Nvidia continues to be a central figure in the AI technology landscape, with CEO Jensen Huang holding significant influence over its advancement. The company's chips are described as essential for the development of advanced AI in the US, but demand is outpacing supply.

## Key facts
- Jensen Huang is the CEO, cofounder, and president of Nvidia.
- Nvidia's chips are considered the foundation for advanced AI technology in the US.
- Demand for Nvidia's chips has consistently exceeded supply.
