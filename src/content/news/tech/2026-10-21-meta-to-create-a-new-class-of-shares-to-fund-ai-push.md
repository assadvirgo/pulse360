Meta is set to issue a new class of shares to support its artificial intelligence initiatives. This decision was announced in the company's third-quarter earnings report, which was released on Wednesday.

## Key facts
- Meta will issue non-voting stock to fund its AI initiatives.
- The new shares will also be used for potential acquisitions and employee compensation.
- Trading for the new class of shares is expected to begin in 2027.
