OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Anthropic have announced plans to adopt a new standard that allows publishers to opt out of being scraped by AI. This initiative aims to enable publishers to apply their AI training preferences across multiple AI companies.

## Key facts
- OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Anthropic will adopt a new standard for publishers to opt out of AI scraping.
- The new standard allows publishers to set their AI training preferences across multiple AI companies.
- The initiative was reported by Search Engine Roundtable.
