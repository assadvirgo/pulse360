Sony's latest high-end noise-canceling earbuds, the WF-1000XM6, are currently available at a significant discount. B&H Photo is offering them for $238, which is $92 off the regular price.

## Key facts
- The WF-1000XM6 earbuds are cheaper than their last-generation counterpart, the WF-1000XM5.
- B&H Photo is selling the WF-1000XM6 in black for an all-time low price.
- Other retailers are not currently offering a discount on the XM6.
