Adam Mosseri, head of Instagram, discussed the growing negativity surrounding artificial intelligence (AI) during an interview. He noted that this sentiment is not limited to young people and creatives, indicating a broader discontent with AI.

## Key facts
- Adam Mosseri acknowledged that many people dislike AI.
- The negativity towards AI is not confined to college students or creatives.
- Mosseri made these comments during an interview on the Colin and Samir podcast.
