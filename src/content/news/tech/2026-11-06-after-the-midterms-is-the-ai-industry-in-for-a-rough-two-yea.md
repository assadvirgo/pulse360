Democrats' victories in the recent U.S. midterm elections may lead to significant changes in the tech industry, particularly affecting data center development. Voters approved 41 data center-related ballot measures, with the majority opting for more restrictions on such developments.

## Key facts
- Democrats won historic victories in the U.S. midterm elections.
- Voters approved 41 data center-related ballot measures.
- In nearly all measures, the majority voted to impose more restrictions on data center development.
