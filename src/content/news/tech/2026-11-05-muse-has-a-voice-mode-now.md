Meta has introduced a voice mode for its Muse AI agent, allowing users to engage in real-time voice conversations. CEO Mark Zuckerberg announced the feature, which is now available on the iOS app.

## Key facts
- Users can have voice conversations with their Muse AI agent.
- The feature was announced by CEO Mark Zuckerberg.
- It has been rolled out to users on the iOS app.
