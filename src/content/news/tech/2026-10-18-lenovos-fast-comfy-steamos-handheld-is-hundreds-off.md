Lenovo has introduced the Legion Go S, a handheld device running SteamOS, which is positioned as an alternative to the Steam Deck OLED. The Legion Go S features a larger eight-inch IPS screen with 1080p resolution and a refresh rate of up to 120Hz, along with double the RAM and storage compared to the Steam Deck OLED.

## Key facts
- The Legion Go S is an alternative to the $789 Steam Deck OLED.
- It runs identical software to the Steam Deck OLED.
- The device includes two-stage triggers.
- It features an eight-inch IPS screen with 1080p resolution and up to 120Hz refresh rate.
- The Legion Go S has double the RAM and storage compared to the Steam Deck OLED.
