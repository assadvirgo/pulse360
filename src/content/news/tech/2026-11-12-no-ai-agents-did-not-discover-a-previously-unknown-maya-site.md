A Google researcher claimed to have discovered a potentially unmapped Maya site in Mexico using AI agents. However, it was later revealed that the site had already been discovered and published in an academic journal last year.

## Key facts
- The claim was made by Google researcher Pietro Miotti.
- The site in question had already been documented in an academic journal.
- The incident received criticism from archeologists online.
