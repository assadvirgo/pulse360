Someone at the White House is reportedly running software that allows outsiders to rent access to the presidential network for a low cost. This information comes from findings by security firms Layer3 Intel and Synthient, which identified residential proxy activity on the White House network.

## Key facts
- The software enables outsiders to rent access to the presidential network for "a few dollars."
- The findings were reported by Krebs on Security and The Register.
- The security firms involved in the discovery are Layer3 Intel and Synthient.
