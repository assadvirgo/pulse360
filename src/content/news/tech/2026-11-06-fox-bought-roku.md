Fox has officially closed its acquisition of Roku for $22 billion. The companies announced the completion of the deal, which allows Fox to control a major streaming platform that reaches over 100 million households in the US.

## Key facts
- Fox's acquisition of Roku was officially announced on Friday.
- The deal is valued at $22 billion.
- Roku reaches more than 100 million households in the US.
- The acquisition allows Fox to bring Fox Sports, news content, and local stations to the platform.
