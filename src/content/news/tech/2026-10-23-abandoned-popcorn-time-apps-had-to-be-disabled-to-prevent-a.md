Development of the Popcorn Time app has been abandoned for years, but it remains installed on millions of devices. These devices are at risk of being exploited, potentially forming a "zombie army" for malicious activities.

## Key facts
- Popcorn Time was known for streaming torrents of movies and TV shows.
- The app has not been actively developed for several years.
- Millions of devices still have the software installed.
