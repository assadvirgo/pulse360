Microsoft is closing down The Garage, a set of maker spaces where employees engaged in experimental projects and collaborated on new apps and hardware during hackathon events.

## Key facts
- Microsoft is winding down The Garage program as a centralized function.
- The Garage was popular among employees for working on experimental projects.
- The spaces were used during the company's annual hackathon events.
