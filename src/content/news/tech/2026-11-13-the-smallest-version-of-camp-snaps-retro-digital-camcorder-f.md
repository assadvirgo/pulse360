Camp Snap has launched the CS-8 Micro, a miniaturized version of its screen-free CS-8 video camera. This new model is even smaller than the original, designed to fit on a keychain and features a grip that is easier for kids to hold.

## Key facts
- The CS-8 Micro is half the size of the original CS-8 video camera.
- The new camera is designed to be screen-free.
- The grip of the CS-8 Micro is smaller, making it easier for children to hold.
