A company called Arctic Fox Technologies has announced a new multifunction mouse called the Super Mouse. This device features a compact touchscreen located beneath the user's hand, replacing traditional customizable shortcut buttons.

## Key facts
- The Super Mouse is designed to replace other peripherals, such as a trackpad.
- It has a boxy design that may not prioritize ergonomics.
- The mouse is part of a trend towards multifunctional devices in tech.
