Automattic has initiated layoffs affecting employees across various teams, including WordPress.com and its enterprise business. This decision follows a recent board shakeup and several executive departures, with indications that more cuts may occur as the company aims to enhance its financial situation.

## Key facts
- Automattic has begun laying off employees across multiple teams.
- The layoffs include staff from WordPress.com and the enterprise business.
- The layoffs follow a board shakeup and a series of executive departures.
- Sources suggest that additional cuts could occur as the company seeks to improve its finances.
