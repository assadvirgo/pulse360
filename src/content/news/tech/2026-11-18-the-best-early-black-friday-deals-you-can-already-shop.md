The early Black Friday shopping season has begun, with retailers offering discounts on various tech products ahead of the official holiday on November 27th. While discounts are available, rising prices mean that these deals may not represent historically low prices.

## Key facts
- Retailers are starting to drop early deals on tech products.
- Black Friday is officially on November 27th.
- Discounts are being offered on gadgets priced under $100, according to Wired.
