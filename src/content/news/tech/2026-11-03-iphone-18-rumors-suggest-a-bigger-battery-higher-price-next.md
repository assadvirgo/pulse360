Apple is rumored to be planning a price increase for the base iPhone 18, along with upgrades including a larger battery. This follows a previous price hike for iPhones earlier this year, according to The Verge.

## Key facts
- Apple is expected to launch the base iPhone 18 with a higher price.
- The rumored upgrades for the iPhone 18 include a larger battery.
- The information comes from a Weibo post by leaker Digital Chat Station, as reported by 9to5Mac.
