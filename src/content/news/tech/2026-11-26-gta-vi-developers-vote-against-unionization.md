title: GTA VI developers vote against unionization

Workers at Grand Theft Auto VI developer Rockstar Games have voted against unionization, with only 25 percent of votes in favor of a formal recognition agreement. The Independent Workers Union of Great Britain (IWGB) stated that Rockstar workers in the union will continue organizing despite the lack of formal recognition.

## Key facts
- Workers at Rockstar Games voted against unionization.
- Only 25 percent of votes were in favor of a formal recognition agreement.
- The Independent Workers Union of Great Britain (IWGB) will continue organizing among Rockstar workers.
