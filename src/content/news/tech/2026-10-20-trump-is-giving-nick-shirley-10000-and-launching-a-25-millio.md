Donald Trump and Vice President JD Vance announced a $25 million bounty program for reporting fraud, alongside a $10,000 payment to right-wing YouTuber Nick Shirley. The announcement follows Shirley's viral video from December 2025, which alleged fraud at Minnesota daycares.

- Donald Trump and Vice President JD Vance announced a $10,000 payment to Nick Shirley.
- A $25 million bounty program for reporting fraud was also launched.
