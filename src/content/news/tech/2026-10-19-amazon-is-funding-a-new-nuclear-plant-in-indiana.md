title: Amazon is funding a new nuclear plant in Indiana

Amazon has signed an agreement with X-energy and American Electric Power (AEP) to fund the construction of a new nuclear energy facility in Indiana. The proposed plant is intended to replace the defunct coal-fueled Breed Generating Station in Fairbanks, Sullivan County.

## Key facts
- Amazon is collaborating with X-energy and AEP for the project.
- The new nuclear plant aims to provide up to 960 megawatts of carbon-free energy.
- The facility will replace the now defunct Breed Generating Station.
