Alex Ridha, known as Boys Noize, has released a new record titled 7LLVSION. The album's creation was not directly influenced by his collaboration with Nine Inch Nails, although he acknowledges learning from the production team of Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross.

## Key facts
- Boys Noize's new record is titled 7LLVSION.
- The collaboration with Nine Inch Nails did not influence the album.
- Most songs on 7LLVSION had been in development prior to the collaboration.
