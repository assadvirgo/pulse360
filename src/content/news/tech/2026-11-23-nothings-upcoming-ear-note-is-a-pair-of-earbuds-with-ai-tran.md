Nothing has announced its upcoming Ear Note, a pair of earbuds designed to record and transcribe conversations. The device includes a microphone-equipped charging case and a connected app for managing recordings, transcriptions, and AI-generated summaries.

## Key facts
- The Ear Note is a set of earbuds with a microphone-equipped charging case.
- It can record and transcribe conversations.
- The connected app allows users to manage recordings, transcriptions, and AI-generated summaries.
- The device is similar to features found in Apple's new Watch and Amazon's Bee wearable.
- Nothing CEO Carl Pei demonstrated the new device.
