OpenAI announced that it will no longer use non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) with public officials for its data center projects. This decision follows recent midterm elections where voters expressed strong opposition to data center expansion. OpenAI is also making new commitments to the communities involved in these projects.

## Key facts
- OpenAI will not use NDAs with public officials for data center projects.
- The announcement follows midterm elections where voters opposed data center expansion.
- Many data center projects have faced scrutiny for using NDAs to conceal discussions.
