title: Data centers could erase decades of air quality gains
---

A surge in new data centers in Texas may significantly impact air quality, potentially reversing decades of improvements. These facilities are consuming large amounts of electricity, raising concerns about the sources of that energy.

## Key facts
- New and proposed data centers in Texas could increase air pollution to levels not seen since the 2000s.
- Texas is becoming a central location for the infrastructure supporting generative AI.
- The electricity demand from these data centers is substantial.
