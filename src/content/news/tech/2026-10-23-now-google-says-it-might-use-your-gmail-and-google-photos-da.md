Google has updated its privacy policy, potentially allowing the use of Gmail and Google Photos data for targeted ads. This change applies to users utilizing personalized AI features.

## Key facts
- Google has added an exception to its previous promise regarding the use of data from Drive, Gmail, or Google Photos for personalized ads.
- The update was reported by Android Authority. 
- The change may affect users who engage with personalized AI features.
