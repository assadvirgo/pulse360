Nintendo president Shuntaro Furukawa stated that the company is not planning any further price increases for the Switch 2. This announcement came during the latest earnings call, following a previous price hike of $50 earlier this year.

## Key facts
- Nintendo's Switch 2 currently has a starting price of $499 after a price increase earlier this year.
- The company reported a 37.4% drop in Switch 2 sales.
- Furukawa indicated that there are "no additional changes" being considered for the Switch 2's price, according to Reuters.
