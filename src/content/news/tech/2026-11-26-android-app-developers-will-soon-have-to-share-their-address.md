Google announced that independent Android app developers will be required to share their phone number and address publicly starting next year. This policy change is intended to comply with European Union regulations under the Digital Services Act, but Google stated that the contact details will be made public worldwide.

## Key facts
- Independent Android app developers are concerned about their privacy due to the new requirement.
- The policy change is part of compliance with the European Union's Digital Services Act.
- Google will make the contact details public globally, not just in the EU.
