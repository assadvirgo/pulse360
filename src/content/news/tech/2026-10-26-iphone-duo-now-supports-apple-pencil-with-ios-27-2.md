Apple has released iOS 27.2, which introduces Apple Pencil support for the iPhone Duo. The update also features an "Edge Light" function designed to enhance video call lighting.

## Key facts
- iOS 27.2 adds Apple Pencil support to the iPhone Duo.
- The update includes an "Edge Light" feature for improved face illumination during video calls.
- Apple had previously announced that Apple Pencil support would be added to the iPhone Duo later this year.
