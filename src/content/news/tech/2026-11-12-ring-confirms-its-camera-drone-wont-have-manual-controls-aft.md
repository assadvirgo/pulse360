Ring has confirmed that its security camera drone will not feature manual controls. The drone, set to be released in January, will operate solely using an "AI Pilot" system for navigation.

## Key facts
- The Ring Drone Cam will not allow users to pilot it manually.
- It will rely on an "AI Pilot" system for navigation.
- The drone is expected to follow pre-programmed routes and navigate autonomously around obstacles.
- The announcement follows a demonstration that occurred with CBS.
