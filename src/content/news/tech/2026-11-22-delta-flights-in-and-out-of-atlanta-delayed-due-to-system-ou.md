All Delta flights in and out of Atlanta are experiencing significant delays due to a system issue that calculates a plane's weight and balance. The Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, which is Delta's largest hub, is among the busiest airports globally. 

## Key facts
- Delta flights in and out of Atlanta are facing significant delays.
- The delays are caused by a problem with the system that calculates a plane's weight and balance.
- Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport is Delta's largest hub.
- The FAA stated that the technical problems at the Atlanta hub are not related to a countrywide outage this month.
