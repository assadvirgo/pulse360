The European Union is proposing new regulations aimed at improving online consumer experiences. The Digital Fairness Act (DFA) targets gaming, social media, and e-commerce platforms to address practices that exploit consumers.

## Key facts
- The proposed Digital Fairness Act (DFA) was published by the EU Commission.
- The DFA aims to make online experiences fairer for consumers.
- The regulations focus on practices that encourage excessive spending of time and money online.
