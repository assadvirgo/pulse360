Tamagotchis, tiny digital pets, became an overnight sensation in the late 1990s, captivating millions of children. Many kids either owned a Tamagotchi or were familiar with someone who did, as they engaged in the responsibilities of caring for these virtual creatures.

## Key facts
- Tamagotchis were released in the late 1990s.
- They became extremely popular among children during that time.
- Many kids either owned a Tamagotchi or knew someone who did.
