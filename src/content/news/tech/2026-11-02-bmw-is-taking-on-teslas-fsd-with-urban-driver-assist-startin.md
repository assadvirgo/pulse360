BMW plans to expand its navigation-guided, partially automated driving capabilities into urban environments starting in 2027. The new system will first launch in Germany, with additional markets to follow.

## Key facts
- BMW's new urban driver assist system will help steer, speed up, and slow down vehicles.
- The system will utilize built-in GPS navigation for its operations.
- The rollout is set to begin in Germany before expanding to other markets.
