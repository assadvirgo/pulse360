iFixit has disassembled the latest iPhone Duo, revealing insights into its design and repairability. The teardown highlights how Apple may be concealing the crease on the device's folding screen and assigns a repairability score of 5 out of 10.

## Key facts
- iFixit conducted a teardown of the iPhone Duo.
- The teardown revealed details about the device's folding screen design.
- The iPhone Duo received a repairability score of 5 out of 10, which is noted as the best score for the device series.
