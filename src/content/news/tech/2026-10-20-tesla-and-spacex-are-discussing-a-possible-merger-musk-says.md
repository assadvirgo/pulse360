Tesla and SpaceX are in discussions regarding a potential merger, as stated by Elon Musk during SpaceX's third quarter earnings call. Special committees have been formed at both companies to explore this possibility.

## Key facts
- Special committees have been established by Tesla and SpaceX to discuss a possible merger, according to Musk.
- The committees consist of independent directors and were formed at the request of shareholders.
- Musk indicated that combining the two companies could be beneficial as the world approaches an AI singularity.
- Musk noted that while discussions are ongoing, a merger is not guaranteed.
