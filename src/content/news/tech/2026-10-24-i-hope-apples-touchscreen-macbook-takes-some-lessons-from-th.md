A touchscreen MacBook from Apple may be announced next week, according to The Verge. The article discusses the Lenovo Yoga 9n, which was used for testing Nvidia's RTX Spark platform, and suggests it has features that could inform the design of the upcoming MacBook.

## Key facts
- The long-rumored touchscreen MacBook might be announced next week.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9n was used for testing Nvidia's RTX Spark platform.
- The author expresses a desire for features from the Lenovo Yoga 9n to be included in the touchscreen MacBook.
