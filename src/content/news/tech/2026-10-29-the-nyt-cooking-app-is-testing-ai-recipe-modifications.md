The New York Times is testing a new AI feature for its Cooking app that assists users in modifying recipes. This feature can recommend substitutions for ingredients and cooking equipment, as well as scale recipes.

## Key facts
- The feature was announced by NYT Cooking senior editor Genevieve Ko.
- It aims to help users modify recipes effectively.
- The introduction of the feature has generated some drama among users.
