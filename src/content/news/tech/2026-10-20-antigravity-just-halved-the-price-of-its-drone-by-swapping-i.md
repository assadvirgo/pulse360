Antigravity has halved the price of its A1 drone by replacing its headset control with wristwatch control. The A1 is noted for being beginner-friendly, allowing users with no prior experience to fly it easily. The drone also features the ability to reframe its 360-degree footage after capture.

## Key facts
- Antigravity's A1 drone is designed for beginners.
- The price of the A1 drone has been halved.
- The control method has been changed from a headset to a wristwatch.
- Users can reframe 360-degree footage after filming.
