Kevin Lewandowski, the founder of Discogs, has been influential in creating a comprehensive online platform for vinyl collectors. Discogs serves as a marketplace for buying and selling records and has evolved into a detailed database for various music formats.

## Key facts
- Kevin Lewandowski founded Discogs 25 years ago.
- Discogs is recognized as a key destination for vinyl collectors and music enthusiasts.
- The platform functions as both a marketplace and a comprehensive database for albums, singles, and their various versions.
