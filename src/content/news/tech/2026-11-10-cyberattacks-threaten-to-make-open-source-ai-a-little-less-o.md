Hugging Face executive Irene Solaiman has warned that increasing cyberattacks on open AI platforms may lead to stricter security measures. This could jeopardize the accessibility that is crucial for open source communities and developers globally.

## Key facts
- Irene Solaiman is an executive at Hugging Face.
- There is a rise in cyberattacks targeting open AI platforms.
- Stricter security restrictions could be implemented as a response to these attacks.
- The accessibility of open source communities is at risk due to potential security measures.
