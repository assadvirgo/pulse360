Nvidia is investigating CEO Jensen Huang regarding his relationship with Alice Huang, an executive at Megaspeed, who has been accused of being recruited by China's Ministry of State Security to spy on the company. Huang has confirmed that he was having an affair with her.

## Key facts
- Nvidia has announced an independent investigation into Jensen Huang's relationship with Alice Huang.
- Alice Huang is reportedly linked to China's Ministry of State Security as a potential spy.
- Jensen Huang admitted to having an affair with Alice Huang in a memo to employees.
- The investigation follows allegations of Huang's ties to a Chinese intelligence asset.
- Lawmakers are seeking answers about Huang's connections amid espionage concerns.
