The United Nations failed to reach an agreement on regulations for autonomous weapons, referred to as "killer robots," during a meeting in Geneva. Representatives concluded the discussions by agreeing to "start discussing options" after a week of negotiations that did not yield significant progress.

## Key facts
- UN diplomats met in Geneva to discuss rules for autonomous weapons.
- The negotiations were described as largely fruitless.
- Representatives agreed to begin discussing options moving forward.
