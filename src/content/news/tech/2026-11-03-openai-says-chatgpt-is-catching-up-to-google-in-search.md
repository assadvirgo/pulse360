OpenAI announced that the volume of search queries run through ChatGPT now equals 20 percent of the volume run through Google. This statement was made by Adam Fry, OpenAI's consumer product lead for ChatGPT, at a press event.

## Key facts
- OpenAI reported that "Ask Chat" has become a common phrase, replacing "Google it" in many circles.
- The search queries through ChatGPT now account for 20 percent of those run through Google.
- The announcement was made during a press event on Monday.
