Kobo is set to launch a new monthly subscription service called Stack. This service will allow users to upload their own ebooks and documents to Kobo's servers for syncing across multiple devices and apps.

## Key facts
- Kobo Stack will enable users to upload their own ebooks and documents.
- The service will sync reading progress, highlights, and annotations across devices.
- Kobo Stack is similar to Amazon's Send to Kindle feature.
