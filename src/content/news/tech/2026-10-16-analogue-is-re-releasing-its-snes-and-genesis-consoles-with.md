Analogue has announced new versions of its Super Nt and Mega Sg retro consoles. The updated models feature a reworked industrial design and built-in wireless connectivity for controllers and accessories.

## Key facts
- The new consoles are named Super Nt Plus and Mega Sg Plus.
- They can play Nintendo and Sega Genesis cartridges.
- The consoles utilize an FPGA processor for compatibility with the original games.
