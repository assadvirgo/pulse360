Salon has announced that it is ceasing publication after 31 years of operation. The company cited changes in the business realities of independent digital journalism as a key factor in its decision.

## Key facts
- Salon launched 31 years ago as one of the first online-only news magazine outlets.
- The company stated that its publishing model is "no longer sustainable."
- The announcement was made in an unsigned post.
