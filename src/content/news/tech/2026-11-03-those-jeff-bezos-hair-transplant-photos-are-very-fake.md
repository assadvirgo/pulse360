Amazon founder Jeff Bezos and actor Jason Statham are the subjects of circulating images that falsely depict them with significantly more hair than usual. These images have appeared on social media platforms including X, Threads, and Bluesky.

## Key facts
- The images of Jeff Bezos and Jason Statham show them with more hair than they typically have.
- Both men are known for being bald.
- The photos are being shared on social media platforms X, Threads, and Bluesky.
