Former President Donald Trump stated that Google CEO Sundar Pichai advised him against renaming the Atlantic Ocean. This comment was made during a rally where Trump discussed his previous actions of renaming Lake Ontario and the Gulf of Mexico on Google Maps.

## Key facts
- Trump mentioned he had contacted Sundar Pichai regarding the renaming of the Atlantic Ocean.
- He indicated that he would "probably pass" on the idea of renaming the Atlantic.
- The information was reported by The Verge, referencing earlier reporting by the Washington Post.
