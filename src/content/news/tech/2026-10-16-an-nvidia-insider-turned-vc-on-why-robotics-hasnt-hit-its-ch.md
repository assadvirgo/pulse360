Amulya Vishwanath, a former product marketing leader for NVIDIA’s robotics platforms, describes the current state of robotics as being in its "Lego era." He notes that fundamental components such as actuators, robotic hands, software, and safety standards are still being reconstructed.

## Key facts
- Amulya Vishwanath spent seven years at NVIDIA focusing on robotics platforms.
- He characterizes the current phase of robotics development as the "Lego era."
- Vishwanath emphasizes that basic building blocks of robotics are still being rebuilt.
