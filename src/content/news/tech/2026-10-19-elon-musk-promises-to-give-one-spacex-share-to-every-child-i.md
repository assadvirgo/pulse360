Elon Musk announced plans to give one of his SpaceX shares to every child in the US starting next year. This initiative could involve distributing shares to approximately 72 million children, amounting to an estimated value of $10.5 billion.

## Key facts
- Musk plans to give one SpaceX share to every child in the US.
- The initiative is set to begin next year.
- There are around 72 million children in the US as of July 2025.
- The total value of the shares to be given away is estimated at $10.5 billion, according to a report from CNBC.
