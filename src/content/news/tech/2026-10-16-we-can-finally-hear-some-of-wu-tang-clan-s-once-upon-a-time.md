Wu-Tang Clan's album "Once Upon A Time in Shaolin" has been largely inaccessible, but listeners can now hear two snippets from the album. This development comes from an episode of the podcast "Pablo Torre Finds Out," which focuses on the exclusive record.

- Two snippets of Wu-Tang Clan's "Once Upon A Time in Shaolin" album are now available to listen to.
- The snippets were shared in an episode of the podcast "Pablo Torre Finds Out."
