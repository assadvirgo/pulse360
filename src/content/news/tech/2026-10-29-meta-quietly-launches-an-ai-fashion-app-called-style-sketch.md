Meta has launched an AI fashion app called Style Sketch. The app integrates with Instagram to create a virtual closet using photos users have shared, and it also allows users to manually add items by taking photos, recording videos, or uploading images from their camera roll.

## Key facts
- The app is named Style Sketch.
- It connects to Instagram to build a virtual closet.
- Users can add items manually by snapping a photo, recording a video, or uploading images from their camera roll.
