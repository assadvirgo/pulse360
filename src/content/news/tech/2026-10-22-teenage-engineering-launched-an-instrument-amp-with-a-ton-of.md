Teenage Engineering has launched the EP-35, a new multi-input, battery-powered instrument amplifier featuring built-in effects. The amp is designed for various instruments and includes multiple input options.

## Key facts
- The EP-35 is a multi-input, battery-powered amp.
- It features built-in effects for instruments.
- The back of the unit includes a line-in port, a 1/4-inch guitar input, and an XLR input for microphones.
- The XLR input supports mics that require phantom power.
