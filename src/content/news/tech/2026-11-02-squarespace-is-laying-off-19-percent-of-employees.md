Squarespace is laying off 19 percent of its employees as part of a restructuring effort. The job cuts will impact approximately 300 of the company's 1,800 employees.

## Key facts
- Squarespace is laying off 19 percent of its workforce.
- The layoffs will affect around 300 employees.
- The company aims to simplify its operations through these job cuts.
- The layoffs are part of a broader restructuring of the organization.
