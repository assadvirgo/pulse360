The NBA announced new "preferred" prediction market deals with Kalshi and Polymarket in response to recent gambling scandals.

## Key facts
- The NBA has introduced new gambling partnerships with Kalshi and Polymarket.
- The announcement was made on a Monday.
- The NBA previously partnered with FTX Arena and NBA Top Shot NFT marketplace.
- Sales for NBA Top Shot dropped to $762,781, a 99.66 percent decrease from its peak in February 2021.
