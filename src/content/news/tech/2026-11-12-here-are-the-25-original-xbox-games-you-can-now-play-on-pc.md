Microsoft has announced that 25 original Xbox games are now available to play on Windows PC. This move is part of their "next-gen game preservation" initiative, coinciding with the 25th anniversary of Xbox.

## Key facts
- Microsoft is making 25 original Xbox games playable on Windows PC.
- Users need to have or purchase digital copies of the games.
- This initiative is part of Microsoft's promise for game preservation.
