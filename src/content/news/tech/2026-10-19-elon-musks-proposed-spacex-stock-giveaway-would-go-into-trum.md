Elon Musk announced plans to give one of his SpaceX shares to every child in America next year. President Donald Trump stated that these shares would be distributed through "Trump Accounts."

## Key facts
- Elon Musk plans to give one SpaceX share to every child in America.
- The giveaway is set to take place sometime next year.
- President Donald Trump announced that the shares will be given through "Trump Accounts."
- Musk thanked Trump Account booster Brad Gerstner for his support in enabling the giveaway.
