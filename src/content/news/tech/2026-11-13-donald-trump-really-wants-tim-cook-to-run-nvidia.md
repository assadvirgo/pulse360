Jensen Huang resigned as CEO of Nvidia, and the company is currently led by its CFO on an interim basis. President Donald Trump has suggested that former Apple CEO Tim Cook should take over the role.

## Key facts
- Jensen Huang resigned as CEO of Nvidia.
- The resignation follows reports of affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy.
- Nvidia is currently being led by its CFO on an interim basis.
- President Donald Trump has proposed Tim Cook as a potential successor.
