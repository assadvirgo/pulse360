Buying the 4K Blu-ray disc version of KPop Demon Hunters is now available for $24.99 at Barnes and Noble, down from its original price of $49.95. This price point is lower than a month of Netflix Premium, which costs $26.99.

- The original price of the 4K Blu-ray was $49.95.
