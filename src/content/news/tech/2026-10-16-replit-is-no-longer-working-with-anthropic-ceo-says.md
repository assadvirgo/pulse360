Replit has ended its partnership with Anthropic, according to the company's CEO. The decision was influenced by various factors, including cost considerations.

## Key facts
- Replit's CEO stated that the decision to end the partnership with Anthropic was made for several reasons.
- Cost was mentioned as one of the factors in the decision.
- Replit announced a partnership with OpenAI in August.
- There was no indication prior to this announcement that Replit was no longer working with Anthropic.
