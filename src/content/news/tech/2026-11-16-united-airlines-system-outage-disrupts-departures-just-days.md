United Airlines experienced a system outage that disrupted departing flights. This incident occurred less than a week after a nationwide ground stop due to air traffic control communication issues.

## Key facts
- United Airlines faced a technology-related issue affecting some departing flights.
- The outage occurred shortly after a previous FAA ground stop caused by air traffic control problems.
- United announced that the problem was fixed at 6:13 PM ET.
