A federal judge has issued a preliminary injunction preventing the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) from conducting an early review of ABC's broadcast licenses. The judge also rejected the FCC's efforts to dismiss ABC's First Amendment lawsuit.

## Key facts
- US District Judge Loren AliKhan blocked the FCC from reviewing ABC's broadcast licenses early.
- The judge rejected the FCC's efforts to dismiss ABC's First Amendment lawsuit.
- ABC has accused the FCC of violating its free speech rights.
- ABC claims the FCC "waged a retaliatory campaign" against it.
