Dream, a new venture launched by former Palantir executives, has secured $17 million in funding from a16z to develop AI world models specifically for the construction industry. The company's approach is based on real physics and construction standards, utilizing a vast dataset of over 100 million square feet of building data sourced from permit archives across the United States.

## Key facts
- Dream is founded by ex-Palantir executives.
- The company has raised $17 million from a16z.
- Dream's world model is trained on over 100 million square feet of building data.
- The data is gathered from permit archives across the United States.
- The model is grounded in real physics and construction standards.
