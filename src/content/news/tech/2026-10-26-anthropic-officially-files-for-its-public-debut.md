Anthropic has officially filed for an initial public offering (IPO). The filing reveals significant revenue growth, increasing losses, widespread enterprise adoption, and a unique governance structure aimed at maintaining founder control and its commitment to AI safety.

## Key facts
- Anthropic has filed for an IPO.
- The company reported soaring revenue and mounting losses.
- There is major enterprise adoption of its products.
- Anthropic has an unusual governance structure designed to preserve founder control.
- The governance structure also focuses on its AI safety mission.
