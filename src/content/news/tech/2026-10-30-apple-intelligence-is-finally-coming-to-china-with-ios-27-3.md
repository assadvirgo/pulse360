Apple will launch Apple Intelligence in China with iOS 27.3, as reported by The Verge. The features will first be available in an iOS 27.3 beta set to launch on November 4th, followed by a full release scheduled for December.

## Key facts
- Apple announced the launch of Apple Intelligence in China on its Chinese newsroom website.
- The features will be available in an iOS 27.3 beta starting November 4th.
- A full release of iOS 27.3 is scheduled for December.
