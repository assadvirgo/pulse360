Apple will implement new age rating requirements for dating apps and romanceable chatbots, restricting access to users over 18. Developers will need to provide additional details about their apps to comply with these changes.

## Key facts
- Apple will require developers to declare if their apps allow users to share their real-world location.
- The new content descriptors were announced on Monday.
- The restrictions apply to dating apps and romanceable chatbots.
