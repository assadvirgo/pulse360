Netflix is prioritizing monetization in response to reports of declining viewing time. In its third-quarter earnings report, the company highlighted improvements to monetization as a key focus, along with enhancing entertainment value and utilizing new technology.

## Key facts
- Netflix is addressing falling viewing time by making monetization a top priority.
- The company included monetization improvements as a main focus in its third-quarter earnings report.
- Netflix is also aiming to boost entertainment value and leverage new technology.
