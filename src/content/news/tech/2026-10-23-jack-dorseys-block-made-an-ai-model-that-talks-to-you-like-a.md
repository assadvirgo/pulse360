Jack Dorsey's Block has announced the development of an AI model named Dessa, which is designed to engage in conversation similar to that of an awkward new friend. The model has been trained entirely on human conversation, and Block has shared examples of its interactions with humans that can sometimes feel awkward or uncanny.

## Key facts
- Block's AI model is called Dessa.
- Dessa was trained "from scratch on nothing but human conversation."
- The company shared examples of conversations that illustrate the model's awkwardness.
