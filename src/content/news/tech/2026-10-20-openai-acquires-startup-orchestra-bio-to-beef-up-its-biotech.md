OpenAI has acquired the startup Orchestra Bio to enhance its biotech business. The acquisition will integrate Orchestra's team and technology into OpenAI's Rosalind, an AI model designed for life sciences research, along with the Rosalind Workbench tool.

## Key facts
- OpenAI is adding Orchestra's team and technology to its operations.
- The focus will be on Rosalind, an AI model for life sciences research.
- The acquisition also involves the Rosalind Workbench tool, which assists researchers in planning experiments and analyzing results.
