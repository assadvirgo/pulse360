Claude Duet, Anthropic's AI agent for consumers, aims to demonstrate the usefulness of AI agents for non-coders. Unlike typical products, Duet offers assistance in documenting user experiences. 

## Key facts
- Claude Duet is an AI agent developed by Anthropic.
- The product is designed for consumers who do not write code.
- Duet offers to help users write up their experiences with the product.
