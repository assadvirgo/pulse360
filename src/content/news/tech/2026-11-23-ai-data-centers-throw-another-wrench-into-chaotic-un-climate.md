The recent United Nations climate conference, COP31, faced challenges related to the increasing demand for energy from data centers. Delegates discussed the implications of this surge in power consumption on the clean energy revolution, which aims to electrify the global economy. The conference concluded in Antalya, Turkey, with a sense of chaos and dissatisfaction.

## Key facts
- The clean energy revolution aims to electrify the global economy.
- There is a surge in power-hungry data centers.
- Delegates at COP31 grappled with these challenges.
- The conference concluded in Antalya, Turkey.
