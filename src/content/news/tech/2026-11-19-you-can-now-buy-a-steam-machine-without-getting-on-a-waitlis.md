Valve announced updates regarding the availability of Steam Machines, stating that customers can now purchase a 512GB Steam Machine without needing to join a waitlist. Previously, interested buyers were required to sign up for Valve's waitlist to make a purchase.

## Key facts
- Customers can now buy a 512GB Steam Machine without a waitlist.
- Previously, buyers had to join a waitlist to purchase a Steam Machine.
- Valve provided updates on Steam Machine availability.
