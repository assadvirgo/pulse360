Polymarket experienced significant trading activity, with over $600 million in trades related to elections occurring after the outcomes were determined. This trading involved contracts linked to elections in Ethiopia and Romania.

## Key facts
- Polymarket saw $600 million in election trades after the elections were over, according to Ars Technica.
- Trading continued long after the election outcomes were effectively settled.
- Contracts tied to elections in Ethiopia and Romania had massive trading volumes, as reported by Wired.
- An expert suggested that the trading activity might indicate some form of volume manipulation.
