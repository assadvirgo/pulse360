The music gear world is experiencing significant changes due to the rise of vibe coding. This trend is characterized by the development of custom firmware for hardware synthesizers, samplers, and grooveboxes, facilitated by advanced vibe-coding tools.

## Key facts
- There has been an explosion of custom firmware for music hardware.
- This trend is particularly noticeable on platforms like r/synthesizers and YouTube.
- Vibe coding tools have become increasingly sophisticated, enabling this custom firmware development.
