SpaceX announced a new version of its satellite internet terminal, the Starlink Mini 2, which now includes a swappable battery. The device integrates a dish, Wi-Fi router, and battery into a single unit about the size of a laptop.

## Key facts
- The Starlink Mini 2 integrates a dish, Wi-Fi router, and battery.
- The device is approximately the size of a laptop.
- Some users reported the price of the Starlink Mini 2 as $999.
