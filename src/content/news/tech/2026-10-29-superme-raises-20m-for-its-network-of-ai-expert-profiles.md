SuperMe has raised $20 million to enhance its network of AI expert profiles. The platform utilizes the knowledge of real-life experts to create AI profiles that can answer user questions by forming group chats with the most suitable experts.

## Key facts
- SuperMe is a network of AI profiles based on real-life experts' knowledge.
- The platform identifies the best-suited experts to answer user questions.
- It creates group chats with AI profiles of selected experts.
