Nvidia, under cofounder Jensen Huang, has been identified as a significant environmental liability in the tech industry. The company's demand for chips has led to the establishment of water- and chemical-intensive factories, increased strain on power grids, and potential e-waste issues.

## Key facts
- Nvidia has become a major player in the AI boom.
- The company's chip production has resulted in new factories that are water- and chemical-intensive.
- Nvidia's operations have drained power grids due to massive data centers.
- There is a looming e-waste problem associated with Nvidia's products.
