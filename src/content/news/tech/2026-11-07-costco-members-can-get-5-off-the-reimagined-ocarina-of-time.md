Costco members can currently receive a $5 discount on the digital version of the remastered Legend of Zelda: The Ocarina of Time. This brings the price down to $54.99, following other retailers that offered preorder discounts.

## Key facts
- Costco members can get $5 off the digital version of the remastered game.
- The discounted price is $54.99.
- An annual Costco membership costs $65.
