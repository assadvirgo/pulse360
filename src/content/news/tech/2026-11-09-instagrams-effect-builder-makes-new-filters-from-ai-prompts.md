Instagram has launched its Effect Builder, a tool that generates custom filters for Stories posts using AI. This feature is currently available for US residents over 18.

## Key facts
- The Effect Builder allows users to describe their filter ideas in a prompt box.
- Users can attach style elements to their filter ideas.
- The launch follows the expansion of Instagram's Edits app to macOS.
