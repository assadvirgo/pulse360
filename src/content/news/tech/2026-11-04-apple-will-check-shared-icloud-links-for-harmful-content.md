Apple will begin checking iCloud items that are accessible to anyone with the link for "harmful content." This change will take effect with iOS 27.3 and other 27.3 updates.

## Key facts
- Apple will check shared iCloud links for harmful content.
- The new feature will start with iOS 27.3 and other 27.3 updates.
- The checks will apply to items that can be accessed by anyone with the link.
