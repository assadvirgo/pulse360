Google's parent company Alphabet reported that its investments in artificial intelligence are contributing to significant year-over-year growth in search queries. This information was shared during its Q3 2026 earnings announcement.

## Key facts
- Alphabet stated that its AI investments are driving overall search query growth.
- The growth includes an increase in commercial queries.
- CEO Sundar Pichai highlighted that the year-over-year query growth rate is the highest.
