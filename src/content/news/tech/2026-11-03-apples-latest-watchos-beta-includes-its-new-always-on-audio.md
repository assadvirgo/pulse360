Apple has released a new developer beta for watchOS 27.3, which includes new Audio Intelligence features for the Apple Watch. These features allow Siri to utilize the watch's microphone for enhanced functionality.

## Key facts
- The new watchOS 27.3 developer beta is rolling out now.
- Siri can now use "ambient listening" to generate conversation summaries with a feature called Siri Recap.
- Another feature, Live Rewind, generates text transcripts of the last 15 minutes of conversation.
