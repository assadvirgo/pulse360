Adobe has announced a new feature for the mobile version of Lightroom that allows users to open closed eyes in photos using generative AI. This functionality is available for iOS, iPadOS, and Android devices.

## Key facts
- The feature can open closed eyes in photos with a single tap.
- The app detects when a person or group of people have their eyes closed.
- Users are presented with an option to open the eyes automatically.
