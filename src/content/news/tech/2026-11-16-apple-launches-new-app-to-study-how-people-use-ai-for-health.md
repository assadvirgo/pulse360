Apple has launched a new app aimed at studying how people use AI for health-related inquiries. The initiative is part of the Apple Health AI Usage Study, which seeks to improve the effectiveness of conversational AI health tools.

## Key facts
- The app will help answer everyday medical questions.
- The study aims to better understand user interactions with AI health tools.
- Apple is collaborating with OpenEvidence, a clinical AI platform used by healthcare professionals.
