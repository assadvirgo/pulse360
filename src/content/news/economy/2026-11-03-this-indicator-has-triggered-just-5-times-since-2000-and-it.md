The current market setup is considered vulnerable due to rising inflation, which limits central banks' ability to ease rates and reflate asset prices, according to MarketWatch. A macro research house has advised reducing stocks and adding bonds and cash in response to this situation.

## Key facts
- The current market setup is vulnerable compared to previous market drawdowns.
- Rising inflation restricts central banks' ability to ease rates.
- A macro research house recommends reducing stocks and increasing bonds and cash.
