The measure of inflation used by the Federal Reserve rose again in October at an accelerated rate, indicating that the central bank will likely increase borrowing costs next month.

## Key facts
- The inflation measure influencing U.S. interest rates rose in October.
- The increase occurred at an accelerated rate.
- This rise virtually assures a forthcoming increase in borrowing costs by the Federal Reserve.
