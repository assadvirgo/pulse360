Pending home sales in the United States have reached a record low, with sales contracts dropping significantly in September. This decline is attributed to rising borrowing costs, which have led to a slowdown in the housing market.

## Key facts
- Sales contracts dropped 7.3% in September, according to the Financial Times.
- Contract signings to purchase homes fell to the lowest level on record in September, as reported by MarketWatch.
- The average 30-year mortgage rate has crossed over 7%, prompting buyers to exit the housing market.
