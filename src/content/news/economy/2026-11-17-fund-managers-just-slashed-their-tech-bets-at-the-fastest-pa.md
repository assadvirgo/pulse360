Investors are reducing their investments in megacap tech stocks at a rapid pace, the fastest in two decades. They are also becoming less bearish on bonds while maintaining low cash positions despite optimistic global growth forecasts.

## Key facts
- Fund managers are shifting their overweight positioning away from megacap tech.
- Investors are getting less bearish on bonds.
- Cash positions among investors remain exceedingly low.
- Global growth forecasts are described as bullish.
