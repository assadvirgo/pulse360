Democrat Scott Colom has won the U.S. Senate race in Mississippi, marking a historic victory as he becomes the first Black person to represent the state since the Reconstruction era.

## Key facts
- Scott Colom is a Democrat who has been projected to win the U.S. Senate race in Mississippi.
- He will be the first Black person elected to the Senate from Mississippi since Reconstruction.
- CNN and the Associated Press projected Colom's victory.
