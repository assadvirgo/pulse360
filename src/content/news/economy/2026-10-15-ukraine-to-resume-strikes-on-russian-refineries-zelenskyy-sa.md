## Ukraine to Resume Strikes on Russian Refineries, President Zelenskyy States

In a recent interview with the Financial Times, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy announced that Ukraine will resume targeted strikes on Russian oil refineries. This decision comes amid escalating tensions and concerns over the ongoing conflict with Russia, particularly in light of perceived reductions in support from the United States.

### Concerns Over U.S. Intelligence Withdrawal

President Zelenskyy expressed significant frustration regarding the "dangerous" withdrawal of U.S. intelligence sharing, which he believes is critical for Ukraine's defense strategy. The Ukrainian leader emphasized that the loss of vital intelligence could hinder Ukraine's ability to effectively respond to Russian military actions. This sentiment reflects a broader anxiety within Ukraine about the sustainability of international support as the conflict persists.

### Impact of Starlink Service Weakening

In addition to the intelligence concerns, President Zelenskyy pointed out the weakening of the Starlink satellite internet service, which has been instrumental in maintaining communication for Ukrainian forces. The service, provided by SpaceX, has played a crucial role in ensuring connectivity in areas where traditional communication infrastructure has been compromised due to the conflict. The president's remarks suggest that any reduction in this service could have serious implications for operational coordination and situational awareness on the battlefield.

### Strategic Military Decisions

The decision to resume strikes on Russian refineries is part of Ukraine's broader military strategy aimed at disrupting Russia's energy supply and economic stability. Targeting these facilities is seen as a way to undermine Russia's ability to fund its military operations. The strikes are expected to focus on key installations that are vital to Russia's oil production and distribution networks.

### Broader Implications for the Conflict

As the conflict continues, the dynamics of international support for Ukraine remain critical. The U.S. has been a key ally, providing military aid and intelligence to bolster Ukraine's defense capabilities. However, any perceived withdrawal of support could embolden Russian forces and complicate Ukraine's military strategy.

The resumption of strikes on Russian refineries indicates a shift in Ukraine's tactical approach, reflecting a determination to maintain pressure on Russia despite potential challenges in intelligence and communication. The situation remains fluid, and the international community is closely monitoring developments as both sides prepare for potential escalations.

### Conclusion

President Zelenskyy's statements underscore the complexities of the ongoing conflict in Ukraine and the vital role of international support. As Ukraine prepares to resume strikes on Russian refineries, the implications of U.S. intelligence sharing and the reliability of communication services like Starlink will be crucial factors influencing the course of the war. The coming weeks may prove pivotal as Ukraine seeks to navigate these challenges while continuing its defense against Russian aggression.
