Nvidia has hired Joon Kim, a former prosecutor, to lead an independent investigation into whether CEO Jensen Huang was targeted in a spy operation involving China. Kim recently joined the Wachtell Lipton law firm.

## Key facts
- Joon Kim will lead the investigation into the allegations against Jensen Huang.
- Kim was previously the acting head of the US attorney’s office for the Southern District of New York.
- The investigation focuses on Huang's alleged affair with a Chinese spy.
