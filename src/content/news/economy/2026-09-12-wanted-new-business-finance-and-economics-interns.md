## The Economist Seeks Interns for Marjorie Deane Internship Program

In an effort to nurture the next generation of business, finance, and economics professionals, The Economist has announced the opening of applications for its prestigious Marjorie Deane internship program. This initiative aims to provide aspiring journalists with hands-on experience in the field, while also contributing to the publication's commitment to high-quality reporting.

### Internship Overview

The Marjorie Deane internship program is designed for university students and recent graduates who are keen to develop their skills in economic journalism. Interns will have the opportunity to work alongside seasoned editors and reporters at The Economist, gaining invaluable insights into the processes of research, writing, and publication in a fast-paced news environment.

### Application Details

Prospective applicants are encouraged to submit their applications promptly, as the selection process is competitive. The internship typically lasts for three months and is based in London, where interns will be immersed in the dynamic atmosphere of one of the world's leading news organizations. Candidates should possess a strong academic background in economics, finance, or related fields, coupled with a passion for journalism and storytelling.

### The Legacy of Marjorie Deane

The internship is named after Marjorie Deane, a notable figure in the world of journalism who made significant contributions to the field of economic reporting. Her legacy continues to inspire young journalists, and this program serves as a tribute to her commitment to excellence and integrity in journalism.

### Benefits of the Internship

Interns will not only enhance their writing and analytical skills but will also gain exposure to a wide range of topics, including global economic trends, financial markets, and policy analysis. The experience gained during this internship can serve as a strong foundation for a career in journalism, finance, or economics.

Furthermore, The Economist's extensive network provides interns with opportunities to connect with industry professionals, potentially paving the way for future employment or collaborations in the field.

### Conclusion

The Marjorie Deane internship program represents a significant opportunity for young individuals eager to make their mark in the world of business and economics journalism. As the application window opens, aspiring candidates are encouraged to prepare their submissions and take a step toward a rewarding career in one of the most influential sectors of the media landscape.
