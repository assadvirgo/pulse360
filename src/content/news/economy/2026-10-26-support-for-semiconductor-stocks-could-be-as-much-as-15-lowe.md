Morgan Stanley's chief stock-market strategist has indicated that support for semiconductor stocks could be as much as 15% lower than current levels. Despite this caution, he maintains a forecast that the S&P 500 will end the year at 8,000.

## Key facts
- Morgan Stanley's chief stock-market strategist has expressed growing caution regarding semiconductor stocks.
- He forecasts that support for the semiconductor sector could be significantly lower than current levels.
- The strategist continues to predict that the S&P 500 will reach 8,000 by the end of the year, despite the concerns.
