Analysts have identified 13 S&P 500 stocks that could potentially rise up to 70% over the next year. This comes as consensus earnings estimates have continued to increase, particularly for semiconductor manufacturers, whose shares have seen significant declines since the end of September.

## Key facts
- Analysts have identified 13 S&P 500 stocks with potential for a 70% rise.
- Consensus earnings estimates have been increasing.
- Semiconductor manufacturers have experienced significant share declines since late September.
