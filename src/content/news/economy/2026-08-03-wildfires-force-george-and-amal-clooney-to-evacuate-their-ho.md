The wildfires in Europe have been exacerbated by extreme weather conditions, including prolonged heat waves and drought, which have created an environment conducive to rapid fire spread. Countries across the continent, particularly in southern Europe, are grappling with the consequences of climate change, leading to more frequent and severe wildfires. France, along with nations like Greece and Spain, has witnessed extensive damage to both natural landscapes and human settlements.
