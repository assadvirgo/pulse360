Jensen Huang has been reinstated as CEO of Nvidia following discussions over the weekend that involved Donald Trump. The interim chief and senior management were reportedly unaware of these discussions.

## Key facts
- Jensen Huang was reinstated as CEO of Nvidia after being targeted in an alleged foreign intelligence operation, according to Al Jazeera.
- Donald Trump intervened, expressing concerns about the potential loss of Huang's expertise, as reported by MarketWatch.
- The interim chief and senior management at Nvidia were not informed about the discussions regarding Huang's reinstatement, according to the Financial Times.
