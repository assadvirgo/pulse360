Oil prices are nearing levels last seen during the Iran conflict, driven by rising tanker rates and concerns over potential regional conflicts that could disrupt global energy supplies. Wall Street appears to be factoring in the possibility of a full-scale regional conflict, which could lead to further turmoil in financial markets, according to MarketWatch.

## Key facts
- Oil prices are approaching highs seen during the Iran conflict.
- Soaring tanker rates are contributing to the increase in oil prices.
- There are concerns about a full-scale regional conflict affecting global energy supplies.
- Wall Street is pricing in the potential for further turmoil in financial markets.
