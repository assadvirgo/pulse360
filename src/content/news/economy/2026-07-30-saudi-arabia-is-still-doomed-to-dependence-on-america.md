## Saudi Arabia's Ongoing Dependence on the United States

In the complex web of international relations, few partnerships have been as pivotal as that between Saudi Arabia and the United States. Recent developments indicate that this relationship is still heavily influenced by the dynamics of American politics, particularly as they relate to the Middle East. The latest comments from U.S. President Joe Biden have raised questions about the extent to which Saudi Arabia can pursue its ambitions independently of American interests.

### The Context of U.S.-Saudi Relations

Historically, the U.S.-Saudi alliance has been built on mutual interests, primarily centered around oil and security. For decades, Saudi Arabia has relied on American military support and diplomatic backing, while the U.S. has depended on Saudi oil to stabilize global energy markets. However, this relationship has often been fraught with tension, particularly as regional dynamics shift and new players emerge on the global stage.

### Recent Political Developments

President Biden's recent remarks have underscored the precarious nature of Saudi Arabia's geopolitical standing. His comments suggest that the preferences of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu continue to take precedence over the ambitions of Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman. This situation highlights a significant challenge for Riyadh as it seeks to assert itself as a regional leader while navigating the complexities of U.S. foreign policy.

The Israeli-Palestinian conflict remains a significant point of contention. Saudi Arabia has expressed interest in normalizing relations with Israel, a move that could enhance its standing in the region. However, Biden's alignment with Netanyahu's policies may limit Saudi Arabia's ability to pursue this goal effectively. The Crown Prince's aspirations for a more assertive foreign policy may be stymied by the realities of U.S. politics, which often prioritize Israeli interests.

### Economic Implications

The economic ramifications of this dependence are profound. Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 initiative aims to diversify its economy away from oil dependency and foster a more sustainable economic future. However, the success of this initiative is closely tied to the kingdom's geopolitical stability and its relationship with the United States.

As the U.S. continues to wield significant influence in the region, Saudi Arabia's economic reforms may be overshadowed by its reliance on American support. The kingdom's efforts to attract foreign investment and develop its non-oil sectors could be jeopardized if U.S. policy shifts or if regional tensions escalate.

### Looking Ahead

As Saudi Arabia navigates its relationship with the U.S., the kingdom faces a critical juncture. The reliance on American support, particularly in light of shifting political landscapes, poses a challenge to its long-term ambitions. For Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, the path forward will require a careful balancing act—advancing national interests while managing the complexities of a partnership that has historically defined Saudi Arabia's foreign policy.

In conclusion, the enduring dependence on the United States, as highlighted by recent political developments, suggests that Saudi Arabia's aspirations may continue to be constrained by external factors. The kingdom's future will depend on its ability to adapt to these realities while striving for greater autonomy on the global stage.
