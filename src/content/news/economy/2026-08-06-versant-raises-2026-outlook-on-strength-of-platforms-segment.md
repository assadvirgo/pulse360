## Versant Raises 2026 Outlook on Strength of Platforms Segment and Advertising Momentum

Versant, a prominent player in the digital entertainment and sports sectors, has recently adjusted its financial outlook for 2026, citing robust performance in its platforms segment. This upward revision is primarily driven by significant revenue growth from its key businesses, including Fandango and GolfNow, alongside the recent acquisition of Full Swing.

### Strong Performance in Platforms Business

The platforms segment of Versant has demonstrated remarkable resilience and growth, which has been pivotal in the company's revised forecast. Fandango, a leading digital movie ticketing platform, continues to attract a substantial user base, bolstered by its extensive partnerships with theaters and film studios. This growth is complemented by GolfNow, a premier online tee time booking service that has seen increased engagement as more individuals return to outdoor activities post-pandemic.

The integration of Full Swing, a company specializing in golf simulators and technology, is expected to further enhance Versant's offerings in the sports domain. This acquisition aligns with the company's strategy to diversify its portfolio and capitalize on the growing interest in golf and related recreational activities.

### Advertising Momentum

In addition to the strong performance of its platforms, Versant has reported encouraging trends in its advertising segment. As businesses increasingly shift their marketing strategies towards digital platforms, Versant has positioned itself to benefit from this transition. The company's advertising revenue has shown promising growth, driven by innovative campaigns and strategic partnerships with brands looking to reach engaged audiences across its platforms.

### Revised Financial Outlook

As a result of these positive developments, Versant has raised its revenue projections for 2026. The company anticipates that the continued strength of its platforms segment, coupled with the momentum in advertising, will contribute to a more favorable financial outlook. This proactive adjustment reflects Versant's confidence in its business model and the effectiveness of its growth strategies.

### Market Response and Future Prospects

Investors and market analysts have responded positively to Versant's announcement, viewing the revised outlook as a sign of the company's resilience in a competitive landscape. The combination of a solid platforms business and a growing advertising segment positions Versant well for future growth.

Looking ahead, Versant is expected to continue exploring opportunities for expansion, both organically and through strategic acquisitions. The company remains committed to enhancing its user experience and leveraging technology to drive engagement across its platforms.

### Conclusion

In summary, Versant's recent adjustments to its 2026 outlook underscore the strength of its platforms segment and the positive trajectory of its advertising revenue. With the integration of Full Swing and a focus on innovation, Versant is well-equipped to navigate the evolving landscape of digital entertainment and sports, promising a bright future for the company and its stakeholders.
