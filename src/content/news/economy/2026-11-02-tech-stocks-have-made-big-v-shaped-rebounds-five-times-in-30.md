Tech stocks are experiencing a shift in investor sentiment, with Deutsche Bank suggesting that it may be time to increase investments in the sector. The bank's analysis indicates that current positioning among equity investors reflects expectations of slower earnings growth, despite the sector's actual performance.

## Key facts
- Deutsche Bank believes the risk-reward profile for tech stocks is favorable.
- The bank suggests that investors should consider going overweight in tech stocks.
- Current investor positioning is aligned with expectations of slower earnings growth in the tech sector.
