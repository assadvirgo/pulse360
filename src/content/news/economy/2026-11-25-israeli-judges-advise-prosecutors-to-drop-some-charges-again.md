Israeli judges have advised prosecutors to drop some charges against Prime Minister Netanyahu, indicating a low likelihood of a bribery conviction. This recommendation comes as part of ongoing legal proceedings against him.

## Key facts
- Israeli judges have suggested that prosecutors drop some charges against Prime Minister Netanyahu.
- The country's attorney-general was informed that there is little chance of securing a bribery conviction.
- Netanyahu has stated that his trial should conclude following the judges' recommendation regarding one of three cases.
