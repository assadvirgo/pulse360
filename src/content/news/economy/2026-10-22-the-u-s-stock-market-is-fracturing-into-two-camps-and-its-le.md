The U.S. stock market is experiencing significant volatility, with contrasting trends emerging among investors. The artificial-intelligence trade is reportedly struggling, while indicators of market sentiment show heightened fear.

## Key facts
- The artificial-intelligence trade is facing challenges.
- CNN’s “Fear & Greed” index indicates a state of fear among investors.
- The yield on the 10-year Treasury note recorded its largest daily drop since the collapse of Silicon Valley Bank, according to MarketWatch.
